    Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

    SkiWelt ski area, Kitzbühel and Tyrolean villages with meadows and mountains.

    The Brixental lies east of the Wildschönau and is part of the Kitzbühel Alps. Villages such as Hopfgarten, Itter, Brixen im Thale, Westendorf and Kirchberg in Tirol lie along the road towards Kitzbühel. In winter a large part belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, with about 284 kilometres of pistes and 90 lifts one of the largest ski areas in Austria. Brixen im Thale, Westendorf, Hopfgarten, Itter and Söll, among others, are connected.

    Kitzbühel itself is an old town with a well-known skiing history and a lively centre. For families it is mainly interesting for a day of shopping, walking or visiting a mountain. In summer the region is popular with hikers and cyclists, with paths over meadows and to huts. According to the tourist board golfers find several courses around Kitzbühel, two of them in the town centre.

    From Niederau this area can be reached in a little under an hour by car, via Wörgl. It is a good choice for those who want to combine a town with mountain villages, or spend a day on another mountain. Check cable-car times, weather and prices beforehand, as they vary by season.

    What to see and do

    • Kitzbühel as a historic town with skiing tradition
    • Hopfgarten, Itter and Brixen im Thale as villages in the valley
    • Westendorf and Kirchberg in Tirol with mountain lifts
    • Ski area in winter, walking and cycling routes in summer
    • A combination of town and mountain village in one day

    From Niederau

    From Niederau you drive via Wörgl and then along the road through the Brixental towards Kitzbühel. Trains and buses also run along this route, but it takes some planning. Allow a little under an hour, depending on traffic.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Kitzbühel worth a visit with children?

    Yes, especially for a stroll through the town and a mountain trip; it can be busy in peak season.

    What is the SkiWelt?

    A large ski area in the Brixental and surroundings, with about 284 kilometres of pistes and 90 lifts. Check current figures and rules with the organisation.

    Can I play golf here in summer?

    Yes, there are several golf courses around Kitzbühel; seasons usually run from spring to autumn. See also our golf page.
