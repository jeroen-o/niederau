Region

Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

SkiWelt ski area, Kitzbühel and Tyrolean villages with meadows and mountains.

The Brixental lies east of the Wildschönau and is part of the Kitzbühel Alps. Villages such as Hopfgarten, Itter, Brixen im Thale, Westendorf and Kirchberg in Tirol lie along the road towards Kitzbühel. In winter a large part belongs to an extensive ski area with many slopes and lifts.

Kitzbühel itself is an old town with a well-known skiing history and a lively centre. For families it is mainly interesting for a day of shopping, walking or visiting a mountain. In summer the region is popular with hikers and cyclists, with paths over meadows and to huts.

From Niederau this area can be reached in a little under an hour by car. It is a good choice for those who want to combine a town with mountain villages, or spend a day on another mountain. Check cable-car times, weather and prices beforehand, as they vary by season.

What to see and do

  • Kitzbühel as a historic town with skiing tradition
  • Hopfgarten, Itter and Brixen im Thale as villages in the valley
  • Westendorf and Kirchberg in Tirol with mountain lifts
  • Ski area in winter, walking and cycling routes in summer
  • A combination of town and mountain village in one day

From Niederau

From Niederau you drive via Wörgl and then along the road through the Brixental towards Kitzbühel. Trains and buses also run along this route, but it takes some planning. Allow a little under an hour, depending on traffic.

Places in this region

Hopfgarten im Brixental

Brixental village with a gondola up the Hohe Salve

Itter

Small village with Schloss Itter in the Brixental

Brixen im Thale

Village at the highest point of the Brixental, with the Filzalmsee

Westendorf

Flower-filled village below the Choralpe, with the Alpinolino children's area

Kirchberg in Tirol

Lively village in the Brixental, at the mouth of the Spertental

Kitzbühel

Historic town in the Kitzbühel Alps, known for the Hahnenkamm race

Frequently asked questions

Is Kitzbühel worth a visit with children?

Yes, especially for a stroll through the town and a mountain trip; it can be busy in peak season.

What is the SkiWelt?

A large ski area in the Brixental and surroundings; ask the organisation about its current size and rules.
