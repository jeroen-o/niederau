Region

Zillertal

A large, well-known valley with Mayrhofen, Hintertux and plenty of mountain experiences.

The Zillertal is one of the best-known valleys in Tyrol and lies south of the Inn valley. The valley is about 47 kilometres long. Villages such as Fügen, Kaltenbach, Zell am Ziller, Gerlos, Mayrhofen and Hintertux lie along the Ziller river. The valley is known for its mountains, its glacier and its music and traditions.

For families it is a large area with plenty of choice. A narrow-gauge train, the Zillertalbahn, runs between Jenbach and Mayrhofen, and cable cars take you up for walks or skiing. Hintertux is known for its glacier ski area, with about 62 kilometres of pistes up to over 3,250 metres, where according to the lift operator skiing is possible all year. Mayrhofen is a lively village with plenty of places to eat and views. The Zillertal 3000 ski area links the glacier and the lifts of Mayrhofen, among others.

From Niederau the Zillertal is about an hour by car, depending on destination. The far end near Hintertux is further. For a day trip it is wise to choose one village or one mountain. Check opening times and weather beforehand.

What to see and do

  • Mayrhofen as a lively village with cable cars
  • Hintertux with a glacier ski area (about 62 km of pistes)
  • Zell am Ziller and Fügen as villages in the valley
  • Gerlos as a pass village towards Salzburg
  • Zillertalbahn: narrow-gauge train between Jenbach and Mayrhofen

From Niederau

From Niederau you drive to the Inn valley and follow the road towards Jenbach, where the valley begins. Then you follow the road along the Ziller to the south. A train also runs from Jenbach. Approximate driving time one hour, depending on the place.

Places in this region

Fügen

Main village of the lower Zillertal with Spieljoch and the Erlebnistherme

Kaltenbach

Zillertal village with Hochzillertal, the Murmelland and the Höhenstraße

Zell am Ziller

Zillertal brewery and gold village with the Gauderfest and Zillertal Arena

Gerlos

Mountain village at about 1,250 m on the road over the Gerlos Pass

Mayrhofen

Well-known village at the end of the Zillertal, with Penken and Ahorn

Hintertux

Glacier village at the head of the Tuxertal, with skiing all year

Frequently asked questions

Is the Zillertal too far for a day trip?

No, the first villages can be reached in about an hour. Better to choose one destination.

Can I go on the glacier in summer?

At Hintertux this is often possible, but it depends on the lifts and weather. Ask beforehand.

Can I take the train to the Zillertal?

Yes, the Zillertalbahn runs from Jenbach in the Inn valley to Mayrhofen. From the Wildschönau it takes some planning; check the timetable.
