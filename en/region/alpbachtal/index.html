    Region

    Alpbachtal

    Flower-filled mountain village Alpbach and the valleys around Reith, Brixlegg and Kramsach.

    The Alpbachtal lies next to the Wildschönau and shares the Ski Juwel ski area with it; the Wiedersbergerhorn (2,128 metres) is the mountain on the Alpbach side. The best-known village is Alpbach, at about 1,000 metres, known for its solid timber houses with flowers and named Austria's most beautiful village in the 1980s. Reith im Alpbachtal, Brixlegg, Rattenberg and Kramsach also belong to the wider region.

    For families there is plenty of variety. With about 400 inhabitants Rattenberg is the smallest town in Austria, a medieval town on the Inn with glassblowers; in Advent there is a Christmas market. Near Kramsach lie the Museum Tiroler Bauernhöfe with about 37 relocated original buildings, and the Reintaler See, one of the warmest swimming lakes in Tyrol. Alpbach has walking and cycling routes and is a base in winter for the slopes.

    From Niederau the Alpbachtal is roughly half an hour's drive, depending on the place. That makes it an easy day trip, for example a small town combined with a lake. Check opening times of museums and attractions beforehand, as they differ by season.

    What to see and do

    • Alpbach with its timber houses and flowers
    • Rattenberg, Austria's smallest town, with glass crafts
    • Museum Tiroler Bauernhöfe in Kramsach
    • Reintaler See as a warm swimming lake
    • Ski Juwel as a ski area shared with the Wildschönau

    From Niederau

    From Niederau you drive via Wörgl or Kundl to the Inn valley and then towards Brixlegg or Reith. The route to Alpbach also goes through the Inn valley. Driving time is about half an hour, depending on destination and traffic.

    Places in this region

    Alpbach

    Tyrol's flower village at about 1,000 m, known for its timber buildings

    Reith im Alpbachtal

    Village at the entrance of the Alpbachtal, with a lake and the Juppi Zauberwald

    Brixlegg

    Market town in the Inn Valley with mining history and a museum

    Rattenberg

    Austria's smallest town, known for glass and Inn-Salzach houses

    Kramsach

    Village with an open-air museum and small lakes by the Inn

    Kundl

    Inn Valley village, start of the Kundler Klamm gorge to the Wildschönau

    Münster

    Quiet village in the Tyrolean lowlands, near Lake Zireiner and the Rofan range

    Radfeld

    Inn valley village next to Rattenberg, Austria's smallest town

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Alpbach known for?

    For its well-preserved village appearance with timber houses and flowers, and its mountain setting.

    Is the Alpbachtal a good day trip with children?

    Yes, thanks to the mix of nature, small towns and museums; do not plan too much in a day.

    Where can I swim in summer?

    Near Kramsach lies the Reintaler See, one of the warmest swimming lakes in Tyrol. Pay attention to local rules and supervision.
