Region

Inn valley

The broad main valley of Tyrol with Innsbruck, Hall, Schwaz and Wörgl.

The Inn valley is the broad main valley of Tyrol, where many people live and the main roads run. Towns along the Inn include Wörgl, Jenbach, Schwaz, Hall in Tirol and Innsbruck. The Wildschönau, the Zillertal and the Alpbachtal are all side valleys of it.

For families it is a good region for day trips. Innsbruck has an old town centre, museums and mountain railways close to the centre. Hall in Tirol is smaller and cosy, with a medieval core. Schwaz is known for its silver-mining past, and Wattens has a well-known crystal attraction. Distances between these places are short.

From Niederau, Wörgl is roughly a quarter to half an hour by car and Innsbruck about an hour. That makes the Inn valley a handy base for combining several destinations. The motorway can be busy; ask about the vignette and traffic information.

What to see and do

  • Innsbruck with an old town and mountain railways
  • Hall in Tirol with a medieval core
  • Schwaz with its silver-mining past
  • Wattens with a crystal attraction
  • Wörgl and Jenbach as rail and road hubs

From Niederau

From Niederau you drive to Wörgl and then take the A12 towards Innsbruck. Trains run along the whole Inn and buses run from the valley to Wörgl. Approximate driving time to Innsbruck is one hour.

Places in this region

Wörgl

Lively town in the Inn valley, handy as a stop and for a rainy day.

Jenbach

Railway town in the Inn valley, gateway to the Achensee and Zillertal.

Schwaz

Old mining town in the Inn valley with silver heritage and a show mine.

Wattens

Small Inn Valley town known for Swarovski Kristallwelten

Hall in Tirol

Medieval town with a historic old town, Hasegg Castle and the mint

Innsbruck

The Tyrolean capital with the Golden Roof, old town and mountains close by

Frequently asked questions

Is Innsbruck a good day trip with children?

Yes, thanks to the mix of old town, museums and nearby mountains. Parking in town is often paid.

Is the Inn valley busy?

The motorway can be busy in holidays and at weekends. Set off early or choose the train.
