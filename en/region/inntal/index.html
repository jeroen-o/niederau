    Region

    Inn valley

    The broad main valley of Tyrol with Innsbruck, Hall, Schwaz and Wörgl.

    The Inn valley is the broad main valley of Tyrol, where many people live and the main roads run. Towns along the Inn include Wörgl, Jenbach, Schwaz, Hall in Tirol and Innsbruck. The Wildschönau, the Zillertal and the Alpbachtal are all side valleys of it.

    For families it is a good region for day trips. Innsbruck has an old town centre, museums and mountain railways close to the centre. Hall in Tirol is smaller and cosy, with a medieval core; the town grew rich on salt and gained the right of coinage. Schwaz is known for its silver mine, where a mine train takes you about 800 metres into the mountain. Wattens, about 20 kilometres east of Innsbruck, has the Swarovski Kristallwelten. Distances between these places are short.

    From Niederau, Wörgl is roughly a quarter to half an hour by car and Innsbruck about an hour. That makes the Inn valley a handy base for combining several destinations. The motorway can be busy; a vignette is needed for Austrian motorways (in 2026 a 10-day vignette costs € 12.80), so ask about the current rate and traffic information.

    What to see and do

    • Innsbruck with an old town and mountain railways
    • Hall in Tirol with a medieval core, salt and mint
    • Schwaz with a silver mine and mine train
    • Wattens with the Swarovski Kristallwelten
    • Wörgl and Jenbach as rail and road hubs

    From Niederau

    From Niederau you drive to Wörgl and then take the A12 towards Innsbruck. Trains run along the whole Inn and buses run from the valley to Wörgl. Approximate driving time to Innsbruck is one hour.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Innsbruck a good day trip with children?

    Yes, thanks to the mix of old town, museums and nearby mountains. Parking in town is often paid.

    Is the Inn valley busy?

    The motorway can be busy in holidays and at weekends. Set off early or choose the train.

    What is there to see in Schwaz and Hall?

    In Schwaz you can visit the silver-mining heritage, in Hall the medieval old town. Check opening times beforehand.
