Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Kufstein fortress, the Wilder Kaiser mountains and lakes for swimming.

The Kufsteinerland lies at the German border, north of the Wildschönau, along the Inn. The town of Kufstein is known for its fortress on a rock above the town. There are also villages such as Söll, Ellmau, Going, Walchsee and Thiersee, and lakes where you can swim in summer.

The Wilder Kaiser is a distinctive limestone range with sharp peaks. It looks rugged and attracts hikers, climbers and cyclists. In winter part of it belongs to a large ski area. For families the lakes and short walks are often the most suitable, while higher routes require more experience.

From Niederau the Kufsteinerland is roughly half an hour to three quarters of an hour by car, depending on destination. That makes it a fine day trip, for example combining a visit to the fortress with a lake. Check opening times and the weather forecast before you leave.

What to see and do

  • Kufstein with its fortress above the town
  • Wilder Kaiser with distinctive limestone cliffs
  • Walchsee and Thiersee with swimming options in summer
  • Söll and Ellmau as villages at the foot of the mountains
  • Walking, cycling and skiing in different seasons

From Niederau

From Niederau you drive to the Inn valley and then along the A12 or the road beside the Inn towards Kufstein. From Kufstein you reach most villages and lakes in a short time. Trains also run to Kufstein. Approximate driving time half to three quarters of an hour.

Places in this region

Söll

Village by the Wilder Kaiser with Hexenwasser and the Hohe Salve

Ellmau

Village at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser, known from the TV series Der Bergdoktor

Kössen

Quiet village in the Kaiserwinkl, close to the German border

Walchsee

Village on a warm lake in the Kaiserwinkl, popular with families.

Thiersee

Quiet valley near Kufstein, known for its Passion Play.

Kufstein

Town on the Inn with an imposing fortress and a pleasant centre.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do in Kufstein?

You can visit the fortress, walk through the old town centre and stroll along the Inn. Check opening times beforehand.

Can you swim in the lakes?

In summer this is possible in several places, often at a lido. Pay attention to local rules and supervision.
