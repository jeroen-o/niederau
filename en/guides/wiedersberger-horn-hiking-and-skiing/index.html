    Wiedersberger Horn near Alpbach: hiking, skiing and ski touring to 2,127 m

    The Wiedersberger Horn above Alpbach: an easy summit hike, ski slopes and ski touring.

    The Wiedersberger Horn (2,127 m) near Alpbach is a mountain for hiking in summer and skiing in winter, and makes a good day out from Niederau. Take the gondola and you start at about 1,800 m, from where a panorama path leads to the summit in roughly 1.5 to 2 hours, about 280 metres of ascent. That makes hiking the Wiedersberger Horn doable for families too.

    The mountain lies in the Alpbachtal and is part of the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area, which you also use from Niederau. If you drive, allow roughly half an hour to 45 minutes from Niederau (approximate).

    In summer you will find an adventure playground and an alpine coaster at the top station. In winter, ski tourers climb on skis, for example from the Greiter Graben. Below you will find what you can do in each season, how to get there and what to watch out for. This is an overview, not a route description to follow blindly.

    Tips

    • Take the gondola: it saves about 700 metres of ascent and makes the summit feasible with children.
    • Check the Wiedersbergerhornbahn timetable for your date beforehand.
    • In good weather plan a stop at Lauserland and the alpine coaster.
    • Bring a windproof jacket: it is often windy on the ridge.
    • Ski touring: check the Tyrol avalanche bulletin and preferably go with a guide.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    About the mountain

    At 2,127 m the Wiedersberger Horn is one of the higher summits around the Alpbachtal and is easy to recognise from the valley. On the mountain side lies a ridge of alpine pastures with huts and grazing cattle. It is not an alpine climbing peak: according to sources the summit can be reached without major technical difficulty, also by children, although it is still high and the weather can turn quickly. Because the ski area belongs to the Ski Juwel, the mountain has slopes in winter. Anyone who wants the summit can also reach it on skis in winter. Height differences vary greatly by route.

    Getting there

    The Wiedersbergerhornbahn is a gondola rising from Alpbach (Inneralpbach). The top station is at roughly 1,800 to 1,850 m, depending on the source. From Niederau you drive there in roughly half an hour to 45 minutes; that is an estimate and depends on traffic and route. The lift's operating period differs between summer and winter. Check the current schedule with the lift company beforehand. For anyone who wants to skip the first 700 metres of ascent by lift, this is the most relaxed way to reach the summit.

    Summer: hiking

    The easiest route to the summit starts at the top station. A panorama path takes you to the top in roughly 1.5 to 2 hours, with about 280 metres of ascent. There is also a circular hike of about 6 km with roughly 330 metres of ascent via the summit and the Hochstand. A route along the south ridge leads to the 2,127 m top. The paths are considered well walkable but remain mountain trails: sturdy shoes and weather gear are needed. Easy walks around the Hornalm and the playground suit younger children.

    Summer: play at the top station

    Next to the top station, in the forest, lies the Lauserland adventure playground, where children can play while parents take a break. There is also the Lauser-Sauser, an alpine coaster on rails that according to sources runs in both seasons. Children from 8 years and 1.30 m may ride alone; from age 3 a child may ride as a passenger. According to one source you can borrow a child carrier free of charge against a deposit at the base station. Ask the lift company about current rules and the season.

    Winter: skiing

    In winter the Wiedersbergerhornbahn serves the slopes on the Wiedersberger Horn, which belong to the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area. One ski pass therefore also gets you into this part of the area from Niederau. How exactly the slopes run and their grades are best checked on the current Ski Juwel piste map. I found no specific tobogganing information for winter; ask the lift company if you want to toboggan with children.

    Winter: ski touring

    There is more than one way to ski tour up the Wiedersberger Horn. A well-known route starts in the Greiter Graben and involves about 1,037 metres of ascent and 2.5 to 3 hours of climbing. A variant close to the pistes from Inneralpbach has around 1,100 metres of ascent. Another route runs via the Moserbaumgartenalm. If you leave your skis at the Horn Hütte (2,025 m), you have to walk the often wind-blown ridge on foot. The descent largely follows the ascent. Conditions vary greatly.

    Huts and refreshments

    Around the top and the top station there are several places to eat and drink, including the Dauerstoaalm, the Hornboden mountain restaurant and the Hornalm. There is also a kiosk at the top station. On tours you can stop at the Horn Hütte (2,025 m). Opening times differ by season and year. Check beforehand with the hut concerned, and always bring water.

    Safety

    For ski touring, always consult the Tyrol avalanche bulletin (lawine.tirol.gv.at). This guide does not replace it and is not a route description to follow blindly, especially if you do not know the route. Avalanche equipment and touring experience are needed; do not go alone. In summer the usual mountain rules apply: good shoes, weather gear, an early start and turning back in thunderstorms. It can be very windy on the ridge. Check the last gondola ride.

    Frequently asked questions

    How high is the Wiedersberger Horn?

    The summit is at 2,127 m. The gondola's top station is at about 1,800 m.

    Can you hike to the Wiedersberger Horn summit with children?

    Yes, from the top station the route is described as family-friendly and takes about 1.5 to 2 hours, around 280 metres of ascent. Bring weather gear and watch for thunderstorms.

    How far is the Wiedersberger Horn from Niederau?

    By car to Alpbach roughly half an hour to 45 minutes, approximately. The gondola then takes you up.

    Is ski touring possible in winter?

    Yes, there are several routes, including from the Greiter Graben. Consult the Tyrol avalanche bulletin and go with a guide if in doubt.
