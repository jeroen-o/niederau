    Ski rental Niederau: every rental shop with address and phone

    Hiring skis, snowboards, cross-country skis and sledges in Niederau, Oberau and Auffach

    Anyone travelling to Niederau by train or plane rarely brings their own skis. Hiring in the Wildschönau is straightforward: the shops sit in the village centres and at the lift base stations, within walking distance of most hotels and apartments. Below they are listed village by village, with address, phone number and what public sources say they rent out.

    This is an independent and non-exhaustive list. A listing is not a recommendation and no payment was made for it. Addresses, opening hours and rates are taken from the official regional sources of Wildschönau Tourismus and Ski Juwel and are subject to change: check them with the shop before you travel.

    Thierbach and Mühltal

    As far as is known, neither Thierbach nor Mühltal has its own ski rental. If you are staying there, hire in Auffach or Niederau and take the ski bus. Allow an extra half hour on the first morning: fitting and adjusting boots takes longer than people expect.

    What does it cost?

    Rates are deliberately not listed here: they change every season and differ per shop, and an out-of-date figure on a website is worse than none. Prices are on request from the shop. What drives the price can be set out, though:

    • Equipment class. Almost every shop works with two or three levels: basic, advanced and premium. The difference lies in the age of the ski and how precisely it holds an edge.
    • Rental period. From about four days on, a week package usually beats separate days. Ask for both rates.
    • Boots and helmet. Often priced separately. As far as is known, Tyrol has no legal helmet requirement, not even for children, whereas most other Austrian federal states do. Hire one anyway, certainly for children.
    • Damage cover. A small daily surcharge covering breakage and theft. Check first whether your travel or liability insurance already covers it.
    • Discounts. At Blachfelder the Wildschönau Card gives seven days of rental for the price of six. Margreiter, Thaler and Ski Auffach state the same 7-for-6 rule for rental equipment, and sledge rental is discounted at several shops. With the Wildschönau Card Winter, one day of snowshoe rental is included at Margreiter and Thaler.

    Rates and conditions are those of the shops themselves. This site does not sell or broker rental equipment, and no rights can be derived from the information above.

    Getting the most out of it

    Go at a quiet moment

    Saturday afternoon and Sunday morning are the busiest, because that is when holiday guests change over. If you arrive on a Saturday, pick up your gear in the late afternoon rather than straight after arrival.

    Bring your own socks and insoles

    One pair of thin ski socks beats two thick pairs. If you use your own insoles, bring them to the fitting: boots feel different with the footbed you are used to.

    Be honest about weight and ability

    The binding setting is calculated from height, weight, boot size and ability. Set too high, the ski will not release in a fall; set too low, it releases on the piste.

    When will you need the equipment?

    In the 2026/27 season the Schatzbergbahn in Auffach runs from 4 December 2026 to 4 April 2027. The Markbachjochbahn and Lanerköpflbahn in Niederau run from 18 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, and the Oberau training lifts and the Riedmann lifts at Roggenboden until roughly mid-March. In the first and last weeks of the season it is worth calling before committing to a week's hire.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need to book ski rental in Niederau in advance?

    In the busy weeks, the Dutch and German Christmas holidays and the February half-term, booking ahead is wise: the common sizes go first. Outside those weeks you can usually walk in on the day. Call or e-mail the shop to ask whether reservation is possible.

    What does ski rental in Niederau cost?

    Rates are on request from the shop. The price depends on the equipment class, the rental period, whether you add boots and a helmet, and whether you take damage cover. Always compare the daily rate with the weekly rate: most shops apply seven days for the price of six.

    Does the Wildschönau Card give a discount on ski rental?

    At Sport Blachfelder the Wildschönau Card gives seven days of rental for the price of six. With the Wildschönau Card Winter, one day of snowshoe rental is included at Ski & Board Margreiter and Skisport Thaler. Check the exact conditions with the shop.

    Is there ski rental in Thierbach or Mühltal?

    As far as is known, no. Guests staying there take the ski bus to Auffach or Niederau and hire there. Allow some extra time on the first morning.

    Can I also hire sledges, cross-country skis or snowshoes?

    Yes. Sledges can be hired at Sport Blachfelder, Ski & Board Margreiter and Skisport Thaler, among others. Ski & Board Margreiter rents cross-country skis. With the Wildschönau Card Winter, one day of snowshoe rental is included at Margreiter and Thaler.
