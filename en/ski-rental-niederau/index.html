    Ski rental in Niederau, Oberau and Auffach: where to rent skis

    Ski, snowboard and cross-country ski rental in the Wildschönau: who, where and what to look out for

    Anyone travelling to Niederau by train or plane rarely brings their own skis. Fortunately renting in the Wildschönau is easy: many rental shops are close to the valley stations of the lifts and in the village centre. On this page you will find the rental shops named in public sources, by village, with what you can rent there according to those sources.

    This is an independent, non-exhaustive list. A listing is not a recommendation and nothing was paid for it. Always check address, times, prices and booking with the rental shop itself.

    Rental by village

    Niederau

    Sport Blachfelder is in the village centre, in Hotel Austria. According to the sources they rent skis, snowboards and touring skis, as well as sleds and bikes. Ski & Board Margreiter is by the car park and lift of the Markbachjoch and rents skis, snowboards, cross-country skis, sleds, snowblades, boots and helmets.

    • Sport Blachfelder website

    Oberau

    In Oberau Ski & Board Margreiter also has a branch; according to the sources it rents skis, snowboards and cross-country skis.

    Auffach

    At the valley station of the Schatzbergbahn is Ski & Board Margreiter, with rental, ski service and a ski and boot depot. Ski Auffach, at the end of the slope, combines rental, ski service, a shop and a ski school. Skisport Thaler is a family business with sports goods and winter rental.

    Thierbach and Mühltal

    In Thierbach and Mühltal we found no rental shop of its own. Guests take the ski bus to Auffach or Niederau.

    Tips for renting

    • Book ahead if you can. Margreiter and Sport Blachfelder mention online booking; it is busy in school holidays.
    • Ask about a ski depot if you do not want to carry your gear to your accommodation every day. Margreiter in Auffach mentions a ski and boot depot.
    • Bring height, weight and shoe size, and ask about a helmet for children. Always let the rental shop set the bindings.
    • Choose a rental shop close to the lift you use. Many are right by the valley stations, which saves carrying.
    • Rental and a ski pass are two separate things: the ski pass is not included in the rental.

    Prices, opening times and conditions change each season and are deliberately not listed.

    Wildschönau Card and rental discount

    According to the listings of Wildschönau Tourismus the Wildschönau Card gives you seven days of rental for the price of six at several rental shops (including Blachfelder, Margreiter, Thaler and Ski Auffach), and snowshoe rental is sometimes included. You get the card from participating accommodations. Check the current conditions with the rental shop and your accommodation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where can I rent skis in Niederau?

    In the village centre at Sport Blachfelder (in Hotel Austria) and at the Markbachjoch at Ski & Board Margreiter.

    Can I rent skis in Oberau or Auffach?

    Yes. In Oberau Margreiter has a branch. In Auffach there are Margreiter at the valley station of the Schatzbergbahn, Ski Auffach and Skisport Thaler.

    Can I also rent cross-country skis or snowshoes?

    According to the sources Margreiter rents cross-country skis. Snowshoe rental is mentioned at several shops; ask beforehand.

    Is the ski pass included in the rental?

    No. Rental and ski pass are separate purchases. Ask Ski Juwel or your accommodation about the ski pass.
