Ski Juwel season opening 2026/27

First lifts run on 4 December 2026, the season lasts until 4 April 2027

4 Dec 2026 - 4 Apr 2027

The Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area, of which Niederau is part, starts the 2026/27 winter season on 4 December 2026. On that day part of the lifts will run. These include the Schatzbergbahn in Auffach, the Wiedersbergerhornbahn in Alpbach and the Pöglbahn. Other parts, such as the link between the Alpbachtal and the Wildschönau and the Reitherkogelbahn, are planned to follow in mid-December.

The season runs until about 4 April 2027, depending on snow. Regular operating hours are generally around 8.30 am to 4.30 pm. Whether a particular lift or slope is open on a given day depends on snowfall and grooming, especially in the first weeks.

So if you come to Niederau in December you can already ski, but not always across the whole area. Before you leave, check the ski area's website for current operating times and snow reports, and confirm with your accommodation what is open. Changes caused by the weather are always possible. As the Christmas period follows quickly, it is wise to arrange lift passes, ski school and any equipment rental well before you leave.

Place: Auffach

Tips

  • Check the Ski Juwel operating times and snow report on the day you travel.
  • Plan your first ski days on the slopes by the Schatzbergbahn, Wiedersbergerhornbahn and Pöglbahn.
  • If you want to use the whole area with the link, choose a period from mid-December.
  • Build up gently: slopes can be harder and narrower in December.
  • Ask the ski school and childcare beforehand whether lessons are already running in the first week.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

What opens on 4 December?

At the start on 4 December 2026 the lifts running include the Schatzbergbahn in Auffach, the Wiedersbergerhornbahn in Alpbach and the Pöglbahn. That means slopes are available in both valleys, but the two areas are not yet linked. For families it means that in the first days you will mainly ski on the slopes by the open lifts. Exactly which slopes are open depends on snow. Look at the current report on the day itself.

What follows in mid-December?

In mid-December more sections are added, including the link between the Alpbachtal and the Wildschönau and the Reitherkogelbahn. Once the link is running you can ski from one valley to the other and both areas form a single whole again: the Ski Juwel. The exact date depends on snow and on the lift company's planning. If you want to see the whole area, the safest bet is to plan from the Christmas holidays onwards. Even then the exact date can shift with snow conditions.

Season, operating times and snow

According to plan the season runs until 4 April 2027, with the final weeks depending on snow. The lifts normally run from about 8.30 am to 4.30 pm. In peak periods around Christmas, carnival and February it can be busier, especially at the valley stations. The area has snowmaking, but in mild temperatures some sections can still stay closed. Check the current operating times and the Ski Juwel snow report beforehand. Also ask about the current snow depth before you set off.

Tips for the first ski days

The start of the season is a good time for those seeking calm: the slopes are often less busy and accommodation prices are usually lower than around the holidays. Do bear in mind that not everything is open yet and that slopes in December can be narrower or harder. Rent or check your equipment early, dress warmly and build up gently, especially with children. Ski school and childcare are not always available from day one; ask beforehand.

Frequently asked questions

When does the 2026/27 ski season start in Ski Juwel?

On 4 December 2026 with part of the lifts; more sections follow in mid-December. Check the current dates with the ski area.

Until when can you ski?

According to plan the season runs until 4 April 2027, depending on snow.

Is the whole ski area open straight away?

No. At the start only part is open and the link between the Alpbachtal and the Wildschönau follows in mid-December. The exact moment depends on snow.
