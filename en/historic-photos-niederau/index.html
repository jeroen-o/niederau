    Historic photos of Niederau: archives and sources

    Where to find old photos and postcards of Niederau and the Wildschönau.

    Old photos show how Niederau changed from a remote farming valley into a winter sports village. This page collects where you can find them. We only show images from external archives when the licence allows it, with credit; otherwise you open them at the source, where the terms of use are also given.

    Digital archive volare (Vorarlberg State Library)

    Volare is the digital archive of the Vorarlberg State Library (Vorarlberger Landesbibliothek). If you search it for ‘Niederau’, the search results show, among other things, historic photographs from the Wildschönau, for example from the Risch-Lau collection. The terms differ per item: some photos carry an open licence, for others you must first accept the terms of use or no licence is stated. Read the credit and the licence for each photo before reusing an image. An example with an open licence is shown above.

    • Volare: search results for ‘Niederau’

    Facebook group ‘Kufstein in alten Bildern’

    In this group, members share old photos and postcards from Kufstein and the surrounding area, including Niederau and the Wildschönau. We use a few images from it on this site, with credit.

    • Search the group for ‘Niederau’

    Historic photos on this site

    You will find a selection of old photos of the village, the lifts and the Markbachjoch in the history section of the Markbachjoch page and on the pages about the villages of the Wildschönau.

    Share photos or report a mistake

    Do you have old photos of Niederau of your own that you would like to share, or do you spot a mistake in a caption? Email jeroen@oversteegen.nl. Only send photos if you hold the rights and all recognisable people agree.
