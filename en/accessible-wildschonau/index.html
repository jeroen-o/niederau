    Accessible holiday in the Wildschönau

    Wheelchair or limited mobility? What the tourist board reports on accessible accommodation, restaurants and excursions.

    The Wildschönau tourist board has bundled the information for guests with a disability. This page gives a summary in our own words. It is an overview, not a guarantee: how accessible a place is depends on your situation, so always call the provider beforehand.

    If you travel in a wheelchair, check the width of doors and lifts, thresholds, toilets and the height difference at the entrance. Ask for concrete measurements instead of just ‘accessible’.

    Arriving by train

    ÖBB and Deutsche Bahn offer help for travellers with reduced mobility through their mobility service. After registering in advance, staff help with boarding, getting off and changing trains. Register well before departure.

    Accommodation and restaurants

    The tourist board names two barrier-free accommodations with an inspection report: Apparthotel Talhof and Hotel Sonnschein. Named as barrier-free restaurants are: Dorferwirt, Hotel Bergkristall, Restaurant Tirolerhof, Markbachjoch Alm, Norderbergalm, Restaurant Drachenherz and Restaurant Talhof.

    Excursions

    • Auenweg in Niederau: a family walk along a small stream with a playground halfway; it starts at the hiking board below the Markbachjoch gondola
    • Drachental family park in Oberau: open all year; since July 2025 there is a swing that can be used with a wheelchair
    • St Francis Path: a path of reflection between Niederau and Oberau with nine bronze stations by sculptor Hubert Flörl
    • Wildschönau open-air pool between Oberau and Niederau: three pools, a sunbathing lawn and a playground
    • Kundler Klamm: the gorge walk is easy and suitable for the whole family
    • Schatzberg cable car in Auffach: maximum wheelchair width 80 cm, entrance at platform level, toilets at the bottom and top stations, a lift at the bottom station and a ramp at the top station

    Source: Wildschönau tourist board. Check measurements and facilities with the provider beforehand.

    Public buildings

    The tourist board reports that the tourist office at the pool in Oberau and the municipal office in Oberau are accessible, including toilets.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are there barrier-free hotels in the Wildschönau?

    The tourist board names Apparthotel Talhof and Hotel Sonnschein with an inspection report. Ask for the exact measurements beforehand.

    Can I use the Schatzberg cable car with a wheelchair?

    According to the tourist board yes, up to a wheelchair width of 80 cm, with entrance at platform level and toilets at both stations.
