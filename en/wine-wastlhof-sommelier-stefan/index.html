    Wine at Hotel Wastlhof: sommelier Stefan and the wine cellar

    A glass of wine after a day in the mountains: Stefan, sommelier at Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau, pours it and tells you about it.

    After a day on the slopes or in the mountains, many guests like a good glass of wine with dinner. Hotel Wastlhof, our partner hotel in Niederau, has a sommelier for that: Stefan. He has completed sommelier training and advises guests on the wines that suit the kitchen of the house.

    The hotel has its own wine cellar, with wooden racks, bottles lying on their sides and a long table where you can sit and taste. On this page you can read what to expect and how to make an appointment.

    The sommelier: Stefan

    A sommelier is the wine specialist of a restaurant or hotel. Stefan has trained for this. He knows the wines in the cellar, knows which dishes they suit and helps you choose, even if you know little about wine.

    It is not about expensive bottles but about a wine that suits you and your plate. Do ask for advice: with a stew you choose something different than with fish or a light starter.

    The wine cellar

    The hotel's wine cellar is an atmospheric room with wooden racks holding the bottles on their sides, an oak barrel and a long table with chairs, glasses and candles. This way you can sit and taste among the wines instead of at an ordinary restaurant table.

    Which wines are in the cellar at the moment changes. Ask the hotel for the current wine list or for the options for a tasting.

    The wine offer: mostly Austrian

    For a Tyrolean hotel it is the obvious choice: the Wastlhof wine list has plenty of Austrian wines. The hotel makes its own selection and regularly tastes new wines to add to the list. The Austrian wines cover a wide range, from light white wines to powerful reds.

    If you want to know what is on the list, you will find an overview of the types below. Prices and the current bottles are on the hotel's own wine list.

    • White wine: Grüner Veltliner, Riesling, Sauvignon Blanc, Gelber Muskateller, Chardonnay, Weißburgunder, Gewürztraminer, Gemischter Satz and Roter Veltliner
    • Rosé: including Zweigelt and Cabernet Sauvignon
    • Red wine: Zweigelt, Blaufränkisch, St. Laurent, Pinot Noir, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and cuvées
    • Wine regions: including Kremstal, Weinviertel, Wachau, Kamptal, Südsteiermark, Vulkanland, Leithaberg, Carnuntum, Neusiedlersee, Mittelburgenland and Thermenregion
    • International: including wines from Germany (Mosel), South Tyrol, Tuscany, Apulia, Spain, Chile, France and Argentina
    • Sweet wines and port, to finish dinner
    • Sparkling wine: Sekt, Secco and champagne

    The list changes and may differ from this overview. Ask the hotel for the current wine list.

    Make an appointment to enjoy it

    Would you like to taste Stefan's wines? Contact Hotel Wastlhof in advance and make an appointment, so there is time and a place for you. On the hotel's wine page you will find more information and the contact details.

    Hotel Wastlhof is a partner of this site; the owner has been coming here for more than fifty years and recommends the hotel personally, without compensation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Hotel Wastlhof have a sommelier?

    Yes. Stefan is the sommelier of Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau and has completed sommelier training.

    Does the hotel have its own wine cellar?

    Yes. In the wine cellar the bottles lie in wooden racks and there is a long table to sit and taste.

    How do I arrange a wine tasting?

    Contact Hotel Wastlhof in advance via the hotel's wine page and ask about the options.

    Which wines does Hotel Wastlhof have?

    Mostly Austrian wines, such as Grüner Veltliner, Riesling, Zweigelt and Blaufränkisch, complemented by international wines, sweet wines and sparkling wine. The list changes; ask the hotel for the current wine list.
