Ellmi’s Zauberwelt, Ellmau

A fairy-tale adventure world on the Hartkaiser

Ellmi’s Zauberwelt sits next to the top station of the Hartkaiserbahn, more than 1,500 metres above Ellmau. The theme is Ellmi, a wizard frog whose failed spells cause strange things: upside-down trees, goblin voices, an enchanted forest and a bewitched pond.

Besides the trail past these spots, there is a large playground with trampolines, slides, a climbing park and a fairy-tale wood full of sounds. The plateau at the top station is suitable for pushchairs, so families with toddlers are welcome too. Plan on half a day or a full day, depending on the children’s ages.

Place: Ellmau

  • AgeAges 2 and up
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostGondola ticket needed; prices vary, check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 40 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationEllmau, Hartkaiser (Wilder Kaiser)

Tips

  • Take a jacket; it can be cooler up top than in the valley.
  • Wear sturdy shoes, even though most paths are easy.
  • The area works well with a pushchair for little ones.
  • Pack a picnic or plan a stop at a mountain hut for lunch.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 40 minutes by car to Ellmau, via Wörgl and Söll. Take the Hartkaiserbahn gondola up; the play world is right by the top station. According to the region it is open daily in summer; check current hours and lift prices beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ellmi’s Zauberwelt suitable for pushchairs?

The plateau by the top station and most paths are suitable for pushchairs.
