Alpenrosenbahn, Westendorf

Gondola, Alpinolino adventure world and ball run above Westendorf

There is no alpine coaster or summer toboggan run in Westendorf to be found. Westendorf in the Brixental does, however, offer a good family outing with the Alpenrosenbahn I and II, which takes you to the Talkaser at about 1,770 m.

Up top is the ErlebnisWelt Alpinolino, a route with small adventures and nature stations for children. There is also the Spoggolino, Tyrol's longest ball run, which leads from the Choralpe back to the Alpenrosenbahn station. From the Talkaser you can also walk across alpine meadows. Everything is calm and easy to grasp, suitable for children from pre-school age. In summer the lift runs from late spring into autumn.

Place: Westendorf

  • AgeSuitable for children from pre-school age
  • DurationAbout 2 to 4 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 35 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (late spring to autumn)
  • LocationWestendorf, Brixental

Tips

  • Check the lift's current timetable beforehand.
  • Bring a jacket; at about 1,770 m it is cooler than in the valley.
  • Allow some extra time for the Spoggolino ball run.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 35 minutes by car to Westendorf, via Wörgl and the Brixental. The valley station of the Alpenrosenbahn is in the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an alpine coaster in Westendorf?

None was found. There is, however, the Spoggolino, a ball run for children, at the Alpenrosenbahn.
