Wildpark Aurach, Kitzbühel

Wildlife park with deer, ibex and lynx, with views of the Kitzbühel Alps

Wildpark Aurach lies at Aurach, close to Kitzbühel, and says it has Tyrol's largest outdoor enclosure for game. You walk past red deer, fallow deer, mouflon, ibex and other animals, and lynx and yaks are among the residents too.

The park has fine views of the Kitzbühel Alps and is open all year, usually Wednesday to Sunday. There is a daily feeding, generally in the afternoon, when you see the animals up close. It is a relaxed half-day trip, even if the weather is not perfect.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1.5–2 hours
  • CostAdmission charged; check current prices
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationAurach near Kitzbühel

Tips

  • Check the opening days beforehand; the park has days off.
  • Time your visit around the daily feeding.
  • Wear sturdy shoes; some paths are uphill.

Practical

Aurach near Kitzbühel is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the B170 towards Kitzbühel. The park can also be reached by bus and on foot.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the wildlife park open all year?

Yes, all year, usually Wednesday to Sunday according to sources; check the current days.
