Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Winter

Family snowshoe walk

Walk through deep snow, away from the beaten track

Snowshoes keep you from sinking deep into the snow, so you can walk well away from cleared paths. It is a quiet way to see the Wildschönau in winter, with no ski skills needed and no need to hurry.

Guided snowshoe walks are offered in the valley, among other things through the Wildschönau Card, and snowshoes can be rented from rental shops in Niederau. For children it is an adventure: looking for animal tracks and ploughing through untouched snow. You can set the length of the walk yourself, or agree it with the guide.

  • AgeFrom about 6 years; keep it short for younger children
  • DurationAbout 2 to 4 hours
  • CostRental and guided walks: please enquire in advance
  • DistanceOften straight from Niederau or about 5–10 min by car
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationNiederau and surroundings, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Dress in layers and bring a dry pair of gloves.
  • Look out for animal tracks in the snow, such as hare, fox or deer.
  • The snow looks best early in the day, and it is quieter.
  • Ask the tourist office about current guided walks and whether any are in Dutch.

Practical

The starting point depends on the chosen walk or guide; from Niederau you can often start on foot or after a short drive. Check offer, times and rental with the Wildschönau tourist office.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you rent snowshoes in Niederau?

Yes, according to the region snowshoes can be rented from rental shops in Niederau. Ask in advance about children's sizes.
