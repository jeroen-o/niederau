Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

Mountain hut hike from Oberau

A half-day walk across alpine pastures to a mountain hut

From Oberau, waymarked paths climb to the alpine pastures and mountain huts of the Wildschönau. The route passes through forest, beside streams and across open pastures, with views over the valley along the way. At the hut a simple Tyrolean meal awaits, such as a platter of bread and cold cuts or fresh buttermilk.

The walk suits families with children from about six years who want half a day on foot. If you want more views, continue towards the Markbachjoch. You can return the same way or take an alternative route along the valley. Pick up a walking map at the tourist office for the current waymarking.

Place: Oberau

  • AgeFrom approx. 6 years
  • DurationAbout 3 to 4 hours
  • CostWalking is free; hut stop at your own cost
  • DistanceAbout 5 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationOberau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Wear sturdy hiking shoes.
  • Bring your own drinks; there are not facilities everywhere on the route.
  • Get a walking map at the tourist office.
  • Allow plenty of time for the hut stop.

Practical

Oberau is about 5 minutes by car from Niederau. The walk starts at the village centre. Allow about 3 to 4 hours, depending on route and breaks.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat along the way?

Yes, the mountain hut offers simple Tyrolean food and drink; bring your own water for the rest.
