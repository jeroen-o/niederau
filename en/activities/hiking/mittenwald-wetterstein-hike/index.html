    Hiking near Mittenwald, Wetterstein

    Bavarian mountain village with rock faces, violin-making tradition and walks

    Mittenwald lies in Bavaria at about 930 m, between the Wetterstein and Karwendel mountains. The village is known as an important centre of violin making and has a pedestrian zone lined with painted facades. In the Geigenbaumuseum you can see stringed instruments from the local tradition.

    Many walking routes start around the village, for example up the Kranzberg with views of the Wetterstein range, or to the Lautersee and Ferchensee lakes. The Leutasch Geisterklamm gorge is also nearby, and the Karwendelbahn cable car takes you up to around 2,300 m. It is a long drive from Niederau, so allow a full day.

    Violin making goes back to Matthias Kloz (1653 to 1743), who learned the craft in Italy; the Geigenbaumuseum is housed in his former home. A fine day trip is the walk from the Kranzberg to the Ferchensee (about 1 hour) and from there via the Lautersee back to Mittenwald (about 2.5 hours). The Karwendelbahn takes you up to over 2,200 m. The village has its own atmosphere: a pedestrian zone, painted houses and the mountain walls just behind the roofs. If you do not want to walk too far, you can spend an afternoon in the centre and then make a short trip to one of the lakes.

    Tips

    • Plan a full day for this trip.
    • Bring your ID to be safe.
    • Combine the hike with the village centre and the violin-making museum.
    • Walk from the Kranzberg to the Ferchensee and back via the Lautersee for a varied day route.
    • Visit the Geigenbaumuseum in Matthias Kloz's former home.

    Practical

    Mittenwald is about 1.5 hours by car from Niederau, via the A12 and Seefeld. The village lies just across the border in Bavaria, so bring your ID to be safe. Choose your route on the spot with the tourist office (Alpenwelt Karwendel) and ask about current conditions, also for the Karwendelbahn. It is a long drive, so set off in good time. Check the border rules beforehand and fill up in good time, as the route passes through the mountains.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Mittenwald known for?

    For its violin-making tradition, painted houses and its setting between the Wetterstein and Karwendel.

    Who was Matthias Kloz?

    The founder of violin making in Mittenwald (1653 to 1743), who learned the craft in Italy.

    How long is the walk to the Ferchensee?

    From the Kranzberg it is about 1 hour, and from the Ferchensee back to Mittenwald via the Lautersee about 2.5 hours.
