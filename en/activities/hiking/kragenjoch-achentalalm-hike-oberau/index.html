Kragenjoch round with Achentalalm from Oberau

An easy family walk along a forest road to an alm

From Oberau village the Kragenjoch round starts, an easy walk that many families choose. The route is roughly 6.8 km long, takes about 2.8 hours and, with around 500 metres of climbing, follows a comfortable forest road for most of the way to the Achentalalm, where you can rest. Other descriptions mention a slightly longer variant of about 8 km, so allow a little over three hours.

The route starts in the village and first follows the Minzweg towards Boarstadl. After the alm comes a steeper final climb to the Georgkapelle on the Kragenjoch, at about 1,425 m. At the top the view sweeps over the highest mountains of the Wildschönau, the Brixental and the Wilder Kaiser, and in clear weather even Kufstein Fortress.

The alm is known for simple dishes: kaiserschmarrn every day from June to September, and Wildschönau noodles on Wednesdays. In early summer the Kragenjoch is also special for its mountain flowers. Check the weather and the alm's opening days beforehand. It is a walk where you meet many families, but the route is wide enough to walk in peace. If you skip the steep final climb, you can turn back at the alm and still have had a fine walk.

Place: Oberau

Tips

  • Wear sturdy hiking shoes.
  • Bring your own drinks for the way.
  • Get a walking map at the tourist office.
  • Allow plenty of time for the hut stop.
  • Tackle the last steep climb to the chapel only while the children are still fresh.

Practical

Oberau is about 5 minutes by car from Niederau. The walk starts at Oberau village. Allow about 3 hours, depending on pace and breaks. Because the route starts in the village, you can leave the car at the starting point and walk for half a day. Bring an extra layer for the top of the joch, where it is cooler than in the valley. Park in the village and ask about the alm's current opening days.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat along the way?

Yes, at the Achentalalm; check its opening days beforehand and bring your own water for the rest.

How high is the Kragenjoch?

The Kragenjoch is at about 1,425 m; Oberau lies considerably lower, hence the roughly 500 metres of climbing.

What can you see from the Kragenjoch?

The highest mountains of the Wildschönau, the Brixental and the Wilder Kaiser; in clear weather also Kufstein Fortress.
