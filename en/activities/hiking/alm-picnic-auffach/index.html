Picnic on an alm near Auffach

Relaxing in a meadow with cowbells as the soundtrack

Above Auffach there are alpine pastures where cattle graze in summer and cowbells ring. It is a lovely spot for a picnic: green grass, a stream nearby and a view over the mountains. You can walk there in about half an hour, or cover part of it by bike or e-bike.

Bring a blanket and your own food, such as bread, cheese and fruit. Sometimes there is an alm nearby where you can buy fresh products. Cows are usually curious but you keep your distance; leave gates as you find them and take your rubbish home. A relaxed half day for the whole family.

Place: Auffach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostFree; bring your own picnic
  • DistanceAbout 10 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationAuffach, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Bring a blanket, drinks and a rubbish bag.
  • Keep away from cows and do not feed them.
  • If possible, buy cheese or sausage at a nearby alm.
  • Check the weather; do not go in rain or thunderstorms.

Practical

Auffach is about 10 minutes by car from Niederau. From the village you walk to the pastures in about 30 minutes, or cycle part of the way. Ask locally for a suitable spot.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the walk to the pastures above Auffach?

About half an hour from the village; the exact path depends on the spot you choose.
