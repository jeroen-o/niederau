Alm walks in the Brandenberg valley

Quiet paths to alpine pastures in a peaceful side valley

The Brandenberg valley is a side valley of the Inn valley, near Rattenberg, and much quieter than the large tourist valleys. From the village of Brandenberg, paths lead through forest and across alpine pastures to Almhütten.

It is an area for those who want peace and quiet and to enjoy the landscape without crowds. The routes vary in length, so you can do a short walk or plan a longer trip. With a little luck you may see wildlife such as chamois, but do not count on it.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2–3 hours
  • CostFree to walk
  • DistanceAbout 40 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationBrandenberg, Brandenberg valley

Tips

  • Take a hiking map or ask at the village office for routes.
  • Bring food and drink; not every hut is always open.
  • Keep your distance from wildlife and grazing animals.

Practical

Brandenberg is about 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Kramsach or Rattenberg into the Brandenberg valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
