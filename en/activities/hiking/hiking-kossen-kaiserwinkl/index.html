    Hiking near Kössen, Kaiserwinkl

    Quiet walks by the Kaiser mountains, away from the crowds

    Kössen lies in the Kaiserwinkl, the north-eastern corner of Tyrol by the German border, between the Zahmer Kaiser and the hills of the northern Chiemgau Alps. The region has about 200 km of marked hiking trails and some thirty huts where you can try regional specialities such as hay-milk cheese.

    It is quieter than the best-known parts around the Wilder Kaiser. There are short walks for small children and longer tours, for example to the Ottenalm, the Edernalm or the Taubensee. A free hiking map with tips is available from the Kaiserwinkl tourist office in Kössen.

    The Edernalm lies at about 903 m and can be reached in around 45 minutes to an hour from Staffen, a part of Kössen; from the Edernalm to the Ottenalm it is a further 40 minutes or so. If you want a longer day, you can walk the route from Walchsee over the Riederalm and Ottenalm to Kössen. That gives you both short and more substantial tours. The landscape consists of gentle alpine meadows, striking limestone rocks and the odd mountain lake. That makes it a pleasant area for walkers who prefer to avoid the crowds on the best-known routes. You can build up the day gradually and stop at a hut along the way.

    Tips

    • Start with a shorter route and see how the children get on.
    • Bring water and a picnic, or plan a stop at an Almhütte.
    • Ask the tourist office about current routes.
    • Pick the Edernalm as a first goal; it is an easily reached tour.
    • On a longer route bring a rain jacket, as mountain weather can change.

    Practical

    Kössen is about 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kufstein towards Kössen. Many walks start in or near the village, for example in the Staffen district for the Edernalm. You can pick up a free hiking map at the Kaiserwinkl tourist office in Kössen; ask there too about current routes and the opening periods of the huts along the way. Parking at the starting points can be limited, so it is better to set off early.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is hiking near Kössen suitable for children?

    Yes, there are short, easy routes as well as longer ones; pick one to suit the age.

    How long does the walk to the Edernalm take?

    From Staffen near Kössen it takes about 45 minutes to an hour uphill.

    Where can I find hiking information in Kössen?

    At the Kaiserwinkl tourist office in Kössen, where a free hiking map with tips is available.
