Söll and the Hohe Salve

Lift, walking paths and mountain biking in the SkiWelt village of Söll

Söll lies at the foot of the Hohe Salve (1,829 m), one of Tyrol's best-known viewpoint mountains, and belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental. In summer the Hochsöll gondola takes you up to Hexenwasser at about 1,355 m, a themed mountain area with water stations, a bee house and witch stories. A second gondola section continues to the summit.

On top stands the Salvenkirchlein, the highest pilgrimage church in Austria, with a mountain restaurant next to it. From the summit you can see around seventy peaks above 3,000 m. A lift at the top station makes the summit accessible without steps. For children there are, among other things, a sky labyrinth and planet ball runs. Walkers find panorama paths here, and cyclists a circular route of about 37 km around the mountain. It is an easy outing, about 35 minutes from Niederau.

Tips

  • Check that the lift is running before you go.
  • Bring a jacket; it can be cool at the top.
  • Plan a stop at a terrace in the village after the walk.

Practical

Söll is about 35 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the B178 towards Söll.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do in Söll in summer?

Walking and mountain biking, with the lift up the Hohe Salve and terraces in the village.
