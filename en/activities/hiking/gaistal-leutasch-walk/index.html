Gaistal near Leutasch

A quiet high valley with meadows and a clear stream

The Gaistal lies near Leutasch on the Seefeld plateau, on the south side of the Wetterstein range. The Leutascher Ache flows through the valley. It is one of the best-known hiking areas on the plateau, with comfortable paths to several alms such as the Gaistalalm, Hämmermoosalm and Tillfussalm.

A popular family route is the three-alm loop from the Salzbach car park: about 10 km, 3 to 3.5 hours and about 290 metres of climbing. Distances are fairly short and there is plenty to see, which suits children well. Maps and tips are available from the Leutasch tourist office.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 3 to 3.5 hours (loop)
  • CostFree to walk
  • DistanceAbout 1 h 20 by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationLeutasch, Seefeld plateau

Tips

  • The wide paths also suit prams and bikes, but check the path first.
  • Bring a jacket; it can get cooler in the valley.
  • Plan a stop at an Almhütte for a break.

Practical

Leutasch is about 1 hour 20 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 towards Telfs and then on to Seefeld and Leutasch.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Gaistal suitable for families?

Yes, the paths are mostly wide and flat, so you can easily walk a stretch with children.
