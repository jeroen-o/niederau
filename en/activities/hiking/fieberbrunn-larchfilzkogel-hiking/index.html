Fieberbrunn: hiking from the Lärchfilzkogel

Take the gondola up and hike over flower-rich alpine meadows

Fieberbrunn lies in the PillerseeTal, at the foot of the Kitzbühel Alps. In summer the gondola takes you up to the Lärchfilzkogel (1,645 m), the start of several hiking routes. From the top station a short walk through flower-rich alpine meadows leads to the Wildsee, where the Wildseeloderhaus sits right on the shore with views of the Kitzbühel Alps. For the longer round trip allow about 3 to 4 hours.

At the middle station you will find Timoks Wilde Welt, a children's adventure world with an alpine coaster, a water playground and a forest rope course. There is also a family walk with ten stations, guided by the mascot Timok. That makes for a day out that suits families with children of different ages, about 50 minutes by car from Niederau.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 4 hours depending on the route
  • CostGondola is charged; ask about fares
  • DistanceApprox. 50 min drive
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationFieberbrunn, PillerseeTal (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Check the current summer timetable and fares with the cable car operator.
  • Bring a jacket and sturdy shoes; it can turn cooler quickly up top.
  • Combine the walk to the Wildsee with a break at the Wildseeloderhaus.

Practical

Fieberbrunn is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via Kitzbühel or St. Johann. Check the current dates and times of the cable car's summer service.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there something for children in Fieberbrunn?

Yes, the middle station has Timoks Wilde Welt with an alpine coaster, a water playground and a forest rope course.
