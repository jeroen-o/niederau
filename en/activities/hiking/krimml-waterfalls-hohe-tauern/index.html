Krimml Waterfalls

Some of Europe's highest waterfalls in the Hohe Tauern National Park

The Krimml Waterfalls in the Hohe Tauern National Park plunge about 380 m in three stages. A wide, well-made Wasserfallweg of about 4 km with viewpoints follows the falls; allow 2 to 3 hours for the whole route. You do not have to go all the way up: even the lowest fall is impressive.

There is a lot of spray, so a rain jacket is handy. Children often find the power of the water impressive. The Wasserfallweg is open for a fee (€ 9 per person in 2026) from mid-April to the end of October. Allow time for the walk back or a break at one of the viewpoints.

Place: Krimml

Tips

  • Bring a rain jacket; the spray is heaviest at the upper falls.
  • You do not have to go all the way up: the lowest fall is already impressive.
  • Check current admission prices and opening times before you go.

Practical

Krimml is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Niederau, via the Zillertal and the Gerlos Pass (toll road). Parking is available at the start of the Wasserfallweg.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How high are the Krimml Waterfalls?

About 380 m in three stages; the Wasserfallweg is about 4 km long.
