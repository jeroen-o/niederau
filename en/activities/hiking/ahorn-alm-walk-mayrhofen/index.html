Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

Alm walk above Mayrhofen, Ahorn

Flower-rich alm route at about 2,000 metres above the Zillertal

Above Mayrhofen lies the Ahorn area, reached by the Ahornbahn, a cable car that climbs roughly 1,300 metres in about 6 minutes. At the top a circular walk starts across alpine pastures with views over the Zillertal. In summer the meadows are full of flowers, and in early summer the alpine roses bloom too.

The route is easy and takes roughly one to two hours; longer hikes are possible for those who want more. A well-known short loop is the GenießerRundWeg, about 2 km long and around three quarters of an hour, past the Ahornsee lake and the Ahornbachl stream, where you can cool your feet on warm days. Experienced walkers can continue towards the Ahornspitze (2,973 m), but that tour is only for sure-footed hikers with a good head for heights.

Along the way there are mountain huts for a break, where you can enjoy the view with a drink. It is a pleasant summer outing for the whole family. Setting off in the morning usually means a quieter experience at the top. Add an extra stop and the walk fills a good half day: the meadows and huts invite you to linger. Bring a sun hat, because at around 2,000 metres the sun is stronger than in the valley.

Place: Mayrhofen

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2–3 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 55 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationMayrhofen, Zillertal

Tips

  • Alpenrose blooms mainly in June and July.
  • Check beforehand that the Ahornbahn is running.
  • Bring a jacket; it is cool at the top.
  • Walk the short loop by the Ahornsee if you are with small children.
  • Leave the Ahornspitze to experienced, sure-footed hikers.

Practical

Mayrhofen is about 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the B169 into the Zillertal. The Ahornbahn leaves from the village and takes you up in a few minutes. Allow about 2 to 3 hours for the circular walk itself, including a break at a mountain hut, and half a day with the journey there and back. Ask beforehand about the lift's running times and rates. A sun hat and a jacket for the top belong in the rucksack.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get up to the Ahorn area?

By the Ahornbahn from Mayrhofen; check beforehand that it is running.

How high is the Ahornspitze?

At 2,973 m; the tour there is only for sure-footed hikers.

Is there a short walk at the Ahorn for children?

Yes, the GenießerRundWeg is about 2 km and takes roughly three quarters of an hour, past the Ahornsee lake and a stream.
