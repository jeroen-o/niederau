Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

Alm walk above Mayrhofen, Ahorn

Flower-rich alm route at about 2,000 metres above the Zillertal

Above Mayrhofen lies the Ahorn area, reached by the Ahornbahn cable car. At the top a circular walk starts across alpine pastures with views over the Zillertal. In summer the meadows are full of flowers, and in early summer the alpine roses bloom too.

The route is easy and takes roughly one to two hours; longer hikes are possible for those who want more. Along the way there are mountain huts for a break, where you can enjoy the view with a drink. It is a pleasant summer outing for the whole family, also for those who prefer a gentle stroll to a long hike. Setting off in the morning usually means a quieter experience at the top.

Place: Mayrhofen

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2–3 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 55 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationMayrhofen, Zillertal

Tips

  • Alpenrose blooms mainly in June and July.
  • Check beforehand that the Ahornbahn is running.
  • Bring a jacket; it is cool at the top.

Practical

Mayrhofen is about 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the B169 into the Zillertal.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get up to the Ahorn area?

By the Ahornbahn from Mayrhofen; check beforehand that it is running.
