Hiking in Fieberbrunn, Pillerseetal

Alm and mountain paths in the Pillerseetal, quieter than Kitzbühel

Fieberbrunn is the largest village in the Pillerseetal, in the Kitzbühel Alps. It is best known as a winter sports resort, but in summer it has a good network of walking routes; the Pillerseetal is a certified hiking-village region with around 400 km of routes.

The Bergbahnen Fieberbrunn run in summer (roughly May to November) up the Wildseeloder, with attractions such as the Wildseelodersee lake and Timoks Wilde Welt, an adventure world for children. It is generally quieter than Kitzbühel. Ask the tourist office for a hiking map.

Official website

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2–3 hours
  • CostFree to walk
  • DistanceAbout 1 h by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationFieberbrunn, Pillerseetal

Tips

  • Pick up a hiking map at the tourist office for current routes.
  • Combine the walk with a stop at the Pillersee.
  • Bring rain gear; mountain weather changes quickly.

Practical

Fieberbrunn is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, Kitzbühel and the B164 or B161.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
