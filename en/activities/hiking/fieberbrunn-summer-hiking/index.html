Hiking in Fieberbrunn, Pillerseetal

Alm and mountain paths in the Pillerseetal, quieter than Kitzbühel

Fieberbrunn lies in the Pillerseetal, in the Kitzbühel Alps. The village is best known as a winter sports resort, but in summer it has a good network of mountain and alm paths, and it is usually quieter than Kitzbühel.

In summer the Streubödenbahn takes you up to the Lärchfilzkogel at about 1,645 m, the starting point for walks to the Wildseelodersee lake and other routes. At the middle station you will find Timoks Wilde Welt, an adventure world for families with an alpine coaster, water play and a forest rope course, among other things. This lets you combine a hike with a day full of activities for children. Ask the tourist office for a map and the current lift times.

Tips

  • Pick up a hiking map at the tourist office for current routes.
  • Combine the walk with a stop at the Pillersee.
  • Bring rain gear; mountain weather changes quickly.

Practical

Fieberbrunn is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, Kitzbühel and the B164 or B161.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
