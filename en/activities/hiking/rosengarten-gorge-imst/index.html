Rosengarten Gorge, Imst

Walk through a gorge with waterfalls and suspension bridges near Imst

The Rosengarten Gorge lies near Imst in the Tyrolean Oberland and is one of the best-known gorges in Tyrol. A path with wooden steps and bridges leads through the narrow gorge, past high rock walls and waterfalls.

It is an appealing walk for families with children from about six, but the path can be wet and slippery. Afterwards you can take a break in Imst itself. It is a long drive from Niederau, so the trip suits a full day.

  • AgeFrom about 6
  • DurationAbout 2–3 hours
  • CostAdmission charged; check the current price
  • DistanceAbout 1.5 h by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationImst, Tyrolean Oberland

Tips

  • Wear shoes with good grip; the path is often wet.
  • Bring a jacket; it is cool in the gorge.
  • Combine with a stop in the centre of Imst.

Practical

Imst is about 1.5 hours by car from Niederau, via the A12 heading west.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Rosengarten Gorge suitable for children?

Yes, from about six, with good shoes as the path can be wet and slippery.
