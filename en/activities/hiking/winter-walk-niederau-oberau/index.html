    Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Winter

    Winter walk between Niederau and Oberau

    Easy, flat winter walk past farms, also with a buggy

    A simple winter walk runs between Niederau and Oberau, following the stream past traditional Tyrolean farmhouses. The flat section along the water can also be done with a snow buggy, which makes the route suitable for families with small children and for grandparents.

    Along the way you pass frozen brooks, wooden bridges and views of the Kitzbühel Alps. There is a shorter version of about 3 km and a longer one of about 6 km; allow 1 to 3 hours. The route starts in the centre of Niederau and can be reached on foot from many accommodations. The tourist office can tell you how the paths currently look.

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationApprox. 1 to 3 hours
    • CostFree
    • DistanceIn Niederau itself
    • SeasonWinter
    • LocationNiederau and Oberau, Wildschönau (Tyrol)

    Tips

    • Wear warm boots with grip; it can be slippery.
    • Take a flask of hot drink.
    • Choose the shorter option if the children tire.

    Practical

    The walk starts in the centre of Niederau; Oberau is about 10 minutes' drive further along the valley. Ask the tourist office about the current condition of the paths.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I do the winter walk between Niederau and Oberau with a buggy?

    The flat section along the water can also be done with a snow buggy; ask the tourist office about the current condition of the paths.
