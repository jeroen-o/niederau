    Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

    Feldalphorn hike from the Markbachjoch

    Mountain walk to a 1,923 m panoramic summit

    From Niederau you take the Markbachjochbahn up to the mountain station. There you follow the signs for the Wildschönauer Höhenweg along a gravel track, and at the first fork you turn right towards the Markbachjochalm at about 1,454 m. Beyond the alpine hut the route climbs along a ridge.

    The final stretch to the summit of the Feldalphorn (1,923 m) is rocky and calls for a little scrambling. At the top the view stretches far across the Wildschönau and on to the high peaks of the Central Alps. On the way you cross open alpine pastures, rich in flowers in summer. It is a moderately demanding mountain walk for people used to hiking in the mountains.

    • AgeFrom about 10 years, with mountain experience
    • DurationAbout half a day
    • CostGondola extra; the walk itself is free
    • DistanceStarts about 5 min from Niederau
    • SeasonSummer (in dry weather)
    • LocationNiederau, Markbachjoch

    Tips

    • Wear sturdy walking boots and bring a jacket; it can turn cold and windy at the top.
    • Check the weather forecast and the time of the last gondola before you set off.
    • Want a longer day? The Wildschönauer Höhenweg links several summits but takes much longer.
    • Expect a rocky final stretch; young children need close supervision here.

    Practical

    The walk starts at the top station of the Markbachjoch gondola, reached from Niederau within a few minutes. Weather and mountain conditions decide whether the walk is sensible.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How high is the Feldalphorn?

    The summit is at 1,923 m, over 1,000 m above Niederau (828 m).

    Is the hike suitable for children?

    Only for experienced children: the final section is rocky. A gentler option is the walk to the Markbachjochalm.
