    Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

    Rosengarten Gorge, Imst

    Short gorge walk with waterfalls close to Imst town centre

    The Rosengarten Gorge starts a few minutes' walk from the centre of Imst, at the Johanneskirche, and runs about 1.5 to 2 km uphill to the Blaue Grotte in Hoch-Imst. The path follows the Schinderbach past rock walls and small bridges. The walls are made of reef limestone formed more than 230 million years ago in the Triassic, and the gorge is regarded as one of Tyrol's most valuable biotopes.

    The Blaue Grotte is an old, man-made mine with two waterfalls; as early as Roman times people searched here for silver-bearing lead glance. From early or mid-May to roughly the end of October the gorge is freely accessible and free of charge. In storms, hail or heavy rain it may be impassable, and in winter it is closed.

    It is a short, varied walk that children often find exciting; the path is wet and slippery in places. If you want more, a larger circuit of about 4.5 km and 3 hours starts from the centre of Imst. Combine the walk with a visit to the town.

    • AgeFrom approx. 6 years
    • DurationApprox. 1 to 1.5 hours
    • CostFree admission
    • DistanceApprox. 1 h 15 min drive
    • SeasonApprox. May to October
    • LocationImst, Upper Inn Valley (Tyrol)

    Tips

    • Wear sturdy shoes with good grip; the path can be wet and slippery.
    • Do not go during storms or after heavy rain.
    • Bring a light jacket: it is cooler in the gorge than outside.
    • Keep small children by the hand at the bridges and along the rock walls.
    • Look closely at the rock walls; they are more than 230 million years old.

    Practical

    Imst is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 towards Innsbruck and further west. The gorge starts within walking distance of the centre. In winter, from November to April or May, the gorge is closed; in spring and autumn check whether the path is open yet or still open. Parking is possible in the centre of Imst near the start of the gorge.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the Rosengarten Gorge charge admission?

    No, the gorge is free and freely accessible from about mid-May to the end of October.

    What is the Blaue Grotte?

    An old, man-made mine with two waterfalls at the end of the gorge.

    Is the gorge open in winter?

    No, the gorge is closed in winter; it is accessible roughly from May to the end of October.
