Rosengarten Gorge, Imst

Short gorge walk with waterfalls close to Imst town centre

The Rosengarten Gorge starts a few minutes' walk from the centre of Imst, at the Johanneskirche, and runs about 1.5 km uphill to the Blaue Grotte in Hoch-Imst. The path follows the Schinderbach past rock walls and small bridges. The Blaue Grotte is an old mine with two waterfalls.

From early or mid-May to roughly the end of October the gorge is freely accessible and free of charge. In storms, hail or heavy rain it may be impassable. It is a short, varied walk that children often find exciting; the path is wet and slippery in places. Combine it with a visit to the town.

  • AgeFrom approx. 6 years
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 1.5 hours
  • CostFree admission
  • DistanceApprox. 1 h 15 min drive
  • SeasonApprox. May to October
  • LocationImst, Upper Inn Valley (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Wear sturdy shoes with good grip; the path can be wet and slippery.
  • Do not go during storms or after heavy rain.
  • Bring a light jacket: it is cooler in the gorge than outside.

Practical

Imst is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 towards Innsbruck and further west. The gorge starts within walking distance of the centre.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Rosengarten Gorge charge admission?

No, the gorge is free and freely accessible from about mid-May to the end of October.
