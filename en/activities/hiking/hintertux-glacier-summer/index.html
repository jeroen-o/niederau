Hintertux Glacier in summer

Glacier air, an ice cave and high mountains in the Zillertal

The Hintertux Glacier, at the end of the Tuxertal in the Zillertal, is worth a visit in summer too. The glacier lifts take you to well over 3,000 m, into a landscape of ice and rock that contrasts sharply with the valley. Skiing on the glacier is not always offered in summer; it depends on snow conditions.

A special stop is the Natur Eis Palast near the top station of the Gletscherbus 3, a system of natural ice caves with ice crystals and a glacier lake. The temperature there hovers around freezing. For families it is mainly a day of experiencing the high mountains; expect cold, even when the valley is warm.

Place: Hintertux

  • AgeAll ages (mind cold and altitude)
  • DurationFull day
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 1 h 15 min by car
  • SeasonYear-round (summer offer varies)
  • LocationHintertux, Zillertal

Tips

  • Take warm clothes, a hat and gloves, even in August.
  • Check beforehand whether glacier skiing is possible during your week.
  • Start early; it often clouds over at the top in the afternoon.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and be mindful of the altitude with young children.

Practical

Hintertux is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the Zillertal. Allow extra time for the ride up and check the current operating times. The Gletscherbus 3 reaches about 3,250 m and generally runs from mid-May to early October.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you ski on the Hintertux Glacier in summer?

Sometimes, but not always. It depends on snow conditions, so ask beforehand.
