Wirbelwindweg

Family trail with play stops from Niederau to Oberau

The Wirbelwindweg is a themed family walk above Niederau and Oberau, mostly through shady forest. The route is about 4.5 km long, takes around 2 hours and is easy, with roughly 200 metres of climbing. Along the way there are six nature play stops where children get play ideas, via QR codes, between forest edge, meadows and streams.

The route starts in Niederau at the Tennladen practice lift, opposite Hotel Harfenwirt, and ends at the Bergbauernmuseum z'Bach in Oberau. The outdoor pool is then another 5-minute walk, handy for cooling off. Thanks to the forest it is a pleasant choice for warm summer days. The route is not suitable for pushchairs.

  • AgeFrom approx. 4 years on foot; all ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 hours
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 4.5 km; starts in Niederau
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationNiederau to Oberau

Tips

  • Bring a phone with a camera to scan the QR codes at the play stops.
  • Plan the museum or the pool at the end.
  • Wear sturdy shoes; the route is not suitable for pushchairs.
  • Bring water and a snack for breaks on the way.

Practical

The start is in Niederau, so you can begin from where you stay. For the way back from Oberau you can use the valley bus or a car.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Wirbelwindweg suitable for pushchairs?

No, the route is not suitable for pushchairs.

Where does the route start and end?

It starts in Niederau at the Tennladen practice lift and ends at the Bergbauernmuseum z'Bach in Oberau.
