Pillersee lake circuit

Quiet walk around the lake at St. Ulrich am Pillersee

The Pillersee lies in the PillerseeTal between St. Ulrich am Pillersee and Sankt Jakob in Haus, surrounded by mountains of the Kitzbühel Alps. Several easy walking routes lead around the lake, and they are also suitable for pushchairs. The Pillerseerunde from St. Ulrich is about 4.4 km and takes roughly 1 hour 10 minutes; the Weiler Runde is about 7.2 km (around 1 hour 50).

Along the way you pass shoreline, meadows and woodland. The routes are flexible: you can walk part of one and turn back, or do the full circuit. Combine it with a visit to St. Ulrich. Ask the tourist office for current routes, as the variants differ in length.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 2 hours
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 55 min drive
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationPillersee, St. Ulrich (Tyrol)

Tips

  • You do not have to finish the circuit; go as far as the children can manage.
  • Bring a picnic for a break by the lake.
  • Ask the tourist office about current routes.

Practical

St. Ulrich am Pillersee is about 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Kitzbühel or St. Johann. There is usually parking near the lake.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the walk around the Pillersee?

The Pillerseerunde from St. Ulrich is about 4.4 km (around 1 hour 10); longer variants such as the Weiler Runde are about 7.2 km.
