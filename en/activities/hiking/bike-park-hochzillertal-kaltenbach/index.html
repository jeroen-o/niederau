Bike Park Hochzillertal, Kaltenbach

Mountain biking in the Zillertal with a trail, trial park and pump track

In Kaltenbach, at the mouth of the Zillertal, you will find the Bike Park Hochzillertal. It offers a downhill trail, a trial park and a pump track, which makes it a compact spot to practise technique or simply ride for a few hours.

The wider Zillertal has a large network of mountain bike and e-bike routes, from relaxed valley rides to demanding mountain tours. If you prefer something calmer, you can pick a route from Kaltenbach towards the Kristallhütte, for example. What the park itself offers varies by season, so check the current information from the lift operator before you ride.

Place: Kaltenbach

  • AgeAges 12 and up, confident riders
  • DurationA few hours to a full day
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 45 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationKaltenbach, Zillertal

Tips

  • A helmet and protective gear are sensible; a full-face helmet is advisable for steeper trails.
  • Start on the pump track to get a feel for the bike before heading onto the trail.
  • Check the opening times and which trails are open before you go.

Practical

Kaltenbach is about 45 minutes by car from Niederau, in the Zillertal via Wörgl and the B169. Parking is available near the village and the valley station.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is there at the Bike Park Hochzillertal?

According to tourism sources, a trail, a trial park and a pump track in Kaltenbach.
