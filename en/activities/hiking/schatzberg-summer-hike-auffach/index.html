Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

Schatzberg above Auffach

Take the Schatzbergbahn up and hike across the alpine pastures

Auffach is the highest village of the Wildschönau. In summer the Schatzbergbahn takes you in about 22 minutes to the mountain station at over 1,770 m. Up top there is a large hiking area with mountain huts such as the Schatzbergalm and 360-degree views across the Wildschönau.

For families the Familienweg is handy: you can even walk down it with a pushchair to the play area at the middle station. At the Berghaus Koglmoos there is, among other things, a slide, a trampoline and animals. Experienced hikers can continue to the Gern or the Joel. The season runs from early June to early October.

Place: Auffach

  • AgeFrom approx. 6 years (Familienweg also younger)
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 4 hours
  • CostLift is charged; ask about fares
  • DistanceApprox. 15 min drive
  • SeasonSummer (approx. June to early October)
  • LocationAuffach, Wildschönau (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Bring a rain jacket: the weather can change quickly up top.
  • Plan a break at the Schatzbergalm or at Koglmoos.
  • Check the timetable of the lift, which only runs in season.

Practical

Auffach is about 15 minutes by car from Niederau, further up the valley. The valley station of the Schatzbergbahn is in the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you use a pushchair on the Schatzberg?

On the Familienweg going down towards the middle station, yes.
