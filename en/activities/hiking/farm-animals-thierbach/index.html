Farm animals in Thierbach

Goats, sheep and cows in the upper valley

Thierbach lies in the quiet part of the Wildschönau and is a true farming village. On the farms and pastures you see goats, sheep and cows, and it is often possible to watch the animals from a safe distance or, with the farmer's permission, stroke and feed them.

For young children this is one of the nicest outings: it costs little, it is close and it introduces them to life on a Tyrolean farm. Always ask permission before entering a farmyard or pasture, follow the farmer's instructions and only offer animals food that the farmer approves. A small contribution towards the feed is appreciated.

  • AgeAll ages, a favourite with toddlers
  • DurationAbout 1 hour
  • CostFree; a small contribution for feed is appreciated
  • DistanceAbout 5 to 10 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationThierbach, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Always ask permission before entering a farmyard.
  • Only give the animals food that the farmer permits.
  • Wear shoes that can get dirty.
  • Wash hands well afterwards.

Practical

Thierbach is about 5 to 10 minutes by car from Niederau, at the end of the valley. You can also walk via a mountain path. Ask the tourist office about farms that welcome visitors.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

May children stroke the animals in Thierbach?

Often yes, but always ask the farmer for permission first.
