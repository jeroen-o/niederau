Winter hike from the Markbachjoch to Niederau

Ride up and walk back down to the valley through winter scenery

This winter hike starts at the mountain station of the Markbachjochbahn and runs almost entirely downhill back to Niederau. The route is about 10.5 km long, takes around 4 hours and is described as moderately difficult, with roughly 676 m of descent.

You walk over gentle slopes and through sections of forest, past traditional farmhouses in Penningdörfl and Rotenau, and across meadows back to the valley. There are places to eat along the way, such as Leam Wirt, and several in Niederau. It is a varied walk, but it does require some fitness and suitable footwear.

  • AgeChildren from approx. 8 with some stamina
  • DurationApprox. 4 hours
  • CostLift is charged; ask about fares
  • DistanceIn Niederau itself
  • SeasonWinter (mainly December to March)
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Wear sturdy winter shoes and dress warmly; check snow conditions beforehand.
  • Bring a drink and a snack, even though there are inns along the way.
  • Check beforehand that the lift and the route are open.

Practical

The valley station of the Markbachjochbahn is in the centre of Niederau; you take the lift up and walk back to where you started.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the winter hike from the Markbachjoch to Niederau?

About 10.5 km and around 4 hours, mostly downhill; the route is moderately difficult.
