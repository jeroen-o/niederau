Picnic on the Markbachjoch

By gondola from Niederau up the mountain for a picnic with a view

The Markbachjoch lies directly above Niederau. The Markbachjochbahn, an eight-seat gondola, takes you in summer from 835 m to 1,465 m in about 8 minutes. That makes it one of the easiest ways to reach the mountain from the village, including with children.

At the top, the Markbachjochalm has a playground and a petting zoo; the Norderbergalm lies on the way to the top station. About half an hour's walk from the top station there is a sculpture park by the storage pond. For a picnic, buy bread, cheese and fruit in the village beforehand, bring a blanket and find a spot with a view.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2 to 4 hours, depending on the route
  • CostBring your own picnic; the lift costs extra
  • DistanceIn Niederau
  • SeasonSummer (about May to October)
  • LocationMarkbachjoch, Niederau

Tips

  • Check the weather forecast beforehand and bring a jacket.
  • Bring a blanket, a rubbish bag and enough water.
  • Keep your distance from grazing cattle and leave gates as you found them.
  • Combine the picnic with the playground or a hut stop.

Practical

The Markbachjochbahn starts in Niederau itself, in the village. You are there within minutes, on foot or by car. In summer the lift runs from May to October; check the current times and fares.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to the Markbachjoch?

By the Markbachjochbahn from Niederau; the top station is at 1,465 m and the ride takes about 8 minutes.
