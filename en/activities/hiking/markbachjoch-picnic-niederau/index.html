Picnic on the Markbachjoch

By gondola from Niederau to alpine pastures for a picnic with a view

The Markbachjoch lies directly above Niederau. The Markbachjochbahn, an eight-seat gondola, takes you in summer from about 835 m to 1,465 m. That makes it one of the easiest ways to reach the mountain from the village, even with small children or pushchairs on the first paths.

At the top there are walking trails with views of the Wilder Kaiser, huts and a playground. A picnic fits well: buy bread, cheese and fruit in the village beforehand, bring a blanket and find a spot with a view. If you prefer to walk, you can also go up or down on foot. There are short routes to places such as the Norderbergalm and the Kasalm.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2 to 4 hours, depending on the route
  • CostBring your own picnic; the lift costs extra
  • DistanceIn Niederau
  • SeasonSummer (approx. May to mid-October)
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Check the weather forecast beforehand and bring a jacket.
  • Bring a blanket, a rubbish bag and enough water.
  • Keep your distance from grazing cattle and leave gates as you found them.
  • Combine the picnic with the playground or a hut stop.

Practical

The Markbachjochbahn starts in Niederau itself, in the village. You are there within minutes, on foot or by car. The lift generally runs from May to mid-October in summer; check the current times.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to the Markbachjoch?

By the Markbachjochbahn from Niederau, or on foot. The top station is at 1,465 m.
