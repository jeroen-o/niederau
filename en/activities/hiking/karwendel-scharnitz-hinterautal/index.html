    Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

    Karwendel near Scharnitz

    Walking in Austria's largest nature park, along the young Isar

    Scharnitz lies close to the German border and is regarded as the gateway to the Karwendel. The Karwendel Nature Park covers about 739 km² and is the largest nature park in Austria. From the village you walk into the Hinterautal, a quiet valley about 14 km long where the young Isar flows.

    A classic outing is the walk to the Isarursprung (the Isar's source) along a broad, fairly level forest road, which is easy but long. The first 2.5 km or so are on an asphalted road, after which a forest road continues up the valley. The Kastenalm lies at about 1,220 m; the round trip to the Isarursprung and Kastenalm is about 28 km and takes some 7 hours, so with children pick a point to turn back halfway.

    If you want to go further, the route continues past the Kastenalm to the Hallerangerhaus at about 1,768 m; that tour is tougher and takes about 6 hours with roughly 850 metres of climbing. Bear in mind the long drive from Niederau. If you are short of time, take a short stretch of the valley: even the first kilometres along the young Isar give a good impression of the landscape, with rock faces on both sides and the river flowing between gravel banks.

    Tips

    • Bring water and food; there are few facilities along the way.
    • Choose the length of the walk to suit the children; you can turn back anywhere.
    • Check with the nature park information beforehand which routes are open.
    • Consider a bike: the broad forest road can also be cycled, which shortens the distance.
    • Plan the day so that you are back in Scharnitz well before dark.

    Practical

    Scharnitz is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 towards Innsbruck and then on towards Seefeld and Scharnitz. It is a long drive for a day's walk, so do not leave too late. From the village the road leads into the Hinterautal, where you start the walk. Bring enough water and food, as facilities along the way are limited.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where does the Isar begin?

    In the Karwendel, in the Hinterautal near Scharnitz; the Isarursprung is a well-known walking destination.

    How long is the walk to the Isarursprung and Kastenalm?

    The round trip is about 28 km and takes roughly 7 hours; you can turn back anywhere along the way.

    How big is the Karwendel Nature Park?

    About 739 km²; it is the largest nature park in Austria.
