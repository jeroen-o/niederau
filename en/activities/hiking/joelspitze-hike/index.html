Joelspitze

Mountain hike from the Schatzberg to a 1,964 m summit

The Joelspitze is a 1,964 m summit in the Kitzbühel Alps and a medium-difficulty mountain hike. The best variant starts with the Schatzberg cable car in Auffach. From the top station you follow a gently rising ridge path and a few moderate climbs, about 400 metres of ascent and descent in total.

On the way you pass alpine meadows and moorland, on the ridge between the Wildschönau and the Alpbach valley. At the summit stands a striking statue of Mary and you have views from the Karwendel to the Kaisergebirge and the Zillertal Alps. The hike is about 7.3 km and takes around 3 hours; you return via the Gernalm to the top station of the Schatzberg cable car.

Place: Auffach

  • AgeFrom approx. 8 years with some mountain experience
  • DurationApprox. 3 hours
  • CostHiking free; Schatzberg cable car costs extra
  • DistanceApprox. 15 min by car to Auffach; route approx. 7.3 km
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationAuffach, Schatzberg

Tips

  • Check the Schatzberg cable car's operating times before setting off.
  • Wear hiking boots and bring rain gear; the weather can change quickly.
  • Plan a break at the Gernalm on the way back.
  • Pick a clear day for the views.

Practical

The Schatzberg cable car starts in Auffach, about 15 minutes by car from Niederau. The route suits walkers with some mountain experience; with young children only if they can walk for a few hours.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How high is the Joelspitze?

The summit is at 1,964 m; from the top station of the Schatzberg cable car it is about 400 metres of ascent and descent.

How long does the hike take?

About 3 hours for approx. 7.3 km, including the return via the Gernalm.
