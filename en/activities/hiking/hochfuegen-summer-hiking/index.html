Hiking in Hochfügen

Hiking and play above the Zillertal, with an adventure playground

Hochfügen lies above Fügen, on the western side of the Zillertal, and is best known as a ski area. In summer it is a starting point for hikes and mountain bike tours, for example the Four-Alm walk or the climb of the Marchkopf (2,450 m). The area borders the Hochzillertal.

For families there is a new adventure playground with a play pond, climbing elements, a tubing run and a bouldering wall, plus themed children's trails such as the Hubertus Wildtierpfad. Check beforehand which lifts run in summer and on which days.

Place: Fügen

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 45 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationHochfügen, Zillertal

Tips

  • Check beforehand whether the lift runs and when the last descent is.
  • Bring a jacket; it can be cool at 2,000 metres.
  • Choose a short route with small children.

Practical

Hochfügen is about 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the Zillertal to Fügen and then uphill.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

At what altitude do you hike in Hochfügen?

The hiking routes start at around 1,500 to 2,000 metres; the Marchkopf is 2,450 m. Check the lifts beforehand.
