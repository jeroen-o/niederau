Alpbach, Tyrol's flower village

Timber farmhouses, flowers and mountains in a side valley of the Inn

Alpbach lies in a side valley of the Inn valley, in the Alpbachtal, and is known for its unity of building style: timber houses with plenty of flowers on the balconies. The village is praised as one of the most beautiful in Austria.

You can spend half a day strolling past the church, farmhouses and terraces, for instance on the village route along the Heimatweg. At the end of summer the village is also known for the Europäisches Forum Alpbach, an international congress. The surrounding hills offer short walks and views over the valley, so the village combines well with a mountain trip or a walk across the meadows.

Place: Alpbach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostA walk through the village is free
  • DistanceAbout 35 min by car
  • SeasonYear-round
  • LocationAlpbach, Alpbachtal

Tips

  • Come in the morning; it is quieter and the light is better for photos.
  • Bear in mind that the road to the village can be narrow and winding.
  • Combine the visit with the Wiedersbergerhornbahn for views.
  • Check the events calendar if you want to see a particular event.

Practical

Alpbach is about 35 minutes by car from Niederau, via Brixlegg or the Inn valley motorway and the Alpbachtal. The road to the village is narrow in places; there are car parks near the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is Alpbach known for?

For its uniform timber architecture with plenty of flowers, and for the Europäisches Forum Alpbach at the end of summer.
