Hahnenkamm in summer

By cable car above Kitzbühel: walking, views and huts

The Hahnenkamm is the mountain above Kitzbühel, known in winter for the Streif downhill. In summer it is a hiking and biking mountain. The Hahnenkammbahn runs from the centre of Kitzbühel to the top station at around 1,650 m. The mountain's highest point, the Ehrenbachhöhe, is at 1,802 m.

From the top station, hiking paths lead off with views of the Kitzbühel Alps and beyond, including a route past small lakes to the Ehrenbachhöhe. There are mountain huts for a break, and for those who like activity there are also cycling routes. The ride up starts right in town, which makes it suitable for half a day.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance

Tips

  • Choose a clear day for the best views.
  • Walk part of the route to the Ehrenbachhöhe if you want a short hike.
  • Combine the day with a visit to Kitzbühel's town centre.
  • Check the lift's operating times in advance.

Practical

Kitzbühel is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the B170. The Hahnenkammbahn starts close to the centre; there is paid parking in town.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
