Hahnenkamm in summer

By cable car above Kitzbühel: walking, views and huts

The Hahnenkamm is the mountain above Kitzbühel, known in winter for the Streif downhill. In summer it is a hiking and biking area with panorama paths, experience stations, flow trails and many huts. The Hahnenkammbahn starts in Kitzbühel itself, at 786 m. The mountain's highest point, the Ehrenbachhöhe, is at 1,802 m.

Walks range widely, for example the four-lakes hike or a route to the Ehrenbachhöhe, with views of the Kitzbühel Alps. There are huts for a break, and if you like you can take the gondola back down for part of the way. Allow half a day.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationKitzbühel

Tips

  • Choose a clear day for the best views.
  • Walk part of the route to the Ehrenbachhöhe for a relaxed hike; you can take the gondola back down.
  • Combine the day with a visit to Kitzbühel's town centre.
  • Check the lift's operating times in advance.

Practical

Kitzbühel is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau. The Hahnenkammbahn starts at the edge of the centre; parking is available in town.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
