Plansee: walking and swimming

Large mountain lake near Reutte with trails and bathing spots

The Plansee lies in the Reutte area in north-western Tyrol, framed by wooded slopes and limestone walls. The water is clear and a blue-green colour. Paths run around the lake, from a short shore walk to a full circuit of roughly 14 km, which takes about four hours.

At the Seespitze on the southern shore you will find parking, a campsite and places to eat. There is a bathing beach and the water is easy to reach in several places. This is a day trip: the drive is long, so plan a full day and bring something to eat.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf day to full day
  • CostWalking is free; parking is charged
  • DistanceApprox. 1.5 to 2 hours' drive
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationPlansee, Reutte area (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Choose the panorama path (about 6 km) from the Seespitze car park if you want a shorter walk with children.
  • Bring swimwear and a towel; the water stays cool even in summer.
  • Arrive early: parking near the lake fills up quickly on fine days.

Practical

From Niederau you drive via the A12 and the Fernpass route to Reutte (about 1.5 to 2 hours); parking at the lake is charged. Public transport is awkward, so the car is most practical.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim in the Plansee?

Yes, there is a bathing beach and several easy access points. The water is cool, though.
