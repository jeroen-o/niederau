Kundler Klamm

Walk through the gorge from Mühltal to Kundl

The Kundler Klamm is a gorge along the Wildschönauer Ache, between Mühltal and the Inn valley near Kundl. The path crosses a wooden bridge, passes rock overhangs and runs through short tunnels. It is a calm, easy route set up as a themed walk, and it suits families well.

On foot it takes roughly an hour in each direction; the full walk is about 6.5 km. If you do not want to walk both ways, you can cover part of it with the small tourist train (Bummelzug), which takes about 20 minutes from Mühltal to the entrance of the gorge. The path is easy to walk, also with a pushchair or wheelchair. The gorge is accessible roughly from April to mid-November.

Place: Mühltal

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 3 hours return
  • CostAsk in advance about train and parking costs
  • DistanceApprox. 15 to 25 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (approx. April to mid-November)
  • LocationBetween Mühltal and Kundl

Tips

  • Use the train for one direction to keep the outing relaxed for small children.
  • Bring a light jacket; it is cooler in the gorge than in the valley.
  • Check beforehand that the gorge and the train are running.
  • The path suits pushchairs, so it also works well with toddlers.

Practical

Mühltal is about 15 minutes by car from Niederau; the entrance on the Kundl side lies at the edge of the Inn valley, roughly 25 minutes by car. There is parking at both ends.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Kundler Klamm suitable for pushchairs?

Yes, the path is easy to walk and accessible for pushchairs and wheelchairs.

How long does the walk take?

Roughly an hour each way; the train from Mühltal to the entrance takes about 20 minutes.
