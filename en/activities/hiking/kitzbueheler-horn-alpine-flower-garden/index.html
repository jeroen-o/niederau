Kitzbüheler Horn and Alpine Flower Garden

Flower-rich mountain walks and a botanical garden high above Kitzbühel

The Kitzbüheler Horn, at 1,996 metres, is one of the mountains above Kitzbühel. The Hornbahn, which runs in summer from May to early October, gets you up quickly. Walking routes start from there, including ones for families.

The Alpenblumengarten covers about 20,000 m² and shows more than 400 species of alpine plants from different parts of the world. The walk through the garden is an easy family route of about 2 km, taking around an hour. What is in bloom depends on the season, from late spring into autumn. From the top you have views over Kitzbühel and the surrounding mountains.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • AgeAll ages (easy route)
  • DurationAbout 3–4 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (May to early October)
  • LocationKitzbüheler Horn, Kitzbühel

Tips

  • Choose a clear day for the best views.
  • Bring a jacket; it is cooler up top than in the valley.
  • Choose a walking route to suit the children's ages.

Practical

Kitzbühel is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau. The Hornbahn runs in summer roughly from May to early October; check current times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

A botanical garden of about 20,000 m² with more than 400 species of alpine plants on the Kitzbüheler Horn.
