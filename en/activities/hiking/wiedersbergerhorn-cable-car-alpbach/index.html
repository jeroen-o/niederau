Wiedersbergerhornbahn, Alpbach

By gondola to alpine pastures and the Lauserland

The Wiedersbergerhornbahn in Alpbach is a six-seat gondola that takes you in summer to a hiking and pasture area at around 1,850 m. At the top there are huts, grazing cattle and panorama paths around the Wiedersberger Horn, with views of the neighbouring valleys.

For families there is the Lauserland, with various play and adventure stations near the top station. Around late June and early July the alpine rose can bloom beautifully. It suits a relaxed half day, for example with a hut stop, but also serves as a starting point for longer hikes.

Place: Alpbach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 35 to 40 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (approx. May to November)
  • LocationAlpbach, Alpbachtal

Tips

  • Check whether you have an Alpbachtal Card; it can make the lift free in summer.
  • Allow time for the Lauserland play stations.
  • Bring an extra layer; it can be cool at the top.
  • Combine the trip with a walk through Alpbach village.

Practical

The valley station is in the Alpbachtal, about 35 to 40 minutes by car from Niederau. The lift generally runs from late May to November in summer; check the current times.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
