Nordic walking in the Wildschönau

Walking with poles through the valley, for young and old

Nordic walking is walking with special poles and works the upper body as well as the legs. Around Niederau, marked routes run through the valley and suit a relaxed workout, including for families and grandparents. From the village you quickly reach paths with views over the valley.

If you do not know the technique, you can be guided; guided groups may run, so ask the tourist office about days and times. Poles may be available to rent, so ask about the cost. Allow about 1.5 to 2 hours for an average route of 5 to 10 km. A good way to get moving without heavy climbs.

  • AgeFrom approx. 8 years
  • DurationApprox. 1.5 to 2 hours
  • CostPole rental possible; check beforehand
  • DistanceIn Niederau itself
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Have the technique shown to you; it makes the workout more effective.
  • Start early to avoid the heat.
  • The routes also work as ordinary walks.

Practical

The routes start in and around Niederau, so you can reach them on foot. The tourist office in the village provides maps and information.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent Nordic walking poles in Niederau?

Possibly; ask the tourist office or your accommodation about options and costs.
