    Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

    Brandstadl above Scheffau

    Walking and playing with the Wilder Kaiser as a backdrop

    In Scheffau am Wilden Kaiser the Brandstadlbahn, an eight-seat gondola, takes you in summer to around 1,650 m. Around the top station lies a pleasant walking area with views of the Wilder Kaiser rock walls. Close to the station you will also find the KaiserWelt, a play area for families.

    There, children can search for gemstones, climb and play beside streams. For walkers there are easy paths to places such as the Jochstub'nsee and the panorama route to the Hartkaiser station in Ellmau. It is a good choice if you want half a day of play or a gentle outing with the children.

    Place: Söll

    Tips

    • Allow time for the KaiserWelt if you travel with children.
    • The panorama route to Ellmau is longer; work out how you will get back.
    • Bring rain gear; mountain weather changes quickly.
    • Check the lift's operating times before you leave.

    Practical

    Scheffau is about 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Söll. The Brandstadlbahn generally runs from mid-May to mid-October in summer; times vary each year.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What can children do at the Scheffau top station?

    There is the KaiserWelt, with gem searching, climbing and playing by streams.
