Hochsöll and Hexenwasser (Söll)

Gondola up to the Hexenwasser, a playful hiking world for families

From Söll the Hochsöll gondola takes you up in a few minutes to the Hexenwasser (about 1,355 m), a mountain adventure world with indoor and outdoor water stations, a bee house with a beekeeper and an old farmhouse as the witches' headquarters. Children can explore the witches' forest barefoot. The paths are easy and full of small surprises.

If you want to go higher, take the second section of the gondola to the Hohe Salve (1,829 m), with views of around 70 peaks over 3,000 m. The 2026 summer season runs from 9 May to 1 November; check current times and fares before you set off.

Place: Söll

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 4 hours
  • CostGondola is charged; ask about fares
  • DistanceApprox. 25 min drive
  • SeasonSummer (9 May to 1 November 2026)
  • LocationSöll/Hochsöll, Kufstein region (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Bring dry clothes for the children: they will get wet at the water stations.
  • Check the current timetable and fares for the gondola beforehand.
  • Plan a stop at one of the mountain huts in the area after the Hexenwasser.

Practical

Söll is about 25 minutes by car from Niederau, in the Kufstein region. The valley station of the gondola is in the village; on the way up you get off at the Hexenwasser.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
