Hochsöll and Hexenwasser (Söll)

Gondola up to the Hexenwasser, a playful hiking world for families

From Söll the Hochsöll gondola takes you up in a few minutes to the Hexenwasser, a mountain adventure world with water stations, a beehive with a beekeeper and an old farmhouse that serves as the witches' headquarters. The paths are easy and full of small surprises, ideal for children.

If you want to go further, take the second section of the gondola to the Hohe Salve (1,829 m), with views of around 70 three-thousand-metre peaks and the Wilder Kaiser. You can also walk from Söll up to the Hexenwasser, a family route of about 8 km return with activity stations along the Spielbach. The season runs from May to early November.

Place: Söll

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 4 hours
  • CostGondola is charged; ask about fares
  • DistanceApprox. 25 min drive
  • SeasonSummer (approx. May to early November)
  • LocationSöll/Hochsöll, Kufstein region (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Bring dry clothes for the children: they will get wet at the water stations.
  • Check the current timetable and fares for the gondola beforehand.
  • Plan a stop at one of the mountain huts in the area after the Hexenwasser.

Practical

Söll is about 25 minutes by car from Niederau, in the Kufstein region. The valley station of the gondola is in the village; on the way up you can get off at the Hexenwasser.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Hexenwasser?

A mountain adventure world at Hochsöll with water stations, a beehive and an old farmhouse, aimed at families.
