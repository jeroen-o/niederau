    Tiroler Zugspitzbahn, Ehrwald

    By cable car up the Zugspitze, Germany's highest mountain

    The Tiroler Zugspitzbahn starts in Ehrwald and takes you up in two panoramic cabins for about 100 people, climbing more than 1,700 metres to the Zugspitzplatt in roughly ten minutes. At 2,962 metres the Zugspitze is Germany's highest mountain and sits on the border with Austria. The cable car is the oldest in Tyrol and turns 100 in 2026.

    In clear weather the view stretches up to 140 kilometres, and the Faszination Zugspitze museum is an option even when the weather is poor. It can be cold at the top, even in summer. Allow for a long drive from Niederau; the summer season runs roughly from May to November.

    Tips

    • Choose a clear day and check the forecast and webcams first.
    • Bring warm clothes and sun cream; it is noticeably colder at the top.
    • Expect a long day and do not plan other outings around it.

    Practical

    Ehrwald is about 1.5 to 2 hours by car from Niederau, via the A12 towards Telfs and on to Ehrwald. Check beforehand about parking at the valley station.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How high is the Zugspitze?

    The summit is at 2,962 metres and is Germany's highest point.
