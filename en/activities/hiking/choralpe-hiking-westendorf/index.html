Choralpe near Westendorf

A quiet Brixental mountain walk with family trails

The Choralpe is the hiking area above Westendorf in the Brixental. In summer the Alpenrosenbahn takes you up, and you are on the mountain pastures straight away. Paths lead from there past mountain huts and along quiet ridges, with views of the surrounding Kitzbühel Alps.

For families there are short themed trails with viewpoints and playful stops, giving children something to discover along the way. If you want more, you can walk longer loops. The area generally feels less busy than the best-known viewpoints in the region, although this depends on the weather and holiday periods.

Place: Westendorf

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 35 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (approx. May to October)
  • LocationWestendorf, Brixental

Tips

  • Choose a short themed trail if your children are under six.
  • Plan a hut stop; many huts serve simple Tyrolean dishes.
  • Check the operating times beforehand; the lift closes in the afternoon.
  • Bring sun cream and a rain jacket; mountain weather can change quickly.

Practical

Westendorf is about 35 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl or Hopfgarten. The Alpenrosenbahn generally runs from late May to late October in summer; exact times differ each year.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Choralpe suitable for children?

Yes, there are short themed family trails as well as longer routes for those who want to walk further.
