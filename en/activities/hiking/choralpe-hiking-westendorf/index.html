    Hiking in the Wildschönau and around · Summer

    Choralpe near Westendorf

    A quiet Brixental mountain walk with family trails

    The Choralpe is the hiking area above Westendorf in the Brixental. In summer the two-section Alpenrosenbahn takes you up to the Talkaser at about 1,770 m, and you are on the mountain pastures straight away. Paths lead from there past mountain huts, including the Alpenrosenhütte, with views of the Kitzbühel Alps.

    For families there is Alpinolino, an adventure world with themed trails, a marmot rally and a flight school with the eagle Bert. Children discover mountain nature through play. If you want more, you can walk longer loops, for instance towards the Fleidingalm. Plan a hut stop and check the weather and the lift's current operating times beforehand.

    Place: Westendorf

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationHalf a day
    • CostPrices vary; check in advance
    • DistanceAbout 30 min by car
    • SeasonSummer (dates vary by year)
    • LocationWestendorf, Brixental

    Tips

    • Choose a short Alpinolino themed trail if you have young children.
    • Plan a hut stop; many huts serve simple Tyrolean dishes.
    • Check the lift's operating times beforehand.
    • Bring sun cream and a rain jacket; mountain weather can change quickly.

    Practical

    Westendorf is about 30 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl or Hopfgarten. The Alpenrosenbahn runs in summer; exact season dates and times differ each year.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the Choralpe suitable for children?

    Yes, Alpinolino has themed trails, a marmot rally and other playful stops; those who want to walk further will also find longer routes.
