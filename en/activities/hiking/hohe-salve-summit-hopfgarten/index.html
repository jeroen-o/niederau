Hohe Salve from Hopfgarten

Panorama peak with a pilgrimage chapel above the Brixental

The Hohe Salve (1,829 m) is known as one of Tyrol's best viewpoints. From Hopfgarten im Brixental the Salvenbahn lifts you up, and you reach the summit in a short time. The view stretches across the Kitzbühel Alps and the Wilder Kaiser and, in clear weather, as far as the glaciated peaks of the Hohe Tauern.

The Salvenkirchlein on the summit is a pilgrimage chapel dating from 1777, described as the highest-situated pilgrimage church in Austria. There are mountain huts and walking trails of various lengths, so the trip suits families who simply want to enjoy the views as well as those who want a half-day hike. You can also walk up from the valley.

Place: Hopfgarten im Brixental

Tips

  • Go in the morning if you can; visibility is often best then.
  • Take a jacket; it is noticeably cooler at the top than in the valley.
  • Check the weather forecast and the Salvenbahn's operating times first.
  • Wear sturdy shoes if you plan to walk the trails.

Practical

Hopfgarten is about 25 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the Brixental. Parking is available at the valley station. The lift runs in summer, generally from mid-May to late October (2026: 14 May to 26 October).

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How high is the Hohe Salve and what is on the summit?

The summit is at 1,829 m. The Salvenkirchlein, a pilgrimage chapel from 1777, stands there.
