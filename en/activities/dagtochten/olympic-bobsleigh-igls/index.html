Olympic bob run Igls

Ride as a passenger in a guest bob on the Winter Games track

At Igls, high above Innsbruck, lies the Olympic bobsleigh and luge track, where the 1964 and 1976 Winter Olympics were held. In the winter season guests can ride in a guest bob (taxi bob) steered by a professional pilot.

The run lasts about a minute over a track of just over 1.2 kilometres and reaches speeds of up to about 100 km/h, with bends and strong lateral forces. You sit behind the pilot as a passenger. It is a short but intense experience, not for young children or anyone prone to motion sickness. Booking is recommended; check age rules and times on the official site.

Place: Innsbruck

  • AgeFrom about 10 years, with a guardian (check beforehand)
  • DurationRide about 1 minute; about 1 hour in total
  • CostPrice varies; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 55 to 65 min by car
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationIgls, Innsbruck

Tips

  • Book ahead; the operator may have limited places.
  • Check the minimum age; according to sources it is 10 years with a guardian.
  • Dress warmly and wear sturdy shoes.
  • Combine with a visit to Innsbruck's old town.

Practical

Igls is about 55 to 65 minutes' drive from Niederau via the A12 and Innsbruck. Parking is possible near the track; book and ask about opening times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How fast is a guest bob?

Up to about 100 km/h over a track of just over 1.2 km; the ride lasts about a minute.
