Zillertalbahn steam train

By narrow-gauge steam train from Jenbach to Mayrhofen

The Zillertalbahn is a narrow-gauge line from Jenbach in the Inn valley to Mayrhofen in the Zillertal. The route is about 32 to 33 kilometres long and runs through villages and meadows alongside the Ziller river. The steam journey takes about an hour and a half each way.

According to sources, the steam train only runs on certain days, roughly from May to early October; in winter the regular trains run. The steam train has a buffet car, an open carriage and a children's carriage. Families can get off en route, for example in Mayrhofen for a walk or a meal. Check the timetable on the official site beforehand.

Place: Jenbach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 3 hours return, plus a stop
  • CostPrice varies; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 35 to 40 min by car to Jenbach
  • SeasonSteam train roughly May to early October; regular trains all year
  • LocationJenbach and Zillertal

Tips

  • Check the timetable: steam trains do not run every day, nor in winter.
  • Take the open carriage for photos, but dress warmly.
  • Book in busy periods or with a group.
  • Plan a stop in Mayrhofen for lunch or a walk.

Practical

Jenbach station is about 35 to 40 minutes' drive from Niederau, via the A12. You can also take the train to Jenbach and change onto the Zillertalbahn.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
