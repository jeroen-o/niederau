Alpenzoo Innsbruck

Zoo with only Alpine animals, high above the city

The Alpenzoo stands at about 727 metres on the edge of Innsbruck and is regarded as Europe's highest zoo. It is a theme zoo, so you only see animals from the Alps and Europe: wolves, lynx, ibex, otters, birds of prey and more; together over 2,000 animals of about 150 species.

The path leads past enclosures on a hillside, with fine views over the city. For children it is a manageable visit and combines well with a city day in Innsbruck. You can get there on foot, by bus or via the Hungerburg funicular; from the station it is roughly 400 metres.

Place: Innsbruck

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2 to 3 hours
  • CostAdmission varies; children pay less, check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 50 to 60 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationInnsbruck, Tyrol

Tips

  • Take the Hungerburg funicular and walk the last stretch; it saves parking stress.
  • Check opening hours beforehand: longer in summer than in winter.
  • Wear comfortable shoes; the grounds are on a slope.
  • Combine with a walk through the old town.

Practical

Innsbruck is about 50 to 60 minutes' drive from Niederau. Parking near the zoo is limited; many visitors come by bus or the Hungerburg funicular.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Which animals can you see at the Alpenzoo?

Only animals from the Alps and Europe, such as wolf, lynx, ibex, otter and birds of prey.
