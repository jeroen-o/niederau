Skiing in Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau · Winter

Children's ski school in Niederau

First ski lessons in Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau

Niederau is home to the 1st Ski School Wildschönau, which teaches children, teens and adults to ski and snowboard in group or private lessons. For children from about 4 years there are kids' ski courses with a dedicated children's area, a magic carpet and a ski carousel; lessons include lunchtime supervision and finish with a small race.

The ski school also has a Kids Club with a slide and fairy-tale figures, among other things, and a kindergarten for children from about 2 years. There are also half-day and full-day courses for children aged 6 to 15, for experienced skiers and beginners. Niederau is part of the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau. Lesson times and rates vary; ask and book well ahead, especially in peak season.

Tips

  • Book well ahead in the busiest weeks.
  • A helmet is strongly advised for children; ask about hire.
  • Ask about the minimum age and lesson times for your child.

Practical

The ski school is in Niederau, where the lift to the ski area also starts. Ask for the meeting point and lesson times when booking; the ski school operates in the winter season.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

From what age can children have ski lessons?

According to the ski school, kids' courses start from about 4 years; younger children can go to the kindergarten. Ask for current conditions.
