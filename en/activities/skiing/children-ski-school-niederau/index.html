Children's ski school in Niederau

First ski lessons in Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau

Niederau lies in the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau, an area known for family-friendly slopes. For children there are ski schools in the valley, offering group lessons and usually private lessons too. That way they learn their first turns in a safe setting.

The ski area has several children's areas and gentle practice slopes for beginners. Exact minimum ages, lesson times and rates vary by provider, so ask the ski school beforehand. Book well ahead in peak season and ask whether lessons in Dutch are possible.

Tips

  • Book well ahead in the busiest weeks.
  • A helmet is strongly advised for children; ask about hire.
  • Ask about lessons in Dutch.

Practical

The ski school is in Niederau, close to the valley station. Niederau is part of the Ski Juwel; ask your accommodation for the nearest meeting point.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Are there children's areas in the ski area?

Yes, the Ski Juwel has several children's areas and gentle practice slopes.
