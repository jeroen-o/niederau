Skiing in Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau

Family-friendly skiing close to your door, with a large area nearby.

Niederau lies in the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area, one of the larger ski areas in Tyrol, linking the valleys of Alpbach and the Wildschönau. From the village you reach the slopes at the Markbachjoch by lift, so you are quickly on the piste in the morning and back at your accommodation in the afternoon.

The area is particularly appreciated by families and intermediate skiers: you will find many wide, easy-to-read slopes at various levels, and mountain restaurants where you can warm up. Beginners can use quiet practice slopes and ski schools in the villages. Advanced skiers find enough variety, though the area is less extreme than some larger Alpine regions.

The winter sports season runs roughly from December into March, depending on snow. Snow reliability is generally best in high season; check the current situation before departure. This page offers a general guide with practical tips; the individual ski activities, ski schools and outings nearby are described below.

Tips

  • Book ski lessons and rental in advance during school holidays.
  • Start early: the slopes are at their best.
  • Pack spare gloves and goggles for children.
  • Plan a break in a mountain hut to warm up.
  • Check snow and lift information before you set off.

For whom?

The area suits families, beginners and intermediate skiers. Children can often start ski lessons from around three or four years old; ask the ski school about minimum age and group size. Book lessons and equipment hire in advance where possible, especially during school holidays. Snowboarders are welcome and many slopes are suitable.

Safety and rules

The international FIS rules apply on the slopes: be considerate of others, adjust your speed and give way to those below or ahead of you. A helmet is strongly recommended for children and worn as standard by many families. Stay on marked slopes, heed avalanche warnings and take out travel or ski insurance that covers rescue costs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Niederau suitable for beginners?

Yes, there are quiet practice slopes and ski schools nearby. Ask the ski school about current lesson options.

When is the ski season?

Roughly December to March, depending on snow. Check the current situation before departure.

Do I need a ski pass for the whole region?

A ski pass is required for Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau. Ask the lift company about current prices and day or multi-day options.
