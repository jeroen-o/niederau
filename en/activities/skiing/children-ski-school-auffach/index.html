Skiing in Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau · Winter

Children's ski school Zwergenland, Auffach

Ski lessons for children in the quieter part of the valley

Auffach lies at the head of the Wildschönau valley and is the base for the Schatzberg. At the middle station of the Schatzbergbahn is the Zwergenland, the children's learning area of the Happy Skischule Wildschönau. Children from 4 years learn to ski playfully here, with two conveyor lifts, a carousel and modern teaching aids.

The ski school offers group courses for beginners and more advanced children of about 5 to 13, on skis or snowboard, as well as private lessons. At the end of a course there is a children's race where every child gets a medal. You meet at the Zwergenland about a quarter of an hour before the start. Ask beforehand about language and rates.

Place: Auffach

Tips

  • Consider a private lesson on the first day.
  • Ask about the teaching language and Dutch-speaking instructors.
  • Be at the Zwergenland in good time.

Practical

Auffach is about 15 minutes from Niederau. The Zwergenland is at the middle station of the Schatzbergbahn, so you first take the gondola. Check the ski bus timetable and current course dates.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a children's area at the Schatzberg?

Yes, the Zwergenland at the middle station of the Schatzbergbahn, for children from about 4.
