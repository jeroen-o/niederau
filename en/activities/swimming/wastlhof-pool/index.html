Swimming and water fun around Niederau · Summer

Hotel Wastlhof swimming pool

Indoor and outdoor pool at partner hotel Wastlhof in Niederau

Hotel Wastlhof, a partner of Niederau.nl, is a four-star wellness hotel in Niederau that has been run by the Brunner family for three generations. It has an indoor pool and an outdoor pool that is heated all year round, with views of the mountains around Niederau.

In summer the outdoor pool is a pleasant place to cool off after a walk or bike ride; in winter you can look out at the snowy mountains from the warm water. The pool is meant for hotel guests. If you are not staying there and want to swim, there is also a heated open-air pool in the valley between Niederau and Oberau. For current information, contact the hotel.

Practical

The pool is on the grounds of Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau. Use is intended for hotel guests; contact the hotel for other options.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

The outdoor pool is heated all year; it is intended for hotel guests. Ask the hotel for current times.
