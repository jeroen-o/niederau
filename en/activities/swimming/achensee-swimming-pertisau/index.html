Swimming at Lake Achensee

Tyrol's largest lake with clear water and small beaches

Lake Achensee lies at about 930 m between the Karwendel and Rofan mountains and is the largest lake in Tyrol. The water is clear and fresh; in summer it warms up to around 22 degrees. Around the lake there are beach baths, including in Pertisau, Achenkirch and Maurach, with sunbathing lawns, jetties and play areas.

Besides swimming you can take pedal boats or rowing boats onto the water. With the lake steamers and walking paths around it, the lake also makes a good outing without swimming. It does take some driving from Niederau, so plan a full day.

Tips

  • The water stays fresh even in summer; take a towel and perhaps water shoes.
  • Choose a beach bath, for instance Pertisau.
  • Combine swimming with a boat trip or a walk.
  • Check the weather and entry fees before leaving.

Practical

Lake Achensee is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via the A12 to the Jenbach exit and on towards the lake. Beach baths have their own or nearby parking; check current access details.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How warm does the water get?

Up to around 22 degrees in summer, so it stays fresh.
