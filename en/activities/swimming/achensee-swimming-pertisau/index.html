Swimming at Lake Achensee

Tyrol's largest lake with clear water and beach baths

Lake Achensee lies at about 930 m between the Karwendel and Rofan mountains and is the largest lake in Tyrol. The water is very clear; it remains a mountain lake and is usually around 18 to 20 degrees, at most about 22 degrees in summer. Around the lake there are bathing spots and beach baths, including near Pertisau, Maurach and Achenkirch.

Besides swimming, the lake makes a good outing in its own right, with the lake steamers and the walking paths along the water. It does take some driving from Niederau, so plan a good day for it.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostVaries by bathing spot; please enquire
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationAchensee, Tyrol

Tips

  • The water stays fresh even in summer; take a towel and perhaps water shoes.
  • Choose a beach bath, for instance near Pertisau.
  • Combine swimming with a boat trip or a walk.
  • Check the weather and access conditions before leaving.

Practical

Lake Achensee is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 to the Jenbach exit and on towards the lake. Check current access and parking at the beach bath you choose.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How warm does the water get?

On average about 18 to 20 degrees, up to around 22 in summer, so it stays fresh.
