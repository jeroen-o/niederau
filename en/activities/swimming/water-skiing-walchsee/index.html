Water skiing and water sports on the Walchsee

Water skiing, tubes and boat rental on one of Tyrol's warmest lakes

The Walchsee in the Kaiserwinkl is Tyrol's fourth-largest lake by area (about 95 hectares) and warms up to roughly 24 °C in summer. It is said to be the only lake in Tyrol where you can water ski behind a boat, a tradition dating back to the 1950s.

The Karl²O water sports centre offers water skiing, tube and wing rides, stand-up paddleboarding, rental of electric boats and pedal boats, and fishing. Ask beforehand whether wakeboarding is available. Minimum ages differ per activity, so ask about the conditions and book ahead when it is busy.

Place: Walchsee

  • AgeDepends on the activity; check beforehand
  • DurationAbout 1 to 2 hours
  • CostPrice varies; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 45 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationWalchsee, Kaiserwinkl

Tips

  • Ask about age limits and availability per activity beforehand.
  • Use sunscreen: the sun is strong on open water.
  • The water is relatively warm, so a fall is less of a shock.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 45 minutes by car via Wörgl and Kufstein towards Walchsee. Prices and times vary by season; ask the water sports centre.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you water ski on the Walchsee?

Yes, the Walchsee is known as the only lake in Tyrol where you can water ski behind a boat; the water sports centre runs it.
