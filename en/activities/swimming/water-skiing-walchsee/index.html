    Water skiing and water sports on the Walchsee

    Water skiing, tubes and boat rental on one of Tyrol's warmest lakes

    The Walchsee in the Kaiserwinkl is Tyrol's fourth-largest lake by area (about 95 hectares) and warms up to roughly 24 °C in summer. It is said to be the only lake in Tyrol where you can water ski behind a boat, a tradition dating back to the 1950s. The Karl²O water sports centre has existed for more than sixty years and was founded by Karl Karl.

    The centre offers water skiing, tube and wing rides, stand-up paddleboarding, rental of electric boats and pedal boats, and fishing. Ask beforehand whether wakeboarding is available.

    Minimum ages differ per activity for children and beginners, so ask about the conditions and book ahead when it is busy. With relatively warm water and the lake set in open scenery between the Kaiser Mountains and the Chiemgau Alps, it is a pleasant place for an active afternoon. If you want to try the water sports centre once, a short session is a good start: first see what suits your family's age and level, then build up. If you do not want to take part yourself, you can enjoy the sight of skiers and tubes on the water from the shore or a terrace.

    Tips

    • Ask about age limits and availability per activity beforehand.
    • Use sunscreen: the sun is strong on open water.
    • The water is relatively warm, so a fall is less of a shock.
    • Try a tube or SUP first if water skiing still feels too daunting.
    • Bring dry clothes and a towel for afterwards.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about 45 minutes by car via Wörgl and Kufstein towards Walchsee. The water sports centre is on the lake, near the village. Prices and times vary by season; ask the water sports centre, and book in good time in fine weather, when it gets busy. Combine it with a swimming afternoon by the lake. Booking is sensible in fine weather and school holidays.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you water ski on the Walchsee?

    Yes, the Walchsee is known as the only lake in Tyrol where you can water ski behind a boat; the water sports centre runs it.

    Which other water sports are there?

    Tube and wing rides, stand-up paddleboarding, rental of electric boats and pedal boats, and fishing.

    How warm does the water get?

    Up to roughly 24 °C in summer.
