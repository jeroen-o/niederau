    Swimming and water fun around Niederau · Summer

    Pillersee near St. Ulrich

    Swimming, boat hire and a stroll around an alpine lake

    The Pillersee lies in the Kitzbühel Alps near St. Ulrich am Pillersee. On the north-east shore there is a freely accessible lido, and boats and SUP boards can be rented by the water.

    The Pillerseerunde runs around the lake: an easy walk of about 4.4 km that takes a little over an hour and is pushchair-friendly. There are many benches along the way, and at the Seestüberl you can go stand-up paddling or pedalling. There is also a 3D archery course by the lake. It makes a relaxed family outing combining swimming, walking and a stop on a terrace.

    The Pillersee lies at about 835 m, covers around 24 hectares and is at most seven metres deep. The lake was formed some 15,000 years ago by a landslide that blocked the outflow at the Öfenschlucht. In summer the water does not get warmer than about 18 degrees, so it is fresh. There are also a climbing park, pedal boats, a marble run and a playground, and anglers come for the trout. The lake suits a quiet day outdoors, without any rush. You can walk for an hour, then go in the water, then have a bite at the Seestüberl and perhaps rent a boat. With the play equipment as well, it is an outing where both small and bigger children will find plenty to do.

    Tips

    • The loop is about 4.4 km; allow a little over an hour.
    • The path is suitable for pushchairs.
    • Rent an SUP board or pedal boat at the Seestüberl.
    • The water stays cool in summer, up to about 18 degrees; bear that in mind when swimming.
    • Children will enjoy the playground, the marble run and the climbing park.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car via Wörgl, Kitzbühel and St. Johann to St. Ulrich am Pillersee. The lido is on the north-east shore of the lake and freely accessible; boats and SUP boards are rented by the water. Parking costs and opening times of the facilities can vary, so check them before you set off. Bring a towel and sun cream, even if you mainly want to walk.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the walk around the Pillersee suitable for pushchairs?

    Yes, the Pillerseerunde is an easy route of about 4.4 km (a little over an hour) and suitable for pushchairs.

    How was the Pillersee formed?

    By a landslide about 15,000 years ago that blocked the outflow at the Öfenschlucht.

    How warm does the Pillersee water get?

    In summer no warmer than about 18 degrees.
