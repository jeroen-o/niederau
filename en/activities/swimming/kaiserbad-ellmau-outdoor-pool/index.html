Kaiserbad Ellmau

Pool complex with outdoor pool and natural pond below the Wilder Kaiser

The Kaiserbad is in Ellmau at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser. In summer the outdoor pool is the heart of the complex, with a wide slide, a children's pool, a play area and a large sunbathing lawn. There is also a natural swimming pond with a diving tower.

The Kaiserbad also has an indoor section with slides and a wellness area, which makes it usable in bad weather too. For families it makes a good half day, for instance combined with a walk or a visit to Ellmi's Zauberwelt nearby.

Tips

  • Check opening hours beforehand; they vary by season.
  • Bring towels and sun cream for the lawn.
  • Summer afternoons are often busiest; go earlier in the day.
  • Combine it with Ellmi's Zauberwelt or a walk.

Practical

Ellmau is about 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Söll. Parking is available at the pool. Check current opening hours and prices with the pool itself.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an indoor pool too?

Yes, the Kaiserbad also has an indoor section with slides, handy in bad weather.
