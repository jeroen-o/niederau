    Swimming and water fun around Niederau · Summer

    Hechtsee beach near Kufstein

    Popular bathing lake with play areas and a lakeside restaurant

    Hechtsee lies close to Kufstein, just a short distance from the German border. It is the largest and, at about 57 m, also the deepest lake west of Kufstein, and counts as one of Tyrol's most popular bathing lakes. The beach bath has generous lawns with sun and shade, a playground, children's pools, a diving platform and floating islands.

    Children can also enjoy beach volleyball, water volleyball and table tennis. Boats can be hired and there is a lakeside restaurant. A level path runs around the lake, suitable for families with children. The lake combines well with a visit to Kufstein and its fortress.

    Place: Kufstein

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationHalf to full day
    • CostAdmission charged; ask about current prices
    • DistanceApprox. 35 min by car
    • SeasonSummer (approx. May to September)
    • LocationKufstein

    Tips

    • Take a towel and sun cream for the lawn.
    • Combine with a visit to Kufstein Fortress.
    • Check the beach bath's opening hours.
    • Arrive early on hot days to find a good spot.

    Practical

    Hechtsee is about 35 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the A12 towards Kufstein. The beach bath is open in summer, usually from May; ask about current opening hours and prices.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you walk around Hechtsee?

    Yes, a level path runs around the lake and is suitable for families with children.
