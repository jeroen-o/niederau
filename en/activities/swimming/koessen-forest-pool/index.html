Kössen Forest Pool

Heated outdoor pool in the woods near Kössen

In Kössen, in the Kaiserwinkl near the German border, you will find the Erlebnis-Waldschwimmbad: a heated outdoor pool with about 700 sq m of water surface, at roughly 24 degrees, set among trees. It is a good alternative if you prefer a pool to a lake.

The pool has two slides, a baby and children's pool, a whirlpool, a snack bar and a large sunbathing lawn of about 6,000 sq m with shady spots, plus a beach volleyball court and table tennis. There is also a paddling area and a water mushroom for the smallest. Parking is free.

The season runs roughly from mid-May to mid-September; in July and August the pool is usually open for longer hours than in early and late season. Check opening times and prices before you go. You can combine the visit with a walk or bike ride through the Kaiserwinkl. For those with small children, a pool with shallow water and plenty of shade is pleasant: there is always somewhere to sit and have a drink. That lets you spend the day calmly, without being tied to the conditions of a lake.

Place: Kössen

Tips

  • Check opening times and the weather beforehand.
  • It is usually quieter on weekdays.
  • Combine with a walk in the Kaiserwinkl.
  • Bring a parasol or sun hat for the lawn.
  • Let the smallest children start in the paddling area before moving to the main pool.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 55 minutes by car via Wörgl and Kufstein towards Kössen. The pool lies in the woods and has free parking. Allow half a day or a full day; in peak season it is quieter on weekdays. Kössen is close to the German border, so a visit also fits well into a day in the Kaiserwinkl. Bring swimwear and a towel, and some cash for a snack.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kössen's bathing spot a lake or a pool?

It is a heated outdoor pool in the woods (Erlebnis-Waldschwimmbad), not a natural lake.

When is the Kössen pool open?

Roughly from mid-May to mid-September; days are longer in July and August. Check the current times beforehand.

How warm is the water?

The pool is heated and sits at roughly 24 degrees.
