Kössen Forest Pool

Heated outdoor pool in the woods near Kössen

In Kössen, in the Kaiserwinkl near the German border, you will find the Erlebnis-Waldschwimmbad: a heated outdoor pool with about 700 sq m of water surface, at roughly 24 degrees, set among trees. It is a good alternative if you prefer a pool to a lake.

The pool has two slides, a baby and children's pool, a whirlpool, a snack bar and a large sunbathing lawn, plus a beach volleyball court and table tennis. Parking is free. The season runs roughly from mid-May to mid-September; check opening times and prices before you go. You can combine the visit with a walk or bike ride through the Kaiserwinkl.

Place: Kössen

Practical

From Niederau it is about 55 minutes by car via Wörgl and Kufstein towards Kössen.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kössen's bathing spot a lake or a pool?

It is a heated outdoor pool in the woods (Erlebnis-Waldschwimmbad), not a natural lake.
