Brixlegg open-air pool

Open-air pool with a children's pool below Lanegg Castle

The Freischwimmbad in Brixlegg lies in the Alpbachtal, right below Lanegg Castle and next to the Mining and Smelting Museum. It has two swimming pools, a children's pool and a large sunbathing lawn.

It is a simple, spacious municipal pool: no spectacular attractions, but a pleasant place to spend a warm afternoon. The pool only opens in good weather, so check on the day whether it is open. Entry is included with the Alpbachtal Card. It fits well into a day out in the Alpbachtal, for example combined with the mining museum or a visit to nearby Rattenberg.

Place: Brixlegg

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf a day
  • CostPrices vary; about € 6 for adults and € 3 for children (check beforehand)
  • DistanceApprox. 25 to 30 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationBrixlegg, Alpbachtal

Tips

  • Check beforehand whether the pool is open if the weather is uncertain.
  • An afternoon rate usually applies after 2 pm.
  • Bring the Alpbachtal Card, which includes entry.
  • Combine it with the mining museum or Rattenberg.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 25 to 30 minutes by car via Wörgl and the A12 or B171 towards Brixlegg. Opening times follow the summer season; check the current hours with the municipality or the tourist office.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is entry included with the Alpbachtal Card?

Yes, according to the tourist board, entry is included with the Alpbachtal Card. Check the conditions with your accommodation.
