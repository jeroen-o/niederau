Hopfgarten Bathing Lake (Salvenaland)

Bathing lake and leisure park at the foot of the Hohe Salve

Hopfgarten im Brixental lies at the foot of the Hohe Salve. In the village is the Salvenaland, a leisure park with a bathing lake, a children's pool with a water play street, an inflatable iceberg for older children, a summer toboggan run, an 18-hole mini golf course and mini carts. There are also volleyball, basketball and football pitches and bouncy cushions.

It is a manageable option for families who want to swim close to Niederau, about 20 minutes by car. The park can be entered freely during opening hours; some attractions, such as mini golf, the mini carts and the toboggan run, are paid separately. There is a restaurant with a sunny terrace at the lake. The exact opening period may change, so check it beforehand. Bring towels and sun cream.

The Salvenaland can be entered freely, and the whole park is open from May to October according to the tourist board. You can rent a summer sledge there for a small fee. In the summer months the mini golf course stays open until 10 pm, handy for an outing after dinner. The park is compact enough to let children play freely for a few hours while parents watch from the terrace. If you like, you can start the day with a swim and end with a run on the summer toboggan track. That fills half a day quickly.

Tips

  • Check the opening period beforehand.
  • Come on a sunny day and bring sun cream.
  • Combine with the summer toboggan run or mini golf.
  • Plan the mini golf for late afternoon; it stays open until 10 pm in summer.
  • Rent a summer sledge on site for a small fee.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 20 minutes by car to Hopfgarten im Brixental, at the foot of the Hohe Salve. The Salvenaland is in the village and freely accessible during opening hours, from May to October. Before you leave, check the current opening hours and prices of the paid attractions such as the toboggan run, mini carts and mini golf, and look at the weather. Park in the village; the park is an easy walk from there.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What else is there to do at the Salvenaland besides swimming?

There are a summer toboggan run, 18-hole mini golf, mini carts and a children's pool with a water play street, among other things.

Do I have to pay admission for the Salvenaland?

No, the park is freely accessible; only certain attractions such as mini golf, mini carts and the toboggan run cost extra.

When is the Salvenaland open?

The season runs from May to October; check the current period and times beforehand.
