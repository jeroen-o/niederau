    Swimming and water fun around Niederau · Summer

    Stand-up paddling on Lake Walchsee

    Paddle on one of Tyrol's warmest bathing lakes

    Lake Walchsee in the Kaiserwinkl covers about 95 hectares, making it one of Tyrol's larger lakes, and is regarded as one of the warmest bathing lakes; in summer the water can reach about 24 degrees. At its deepest point the lake is just over 21 metres deep. That makes it a pleasant place for a first go at stand-up paddling: falling in is no problem.

    By the lake is the Karl2o water sports centre, where SUP boards, electric boats and pedal boats, among other things, can be hired. On the east shore there is also a beach bar that hires out SUP boards and boats. While paddling you see the mountains around the lake. It lies about 45 minutes by car from Niederau, so it fits well into a day that also includes a swim at the beach.

    Tips

    • Start on your knees and stand up once you have your balance.
    • Wear swimwear or clothes that can get wet.
    • Book ahead with the hirer when it is busy.
    • Combine it with an afternoon swim at the beach.

    Practical

    Lake Walchsee is about 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, Kufstein and Walchsee. Check current rates, age limits and opening times with the hirer.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where can you hire a SUP board on Lake Walchsee?

    At the Karl2o water sports centre and at the beach bar on the east shore; check current opening times.
