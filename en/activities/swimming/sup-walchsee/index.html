Stand-up paddling on Lake Walchsee

Paddle on one of Tyrol's warmest bathing lakes

Lake Walchsee in the Kaiserwinkl is regarded as one of the warmest bathing lakes in Tyrol. In summer the water can reach about 24 to 25 degrees. That makes the lake a pleasant place for a first go at stand-up paddling: falling in is no problem.

By the lake there is a water sports centre where boats and SUP boards can be hired. There is also a beach bath with a playground and other facilities. While paddling you see the mountains around the lake.

Place: Walchsee

Tips

  • Start on your knees and stand up once you have your balance.
  • Wear swimwear or clothes that can get wet.
  • Book ahead with the hirer when it is busy.
  • Combine it with an afternoon swim at the beach.

Practical

Lake Walchsee is about 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, Kufstein and Walchsee. Check current rates, age limits and opening times with the hirer.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
