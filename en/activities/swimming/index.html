Swimming and water fun around Niederau

Mountain lakes, pools and water fun within driving distance of the Wildschönau.

A holiday in the Alps is not only about mountains: around Niederau there are also lakes and swimming pools where families can cool off on a warm day. Within about half an hour to three quarters of an hour by car you reach several bathing lakes in the Tyrolean Unterland, including those around Kramsach and near Walchsee.

Tyrolean mountain lakes are generally clear and green, but the water is often cooler than you may be used to from lowland lakes. Many lakes have a sunbathing lawn, a small snack bar and a marked swimming area; some are free to enter, while others charge an entrance fee. For young children, shallow areas and play meadows are a plus.

In poor weather an indoor pool is a good alternative. The region has swimming pools and spa facilities, and many accommodations have their own pool or sauna for guests. This page offers tips for a successful day at the water; the individual lakes and pools are described below. Always check opening times and the season beforehand.

Tips

  • Bring water shoes for stony shores.
  • Arrive early on warm days: the most popular lakes fill up.
  • An extra towel and dry clothes for children are worth packing.
  • Check weather and opening times beforehand.
  • Plan an ice cream or a break after swimming.

When to swim?

The bathing lakes are generally suitable in summer, especially in July and August when the water is warmest. In June and September the water can still be cool. Many outdoor pools open only in the summer season, so ask about dates in advance. In a thunderstorm or cold spell, choose an indoor pool.

Safety with children

Always keep small children within reach, even in shallow water. Mountain lakes get deep quickly and may have cold currents. Swim only in designated areas, heed warning signs and do not let children enter the water alone with inflatables. Bring sun cream and a hat: the sun is stronger at altitude than you expect.

Frequently asked questions

How warm is the water in the mountain lakes?

It varies by lake and by day. In high summer it is often pleasant but cooler than lowland lakes. Ask on site.

Are there swimming options when it rains?

Yes, the region has indoor pools and spa facilities, and many accommodations have their own pool. Check opening times and access rules beforehand.

Do I have to pay entrance at the lakes?

Some lakes are freely accessible, others have a lido with an entrance fee. It varies, so check the page for the lake in question first.
