    Swimming and water fun around Niederau · Summer

    Schwarzsee Kitzbühel

    Warm moor lake with an open-air lido near Kitzbühel

    The Schwarzsee lies a few kilometres from Kitzbühel and is a moor lake. The water is brownish and not crystal clear, but warms up remarkably in summer: it is regarded as one of the warmest moor lakes in Tyrol, with temperatures up to around 27 degrees. On average the water temperature is around 22 degrees.

    The open-air lido at the lake has water slides, a diving tower, a children's pool, a playground and beach volleyball and football pitches. During the summer season it is open daily during the day, with a reduced afternoon rate for late arrivals. There are also a few beaches around the lake. A visit fits well with an afternoon in Kitzbühel.

    The lake lies at about 762 m, some two kilometres west of Kitzbühel, and its water quality is rated excellent. A walking path with a fitness course runs around the water all year, which is handy for anyone who does not want to swim. The lido also has deckchairs and parasols for rent, and a buffet. With its combination of warm water, green surroundings and plenty to play on, it is a popular spot for families. Those who mainly want to relax will find peace on the grassy areas and by the shore; those who want activity can jump, slide or kick a ball around. Half a day is usually plenty.

    Tips

    • The water is peaty and not crystal clear; that is normal.
    • Combine with a visit to Kitzbühel town centre.
    • Check the lido's opening hours beforehand.
    • Take a stroll along the path around the lake if you do not feel like swimming for a while.
    • Rent a deckchair or parasol at the lido if you are staying for a long day.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about 45 minutes by car to Kitzbühel; the lake lies a few kilometres outside town, between Kitzbühel and Reith bei Kitzbühel. For an afternoon it combines well with a stroll through the centre of Kitzbühel. Check the lido's opening hours and current admission prices beforehand, as they can change from season to season. An afternoon at the lake fits well with a day of sightseeing in Kitzbühel.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What kind of lake is the Schwarzsee?

    A moor lake with brownish, less clear water that can reach around 27 degrees in summer.

    Is the water of the Schwarzsee clean?

    The bathing water quality is rated excellent, even though the peat makes the water brownish.

    Can you walk around the Schwarzsee?

    Yes, a walking path with a fitness course runs around the lake all year.
