Swimming at Lake Pillersee

Natural mountain lake with a beach in the Pillerseetal valley

Lake Pillersee lies near St. Ulrich am Pillersee in the Kitzbühel Alps at about 835 m. It is a natural bathing lake; in summer the water gets no warmer than about 18 degrees, so it is refreshing rather than warm. The beach bath on the north-east shore is freely accessible and has a sunbathing lawn, a kiosk and a playground.

On and around the lake you can hire SUP boards and boats, and there are a climbing park, mini golf and walking paths. It is a quiet alternative to busier lakes and works well for a day with children.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostBeach bath freely accessible; rentals charged
  • DistanceAbout 55 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationSt. Ulrich am Pillersee

Tips

  • The water is fresh; small children will want short dips.
  • Bring a picnic or visit the kiosk.
  • Hire a boat or SUP board to explore the lake.
  • Check the current parking rules.

Practical

St. Ulrich am Pillersee is about 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel. Check current parking options and costs.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lake Pillersee warm enough for swimming?

The water stays below about 18 degrees in summer, so it is fresh; on warm days it is fine for a refreshing dip.
