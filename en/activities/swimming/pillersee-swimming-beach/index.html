Swimming at Lake Pillersee

Natural mountain lake with a beach in the Pillerseetal valley

Lake Pillersee lies near St. Ulrich am Pillersee in the Kitzbühel Alps. It is a natural bathing lake; on hot days the water reaches no more than about 20 degrees, so it is refreshing rather than warm. On the shore there is a sunbathing lawn, a snack bar and a playground.

Boats and SUP boards can be hired, and there are walking paths around the lake. It is a quiet alternative to busier lakes and works well for a day with children. A small fee may apply for loungers.

Tips

  • The water is fresh; small children will want short dips.
  • Bring a picnic or visit the snack bar.
  • Hire a boat or SUP board to explore the lake.
  • Check current rules for loungers and parking.

Practical

St. Ulrich am Pillersee is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel. There is parking near the lake; check current access and costs.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
