Thiersee Lake Beach

A quiet mountain lake with a bathing beach in the Kufsteinerland

The Thiersee is a mountain lake of roughly 25 hectares and up to about 12 metres deep, close to the Bavarian border, in the quiet Thierseetal valley.

At the lido you find a diving tower, an Aqua Jump, a beach volleyball court and a restaurant nearby. Rowing boats, dolphin boats and pedalos can be hired for a small fee. It suits families who prefer swimming in a manageable lake to a large pool. Bring towels and sun cream; it is easy to spend half a day or a full day here.

Place: Thiersee

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf or full day
  • CostSmall admission fee; boat hire charged; please enquire
  • DistanceAbout 35 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationThiersee

Tips

  • Come on a weekday or later in the afternoon for more peace and quiet.
  • There is a restaurant nearby; you can also bring a picnic.
  • Hire a rowing boat or pedalo for a trip around the lake.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 35 minutes by car via Wörgl and Kufstein, then towards Thiersee. Check current opening times, admission and parking.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim in the Thiersee?

Yes, swimming is possible at the lido; there are a diving tower and an Aqua Jump, among other things.
