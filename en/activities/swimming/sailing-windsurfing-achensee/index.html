Sailing and windsurfing at Lake Achensee

Thermal winds make the lake popular with sailors and surfers

Lake Achensee, at nearly 21 km long, is known for its dependable thermal wind. A breeze usually builds up in the late morning, which makes the lake a favourite with sailors, windsurfers and kitesurfers. Pertisau and Maurach sit right on the water and are natural bases.

Sailing and surf schools at the lake run courses and hire out equipment, for beginners and more experienced people alike. Ask the school about the minimum age for children and about its season; some providers only operate from June to September. The lake is big enough for longer trips, but on the water you always depend on wind and weather.

Tips

  • Book courses or rentals in advance.
  • The wind usually picks up in the late morning; plan your time around it.
  • Wear a life jacket if the school or equipment requires it.
  • Pack dry clothes.

Practical

Lake Achensee is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 to the Jenbach exit. Ask the sailing or surf school about current rates, age limits and booking.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is it windiest on Lake Achensee?

A thermal wind usually builds in the late morning; the exact strength depends on the weather.
