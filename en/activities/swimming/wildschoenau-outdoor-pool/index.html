    Swimming and water fun around Niederau · Summer

    Wildschönau Outdoor Pool

    Outdoor pool between Niederau and Oberau with a big water slide

    The Freischwimmbad Wildschönau lies between Niederau and Oberau and is open daily in summer when the weather allows. It offers over 1,000 m² of water, split between a sports pool, a multi-purpose pool and a baby pool. A large water slide is the eye-catcher for children.

    Beside the water there is a sunbathing lawn, a beach volleyball court, a buffet and a large playground with sandpit and slides. That makes the pool an easy choice for a warm afternoon with children of different ages. It is also close to the end of the Wirbelwindweg and the Bergbauernmuseum, so it combines well with a walk.

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationHalf a day to a full day
    • CostAdmission with child rate; ask for current prices
    • DistanceApprox. 5 min by car
    • SeasonSummer (approx. May to September, weather permitting)
    • LocationBetween Niederau and Oberau

    Tips

    • Check on uncertain days that the pool is open; it opens in good weather.
    • Bring sunscreen and something to sit on for the lawn.
    • Combine with the Wirbelwindweg and the Bergbauernmuseum.
    • The Wildschönau Card may give a discount or free entry; ask about it.

    Practical

    The pool lies between Niederau and Oberau, about 5 minutes by car from Niederau. It can also be reached on foot via the Wirbelwindweg.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is there for children?

    A large water slide, a baby pool and a playground with sandpit and slides.

    When is the pool open?

    In summer, roughly May to September, weather permitting; check current times beforehand.
