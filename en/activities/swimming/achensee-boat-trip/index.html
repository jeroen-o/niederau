Achensee boat trip

By boat across Tyrol's largest lake

The Achensee lies between the Karwendel and Rofan ranges and is the largest lake in Tyrol. Boats have been sailing its emerald-green water since 1887, today with several landing stages along the shore.

A trip on the lake is a relaxed outing for families: you sit outdoors, watch the mountain slopes glide by and can get off along the way, for example at Pertisau for a walk by the water. At Seespitz the historic Achenseebahn rack railway connects. Sailing times vary by day and season, so check them in advance.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1–3 hours, depending on the trip
  • CostPrice depends on the trip; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
  • SeasonMainly summer; check sailing times
  • LocationAchensee (Pertisau)

Tips

  • Check the timetable in advance.
  • Views are best in clear weather.
  • Get off at Pertisau for a walk or lunch by the water.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 50 minutes by car via the A12 (towards Jenbach/Achensee) to Pertisau or Seespitz.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
