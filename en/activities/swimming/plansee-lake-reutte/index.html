Plansee near Reutte

Tyrol's second-largest lake, for swimming and walking

The Plansee lies in the Reutte nature park region and, after the Achensee, is the largest lake in Tyrol. It has two basins about 78 and 72 metres deep and is known for its clear water.

On the shore there is a lawn of roughly 4,000 m² with a stony water's edge. There are no showers or changing cabins, but toilets are available at restaurants and kiosks. This is a natural swimming spot, not a pool complex. You can swim, walk along the water and picnic. It is a long drive from Niederau, so plan it as a day trip, perhaps combined with other sights around Reutte.

Place: Reutte

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationFull day
  • CostFreely accessible bathing spot; costs vary by facility
  • DistanceApprox. 1.5 to 2 hours by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationPlansee, Reutte nature park region

Tips

  • Bring water shoes: the water's edge is stony.
  • There are no showers or changing cabins; bring a towel and dry clothes.
  • The water of a mountain lake is cool even in summer.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 1.5 to 2 hours by car via the A12 and the Fernpass route towards Reutte. It is therefore best as a day trip.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Are there showers or changing cabins at the Plansee?

No, showers and changing cabins are not available at the bathing spot. Toilets are at restaurants and kiosks.
