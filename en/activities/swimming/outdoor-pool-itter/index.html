    Swimming and water fun around Niederau · Summer

    Itter Outdoor Pool

    Quiet village pool with a children's pool and slide

    The Itter outdoor pool is a small, quiet open-air pool in the village of Itter, between Hopfgarten and Söll. Besides the swimming pool there is a separate children's pool with a slide. It is not a big water park, which is exactly why it suits a calm afternoon with young children.

    According to the Hohe Salve tourist board, guests with the guest card can often enter free of charge or at a reduction in summer; ask at your accommodation. The pool is open in summer only, and exact dates depend on the weather.
