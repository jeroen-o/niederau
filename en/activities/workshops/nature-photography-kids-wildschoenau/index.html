Nature photography for children

Learn to look and photograph flowers, insects and mountains

In this photography workshop children and teenagers learn to look more carefully: at light, composition and detail. With tasks such as finding an insect, photographing a flower up close or making a panorama, they discover how much there is to see in nature.

You use your own smartphone or camera, with a guide on hand. At the end the photos can be viewed and discussed together. Ask beforehand about the age range and whether the workshop can be run in English.

  • AgeAbout 7 to 16 years
  • DurationAbout 2 hours
  • CostPrice varies; ask beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 10-15 min
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationWildschönau

Tips

  • Your own smartphone is perfectly fine.
  • Insects and butterflies are hard to photograph; be patient.
  • Bring a fully charged battery.
  • Wear weatherproof clothing.

Practical

The workshop takes place in the Wildschönau, usually within about 10 to 15 minutes of Niederau. Booking and current dates go through the tourist office or your accommodation.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
