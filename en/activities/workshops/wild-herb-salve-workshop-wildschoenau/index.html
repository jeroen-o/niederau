Wild herb salve workshop

Recognise mountain herbs and turn them into your own salve

The mountain pastures hold herbs that have been used in folk medicine for centuries. In this workshop you learn to recognise some of them, such as thyme and mint, and how to turn them into a soothing salve. Part of the time may be spent outdoors on the alp.

Each participant makes their own jar to take home. The workshop is educational for school-age children and adults alike. Ask the Wildschönau tourist office for dates and conditions.

  • AgeFrom about 7 years
  • DurationAbout 1.5 hours
  • CostPrice varies; ask beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 10-15 min
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationWildschönau

Tips

  • Do not take home plants you cannot name.
  • Combine with a short walk on the alp.
  • Wear sturdy shoes.
  • Keep herb allergies in mind.

Practical

The workshop takes place in the Wildschönau; the exact spot depends on the organiser. Ask the tourist office or your accommodation about booking and the distance from Niederau (usually about 10 to 15 minutes).

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
