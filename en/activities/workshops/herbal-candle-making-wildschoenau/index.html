Herbal candle making

Make your own scented candle as a souvenir

In a creative workshop you pour your own candle, possibly with fragrant herbs from the mountains. Children choose colour and scent and pour the candle into a prepared mould. Afterwards you take the result home as a holiday keepsake.

It is a calm activity for a rainy afternoon or between two outings. Workshops like this are offered in the valley at varying times; provider, place and price can differ. Ask the Wildschönau tourist office or your hotel about the current options and book in advance. Dress children in clothes that can get stained.

  • AgeFrom about 5 years
  • DurationAbout 1.5 hours
  • CostPlease enquire in advance
  • DistanceWithin the valley
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationWildschönau

Tips

  • Book in advance.
  • Children like choosing colour and scent themselves.
  • Pack the candle safely for the journey home.
  • Wear clothes that can get stained.

Practical

The workshop location is given when you book; ask the tourist office for place and time. No fixed distance is known; allow a few minutes to roughly a quarter of an hour by car within the valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book?

Usually yes; ask the tourist office for the current provider.
