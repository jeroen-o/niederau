Workshops and crafts in the area

Try glass, wood, cheese or bread yourself: crafts for young and old.

A workshop is a lovely way to take something of the region home. In Tyrol crafts have a long tradition: wood carving, glasswork, making cheese and butter on the alp, baking bread and working with wool or leather. At some places guests can make something under guidance, at others you watch in a workshop or farm.

For children it is often a highlight, because they really get something in their hands and may take the result home. What is available depends on the season and the provider; some workshops run only in summer or holiday periods, others on request. In the Wildschönau and the nearby Alpbachtal you come across them at farms, museums and small businesses.

It is important to ask in advance what is possible and whether booking is needed. Also ask about minimum age, duration and whether there is a choice of language. That way you avoid arriving at a closed door or a workshop that does not suit your children's ages.

Tips

  • Ask beforehand about age, duration and booking.
  • Wear clothes that can get dirty.
  • Pack your creation carefully for transport.
  • Combine a workshop with a museum or farm visit.
  • Ask whether instruction is available in English or German.

What to expect

Workshops usually last from one to a few hours and are meant for beginners. You get instructions, materials and help, and often make something small to take with you. Some providers organise it separately for children, at others parents and children take part together. Wear clothes that may get dirty and expect a contribution for materials; prices vary greatly and are not listed here.

Season and booking

Many small providers work by appointment, especially outside the holidays. Get in touch a few days or weeks beforehand via your accommodation or the provider itself. In peak season places are limited. In bad weather an indoor workshop is a good choice for a day without hiking plans.

Frequently asked questions

From what age can children join?

It differs per workshop. Often from pre-school age, sometimes with a parent's help. Ask beforehand.

Do I need to book?

Usually yes, especially in peak season. Contact the provider, or ask your accommodation for help.

Are workshops also held in winter?

Some are, mostly indoors. The offer is smaller, so ask in good time.
