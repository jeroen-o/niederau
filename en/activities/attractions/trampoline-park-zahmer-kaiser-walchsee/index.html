Zahmer Kaiser trampoline park, Walchsee

Trampolines, airbag jumping and mini-karts in the Kaiserwinkl

The trampoline park is part of the Freizeitpark Zahmer Kaiser in Walchsee, in the Kaiserwinkl. The indoor hall of about 2,000 m² has a free-jump zone, a Bagjump airbag for bold leaps, a freestyle area, wall running and a Ninja Warrior course.

The youngest have their own kids’ playground, and there is a mini-kart track that bills itself as the longest in western Austria. Because everything is indoors, the park also works well on a rainy day. It is mostly fun for energetic children and teens. Opening hours and prices vary, so check the park’s website beforehand.

Place: Walchsee

  • AgeChildren and teens
  • DurationHalf day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 45 min by car
  • SeasonIndoors; also in bad weather
  • LocationWalchsee, Kaiserwinkl

Tips

  • Wear sports clothes and clean socks for the trampolines.
  • Check opening times beforehand; they differ by weekday.
  • A handy rainy-day option, as it is an indoor hall.
  • Bring something to drink; jumping is thirsty work.

Practical

From Niederau, Walchsee is about 45 minutes away, via Wörgl and Kufstein. Check opening times and prices beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do at the Zahmer Kaiser trampoline park?

Among other things free jumping, Bagjump, wall running, a Ninja Warrior course and mini-karting.
