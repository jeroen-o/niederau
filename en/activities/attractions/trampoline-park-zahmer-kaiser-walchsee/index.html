Zahmer Kaiser trampoline park, Walchsee

Trampolines, airbag jumping and mini-karts in the Kaiserwinkl

The trampoline park is part of the Freizeitpark Zahmer Kaiser in Walchsee, in the Kaiserwinkl. The indoor hall of more than 2,000 m² has a free-jump zone, a Bagjump airbag for bold leaps, a freestyle area, wall running and a Ninja Warrior course.

The youngest have their own kids' playground, and there is a mini-kart track that bills itself as the longest in western Austria. That gives different ages something to do, even if not everyone is equally sporty: the little ones play, the teens jump and the rest watch or take a lap in a kart.

Because everything is indoors, the park also works well on a rainy day, when the lake is less appealing. It is mostly fun for energetic children and teens. Opening hours and prices vary, so check the park's website beforehand. A visit often lasts a few hours: start by warming up on the regular trampolines, then try the Bagjump and let the older children test the Ninja Warrior course. If you want a breather, you can watch others race round the kart track or keep an eye on the little ones at the playground.

Place: Walchsee

  • AgeChildren and teens
  • DurationHalf day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 45 min by car
  • SeasonIndoors; also in bad weather
  • LocationWalchsee, Kaiserwinkl

Tips

  • Wear sports clothes and clean socks for the trampolines.
  • Check opening times beforehand; they differ by weekday.
  • A handy rainy-day option, as it is an indoor hall.
  • Bring something to drink; jumping is thirsty work.
  • Combine with a visit to the Walchsee on a day of changeable weather.

Practical

From Niederau, Walchsee is about 45 minutes away, via Wörgl and Kufstein. The hall is in the village, not far from the lake. Check opening times and prices beforehand; sessions are often by time slot, and it can be busier on holidays. The hall is in the village and is also handy as a complement to a swimming day at the Walchsee. Expect small age and height limits for each attraction.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do at the Zahmer Kaiser trampoline park?

Among other things free jumping, Bagjump, wall running, a Ninja Warrior course and mini-karting.

Is the park indoors?

Yes, the hall is more than 2,000 m², so it also suits rainy weather.

Is there something for the youngest?

Yes, there is a dedicated kids' playground.
