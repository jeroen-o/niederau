Thiersee leisure area

Lido, mini golf, tennis and beach volleyball at Lake Thiersee

On the north shore of Lake Thiersee there is a leisure area around the lido, where you can swim in summer and also play mini golf, tennis and beach volleyball. Pedal boats and rowing boats can be hired, and there are a water slide and a diving tower. The lake is green and quiet and warms up well in summer.

The lido is open roughly from May to September, with the start and end depending on the weather. What exactly is open and what it costs changes from year to year, so check beforehand with the Thiersee tourist office or the lido itself. It makes a good half day for families with children of any age.

Place: Thiersee

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationHalf day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 40 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationThiersee, Kufsteinerland

Tips

  • Bring swimwear and a towel.
  • Hire a pedal boat or rowing boat for an hour on the lake.
  • Pick a warm, dry day for the lido.
  • Ask on site what is open on your day.

Practical

From Niederau, Thiersee is about 40 minutes by car, via Kufstein. Check prices and opening times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do there?

Swimming, pedal boating, mini golf, tennis and beach volleyball; the lido is open roughly from May to September.
