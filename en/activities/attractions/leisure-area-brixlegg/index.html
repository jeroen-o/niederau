Brixlegg outdoor pool

Outdoor pool with a playground in the Alpbachtal

Brixlegg's outdoor pool lies below Schloss Lanegg, next to the museum on mining and smelting. It has two pools, including a children's pool, and a large sunbathing lawn with shade trees. A large playground can also be found around Brixlegg, and there are tennis courts in the village.

It is a quiet, local spot without mass tourism. Brixlegg combines well with a visit to the glassblowing town of Rattenberg or the mountain village of Alpbach. Entrance prices differ by age and time of day and are not given here; check the current rates and opening hours with the municipality or the Alpbachtal tourist office.

Tips

  • Bring swimwear and sun cream.
  • Combine with Rattenberg or Alpbach.
  • Check the pool's opening times beforehand.
  • Bring a picnic for the lawn.

Practical

From Niederau, Brixlegg is about 25 minutes by car via the A12. Check rates and opening times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What else is there nearby?

Rattenberg with its glassblowers and the mountain village of Alpbach are close by; the mining museum is next to the pool.
