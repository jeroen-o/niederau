    Brixlegg outdoor pool

    Outdoor pool with a playground in the Alpbachtal

    Brixlegg's outdoor pool lies directly below Burg Lanegg, next to the Tiroler Bergbau- und Hüttenmuseum, which covers mining and smelting and the town's centuries of copper working. It has two pools, including a children's pool, and a large sunbathing lawn with shade trees. A large playground can also be found around Brixlegg, and there are tennis courts in the village.

    It is a quiet, local spot without mass tourism. Brixlegg lies at the foot of the Alpbachtal and combines well with a visit to Rattenberg, the smallest town in Austria and known as Tyrol's glass town, or to the mountain village of Alpbach. That turns a pool afternoon into a varied day.

    Entrance prices differ by age and time of day and are not given here; check the current rates and opening hours with the municipality or the Alpbachtal tourist office. The pool suits a calm summer day without many plans: swimming, playing, an ice cream, and then a short drive to Rattenberg to look in on the glassblowers. A playground nearby keeps even the youngest entertained.

    Tips

    • Bring swimwear and sun cream.
    • Combine with Rattenberg or Alpbach.
    • Check the pool's opening times beforehand.
    • Bring a picnic for the lawn.
    • Visit the mining museum next to the pool if rain sets in after your swim.

    Practical

    From Niederau, Brixlegg is about 25 minutes by car via the A12. The pool lies below the castle and next to the museum, near the centre. That makes it fit well into a day in the Alpbachtal: swimming in Brixlegg, then on to Rattenberg or Alpbach. Check rates and opening times beforehand. There is parking nearby; ask locally. Bring cash for a snack.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What else is there nearby?

    Rattenberg with its glassblowers and the mountain village of Alpbach are close by; the mining museum is next to the pool.

    What kind of museum is next to the pool?

    The Tiroler Bergbau- und Hüttenmuseum, on mining and smelting.

    What is Rattenberg known for?

    It is the smallest town in Austria and is known for its glassblowers.
