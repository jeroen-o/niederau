    Patscherkofel Alpine Garden

    University botanical alpine garden above Innsbruck-Igls

    The Alpengarten near the top station of the Patscherkofelbahn has belonged to the University of Innsbruck since 1930 and is regarded as Austria's highest-altitude botanical garden. It lies between about 1,890 and 1,950 m, just below the Patscherkofelhaus, where forest gives way to open mountain terrain. That makes it a good place to see what plant communities look like at this altitude.

    The garden opened in 1935. It was then closed for more than twenty years; since 1994 a newly built, signposted circular path has made it accessible to the public again. Along the way you see dwarf-shrub heath, mountain forest and high herb meadows. The university also researches here how alpine plants, conifers in particular, cope with cold and drought. Take your time with the signs along the path: they make clear why plants at this altitude are smaller, more compact and hardier than in the valley. The area around the Patscherkofelhaus also invites a break with a view over the Inn valley.

    The garden is freely accessible during the day in the summer months (roughly June to the end of September). You can keep the walk short or combine it with a route on the Patscherkofel, a calm outing for families interested in nature. Check the operating times of the gondola beforehand.

    Tips

    • Let children look for plants and make it a game.
    • Take a jacket; it is cooler up top than in the valley.
    • Check the gondola's operating times beforehand.
    • Wear sturdy shoes; at almost 2,000 m the weather can change quickly compared with the valley.
    • Read the signs along the circular path; they explain the plant communities.

    Practical

    Igls is near Innsbruck, about 1 hour by car from Niederau via the A12. The Patscherkofelbahn runs up from Igls; the garden is at the top station at over 1,900 m. The garden itself is freely accessible in summer, the gondola costs extra and only runs in certain months. Check the current times of the cable car beforehand. Parking is possible at the valley station in Igls; ask on site about the options.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the Patscherkofel Alpine Garden?

    By the top station of the Patscherkofelbahn above Igls, at over 1,900 metres.

    Do you have to pay to enter the garden?

    No, the garden is freely accessible during the day in summer; only the gondola costs money.

    How long has the garden existed?

    The university received the site in 1930, the garden opened in 1935 and, after a long closure, has been accessible again since 1994.
