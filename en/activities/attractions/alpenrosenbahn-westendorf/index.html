Alpenrosenbahn, Westendorf

Gondola, Alpinolino adventure world and ball run above Westendorf

Westendorf in the Brixental valley is a good destination for a family outing with the Alpenrosenbahn I and II. These gondolas take you up to the Talkaser at around 1,770 m, from where the alpine pastures are right on your doorstep.

Up top you will find the ErlebnisWelt Alpinolino, a trail with small adventures and nature stations for children. There is also the Spoggolino, the longest ball run in Tyrol, which leads from the Choralpe back down to the Alpenrosenbahn station. From the Talkaser you can also hike across alpine meadows. The whole outing is calm and easy to follow, and suits children from nursery age. In summer the lifts run from late spring into autumn.

Place: Westendorf

  • AgeSuitable for children from pre-school age
  • DurationAbout 2 to 4 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 35 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (late spring to autumn)
  • LocationWestendorf, Brixental

Tips

  • Check the lift's current timetable beforehand.
  • Bring a jacket; at about 1,770 m it is cooler than in the valley.
  • Allow some extra time for the Spoggolino ball run.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 35 minutes by car to Westendorf, via Wörgl and the Brixental. The valley station of the Alpenrosenbahn is in the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
