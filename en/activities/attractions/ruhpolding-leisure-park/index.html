Freizeitpark Ruhpolding

Family theme park with many attractions in the Chiemgau

The Freizeitpark Ruhpolding lies in Bavaria’s Chiemgau, between Munich and Salzburg. It is a fairy-tale and adventure park with more than sixty attractions. The eye-catcher is the Gipfelstürmer mountain coaster from 2011, a shuttle coaster developed specially for this park. The Dragon Ride and the cosy Bockerlbahn train are part of the offer too.

So both younger and older children have plenty to do. The park is open daily roughly from late March or April until early November. Given the travel time it makes a full day out; check current opening hours and prices beforehand.

  • AgeFamilies with children
  • DurationFull day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 1 hr 20 min by car
  • SeasonRoughly late March/April to early November
  • LocationRuhpolding, Bavaria (Germany)

Tips

  • Arrive early and start the day at a relaxed pace.
  • Bring a rain jacket, as much of the park is outdoors.
  • Check the height requirements per attraction beforehand.
  • Ask about family and child rates.

Practical

From Niederau, Ruhpolding is about 1 hour 20 minutes by car, via Kufstein and the Bavarian border. It is in Germany; bring ID. Check prices beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Which attractions does Freizeitpark Ruhpolding have?

Among others the Gipfelstürmer mountain coaster, the Dragon Ride and the Bockerlbahn.
