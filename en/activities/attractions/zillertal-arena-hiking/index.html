Hiking in the Zillertal Arena

Cable cars and family routes around Zell am Ziller and Gerlos

The Zillertal Arena is the area around Zell am Ziller, Gerlos, Königsleiten and Krimml. In summer its cable cars are the start of a large network of marked trails, with over 400 km of routes, themed walks and dozens of huts and mountain restaurants.

For families the Rosenalmbahn at Zell am Ziller is especially handy, taking you into the Kreuzjoch hiking area. There is a summer toboggan run at the valley station, and up top there are play areas and themed paths for children. You can walk a short loop and ride back down, or plan a longer trip across alpine meadows and past huts. Let the children's ages and the weather decide.

Place: Zell am Ziller

Tips

  • Pack walking shoes and rain gear; mountain weather can change quickly.
  • Start with a short loop near the top station and extend if the children are keen.
  • Plan a stop at a hut; many serve simple local dishes.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 50 minutes by car to Zell am Ziller, via the A12 and then into the Zillertal. The valley stations are in and near the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
