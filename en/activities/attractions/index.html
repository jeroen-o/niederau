Attractions and family outings in and around the Wildschönau

Fun parks, experience venues and outings that fill a day for the whole family.

Besides skiing, hiking and swimming, families often want an outing that is simply fun. In and around the Wildschönau there is plenty: from small experience venues and mountain playgrounds to larger destinations in the Inn valley and the Kitzbühel Alps. The offer is mixed, from natural and simple to more commercial and extensive.

When choosing, it helps to start from the children's ages. Toddlers and pre-schoolers are often happiest somewhere with space, animals and play areas. Older children and teenagers tend to look for something active or spectacular, such as an adventure park, a gondola ride or a viewpoint with something to do.

Whatever you pick, be realistic about time. A day out starts with the drive, and the town or valley next door is often worth a look too. Check opening times, prices and weather dependence on the provider's website beforehand, as these change regularly.

Tips

  • Always keep a rainy-day plan B.
  • Read height and age limits before you leave.
  • Set off early in the holidays to avoid the crowds.
  • Ask your accommodation about a guest card or discounts.
  • Bring lunch or a picnic for more freedom.

Choose by age and weather

Many attractions work best in dry weather, others in rain. Always keep a plan B, such as an indoor venue or a museum. Some places have height or age limits, for example on slides, climbing elements or rides. Read these in advance to spare the children a disappointment. A picnic or lunch on the way often makes the day easier.

Practical planning

In holiday periods it can be busy, especially in fine weather. Setting off early helps against traffic and queues. Many providers offer guests of the region a discount or benefit via a guest card; ask at your accommodation whether this is part of your stay. Prices and opening times vary from year to year, so do not fix your budget too early.

Frequently asked questions

What is fun for small children?

Places with plenty of space, animals, a playground or a short walking path. Large, busy destinations are often too much for toddlers.

Are there attractions that work in the rain?

Yes, think of museums, indoor pools, experience venues with a roof or a visit to an old town. Check beforehand whether the venue is open in poor weather.

How far ahead should I plan?

For most attractions this is not necessary. For special programmes or in busy weeks a reservation may be needed.
