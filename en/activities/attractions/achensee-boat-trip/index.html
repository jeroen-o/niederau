Boat trip on Lake Achensee

A cruise between the Karwendel and Rofan mountains

Boats have sailed on Lake Achensee, Tyrol’s largest lake, since 1887. The first vessel was the steamship St. Josef, built on the initiative of the abbot of Fiecht Abbey. Today modern ships run the service, such as the motor ship Tirol from 1995, which carries up to 600 passengers and is accessible for wheelchair users.

Out on the water you see the emerald-green lake framed by the steep walls of the Karwendel and Rofan ranges. You can take a round trip or hop on and off at one of the landing stages along the shore, for example to go for a walk. For children the boat ride is an outing in itself, and in good weather the views are superb.

Place: Jenbach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 2 hours, depending on the trip
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 55 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (also outside peak season)
  • LocationLake Achensee, Tyrol

Tips

  • Combine the boat with the Achenseebahn or the Rofanbahn for a full day.
  • Bring a windproof jacket; it is often breezy on the water.
  • Check the timetable beforehand; sailing times vary by season.
  • Hop off for a walk along the shore.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 55 minutes by car to Lake Achensee, via the A12 and Jenbach. At Seespitz (Maurach) the Achenseebahn connects with the landing stage. Check timetable and fares beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Since when has there been a boat service on Lake Achensee?

Since 1887, when the steamship St. Josef entered service.
