Familienland Pillersee (St. Jakob in Haus)

Children's leisure park with slides, trampolines and water fun in the Pillerseetal

There is no park called Familypark in St. Johann in Tirol. However, nearby in St. Jakob in Haus in the Pillerseetal you will find Familienland Pillersee, a leisure park aimed at young children.

The park is all about playing and climbing: lots of slides, playgrounds and trampolines, plus a white-water ride in which a small group is lifted up and sent back down. It suits toddlers, pre-schoolers and primary-school children best, and works well for a day when the weather is uncertain or the children want to burn off energy. Attractions, prices and opening times change from year to year, so check with the park beforehand.

  • AgeMainly toddlers to primary-school children
  • DurationHalf a day to a full day
  • CostAdmission varies; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 1 hour by car
  • SeasonMainly summer; check opening times
  • LocationSt. Jakob in Haus, Pillerseetal

Tips

  • Check opening times and prices beforehand; they change by season.
  • Pack spare clothes for the children if you want to try the water ride.
  • The park is mainly fun for children up to around primary-school age.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 1 hour by car, usually via Wörgl and Kitzbühel towards the Pillerseetal. The park is in St. Jakob in Haus; parking is normally at the park.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Familypark in St. Johann in Tirol?

No, there is no park under that name. Nearby there is Familienland Pillersee in St. Jakob in Haus.
