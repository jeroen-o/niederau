Hexenwasser Söll

A witch-themed water adventure world on Hochsöll

Hexenwasser is a water-themed adventure world on Hochsöll, above Söll in the Wilder Kaiser area, at roughly 1,150 to 1,350 metres. You reach it by the gondola from Söll. Along the walking route are water play areas and small surprises for children.

Highlights include the House of the Bee Queen, humming stones, the Hexenbach with a witches’ washing place and stone mill, and the Simonalm, a farmhouse some four hundred years old where everything revolves around eggs. The route also includes Austria’s longest barefoot trail with more than sixty stations. At the Stöcklalm you can stop for a bite or a drink.

Place: Söll

  • AgeFamilies with children
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostGondola fare; prices vary, check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 35 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationHochsöll, Söll (Wilder Kaiser)

Tips

  • Pack spare clothes; children will certainly get wet here.
  • Wear shoes that come off and on easily for the barefoot trail.
  • Look in at the Simonalm for its egg theme.
  • Plan a break at the Stöcklalm.

Practical

From Niederau, Söll is about 35 minutes by car. Park at the valley station of the gondola and ride up to the Hexenwasser. Check opening times and prices beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to the Hexenwasser?

By the gondola from Söll; up top you follow the route past the water play areas.
