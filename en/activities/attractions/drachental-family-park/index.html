Drachental Family Park

Family park in Oberau with the Drachenflitzer alpine coaster

The Familienpark Drachental is in the centre of Oberau and open all year. Entry to the park itself is free, with among other things a motor-skills course with a jumping tower, a dragon tower with ball track and slide, and a play lake. Individual attractions are paid separately.

The highlight is the Drachenflitzer, an alpine coaster over 2 km long that runs in summer and winter, also after dark. Children aged 3 to 7 ride with an adult; from 8 years you may ride alone. There are also a 5D cinema, a tubing track, a cart track and an 18-hole adventure golf course.

Place: Oberau

  • AgeCoaster from approx. 3 years; park for all ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 4 hours
  • CostPark entry free; attractions paid separately, ask for rates
  • DistanceApprox. 5 to 10 min by car
  • SeasonAll year (main season summer)
  • LocationOberau

Tips

  • Plan half a day; the free part of the park is already fun for small children.
  • Children aged 3 to 7 must ride the Drachenflitzer with an adult.
  • An evening ride on the coaster is a special experience.
  • Check opening times beforehand; they vary by season.

Practical

The park is in Oberau, roughly 5 to 10 minutes by car from Niederau; there is also public transport in the valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is entry to the park free?

Yes, the park itself is free; the Drachenflitzer and other attractions are paid separately.

From what age can children ride the Drachenflitzer?

From 3 to 7 years with an adult, from 8 years alone.
