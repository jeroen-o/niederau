Zahmer Kaiser leisure park, Kössen

Trampolines and airbag jumping in the Kaiserwinkl

The Freizeitpark Zahmer Kaiser is in Kössen, in the Kaiserwinkl region. The eye-catcher is a large trampoline facility with six jumping surfaces, where you can practise somersaults and other tricks. There are also Bagjump airbags that give a soft landing after a jump.

The region is very family-oriented, and the nearby Zahmer Kaiser cable car also offers activities for children. The park is mostly fun for energetic children and teens. Which attractions are open and what they cost can change, so check the park’s website or the Kössen tourist office beforehand.

Place: Kössen

  • AgeChildren and teens
  • DurationHalf day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 55 min by car
  • SeasonSummer; check opening times
  • LocationKössen, Kaiserwinkl

Tips

  • Wear sports clothes and clean socks for the trampolines.
  • Check opening times and minimum age per attraction beforehand.
  • Combine with the Zahmer Kaiser cable car for a full day out.
  • Bring something to drink; jumping is thirsty work.

Practical

From Niederau, Kössen is about 55 minutes away, via Wörgl and Kufstein or Walchsee. Kössen is close to the German border. Check opening times and prices beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do at the Zahmer Kaiser leisure park?

Among other things, jump on a trampoline facility with six surfaces and on Bagjump airbags.
