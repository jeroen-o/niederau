Penkenbahn, Mayrhofen

By cable car up the Penken, with play and sports facilities

The Penkenbahn is a modern three-rope cable car in Mayrhofen in the Zillertal. The top station is at about 1,790 m. Up top you find the FunSportStation Penken with a playground, a motor-skills course and activities such as E-Trial and the Bergroller.

Right next to the top station is the LärchwaldBachl water playground, with a water wheel, a spiral bucket and wooden barrels and gutters, among other things. On warm days it is a firm favourite with children. The FunSportStation is open in summer, roughly from late June to September. Allow plenty of time and check the weather and current information beforehand so the visit goes smoothly.

Place: Mayrhofen

  • AgeFamilies with children
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 55 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (FunSportStation approx. late June to September)
  • LocationMayrhofen, Zillertal

Tips

  • Bring spare clothes for the water playground.
  • Check age and height requirements for the Bergroller.
  • Check the season dates and operating times of the lift and the FunSportStation beforehand.
  • Allow plenty of time; children often stay at the water for a long while.

Practical

From Niederau, Mayrhofen is about 55 minutes by car, via Wörgl and the Zillertal road. You can also take the train from Jenbach. Check current fares and times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is there for children on the Penken?

A playground, the LärchwaldBachl water playground and a motor-skills course at the FunSportStation Penken.
