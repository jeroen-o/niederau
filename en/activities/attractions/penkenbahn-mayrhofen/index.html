Penkenbahn, Mayrhofen

By cable car up the Penken, with play and sports facilities

The Penkenbahn is a modern cable car in Mayrhofen in the Zillertal. In summer it runs daily from late May to early October. The top station is at about 1,800 metres.

Up top you find the FunSportStation Penken with a playground with swings, a climbing tower, a slide and a water play area, plus a motor-skills course and activities such as E-Trial and the Bergroller. Children can play for hours at the LärchwaldBachl water playground. There is also a pushchair-friendly circular walk, so families with young children are well catered for.

Place: Mayrhofen

  • AgeFamilies with children
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 1 hour by car
  • SeasonSummer (late May to early October)
  • LocationMayrhofen, Zillertal

Tips

  • Bring spare clothes for the water playground.
  • Pick the pushchair-friendly circular walk with young children.
  • Check age and height requirements for the Bergroller.
  • Combine with the Zillertalbahn for a day trip.

Practical

From Niederau, Mayrhofen is about 1 hour by car, via Wörgl and the Zillertal road. You can also take the train from Jenbach. Check current fares beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is there for children on the Penken?

A playground with the LärchwaldBachl water playground, a motor-skills course and a pushchair-friendly circular walk.
