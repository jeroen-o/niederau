Salvenaland Hopfgarten

Leisure park with a bathing lake in the Brixental

Salvenaland is a leisure park with a bathing lake in Hopfgarten im Brixental, popular with families and teenagers. The lake has an inflatable iceberg for older children, and the youngest have a paddling pool with a water play area. Anyone who finds the lake water too chilly can use a heated pool.

The grounds hold an 18-hole mini-golf course, mini-karts, a summer toboggan run, a bouncy cushion and courts for volleyball, basketball and football. A restaurant with a terrace stands by the lake. The park is open in summer, in 2026 generally daily during the day from 1 May; check current times beforehand.

Place: Hopfgarten im Brixental

  • AgeFamilies with children and teens
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 25 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (roughly May to August)
  • LocationHopfgarten im Brixental

Tips

  • Bring swimwear and a towel for the lake.
  • Pick a warm day; the lake is most fun then.
  • Check age limits for the kart and toboggan tracks.
  • Plan on half a day to a full day.

Practical

From Niederau, Hopfgarten is about 25 minutes by car. You can also cycle or walk along the panorama route. Check prices and opening times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim at Salvenaland?

Yes, there is a bathing lake with an inflatable iceberg and a paddling pool, plus a heated pool.
