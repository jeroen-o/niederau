Achensee boat trip

By boat across Tyrol's largest lake

The Achensee lies between the Karwendel and Rofan ranges and is the largest lake in Tyrol. Boats have been sailing its emerald-green water since 1887; the company was founded by Abbot Albert Wildauer of Fiecht Abbey, with the steamship St. Josef as its first vessel.

A trip on the lake is a relaxed outing for families: you sit outdoors, watch the mountain slopes glide by and can get off along the way, for example at Pertisau for a walk by the water. At Seespitz the historic Achenseebahn steam rack railway connects. Boats run from early May to late October; sailing times vary by day, so check them in advance.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1–3 hours, depending on the trip
  • CostPrice depends on the trip; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
  • SeasonEarly May to late October; check sailing times
  • LocationAchensee (Pertisau)

Tips

  • Check the timetable in advance.
  • Views are best in clear weather.
  • Get off at Pertisau for a walk or lunch by the water.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 50 minutes by car via the A12 (towards Jenbach/Achensee) to Pertisau or Seespitz. Check the current timetable and prices beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Since when have boats sailed on the Achensee?

Boat services on the Achensee have existed since 1887.

When do the boats run?

The regular service runs roughly from early May to late October; check current times.
