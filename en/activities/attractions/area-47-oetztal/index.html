Area 47 in the Ötztal

Large outdoor adventure park with water park, climbing and adventure activities

Area 47 is a large outdoor and adventure park at Ötztal-Bahnhof station, in the municipality of Haiming in the Ötztal valley. The grounds are divided into zones for water, climbing, mountain biking and wakeboarding, among others, so there is enough to fill a whole day.

Attractions include a water park with slides, a high rope course, one of Austria's highest artificial climbing walls and a zip line. There are also guided activities such as rafting and canyoning, mostly for older children and teenagers. Age and height limits apply to each activity, so decide with the children beforehand what suits them.

Official website

  • AgeVaries by activity; many parts suit primary-school children and up
  • DurationHalf a day to a full day
  • CostPrice varies by activity; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 1 h 15 min by car
  • SeasonMainly summer
  • LocationHaiming, Ötztal

Tips

  • Bring swimwear, towels and spare clothes.
  • Check age and height limits for each activity beforehand.
  • Book guided activities such as rafting or canyoning in advance.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car via the A12 towards Innsbruck and on to the Ötztal. The park is right by Ötztal-Bahnhof station.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
