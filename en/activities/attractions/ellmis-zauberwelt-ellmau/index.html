    Ellmi’s Zauberwelt, Ellmau

    A fairy-tale adventure world on the Hartkaiser

    Ellmi’s Zauberwelt sits by the top station of the Hartkaiserbahn above Ellmau and has existed since 2006. It is an adventure park for families, with puzzles and game stations in an enchanted forest, several play areas, a topsy-turvy alp and water play areas with a raft, a ship and fountains.

    Along the way you also find the Kaiserloge, a viewpoint towards the Wilder Kaiser, Wally the talking owl, an eagle’s nest and a botanical garden with alpine plants. You reach the park by the Hartkaiserbahn from Ellmau. Plan on half a day or longer, and bring spare clothes for children who like to splash.

    Tips

    • Take a jacket; it is often cooler up top than in the valley.
    • Pack spare clothes for the water play areas.
    • Visit the Kaiserloge for the view of the Wilder Kaiser.
    • Check current opening times and the cable car ticket beforehand.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about 40 minutes by car to Ellmau, via Wörgl and Söll. Take the Hartkaiserbahn up; the park is by the top station. In summer it is generally open daily during the day; check current hours and prices beforehand.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you get to Ellmi’s Zauberwelt?

    By the Hartkaiserbahn from Ellmau; the park is by the top station.
