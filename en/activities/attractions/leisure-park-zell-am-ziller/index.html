Zell am Ziller leisure park

Mini golf, outdoor pool and playground for the whole family

The leisure park in Zell am Ziller covers more than 45,000 m² and brings many facilities together. There is an adventure mini golf with 18 holes themed around the treasures of the Zillertal, an outdoor pool with a sports pool, a white-water channel and two large slides, a gold-digger-themed playground, a climbing tower and a Fun Court for volleyball and basketball. There are also tennis courts, beach volleyball courts and a large jump bubble.

In winter there is ice skating. The outdoor pool is open in the summer season, roughly May to September. Prices and opening hours differ per facility and per year, so check beforehand with the Zell am Ziller tourist office. The park suits families with children of different ages and is good for half a day.

Tips

  • Combine mini golf and swimming for a full afternoon.
  • Bring swimwear and a towel.
  • Check beforehand whether the outdoor pool is open.
  • Also visit the village centre.

Practical

From Niederau, Zell am Ziller is about 50 minutes by car, via Wörgl and the Zillertal. Check prices and opening times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is there for children?

A gold-digger-themed playground, mini golf, a climbing tower, a jump bubble and the outdoor pool with slides.
