Rofanseilbahn, Achensee

By cable car into the Rofan mountains above Lake Achensee

The Rofanseilbahn starts in Maurach on Lake Achensee and takes you up into the Rofan mountains, near the Erfurter Hütte at over 1,800 metres. Many walking routes start from the top station, from gentle panoramic walks to demanding mountain tours to the surrounding peaks.

For children there is the AIRROFAN, an eagle-shaped flying device that reaches around 80 km/h and glides more than 200 metres below the Gschöllkopf (2,040 m). In summer the cable car runs continuously, with adjusted hours in spring and autumn. The views stretch over the lake and the mountains around it.

Place: Jenbach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 4 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceApprox. 55 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (and winter)
  • LocationMaurach, Lake Achensee, Rofan mountains

Tips

  • Bring hiking boots and a warm sweater; it is cooler up top.
  • Check the weather; views are best in clear conditions.
  • Check age and height requirements for the AIRROFAN.
  • Combine with the boat or the Achenseebahn.

Practical

From Niederau, Maurach is about 55 minutes by car via the A12, exit Jenbach/Achensee. You can also take the Achenseebahn to Seespitz and walk or take a bus. Check fares beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
