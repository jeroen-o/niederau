Innsbruck Festival of Early Music

Baroque music on period instruments in historic venues

The Innsbruck Festival of Early Music has run since 1976 and is among the best-known festivals for early music. It takes place every summer from late July to late August. In 2026 it was the anniversary edition, with 62 events at 20 venues.

Concerts are held in historic settings such as the Spanish Hall at Ambras Castle, churches and other venues in and around Innsbruck. Renaissance and Baroque music is played on instruments of the period. The programme suits music lovers and older children best; some youth-oriented events also exist, which you can find in the programme book.

Place: Innsbruck

  • AgeMainly teenagers and adults
  • DurationApprox. 2 hours per concert (varies)
  • CostVaries by concert; book via the official website
  • DistanceApprox. 1 hour by car
  • SeasonSummer (late July to late August)
  • LocationInnsbruck

Tips

  • Book popular concerts well in advance via the official website.
  • Look in the programme book for child-friendly or shorter concerts.
  • Combine a concert at Ambras Castle with a visit to the castle itself.
  • Plan for the drive back; evening concerts end late.

Practical

Concerts take place at various venues in Innsbruck, about 1 hour by car from Niederau. Check the venue for each concert; the old town is easy to reach by train from Wörgl.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Festival of Early Music held?

Every summer from late July to late August; the 2026 anniversary edition ran from 24 July to 30 August.
