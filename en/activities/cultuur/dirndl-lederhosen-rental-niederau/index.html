Dirndl and Lederhosen hire

A day in Tyrolean costume, also for the family photo

Dirndl and Lederhosen belong to the Alpine landscape, and in the Wildschönau many people wear them at festivals and processions. Sometimes it is possible to hire a set for a day, for example for a folklore evening or a photo moment in the snow.

Whether and where that is possible in Niederau varies; ask your accommodation or the tourist office about nearby providers and the cost. Give sizes beforehand and allow for a fitting. For children dressing up is often a fun game, and it makes lovely family photos, especially by a traditional timber farmhouse.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 hour for the fitting
  • CostPrice varies; ask in advance
  • DistanceIn or near Niederau
  • SeasonAll year (offer varies)
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Ask beforehand whether hire is available and what it costs.
  • Give sizes for the whole family in advance.
  • Plan a fitting before you need the costume.
  • Take the photos by a traditional timber farmhouse.

Practical

The offer and location vary; ask your accommodation or the tourist office where in or near Niederau you can go.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
