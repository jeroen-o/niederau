St. Johann in Tirol town centre

A market town with painted facades, a church and a Friday market

St. Johann in Tirol is a market town at the foot of the Kitzbüheler Horn and the Wilder Kaiser. The centre around the main square has baroque houses with Lüftlmalerei, painted facades, such as the 18th-century Schellhornhaus. You will also find a parish church, shopping streets and cafés.

According to the tourist events calendar there is a weekly market on Fridays from 10 am to 5 pm. A visit takes about an hour and a half; it combines well with another outing in the Kitzbüheler Alpen region, such as Kitzbühel or the Kitzbüheler Horn. The town has mountains on both sides.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1.5 hours
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 40 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationSt. Johann in Tirol

Tips

  • Plan your visit for a Friday if you want to see the weekly market.
  • Look closely at the painted facades (Lüftlmalerei).
  • Park in one of the car parks near the centre.
  • Combine with Kitzbühel or the Kitzbüheler Horn.

Practical

St. Johann in Tirol is roughly 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the Brixental road (B170) or the A12/B178.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is the weekly market in St. Johann?

According to the tourist events calendar on Fridays from 10 am to 5 pm; check current times beforehand.
