Village stroll in Scheffau

A quiet village at the Wilder Kaiser, among meadows and alpine pastures

Scheffau am Wilden Kaiser is a small village in the SkiWelt area, smaller and quieter than its neighbours Söll and Ellmau. It has few shops, quiet streets and farmhouses with well-kept flower gardens. Its position at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser still makes it an attractive spot.

You can walk a loop through the village and then follow one of the lanes or paths up to the meadows above it. There you pass farms and pastures with mountain views. Cows on or beside the path are part of the scene; keep your distance and close gates behind you.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 1.5 hours
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 40 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationScheffau am Wilden Kaiser

Tips

  • Keep your distance from cows and grazing animals.
  • Bring water and a snack; there are few facilities.
  • Wear sturdy shoes if you head up to the meadows.
  • Combine with a visit to Ellmau or Söll.

Practical

Scheffau is roughly 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl towards the Wilder Kaiser.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scheffau busy?

No, it is a small, quiet village with few shops; it suits a short, relaxed walk.
