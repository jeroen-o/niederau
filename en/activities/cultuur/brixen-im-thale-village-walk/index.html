Village stroll in Brixen im Thale

A quiet Brixental village with a church and flowery houses

Brixen im Thale lies in the Brixental and has a well-kept village centre with a church as its eye-catcher. Around it stand houses and farms with flowers at the windows and on balconies. It is a compact village where you can see everything within an hour.

The walk combines easily with other outings nearby, such as the village's cable car or the summer toboggan run in the Brixental. It is mostly a pleasant stop when you are passing through the Brixental and want to get out for a coffee or a roll from a bakery.

Place: Brixen im Thale

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 hour
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 30 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationBrixen im Thale, Brixental

Tips

  • Take a look inside the church, but keep quiet.
  • Combine with a cable car or toboggan run if you have children.
  • Park in the centre and take the loop at an easy pace.
  • Check opening times of local shops.

Practical

Brixen im Thale is roughly 30 minutes by car from Niederau, via the B170 through the Brixental.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a visit to Brixen im Thale take?

A walk through the centre takes about an hour; combine with a cable car or another outing for half a day.
