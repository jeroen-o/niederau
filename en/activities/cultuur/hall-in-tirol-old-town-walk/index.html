Old town walk in Hall in Tirol

A well-preserved medieval old town on the Inn

For centuries Hall in Tirol was a wealthy town thanks to salt and later the mint. Much of that survives in the old centre: narrow streets, historic houses, churches and the Oberer Stadtplatz. It is quieter than nearby Innsbruck but well worth a visit.

A walk through the old town takes about an hour and a half, longer if you go inside anywhere. You can combine it with Hasegg Castle and the mint museum on the edge of the centre. There are plenty of terraces and cafés on the way, so the round easily stretches into half a day in a relaxed town.

Place: Hall in Tirol

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1.5 hours
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 50 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationHall in Tirol

Tips

  • Combine the walk with Hasegg Castle.
  • Plan a coffee stop on one of the terraces.
  • Wear comfortable shoes; some lanes are cobbled.
  • Park on the edge of the centre.

Practical

Hall in Tirol is roughly 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 towards Innsbruck. The old town is largely car-free or restricted; park on the edge.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hall in Tirol a good alternative to Innsbruck?

Yes, it is quieter and more compact, with a well-preserved old centre.
