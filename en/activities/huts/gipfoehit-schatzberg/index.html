    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Gipföhit (Schatzberg)

    A hut just below the Schatzberg summit

    The Gipföhit stands high on the Schatzberg above Auffach. The spelling varies between sources (Gipfö Hit, Giphöhit), but they all refer to the same hut. According to the route descriptions it is about half an hour on foot from the top station of the Schatzbergbahn.

    The hut features in several hiking and ski-touring routes, including a circular walk of around four kilometres past the Schatzbergalm and the summit area (highest point 1,882 m). The kitchen serves regional dishes. It suits people who want a break after a stretch on foot, rather than a quick coffee right beside the piste.

    Place: Auffach

    • DistanceAbout 20 minutes by car from Niederau to the valley station in Auffach, then the gondola and about 30 minutes on foot
    • LocationSchatzberg, Auffach (Wildschönau)

    Tips

    • Plan a circular walk from the top station and use the hut as a rest stop.
    • Wear sturdy shoes: the way there climbs and lies at altitude.
    • Ask the hut or check the official site for current information beforehand.

    Practical

    Take the Schatzbergbahn to the top station, then walk about half an hour according to route descriptions.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the hut's exact name?

    Sources write Gipföhit, Gipfö Hit or Giphöhit; it is the same hut on the Schatzberg.

    How long is the walk from the lift?

    Route descriptions give roughly half an hour from the top station.
