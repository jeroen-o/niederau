    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Kropfraderjochalm

    A quiet alm at around 1,500 m

    According to Tirol.at the Kropfraderjochalm lies at about 1,500 m, in a quiet spot with mountain views. Ski Juwel lists it among the huts and restaurants of the ski area. The Wildschönau tourist board describes a winter walk from the Markbachjoch to the Kropfraderalm: a little over 1 km, roughly an hour on foot.

    The menu features Tyrolean dishes. For families it is a calmer alternative to the busier huts right beside a top station. The sources differ on details such as the operator's address, so check those with the hut or on the official site.

    Official website

    • DistanceNear the Markbachjoch, about an hour's walk from the top station
    • LocationMarkbachjoch, Wildschönau

    Tips

    • Plan the winter walk from the Markbachjoch as a calm outing of about an hour.
    • Bring warm clothing; it cools quickly at this altitude.
    • Ask the hut or check the official site beforehand.

    Practical

    From Niederau take the Markbachjochbahn, then walk or ski towards the alm.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long is the walk from the Markbachjoch?

    According to the Wildschönau tourist board about 1.2 km and an hour on foot in winter.

    Is the alm suitable for families?

    It is a quiet spot; the walk may be long for young children. Ask locally.
