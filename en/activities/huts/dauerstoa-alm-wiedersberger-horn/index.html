    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Dauerstoa Alm

    Terrace and chill zone at 1,840 m

    The Dauerstoa Alm sits at about 1,840 m by the top station of the Wiedersbergerhornbahn. On the official Ski Juwel page it is placed between the top stations of the Pöglbahn and the Wiedersbergerhornbahn, so skiers pass it on the connecting runs.

    The hut has a large terrace, a chill zone and an outdoor bar alongside the indoor restaurant. In summer it is a rest stop for walkers around the Wiedersberger Horn, and the Lauserland adventure area in the woods is close by, a natural outing for families. It is run by a family from Alpbach.

    Place: Alpbach

    Official website

    • DistanceOn the Alpbachtal side of Ski Juwel; from Niederau via the Ski Juwel link or by car to Alpbach (approximate)
    • LocationWiedersberger Horn, Alpbach

    Tips

    • Pair a break on the terrace with a visit to Lauserland.
    • On sunny days the chill zone is a pleasant place to recover.
    • When on the Wiedersberger Horn, check which hut is closest to your run first.

    Practical

    By the top station of the Wiedersbergerhornbahn (Alpbach) or via the runs and paths around it. For current information ask the hut or check the official site.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does the Dauerstoa Alm offer?

    A large terrace, a chill zone, an outdoor bar and an indoor restaurant.

    Is there anything for children nearby?

    Yes, the Lauserland adventure area is close by in the woods.
