    Joe's Salett'l

    Restaurant, café and bar at the Pöglbahn valley station

    Joe's Salett'l stands right by the Pöglbahn valley station in Inneralpbach. It combines restaurant, café and bar, which makes it a spot to start or end a ski day at the point where the gondola to the Wiedersberger Horn departs.

    According to the Tirol tourism page, the easiest way in is down piste 47, and the ski bus stop is next to the Pöglbahn car park. That also suits anyone arriving without skis, for instance to meet family members who ski. The venue is also mentioned for parties and gatherings.

    Place: Alpbach

    Official website

    • DistanceOn the Alpbachtal side of Ski Juwel; from Niederau a drive or long ski day via the Ski Juwel link (approximate)
    • LocationInneralpbach, Alpbachtal

    Tips

    • Use it as a meeting point if the family splits across different runs: the valley station is easy to find.
    • Arriving by ski bus, get off at the Pöglbahn car park.
    • If you plan a party, ask the venue directly.

    Practical

    On skis via piste 47 or by ski bus to the Pöglbahn car park. For current information, ask the venue or check the official site.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is Joe's Salett'l?

    At the Pöglbahn valley station in Inneralpbach.

    How do I get there without a car?

    There is a ski bus stop next to the Pöglbahn car park.
