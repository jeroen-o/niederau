    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Liftcafé Heisn

    Restaurant with a play terrace at the Reitherkogelbahn

    The Liftcafé Heisn is in Reith im Alpbachtal, right beside the valley station of the Reitherkogelbahn. That makes it a handy stop before you head up or once you are back down. Inside there is room for about 130 guests, with around 60 more places for larger groups.

    The cooking is traditional Tyrolean. Outside is a sunny terrace with views of the mountains and the piste, a play area for children and car parking. The official Ski Juwel site also lists it among the summer mountain huts, so it is not only a winter address. A nearby holiday-let host mentions that guests can book half board here. From Niederau it is a drive along the valley, not a hut you ski to.

    Place: Reith im Alpbachtal

    Official website

    • DistanceAbout 30 minutes by car from Niederau (approximate)
    • LocationReith im Alpbachtal, Alpbachtal

    Tips

    • Let the children use the play area by the terrace while you catch your breath.
    • It also works as a stop by car, as there is parking.
    • For a larger group, book ahead; there are extra places for parties.

    Practical

    Drive to Reith im Alpbachtal; the café stands at the valley station of the Reitherkogelbahn. Ask the hut or check the official site beforehand.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a play area for children?

    Yes, the description mentions a play area at the terrace.

    Where is the Liftcafé Heisn?

    In Reith im Alpbachtal, next to the valley station of the Reitherkogelbahn.
