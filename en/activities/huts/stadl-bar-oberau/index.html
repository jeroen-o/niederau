    Stadl Bar Oberau

    A cosy bar right by the slope in Oberau

    The Stadl Bar is in Oberau, the village between Niederau and Auffach, and the Wildschönau tourist board lists it as a cosy bar directly by the slope. It is an après-ski spot for the winter months. The contact details in the listing point to Hotel Tirolerhof in Oberau, so the hotel plausibly runs the bar, although the listing does not say so outright.

    Oberau is quieter than Niederau and especially known among cross-country skiers. The Stadl Bar fits that picture: not a high mountain hut, but a valley spot where you can drop in after skiing without taking another lift. The listing gives little more detail, so ask on the spot about what is on offer.

    Place: Oberau

    Official website

    • DistanceOberau is close to Niederau, about 5 minutes by car
    • LocationOberau, Wildschönau

    Tips

    • Combine it with a day of skiing or cross-country skiing in and around Oberau.
    • Take the car or ski bus from Niederau to Oberau; the village is close by.
    • Check with the bar or the official site for current information.

    Practical

    In Oberau, right by the slope; a short ride from Niederau.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the Stadl Bar in Niederau?

    No, it is in Oberau, the neighbouring village.

    Is it a mountain hut?

    No, it is a bar in the valley, by the slope.
