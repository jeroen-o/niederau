    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Berggasthof Hornboden

    Family-run inn with self-service and a restaurant below the Wiedersberger Horn

    The Berggasthof Hornboden stands at about 1,827 m directly below the top station of the Wiedersbergerhornbahn in Alpbach. According to the official page, the gondola ride up takes around a quarter of an hour. The house is on the piste, with a self-service area on the ground floor, a waiter-service restaurant upstairs and a sun terrace.

    The Ski Juwel page promotes a breakfast on the mountain, and a German hut review mentions a Prosecco breakfast after the first run and a cosy atmosphere. The house is run by the Larch family and can host events for up to around five hundred people. On the first floor there is a restaurant with a terrace.

    Place: Alpbach

    Official website

    • DistanceOn the Alpbachtal side of Ski Juwel; from Niederau via the Ski Juwel link or by car to Alpbach (approximate)
    • LocationWiedersberger Horn, Alpbach

    Tips

    • If you fancy breakfast on the mountain, ask in advance how it works.
    • In a hurry, use the self-service area downstairs; for a relaxed seat, the restaurant upstairs.
    • After lunch you can go straight back onto the piste or head for the nearby Wiedersberger Horn.

    Practical

    By the Wiedersbergerhornbahn from Alpbach, or on skis via the surrounding runs. For current information ask the house or check the official site.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What kind of hut is the Hornboden?

    A mountain inn on the piste with self-service downstairs and a waiter-service restaurant upstairs.

    Is there a sun terrace?

    Yes, according to the official description.
