    Berghaus Koglmoos (Schatzberg)

    A panoramic terrace at the Schatzbergbahn mid station

    Berghaus Koglmoos sits at roughly 1,300 metres next to the mid station of the Schatzbergbahn above Auffach. It is a mountain restaurant serving regional food, with a large terrace looking out over the Kitzbühel Alps. Arriving by gondola, you get off right at the door and do not need to climb any further.

    Families will find it practical because a children's playground with a water feature is nearby, according to a summer walking-route description from the Schatzberg. In winter the house stands by the piste, close to a small practice lift for beginners. It is also a stopping point on walking routes between Mühltal, Bernau and Auffach.

    Place: Auffach

    Official website

    • DistanceAbout 20 minutes by car from Niederau to the valley station in Auffach, then the gondola
    • LocationAuffach, Wildschönau

    Tips

    • Take the gondola to the mid station, or in summer walk down from the Schatzberg to Koglmoos (about 6 km) rather than riding the whole way.
    • Combine your visit with the nearby play area if you travel with young children.
    • Check with the hut or the official Ski Juwel site for current information before you go.

    Practical

    From Auffach take the Schatzbergbahn to the mid station; it can also be reached on foot, for example from Mühltal via Bernau or in summer from the summit.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is Berghaus Koglmoos?

    At the mid station of the Schatzbergbahn, at around 1,300 metres above Auffach.

    Is it suitable for children?

    Yes, a route description mentions a play area nearby, and there is a small practice lift for beginners.
