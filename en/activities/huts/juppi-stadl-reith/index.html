    Juppi Stadl

    Drinks and mulled wine by the Juppi family area

    The Juppi Stadl stands on the Reither Kogel above Reith im Alpbachtal, close to the family area built around the mascot Juppi, which you reach by the Reitherkogelbahn. The name refers to a Stadl, a wooden barn, and that suits the character: a small, informal place to pause rather than a big restaurant.

    According to the Alpbachtal tourism listing, it is mainly about drinks, such as draught beer and sparkling wine, with mulled wine in winter. That makes it a stop for a short break while the children are busy nearby. The Juppi Zauberwald, a summer walking trail, starts at the top station of the same lift. For a full meal you are better off with the huts further along.

    Place: Reith im Alpbachtal

    Official website

    • DistanceAbout 30 minutes by car from Niederau to the valley station in Reith, then by lift (approximate)
    • LocationReith im Alpbachtal, Alpbachtal

    Tips

    • Combine the stop with the Juppi family area at the Reitherkogelbahn.
    • Expect drinks and a short break rather than a full meal.
    • If you want something hot to eat, look at the Almstüberl Gschwendt on the same family run.

    Practical

    Take the Reitherkogelbahn from Reith im Alpbachtal. Ask the hut or check the official site beforehand for the current situation.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you have a full meal at the Juppi Stadl?

    The official description mainly mentions drinks. Other huts nearby are better for a hot meal.

    How do you get to the Juppi Stadl?

    By the Reitherkogelbahn from Reith im Alpbachtal up the Reither Kogel.
