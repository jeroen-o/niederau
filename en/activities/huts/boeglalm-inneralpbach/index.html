    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Böglalm

    Rustic alm above Inneralpbach known for its big pan of Kasspatzl

    The Böglalm sits at about 1,220 m above Inneralpbach and, in winter, on the piste by the middle station of the Pöglbahn. It is known for Alpbacher Kasspatzl, cooked in a very large pan. The menu also lists Tyrolean classics such as Gröstl, schnitzel and dumplings, and sometimes grilled chicken.

    In summer it is a walking destination. According to Alpbachtal Tourism, the route from the car park in Inneralpbach is easy and suitable for a pushchair; allow about an hour on foot. The alm also offers an alpine breakfast with regional produce.

    Place: Alpbach

    Official website

    • DistanceOn the Alpbachtal side of Ski Juwel; from Niederau a drive or long ski day via the Ski Juwel link (approximate)
    • LocationInneralpbach, Alpbachtal

    Tips

    • In summer a pushchair is fine on the route from Inneralpbach.
    • If you have a particular dish in mind, ask the hut beforehand.
    • Combine the hike with a return via the Greiter farms, a route described by Alpbachtal Tourism.

    Practical

    On foot from the car park in Inneralpbach, or on skis via the piste by the Pöglbahn middle station. For current information ask the hut.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you walk to the Böglalm?

    Yes, from the car park in Inneralpbach it is an easy walk of about an hour.

    What is the hut known for?

    For Alpbacher Kasspatzl from a large pan.
