    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Rübezahl-Hütte

    A rustic mountain hut near the top station

    The Rübezahl-Hütte sits on the Markbachjoch above Niederau, at roughly 1,450 m and about 150 m from the top station of the Markbachjochbahn. According to its own pages and tourism sites, it calls itself the most rustic hut in the Wildschönau. The timber hut stands in the middle of the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area and is also a regular stop for walkers on the Markbachjoch.

    The menu is best known for Kaiserschmarrn, the thick shredded Tyrolean pancake, which goes well with coffee or hot chocolate. Live music is played on certain days, with grilled dishes alongside. The hut also offers rooms to stay in. With children the walk from the top station stays short, since the final stretch is brief.

    Official website

    • DistanceAbove Niederau: gondola plus about 150 m on foot
    • LocationMarkbachjoch, Niederau (Wildschönau)

    Tips

    • Try the Kaiserschmarrn, which is what the hut is known for.
    • Combine the visit with a walk along the Markbachjoch and return to the gondola afterwards.
    • Ask the hut or check the official site beforehand about live music and overnight stays.

    Practical

    Take the Markbachjochbahn from Niederau; from the top station it is about 150 m on foot.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I reach the Rübezahl-Hütte without skiing?

    Yes, you reach it by the Markbachjochbahn and a short walk from the top station. Ask the hut for current information.

    What is the hut known for?

    Mainly for its rustic atmosphere and the Kaiserschmarrn.
