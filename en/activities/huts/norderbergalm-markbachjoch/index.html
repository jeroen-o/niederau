    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Norderbergalm

    A gourmet hut half an hour from the Markbachjoch

    The Norderbergalm lies at about 1,360 m, around half an hour on foot from the Markbachjoch top station along a forest track that runs gently downhill. Hiking sites sometimes give a different altitude (up to 1,500 m), so take it as a rough figure. From Niederau it is about an hour and a half uphill.

    The hut is known for Tyrolean specialities and home-baked cakes, and reviewers praise the Kaiserschmarrn. There is a cycling evening on Thursdays in summer and a ski-touring evening in winter. A nice family route is the gondola up, a walk to the alm and back to the top station. Ask the hut or check the official site for what is currently possible.

    Official website

    • DistanceAbout 30 minutes on foot from the Markbachjoch top station
    • LocationNorderberg, Wildschönau (near Niederau)

    Tips

    • Take the gondola up and walk to the alm; the way back climbs, so allow extra time.
    • Try the home-baked cake or the Kaiserschmarrn.
    • Ask in advance about the cycling and ski-touring evenings.

    Practical

    Take the Markbachjochbahn to the top station and walk about 30 minutes, or climb from Niederau in about 1.5 hours.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How far is it from the top station?

    About half an hour on foot according to hiking sources.

    What is it known for?

    Tyrolean specialities, home-baked cake and Kaiserschmarrn.
