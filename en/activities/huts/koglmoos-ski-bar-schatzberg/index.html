    Koglmoos Ski Bar

    Après-ski bar right on the piste at the mid station

    The Koglmoos Ski Bar stands at the mid station of the Schatzbergbahn, next to Berghaus Koglmoos and right on the piste. The Wildschönau tourist board's listing describes it as a meeting place for skiers and après-ski fans. Other sources also call it Schneebar or Schirmbar Koglmoos, so the name varies from source to source.

    It does not appear to have a kitchen of its own: food is left to the neighbouring Berghaus. It is a spot for a drink along the way, on the final run down to Auffach. Skiresort.info describes the mood as mostly relaxed, with the occasional longer afternoon in peak season and at weekends.

    Place: Auffach

    Official website

    • DistanceAbout 20 minutes by car from Niederau to the valley station in Auffach, then the gondola
    • LocationAuffach, Wildschönau

    Tips

    • Pair it with lunch at the neighbouring Berghaus Koglmoos.
    • Plan the last run down to Auffach before it gets dark.
    • Check with the hut or the official site for current information.

    Practical

    Take the Schatzbergbahn to the mid station, or ski down from the mountain; the bar sits on the piste.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is food served at the Koglmoos Ski Bar?

    One source says the bar itself serves no food; the neighbouring Berghaus Koglmoos has a kitchen.

    Is this the same as the Schirmbar?

    Probably: different sources use Ski Bar, Schneebar and Schirmbar Koglmoos for the bar at the mid station.
