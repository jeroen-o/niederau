    Mountain huts and ski huts around Ski Juwel · Winter

    Kafner Ast

    Self-service hut with a sun terrace on the Wiedersbergerhorn

    The Kafner Ast is a ski hut at roughly 1,600 m on the Wiedersbergerhorn above Alpbach. It stands beside the piste under the Gmah six-seater chairlift, in the middle of the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau area. It works on a self-service basis: you collect your own food and drink and then pick a table.

    Indoors there are panelled wooden parlours, and outside is a sunny terrace. According to the official Ski Juwel page, home-baked cakes are on offer and the hut is a meeting point for locals and visitors alike. A Tirol.at article describes it as family-run and recently renovated. One overview lists no beds, so think of it as a daytime stop rather than a place to stay.

    Place: Alpbach

    Official website

    • DistanceAbout 35 minutes by car from Niederau to Alpbach, then up by the Wiedersbergerhornbahn (approximate)
    • LocationAlpbach, Alpbachtal

    Tips

    • Try a slice of the home-baked cake and take a seat on the terrace when the sun is out.
    • The hut sits right by the piste, so you can stop without changing your route.
    • Do not plan an overnight stay: one overview lists no beds.

    Practical

    You reach the hut on skis or snowboard via the piste beneath the Gmah lift, riding up from the valley on the Wiedersbergerhornbahn. Check with the hut or the official Ski Juwel site beforehand.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the Kafner Ast a self-service hut?

    Yes, the official Ski Juwel page describes it as self-service.

    Which lift is the Kafner Ast on?

    On the piste beneath the Gmah six-seater chairlift on the Wiedersbergerhorn.
