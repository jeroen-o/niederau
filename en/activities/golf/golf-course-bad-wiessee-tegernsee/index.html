    Tegernseer Golf-Club Bad Wiessee

    Hilly 18 holes above the Tegernsee

    The Tegernseer Golf-Club sits on the Rohbognerhof estate in Bad Wiessee, high above the lake's eastern shore. The club started in 1958 with six holes and was extended to a full 18 in 1983. The ground is hilly with a few water hazards, and the course is regarded as sporty and fairly demanding.

    It plays to par 70. From the white tees it measures a little over 5,500 m, and about 4,970 m from the ladies' tee. That is shorter than a typical championship course, but the elevation changes make the round harder than the yardage suggests. A driving range with 20 bays and practice greens are available for warming up. Visitors are welcome, but they need a club card with a registered handicap, so players without a handicap certificate cannot simply turn up for a round. From Niederau it is a long drive, so plan a full day.

    Tips

    • Bring your club card with handicap; without it you will not be admitted.
    • Expect plenty of elevation change, so a trolley is worth having.
    • Book ahead, especially at weekends.

    Practical

    About 38 km as the crow flies from Niederau; approx. 1.5 hours by car. Close to Lake Tegernsee.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many holes and what par?

    18 holes, par 70; the white tee measures a little over 5,500 m.

    Can visitors play?

    Yes, with a club card showing a registered handicap, preferably after booking.
