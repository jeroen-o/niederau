    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Kitzbühel-Kaps Golf Course

    Traditional nine holes at Schloss Kaps, with island greens

    Kitzbühel's oldest golf course lies at Schloss Kaps, on the sunny side of town. Golf has been played here since 1955 on nine holes (par 35) spread across more than 30 hectares of parkland. The tees range from 2,157 to 2,805 m. The views take in Kitzbühel and the surrounding mountains.

    The closing holes are the showpiece: holes 8 and 9 have island greens in the middle of a lake, reached over small bridges. Anyone who has to play across the water will find that this compact round has teeth. Beside the course are a pro shop, a golf academy, a short-game practice area and a restaurant terrace, which makes it a good place for a short round or a first taste of golf. Being close to town, it is the easiest course to combine with a visit to Kitzbühel.

    Tips

    • Save your focus for the last two holes: the island greens are the highlight.
    • Short on time? Nine holes fit comfortably into a few hours.
    • Use the short-game area to warm up before you tee off.

    Practical

    About 40 minutes by car from Niederau; the course lies close to the centre of Kitzbühel.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
