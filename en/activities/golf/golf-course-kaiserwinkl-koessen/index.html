Kaiserwinkl Golf, Kössen (Lärchenhof)

18 holes along the Kohlenbach, beneath the Kaiser mountains

Kaiserwinkl Golf Kössen, also known as Lärchenhof, is not a nine-hole course but a full 18-hole layout of par 72. It opened in 1989 and was designed by Donald Harradine. It measures 5,645 m from the men's tees and 4,884 m from the ladies' tees.

The terrain lies on both sides of the Kohlenbach stream and is rolling, with old trees, the rock massif of the Kaiser mountains as a backdrop and several water hazards. Some fairways are narrow and the greens well guarded, so accuracy counts for more than length. There is a driving range with a practice area. Kössen lies close to the German border and suits a day in the Kaiserwinkl with lakes and walking trails. The contrast with the larger courses in the region makes the drive worthwhile.

Tips

  • Aim for the middle of the fairway: narrow stretches and water punish wild shots.
  • Plan a round followed by a walk or a dip in one of the nearby lakes.
  • Allow for a longer drive and turn the trip into a day in the Kaiserwinkl.

Practical

About 60 minutes by car from Niederau, to Kössen in the Kaiserwinkl.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is it a nine- or 18-hole course?

An 18-hole course, par 72.

Donald Harradine; it opened in 1989.
