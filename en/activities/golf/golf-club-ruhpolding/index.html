    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Ruhpolding Golf Club

    A Thomas Himmel parkland course below the Rauschberg

    Golfclub Ruhpolding lies in the Zell district of the village, on Rauschbergstraße. It is an 18-hole course (par 72) by the German architect Thomas Himmel. According to the Ruhpolding tourist board the men's tees measure about 6,004 m. The toughest hole is the 13th, a 571 m par 5.

    The terrain is gently hilly with water hazards and ranges from sporty to demanding. For warming up there is a driving range, putting and chipping greens and a three-hole short course. The season runs from about April to November. Guests register beforehand and show a club card with a handicap. From Niederau you drive there via the Kaiserwinkl.

    Tips

    • On the par 5 at hole 13, play for position rather than power.
    • Use the three-hole practice course to test your short game.
    • Combine the day with Ruhpolding itself, for example the Rauschberg cable car.

    Practical

    About 80 minutes by car from Niederau (approx. 55 km as the crow flies), via the Kaiserwinkl and Reit im Winkl.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who designed the course?

    Thomas Himmel; 18 holes, par 72.

    Are there practice facilities?

    Yes: a driving range, putting and chipping greens and a three-hole short course.
