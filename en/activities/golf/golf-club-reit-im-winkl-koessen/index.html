Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

Reit im Winkl-Kössen Golf Club

A cross-border 18-hole course: six holes in Tyrol, twelve in Bavaria

Playing here means crossing the Austrian-German border more than once. Of the 18 holes (par 70), six are in Tyrol and twelve in Bavaria, with the clubhouse on the Austrian side. The course reportedly opened in 1985 and lies on a plateau east of Kössen, with views of the Kaiser mountains and the Chiemgau Alps. The yellow tees measure 5,461 m, the red 4,794 m.

Guests are welcome but must register and show a club card with a registered handicap (up to 54; 36 at weekends and on public holidays). There is a driving range with 35 tees, 10 of them covered, plus putting, pitching and chipping greens. The season runs from April to November.

Place: Kössen

Official website

  • CostGreen fee varies; check in advance
  • DistanceApprox. 60 min drive (approx. 35 km as the crow flies)

Tips

  • Register in advance and bring proof of your handicap; without it you cannot play.
  • Warm up on the range before teeing off; 10 of its 35 tees are covered.
  • Bring ID: the course crosses the national border.

Practical

About 60 minutes by car from Niederau to Kössen; roughly 35 km as the crow flies.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can visitors play?

Yes, after registering and with a club card showing a registered handicap.

What makes the course unusual?

It lies in two countries: six holes in Tyrol and twelve in Bavaria.
