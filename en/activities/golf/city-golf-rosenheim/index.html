Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

City Golf Rosenheim

Public short course with driving range, no membership needed

City Golf Rosenheim is in the Happing district on the edge of Rosenheim and is public: you can play without membership or a Platzreife certificate. According to its own site it is a nine-hole par-3 course of about 930 m; other sources mention six holes. The number of holes is therefore not clear, so ask beforehand.

There is a driving range with 35 tees, 7 of them covered, plus putting, pitching and chipping greens and a practice bunker. According to the site it is open all year. It is a good place to try golf or practise the short game, but not a full 18-hole round. Handy as a stop in Rosenheim on the way to or from Niederau.

Official website

  • CostGreenfee varies; check in advance
  • DistanceApprox. 60 min drive
  • SeasonAll year (per the facility)
  • LocationRosenheim-Happing, Bavaria

Tips

  • Ideal for a first taste: no club card or Platzreife needed.
  • Use the range and short-game area to test your swing.
  • Ask beforehand how many holes are currently played.

Practical

About 60 minutes by car from Niederau (approx. 48 km as the crow flies), via Kufstein and the A93.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a club card?

No, you can play without membership or a Platzreife.

Is there a driving range?

Yes, with 35 tees, 7 of them covered.
