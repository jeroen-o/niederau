Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

Golf Club Kitzbühel-Schwarzsee-Reith

An 18-hole course with mountain views near Kitzbühel

The Golf Club Kitzbühel-Schwarzsee-Reith has an 18-hole, par 72 course dating from 1987 in the Kitzbühel Alps. Designed by Gerold Hauser, it has gently rolling terrain, water hazards and mountain views. It has also hosted the European Challenge Tour.

Besides the course there are a driving range, short-game area, golf school, restaurant, pro shop and rental of clubs and carts. The season runs roughly from May to October. It is an option for golfers who want a round during their stay; reserve a tee time in advance and ask about fees and rules.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • AgeFor golfers; juniors welcome
  • DurationAbout 4 to 5 hours per round
  • CostGreen fee; check current rates
  • DistanceAbout 45 to 50 min by car
  • SeasonRoughly May to October
  • LocationKitzbühel, Schwarzsee

Tips

  • Reserve a tee time in advance.
  • Bring proof of handicap.
  • Ask about the dress code, and about the golf school if you want to practise.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 45 to 50 minutes by car to the Schwarzsee area near Kitzbühel. Ask the club about fees, rules and dress code.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions
