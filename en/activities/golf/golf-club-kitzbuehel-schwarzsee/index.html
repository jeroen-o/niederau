Golf Club Kitzbühel-Schwarzsee-Reith

An 18-hole course with mountain views near Kitzbühel

The Golf Club Kitzbühel-Schwarzsee-Reith has an 18-hole course, built in 1987, in the Kitzbühel Alps. It has gently rolling fairways, a few water hazards and mountain views. It has also hosted the European Challenge Tour.

It is an option for golfers who want a round during their stay in the Wildschönau. Bring proof of handicap and reserve a tee time in advance. It suits those who can already play; beginner courses may be available, but ask the club.
