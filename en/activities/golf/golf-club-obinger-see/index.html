    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Golf Club am Obinger See

    Nine links-style holes, opened in 2002

    Golf Club am Obinger See lies near Obing, in the Kleinornach district, north-east of the Chiemsee. The course opened in June 2002 and was designed by Tony Ristola. It is a nine-hole links-style layout. For 18 holes you play the loop twice; from the yellow tees that adds up to 6,014 m (par 72).

    It is not the nearest course to Niederau, but its open links character appeals to those who like wind and wide skies. Guest conditions (handicap, registration) vary, so ask beforehand. If you are visiting the Chiemsee area it makes a quiet extra round.

    Official website

    • CostGreenfee varies; check in advance
    • DistanceApprox. 100 min drive
    • SeasonSummer
    • LocationObing (Kleinornach), Bavaria

    Tips

    • Expect wind: on an open links-style course it can matter a lot.
    • For 18 holes you play the loop twice; allow enough time.
    • Ask beforehand about conditions for guests.

    Practical

    About 100 minutes by car from Niederau (approx. 65 km as the crow flies), via Kufstein and the A8.
