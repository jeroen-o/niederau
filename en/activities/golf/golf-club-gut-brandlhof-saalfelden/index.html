    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Gut Brandlhof Golf Club, Saalfelden

    A championship course where the Saalach is crossed up to six times

    Gut Brandlhof near Saalfelden has an 18-hole championship course of par 72 with a view of the Steinernes Meer. The ground is flat and watery; the Saalach flows through it and, depending on the tee, has to be crossed up to six times. The course measures 6,079 m from the white tees, 5,710 m from yellow, 5,286 m from blue and 4,987 m from red.

    It is a good fit for a mixed group: the gap between the shortest and longest tee is big enough to match everyone's level. The course belongs to a hotel with golf packages, which is handy if you want to stay nearby for a few days. I found no confirmed details on opening year or designer.

    Tips

    • Choose your tee deliberately: the Saalach comes into play more often from the long tees.
    • Plan a full day; the drive is long.
    • Combine with Saalfelden or Leogang nearby.

    Practical

    About 75 minutes by car from Niederau to Saalfelden; roughly 57 km as the crow flies.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long is the course?

    Par 72; from 6,079 m (white) down to 4,987 m (red).

    Which river crosses the course?

    The Saalach, crossed up to six times.
