    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Zell am See-Kaprun Golf Club (Schmittenhöhe and Kitzsteinhorn)

    Two 18-hole courses on the plain between Zell am See and Kaprun

    On the plain between Zell am See and Kaprun this club runs two 18-hole courses, Schmittenhöhe and Kitzsteinhorn, making it one of the larger golf complexes in Austria. Both lie in a flat, parkland setting with lakes and reed beds and suit a range of abilities.

    Schmittenhöhe opened in 1984 to a design by Donald Harradine and is a par 72; it measures 6,262 m from the white tees and 5,253 m from the red. Sources disagree on par and length for Kitzsteinhorn, so ask the club. The season runs from April to November. If you stay in Niederau, you can combine the drive with a day at the Zeller See or an outing to Kaprun.

    Place: Zell am See

    Official website

    • CostGreen fee varies; check in advance
    • DistanceApprox. 70 min drive (approx. 56 km as the crow flies)
    • SeasonApril to November
    • LocationZell am See and Kaprun, Pinzgau

    Tips

    • Choose the course to suit your level; Schmittenhöhe is the better documented one.
    • Allow for a long drive and make a day of it with the Zeller See.
    • Ask about par and distances for Kitzsteinhorn beforehand.

    Practical

    About 70 minutes by car from Niederau to Zell am See; roughly 56 km as the crow flies.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many courses does the club have?

    Two 18-hole courses: Schmittenhöhe and Kitzsteinhorn.

    Who designed Schmittenhöhe?

    Donald Harradine; it opened in 1984 (par 72).
