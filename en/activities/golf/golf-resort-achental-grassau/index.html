    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Golf Resort Achental, Grassau

    A flat 18-hole course with water and 64 bunkers

    Next to the Das Achental resort in Grassau lies an 18-hole course (par 72) that opened in 2013. It was designed by Thomas Himmel. The terrain is flat with links and moorland features. It measures 5,835 m from the white tees and 4,316 m from the shortest ladies' tees.

    According to the resort the course has water hazards, thick rough, well-guarded greens and 64 bunkers, some of them very large. That makes it easy to walk but not toothless. One source says the course was rated five-star superior by the Bavarian golf body BVGA (2020). From Niederau, Grassau lies on the Bavarian side of the Chiemgau Alps, on the way towards the Chiemsee.

    Tips

    • In wind, take an extra club: on a flat course with water the elements show.
    • Check beforehand how guests not staying at the resort can book.
    • Afterwards, the Chiemsee is only a short drive away.

    Practical

    About 70 minutes by car from Niederau (approx. 47 km as the crow flies), via Kössen and Reit im Winkl or via Kufstein.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    How long is the course?
