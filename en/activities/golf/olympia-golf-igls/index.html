    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Olympia Golf Igls (short course)

    Pay-and-play short course below the Patscherkofel

    Olympia Golf in Igls is a short nine-hole course at the foot of the Patscherkofel, just outside Innsbruck. According to the Tyrol tourist board it is pay-and-play: no membership needed, but a verifiable handicap up to -54 is required. Seven holes are par 3 and two are par 4, adding up to par 58 and about 2,800 m.

    The short holes call mostly for accurate iron play, and plenty of water and well-protected greens keep it interesting. There are a driving range, putting, pitching and chipping greens, a practice bunker, a clubhouse with restaurant and a golf school with PGA professionals. It is not meant for a long day's programme but for a round of about an hour and a half. The season runs roughly from March to November.

    Place: Innsbruck

    Tips

    • Bring proof of handicap; without it you will not be allowed on the course.
    • A small set of irons is enough on these short holes.
    • Combine the round with a visit to Innsbruck or the Patscherkofel cable car.

    Practical

    About 60 minutes by car from Niederau, through the Inn valley to Innsbruck-Igls; roughly 56 km as the crow flies.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is membership required?

    No, it is pay-and-play; a handicap up to -54 is required.

    What kind of course is it?

    A short course with nine holes (seven par 3, two par 4).
