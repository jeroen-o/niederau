Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

Wilder Kaiser Golf Club, Ellmau

27 holes in three loops, April to November

This is the biggest complex within reach of Niederau: 27 holes arranged in three loops called Wilder Kaiser, Ellmau and Tirol, which can be played in any combination. Wilder Kaiser plus Ellmau forms an 18-hole course of par 72, up to 6,104 m from the white tees.

The fairways are fairly flat and the sunny location allows a long season, from April to November. The club has a driving range with 16 tees, 8 of them covered. The view of the rugged limestone walls of the Kaiser mountains is the main reason to play here, though it is also a good spot if you like having three different nine-hole rounds to choose from. Groups of mixed ability can easily settle on a route together.

Place: Ellmau

Tips

  • Pick your loop: when it is busy you can often be offered a different combination.
  • Pack a fleece for April and October; it can be cool beneath the rock face.
  • Pair the round with a walk in the Kaiser mountains.

Practical

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau to Ellmau, through the Kufsteinerland.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How many holes can I play?

Three nine-hole loops; you can play nine or combine them into 18.

When is the season?

Roughly April to November.
