Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

Kitzbühel-Schwarzsee Golf Course

Water, hills and the par 3 over the Mausefalle gorge

Kitzbühel-Schwarzsee-Reith Golf Club opened in 1989 to a design by Gerold Hauser. Its 18 holes (par 72) measure 6,104 m from the white tees. In effect you get two courses in one: holes 1 to 9 are flat and threaded with water, while holes 10 to 18 rise and fall and ask more of your legs and your club selection.

The layout has 12 water hazards and 32 bunkers. The best-known hole is the 16th, the Mausefalle (mousetrap), a par 3 where the ball has to carry a small gorge. The course has hosted events of the European Challenge Tour. From Niederau it is the most ambitious option, at about forty minutes by car. It is less of a gentle stroll and more a course for players who enjoy variety.

Tips

  • The front nine are flat and watery, so pace yourself for the more demanding back nine.
  • On hole 16, the Mausefalle, take enough club; the gorge punishes a short shot.
  • Choose the tee that suits your level: there are five tees, from 4,850 to 6,104 m.

Practical

Towards Kitzbühel, about 40 minutes by car from Niederau; the club lies by the Schwarzsee near Reith.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is special about the course?

The split between a flat, watery front nine and a hilly back nine, plus the par-3 Mausefalle on hole 16.

How long is the course?

6,104 m from the white tees (par 72); the shortest ladies' tee measures 4,850 m.
