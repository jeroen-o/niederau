Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

Kitzbühel-Schwarzsee Golf Course

18 holes with water hazards and a hilly back nine

Near the Schwarzsee lake, just outside Kitzbühel, lies an 18-hole course (par 72). The first nine holes run over flat ground, while the back nine rise and fall across hilly terrain. Water hazards give the course its character.

It is one of four courses around Kitzbühel, together offering 54 holes. For guests staying in Niederau it is one of the larger courses within an hour's drive.

Place: Kitzbühel

Tips

  • Book a tee time in advance, especially in high season.
  • Ask about the rules for guests without club membership and any required handicap or course-permit.
  • Check the forecast; mountain weather can change quickly.

Practical

About 40 minutes by car from Niederau, towards Kitzbühel.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a member to play?

Visitors can usually play for a green fee; ask about the conditions beforehand.
