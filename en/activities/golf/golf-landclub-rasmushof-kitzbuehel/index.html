    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Rasmushof Golf & Country Club, Kitzbühel

    Nine par-3 holes in the Streif finish area

    At the foot of the Hahnenkamm, in the finish area of the famous Streif downhill, lies Rasmushof: nine holes, all of them par 3. According to Tirol Werbung the course opened in 1977 as Austria's first nine-hole par-3 facility, the idea being to make sporting use of the ski slope in summer too. Some holes climb the slope, and the greens are small, rewarding accurate iron play.

    The facility includes a driving range, a short-game area, a golf school, a restaurant and rental of clubs and trolleys. Guests can play daily after booking; proof of handicap is requested. Sources disagree on the exact length, so it is better not to rely on a fixed figure. It suits a short round in or near Kitzbühel.

    Tips

    • Reserve in advance and bring proof of your handicap.
    • Irons and a putter are usually enough on a par-3 course.
    • Combine the round with a visit to Kitzbühel town centre.

    Practical

    About 40 minutes by car from Niederau to Kitzbühel; roughly 23 km as the crow flies.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What kind of course is Rasmushof?

    A nine-hole course on which every hole is a par 3.

    Can visitors play?

    Yes, after booking and with proof of handicap; check in advance.
