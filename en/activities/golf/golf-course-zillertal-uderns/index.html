    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Zillertal-Uderns Golf Course

    18 holes at 550 m, March to November

    In the broad Zillertal valley, at about 550 m, lies Zillertal-Uderns Golf Course: an 18-hole layout (par 72) on more than 65 hectares. Its length depends on the tee and ranges from 4,752 to 6,027 m. Thanks to the low altitude the season starts in March and runs into November, longer than at many courses in the region.

    The practice facilities are the most extensive of this selection. The range has 35 tees, 18 of them covered, plus a Trackman setup for measuring your shots. There is also a large chipping area, pitching and putting greens, and a small three-hole course for short-game work. It is particularly good if you are still learning the game. Everything is close together, which keeps a day at the course straightforward.

    Place: Fügen

    Tips

    • Come in spring or autumn: this is often among the first courses to open and the last to close.
    • Book a lesson or a Trackman session if you want to work on your swing.
    • Use the three-hole short course to sharpen your short game before the main round.

    Practical

    About 35 minutes by car from Niederau, through the Inn valley to Uderns in the Zillertal.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is the season?

    From about March to November.

    What practice options are there?

    A range with 35 tees (18 covered), Trackman, chipping, pitching and putting greens and a three-hole short course.
