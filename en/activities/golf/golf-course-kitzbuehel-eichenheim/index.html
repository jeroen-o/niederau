    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Eichenheim Golf Course, Kitzbühel

    A Kyle Phillips design with 116 m of elevation change

    Eichenheim, near Kitzbühel-Aurach, was laid out around 2000 by the American architect Kyle Phillips. The course is par 71 and 6,092 m long. The clubhouse has a golf lounge, a golf bistro and a restaurant. Practice facilities are generous too, with a 30-tee driving range (13 of them covered), a practice bunker and a putting green. A golf school makes the club attractive if you want a few lessons. That way you can combine sport and a good meal in one place.

    Place: Kitzbühel

    Tips

    • Expect a walk with real elevation change and allow plenty of time; a buggy can help.
    • Warm up on the driving range: part of its 30 tees are covered, so it works in a shower too.
    • Round off the day with lunch or dinner in the clubhouse.

    Practical

    About 40 minutes by car from Niederau, on the edge of Kitzbühel towards Aurach.

    Details checked: October 2026

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who designed the course?

    Kyle Phillips; the course opened around 2000.

    Can I practise and take lessons?

    Yes, there is a driving range with 30 tees, a putting green, a practice bunker and a golf school.
