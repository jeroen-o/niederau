    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Innsbruck-Igls Golf Club, Championship Course Rinn

    18 holes on the slopes of the Glungezer, reopened in 2024

    The championship course of Innsbruck-Igls Golf Club lies near Rinn, on the plateau on the southern slopes of the Glungezer. After a two-year renovation the 18-hole course reopened in 2024. It is a par 71; from the white tees it measures around 6,050 m, with an elevation change of nearly 250 m.

    The fairways run across the plateau like two concentric circles and are partly hilly. According to a German-language source, hole 12 has an island green. Guests should expect to book in advance and show proof of handicap. The season runs from about April to October. Expect a drive through the Inn valley towards Innsbruck: this is an outing for those who want to give a whole day to golf.

    Place: Innsbruck

    Tips

    • Plan a full day; the drive is long and the course has considerable elevation change.
    • Book ahead and bring proof of handicap.
    • Choose tees to suit your level: lengths range from about 5,200 to 6,050 m.

    Practical

    About 55 minutes by car from Niederau, through the Inn valley towards Innsbruck and then up to Rinn; roughly 46 km as the crow flies.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the par?

    Par 71, 18 holes.

    When did it reopen?

    In 2024, after a two-year renovation.
