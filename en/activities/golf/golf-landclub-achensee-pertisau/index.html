    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Golf & Landclub Achensee, Pertisau

    Tyrol's oldest golf club, at roughly 1,000 m by Lake Achensee

    The club in Pertisau was founded in 1934 and is regarded as the oldest in Tyrol. It is an 18-hole course (par 71) at about 1,000 m altitude, at the foot of the Karwendel range. If you are planning a day trip from Niederau to Lake Achensee, a round here is an easy addition.

    According to tourism sources the driving range is covered, and there are a practice bunker, practice greens, a golf school, a pro shop and a restaurant. Tee lengths differ considerably: from the white tees a golf portal gives just over 6,100 m, from the red ladies' tees about 4,950 m. Guests are welcome, but a tee time has to be reserved and proof of handicap is asked for. The season runs roughly from April to November.

    Place: Jenbach

    Tips

    • Book a tee time and bring proof of your handicap.
    • At about 1,000 m it can be cool even in summer; an extra layer is sensible.
    • Pair the round with a boat trip or a walk by Lake Achensee.

    Practical

    About 50 minutes by car from Niederau, through the Inn valley and Jenbach to Pertisau on Lake Achensee; roughly 27 km as the crow flies.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many holes does the course have?

    18 holes, par 71.

    Can guests play?

    Yes, with a reserved tee time and proof of handicap.
