Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

Kaisergolf Ellmau (par 3)

Nine par-3 holes: ideal for trying golf

Kaisergolf Ellmau is a public nine-hole course on which every hole is a par 3. It opened in 1990 and has narrow fairways, small greens and varied slopes. That may sound simple, but on short holes like these a precise iron shot is worth more than power.

It is the place for people without a handicap, or for a family that wants a few hours of play together without long walks. Experienced players also use the short course to sharpen their iron play. According to a golf portal the season runs from May to mid-October, so it starts later and ends sooner than the big courses. Overnight stays in suites are also available on site. A round does not usually take long, leaving time for the rest of the day.

Place: Ellmau

  • CostPrices vary by season; check in advance
  • DistanceApprox. 45 min drive
  • SeasonApprox. May to mid-October
  • LocationEllmau, Wilder Kaiser

Tips

  • Irons are all you need; a small set will do.
  • Children can join in easily because the holes are short.
  • In spring and autumn, check whether the course is open yet or still open.

Practical

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau, in Ellmau, close to the big club.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is a par-3 course?

A course where every hole is meant to be played in three shots; here nine holes, good for beginners.

When is it open?

Roughly May to mid-October; check beforehand.
