    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Schloss Elkofen Golf Club

    Hilly 18 holes by Donald Harradine, opened in 1987

    Schloss Elkofen Golf Club near Grafing, east of Munich, has an 18-hole course that opened in 1987 and was designed by Donald Harradine. It is a par 71 and, according to one reviewer, hilly and challenging in strategy, terrain and length.

    This is the farthest course in this selection, about 61 km as the crow flies from Niederau, so it is mainly of interest if you are heading towards Munich anyway. I could not confirm the lengths per tee; check the club's scorecard. Guests register beforehand.

    Official website

    • CostGreenfee varies; check in advance
    • DistanceApprox. 85 min drive
    • SeasonSummer
    • LocationGrafing (Elkofen), Bavaria

    Tips

    • Expect elevation changes and allow plenty of time.
    • Request the scorecard for the tee lengths.
    • Register in advance as a guest.

    Practical

    About 85 minutes by car from Niederau (approx. 61 km as the crow flies), via Kufstein and the A8.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who designed the course?

    Donald Harradine; it opened in 1987.

    What is the par?

    Par 71 over 18 holes.
