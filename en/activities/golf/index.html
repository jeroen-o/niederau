Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol

Golf with a mountain backdrop: a general guide for families and beginners.

Tyrol is an attractive region for golf in summer. Courses often lie among meadows and forests, with views of mountain peaks, and at altitude the climate is cooler than in the valley. The Kitzbühel Alps region and surrounding valleys have several courses of different levels, from short, relaxed courses to longer and more demanding ones.

There is also plenty for those who do not yet play. Many clubs offer trial lessons, driving ranges and practice facilities, and some have smaller courses for beginners and children. That lets you try it without playing a full round. For families it can be an enjoyable activity, provided the children are old enough to listen and be patient.

The mountain golf season is fairly short: usually from spring into autumn, depending on snow and altitude. Ask the club beforehand about opening times, reservations, handicap requirements and rental equipment. Which course suits you best depends on level, time and distance.

Tips

  • Start with a trial lesson or driving range.
  • Book a tee time beforehand.
  • Ask about rental equipment and children's options.
  • Watch the weather and take shelter in a thunderstorm.
  • Respect course etiquette.

Beginners and trial lessons

If you want to try golf, start with a trial lesson or an hour on the driving range. A professional teaches you the basics of grip, stance and swing. Clubs often rent out clubs and balls, so you need not buy anything. A full course sometimes requires proof of skill or a handicap; smaller practice courses usually do not. Dress comfortably and wear shoes with good grip.

Etiquette, weather and safety

On a golf course fixed rules of conduct apply: stay quiet, repair marks in the grass and wait until the group ahead has played. Children must understand this well. In the mountains the weather can change quickly, with thunderstorms as a risk: go indoors straight away if one approaches. Wear sunscreen and bring water. Keep in mind walkers and animals on or beside the course.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a handicap?

For full courses often yes, or proof of skill; for practice courses usually not. Ask the club beforehand.

When is the golf season?

Usually from spring into autumn, depending on snow and altitude. Check current opening dates.

Is golf suitable for children?

Yes, from a certain age, often through children's lessons or smaller courses. Ask about what is on offer.
