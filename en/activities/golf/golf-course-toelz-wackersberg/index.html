Tölzer Golfclub Wackersberg

Nine holes, eighteen options near Bad Tölz

The Tölzer Golfclub plays at Straß in Wackersberg, close to Bad Tölz. The course has nine holes, but extra tees allow eighteen to be played. There are three ways to do it: the somewhat more demanding first nine (holes 1 to 9), the gentler second nine (holes 10 to 18), or the full round.

The terrain is hilly, partly lined with old trees, and includes water hazards. The eighteen-hole round is par 71 from the yellow tee, at a little over 5,290 m. This is not a championship layout, but a calm, easy-to-read option for a shorter day. The atmosphere is that of a small club. Visitors pay a green fee; check beforehand whether a club card or handicap certificate is required and what condition the greens are in, as recent reviews report variable quality. From Niederau it is about 1 hour 45 minutes by car.

Tips

  • Choose for yourself: nine holes for a short round, or eighteen using the other tees.
  • Ask about the condition of the greens before booking.
  • Combine it with a visit to nearby Bad Tölz.

Practical

About 54 km as the crow flies from Niederau; approx. 1 hour 45 minutes by car.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a 9- or 18-hole course?

Nine holes, with extra tees that make eighteen possible.

What is par?

Par 71 over eighteen holes from the yellow tee (about 5,290 m).
