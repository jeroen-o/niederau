    Golf in the Kitzbühel Alps and Tyrol · Summer

    Schloss Maxlrain Golf Club

    Castle park, 140 hectares and a short course for beginners

    Schloß Maxlrain Golf Club near Tuntenhausen has an 18-hole course that opened in 1988, designed by Paul Krings. Most of it runs through the 19th-century castle park, within an estate of about 140 hectares. It measures 6,370 m from the white tees (par 72) and 6,090 m from the yellow.

    The terrain is flat to gently hilly with water hazards and is described as demanding. Alongside the main course there is a public nine-hole short course aimed at beginners. Per one source it is charged by the day; ask about current terms. From Niederau it is one of the nearer large courses on the Bavarian side.

    Official website

    • CostGreenfee varies; check in advance
    • DistanceApprox. 70 min drive
    • SeasonSummer
    • LocationTuntenhausen (Maxlrain), Bavaria

    Tips

    • Beginners can try the short course first.
    • Choose the tee by level: 5,400 to 6,370 m.
    • Take a stroll through the castle park after your round.

    Practical

    About 70 minutes by car from Niederau (approx. 49 km as the crow flies), via Kufstein, Rosenheim and the A8.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who designed the course?

    Paul Krings; the course opened in 1988.

    Is there a course for beginners?

    Yes, a public nine-hole short course.
