3D archery in Kössen

Stalk 3D targets through the forest with a bow

In Kössen, in the Kaiserwinkl, you can try guided archery on a forest course. After a short practice round on a target you walk through the terrain looking for 3D targets shaped like animals. The route runs through shady woodland, which is welcome on warm days.

The activity takes about two hours and suits families; children from about 8 years old can join. No experience is needed because you get instruction and equipment. It is a calm, focused sport that also challenges the parents. Registration and the meeting point are handled by the provider SportundNatur in Kössen.

Place: Kössen

Tips

  • Book in advance; places are limited.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and clothing suited to the weather.
  • Ask the provider about prices and group size.
  • A discount may apply with the Kaiserwinkl guest card; ask about it.

Practical

Kössen is about an hour’s drive from Niederau, in the Kaiserwinkl. Registration and meeting are via the provider (SportundNatur) in Kössen; confirm time and place when booking.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
