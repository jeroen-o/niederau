3D archery in Kössen

Stalk 3D targets through the forest with a bow

In Kössen, in the Kaiserwinkl, you can try guided archery on a forest course. After a short practice round on a target you walk through the terrain looking for 3D targets shaped like animals. The route runs through shady woodland, which is welcome on warm days.

The activity takes about two hours and suits families; children from about 8 years old can join. No experience is needed because you get instruction and equipment. It is a calm, focused sport that also challenges the parents: you first learn the basics of traditional archery and then apply them on the course. The course is a good way to keep children outdoors for a few hours without screens: they pay attention to their surroundings, to each other and to their own technique. Afterwards there is usually something to talk about: who hit which animal best?

Registration and the meeting point are handled by the provider SportundNatur in Kössen, an adventure sports company that also runs canyoning, rafting and trekking. The company focuses on nature experiences without permanent installations in the landscape.

Tips

  • Book in advance; places are limited.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and clothing suited to the weather.
  • Ask the provider about prices and group size.
  • A discount may apply with the Kaiserwinkl guest card; ask about it.
  • Bring water and use insect repellent; you will be walking through woodland for two hours.

Practical

Kössen is about an hour's drive from Niederau, in the Kaiserwinkl, near the Bavarian border. Registration and meeting are via the provider (SportundNatur) in Kössen; confirm time and place when booking. You can combine the activity with a visit to the Kaiserwinkl, for example for a walk or a swim in a nearby lake. The terrain is natural; a little sure-footedness is enough, no special fitness is needed.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum age?

From about 8 years; confirm when booking.

Do you need archery experience?

No, you get instruction and equipment and start with a short practice round on a target.

How long does the course take?

The activity takes about two hours, including instruction and the practice round.
