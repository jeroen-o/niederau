Tandem paragliding from the Markbachjoch

Glide over the Wildschönau with an experienced pilot

The Wildschönau is regarded as one of the better areas in Tyrol for paragliding. The Markbachjochbahn from Niederau takes you to the take-off area at about 1,500 metres in a few minutes. From there you fly down with an experienced tandem pilot; you do not need any skills yourself.

The flight lasts roughly 8 to 10 minutes and the whole activity a little over half an hour to three quarters of an hour. The height difference is about 600 metres. The high valley is fairly sheltered from strong winds. According to providers, children can often join from about 8 years old; a maximum weight applies, so ask first.

  • AgeOften from about 8 years; ask
  • DurationAbout 45 minutes in total, flight 8 to 10 min
  • CostPrice varies; ask in advance
  • DistanceIn Niederau
  • SeasonSummer (weather permitting)
  • LocationMarkbachjoch, Niederau

Tips

  • Flights only go ahead in suitable weather; allow for rescheduling.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and layered clothing.
  • Ask about the age and weight limits when booking.
  • The lift ride to take-off may be charged separately.

Practical

The Markbachjochbahn starts in Niederau itself, so the valley station is only minutes away. You can book with local pilots or via Wildschönau Tourism; prices vary by provider.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the flight start?

By the Markbachjochbahn at Niederau to the take-off area at about 1,500 metres.
