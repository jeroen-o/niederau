Tennis in St. Johann in Tirol

Tennis with mountain views in the Leukental

According to the source, St. Johann in Tirol has several tennis courts with views of the surrounding mountains, with lessons for children and beginners. The region usually has both outdoor and indoor courts, which is handy in changeable mountain weather.

The exact court location, rates and lessons have not been checked. Ask the St. Johann tourist office or the sports centre about the current offer and book a court beforehand. Rackets and balls can usually be hired, and a form of mini tennis often exists for young children. It fits a sporty morning between walking days, and a terrace by the courts often makes for a pleasant break.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1 to 2 hours
  • CostCourt rates vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 35 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (outdoor); winter (indoor)
  • LocationSt. Johann in Tirol

Tips

  • Book a court beforehand.
  • Ask about racket and ball hire and about mini tennis for young children.
  • Plan for an indoor court if the weather is changeable.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 35 minutes by car to St. Johann in Tirol. Ask beforehand where the courts are and how to book.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Are there lessons for children?

According to the source there are; ask the sports centre about current options.
