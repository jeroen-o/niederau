    Tennis in St. Johann in Tirol

    Tennis with mountain views in the Leukental

    St. Johann in Tirol lies in the Leukental valley, at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser, and has tennis courts with views of the surrounding mountains. Children and beginners are welcome too, for instance with lessons. In the region you usually find both outdoor and indoor courts, which is handy when the mountain weather changes; in the winter months there are tennis halls in St. Johann and nearby Erpfendorf.

    Ask the St. Johann tourist office or the sports centre about the current courts, rates and lessons, and book a court in advance. Rackets and balls can usually be hired, and young children often have a form of mini tennis.

    It fits well with a sporty morning between hiking days, and a terrace near the courts often makes for a pleasant break. Since St. Johann is a proper town with shops and cafés, it is easy to combine with an errand or an ice cream afterwards. Tennis is an excellent addition to hiking and cycling: an hour on the court is also a nice way for many families to do something together without a long outing. Beginners can start with a short lesson and then carry on at their own level.

    Place: St. Johann in Tirol

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationAbout 1 to 2 hours
    • CostCourt rates vary; check beforehand
    • DistanceAbout 35 min by car
    • SeasonSummer (outdoor); winter (indoor)
    • LocationSt. Johann in Tirol

    Tips

    • Book a court beforehand.
    • Ask about racket and ball hire and about mini tennis for young children.
    • Plan for an indoor court if the weather is changeable.
    • Bring proper tennis shoes; hiking shoes damage the court.
    • Combine a morning of tennis with an afternoon in the centre of St. Johann.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about a 35-minute drive to St. Johann in Tirol, via Wörgl and the road towards Kitzbühel. Ask the tourist office where the courts are and how to book. Outside the summer season it is mainly the indoor courts that matter; check beforehand what is open and which rates apply. Bear the travel time in mind: for an hour of tennis it is a fair drive, so plan an afternoon of shopping or a visit to the town around it.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are there lessons for children?

    Lessons for children and beginners are usually available; ask the sports centre about current options.

    Can you also play tennis in winter?

    Yes, in the winter months there are tennis halls in St. Johann and nearby Erpfendorf.

    Do I need to book a court?

    It is sensible, especially in high season; ask the tourist office how.
