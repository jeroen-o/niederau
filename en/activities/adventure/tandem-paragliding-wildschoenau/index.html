Tandem paragliding from the Markbachjoch

A tandem flight with a pilot over the Wildschönau

The Wildschönau is regarded as a good area for paragliding, partly because the valley is sheltered from strong wind. The take-off is next to the top station of the Markbachjochbahn in Niederau. One operator, Fly2 Tandemflights, runs tandem flights with experienced pilots.

You are strapped to a pilot and descend roughly 600 m in height from about 1,500 m. The flight itself lasts several minutes, the whole activity a little over half an hour to three quarters of an hour. Flights depend on wind and weather, so a back-up date is often needed. Age and weight limits vary by operator; ask beforehand. The view over the Kitzbühel Alps is the big reward.

Official website

  • DurationAbout 45 min in total, flight a few minutes
  • CostAround € 120 per person; ask for the current price
  • DistanceIn Niederau itself
  • SeasonSummer, weather permitting
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Book in advance and keep a second day free in case the weather does not cooperate.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and layered clothing.
  • Ask beforehand about age and weight limits and whether photos or video are possible.

Practical

The take-off is at the top station of the Markbachjochbahn in Niederau, in the village itself and so only minutes from where you stay. The cable car runs in summer from May to October.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you take off for paragliding in Niederau?

Next to the top station of the Markbachjochbahn, at about 1,500 m.
