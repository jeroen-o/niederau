    Monsterroller and MountainCart on the Spieljoch

    Rolling down the mountain with a guide and helmet near Fügen

    In summer you can ride down the Spieljoch above Fügen in the Zillertal on a Monsterroller or a MountainCart. A Monsterroller is a sturdy scooter with fat, air-filled tyres; a MountainCart is a seated cart with its own steering and brakes. You follow a guide, wearing a helmet, along the forest road from the top station to the middle station. The descent is about 6.5 kilometres long.

    The ride is meant for adults and young people from 10 years of age, depending on body height. The number of vehicles is limited, so book in advance. On some tours you can carry on to the valley station. It is a fun addition to a hike or a day in the Zillertal when you travel with teenagers or an active group.

    Place: Fügen

    Tips

    • Book in advance; the number of vehicles is limited.
    • Wear sturdy shoes and clothes that can get dirty.
    • Check the Spieljochbahn website for current prices, times and the age and height rules.
    • Combine the ride with a hike or lunch on the mountain.

    Practical

    Fügen is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, the A12 and the Zillertal exit (Strass). The Spieljochbahn takes you to the top station, where the guided ride of about 6.5 km to the middle station starts. In 2026 the cable car runs from late May to late October and the descent leaves daily from about 11:00 a.m. The Monsterroller is for one person; the MountainCart seats two. On the BigTour you carry on to the valley station at the end of the day. 2026 prices according to the operator: Monsterroller € 20, MountainCart € 30, BigTour € 32, plus a one-off € 3 for equipment. Prices, times and season can change from year to year; check them and book in advance with the Spieljochbahn, as the number of vehicles is limited.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    From what age can you take part?

    From about 10 years, depending on body height. Check the current rules with the Spieljochbahn.

    What is the difference between a Monsterroller and a MountainCart?

    The Monsterroller is a scooter with fat tyres that you stand on; the MountainCart is a seated cart with steering and brakes.

    What does a Monsterroller or MountainCart cost?

    For 2026 the operator lists € 20 for the Monsterroller, € 30 for the MountainCart (for two people) and € 32 for the BigTour, plus a one-off € 3 equipment fee. Prices can change; check with the Spieljochbahn.
