Adventure in Tyrol for families · Winter

Schatzberg cable car in winter

Views over the Wildschönau from the top station

The Schatzbergbahn in Auffach is the gondola to the Schatzberg ski area, which belongs to Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau. Non-skiers can in principle take a look too, but ask beforehand whether a pedestrian ticket exists and which station you can use. The top station is at just over 1,700 metres; some sources give a slightly different height.

Up there you have views over the valley and surrounding peaks, and mountain restaurants for a hot drink. In clear weather it is a calm outing for families with young children or for a day without skis. Bear in mind that it is clearly colder at the top than in Niederau.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1 to 2 hours
  • CostFares vary; ask in advance
  • DistanceAbout 10 to 15 minutes by car
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationAuffach, Schatzberg

Tips

  • Pick a clear day for the best views.
  • Bring warm clothing, a hat and sunglasses.
  • Ask beforehand about the pedestrian fare and how far you may go.
  • Plan a stop at a mountain restaurant for a hot drink.

Practical

Auffach is about 10 to 15 minutes by car from Niederau. Public transport usually runs within the valley; ask Wildschönau Tourism or Ski Juwel for the current timetable.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you go up without skis?

This is often possible with a pedestrian ticket, but confirm with Ski Juwel beforehand.
