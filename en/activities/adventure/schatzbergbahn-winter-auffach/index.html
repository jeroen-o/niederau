Schatzberg cable car in winter

Views over the Wildschönau from the top station

The Schatzbergbahn in Auffach is the gondola to the Schatzberg ski area, part of Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau. The modern gondola takes skiers from the valley to the top in roughly a quarter of an hour; the top station is at just over 1,770 metres. In winter 2025/26 it ran daily from about 8:30 am to 4:30 pm.

If you do not ski, it is best to ask beforehand whether a pedestrian ticket exists and which station you may ride to. Up there you have views over the valley and peaks, and mountain restaurants for a hot drink. It is clearly colder at the top than in Niederau.

Place: Auffach

Tips

  • Pick a clear day for the best views.
  • Bring warm clothing, a hat and sunglasses.
  • Ask beforehand about the pedestrian fare and how far you may go.
  • Plan a stop at a mountain restaurant for a hot drink.

Practical

Auffach is about 10 to 15 minutes by car from Niederau. Ask Wildschönau Tourism or Ski Juwel for the current timetable, fares and any ski bus.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you go up without skis?

That is not certain; ask Ski Juwel beforehand whether a pedestrian ticket is available.
