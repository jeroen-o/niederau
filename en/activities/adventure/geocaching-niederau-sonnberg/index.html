Geocaching round on the Niederauer Sonnberg

Walking and treasure hunting with GPS in Niederau

Near Niederau there is a geocaching walk over the Niederauer Sonnberg. The round is about 8.5 km long, has nine stations and takes roughly three hours. Geocaching is a kind of modern treasure hunt: with an app or GPS device you look for hidden containers.

So you combine walking with puzzles and searching. That works well for children who quickly get bored on an ordinary walk, since there is always a goal. For the caches themselves you use the Geocaching app or a GPS device; for the route, the description from the tourist office is handy. Plan on a walk with sturdy shoes and enough to drink.

Official website

Tips

  • Download the app and the route beforehand so you do not depend on reception en route.
  • Bring a pen to sign the logbook in a cache.
  • Let the children handle the map or app; it makes the trip more fun.

Practical

The round starts in or near Niederau; from the village it is a matter of a few minutes. Ask the tourist office for the starting point and the current route description.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
