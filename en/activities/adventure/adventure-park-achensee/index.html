Adventure in Tyrol for families · Summer

High ropes course at Achensee

Treetop climbing courses by Lake Achensee

The Abenteuerpark Achensee is in Achenkirch, on the road along Lake Achensee at the Achenseehof boat landing. It has seven courses of varying difficulty at heights of roughly 2 to 15 metres. You cross bridges and nets, balance along swinging logs and finish with a flying fox.

Climbing is not all: there is also a slackline course, OneWheel (a self-balancing electric all-terrain board), archery, a playground and an outdoor escape game. That makes it a good choice for families with children of different ages, for instance as part of a day at the lake. Note that the park is in Achenkirch, not Pertisau.

Official website

  • DistanceAbout 1 hour by car
  • SeasonSpring to autumn; hours vary by season
  • LocationAchenkirch, Lake Achensee

Tips

  • Ask beforehand about the minimum height or age for each course.
  • Wear sturdy, closed shoes and clothes you can move in.
  • Combine the visit with a boat trip or a walk along Lake Achensee.
  • Check opening times in advance; they depend on season and weather.

Practical

From Niederau it is about an hour by car via Wörgl and the A12 (Jenbach exit) towards Lake Achensee and Achenkirch. Check current details with the park or the regional tourist office.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the climbing park in Pertisau or Achenkirch?

The Abenteuerpark Achensee is in Achenkirch, on the main road along the lake between Maurach and Achenkirch.
