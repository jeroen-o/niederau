    Minigolf near Kirchdorf (St. Johann)

    A relaxed round of mini golf for the whole family

    At Hotel Neuwirt in Kirchdorf in Tirol, in the St. Johann region, there is a twelve-hole minigolf course. It is an ordinary course open to everyone, including people who are not staying at the hotel.

    Minigolf is a relaxed break: a short, sporty contest that young and old can join without any experience or equipment of their own. Equipment is lent out on site. Allow about an hour, and combine it with an ice cream or a cup of coffee. It is a good option for an afternoon when you do not want to drive far or when the children need to burn off some energy.

    Place: Kirchdorf in Tirol

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationAbout 1 hour
    • CostAround € 3 to € 5 per person; check beforehand
    • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
    • SeasonSummer
    • LocationKirchdorf in Tirol, St. Johann region

    Tips

    • Check beforehand whether the course is open and whether you need to book.
    • Keep score; it makes it extra exciting for children.
    • Combine it with a visit to St. Johann in Tirol or an ice cream afterwards.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about a 50-minute drive to Kirchdorf in Tirol, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel. Ask the hotel or the tourist office about opening times beforehand.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many holes does the course have?

    The course at Hotel Neuwirt in Kirchdorf in Tirol has twelve holes and is also open to non-guests.
