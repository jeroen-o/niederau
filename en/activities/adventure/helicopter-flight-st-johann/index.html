Helicopter sightseeing flight from St. Johann

Mountains and lakes from the air, departing near Erpfendorf

Scenic helicopter flights depart from the heliport at Erpfendorf, close to St. Johann in Tirol. One operator in the region is Heli Austria.

On a flight you see the Kitzbühel Alps, the Wilder Kaiser and the surrounding valleys and lakes from above. Flights usually last between half an hour and an hour and are often offered as a charter for a small group, so the cost per flight can be high. It is therefore more of a special-occasion treat than a standard family outing. Flights and times depend on the weather; ask in advance about price, duration and age limits.

Place: St. Johann in Tirol

  • DurationAbout 30 to 60 min flight
  • CostCostly; price per flight is high, check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car
  • SeasonMainly summer, weather permitting
  • LocationErpfendorf / St. Johann in Tirol

Tips

  • Ask beforehand for the total price per flight and how many people can join.
  • Book well ahead and allow for a back-up date.
  • Bring a camera, but ask about the rules for taking photos on board.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 50 minutes by car to St. Johann in Tirol and the heliport at Erpfendorf. Book beforehand; flights without an appointment are not usual.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
