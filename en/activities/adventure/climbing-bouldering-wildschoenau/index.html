Boulder wall on the Wirbelweg

A first climbing experience for children on the play trail near Niederau

Along the Wirbelweg, the themed family trail near Niederau, there is a large climbing wall with bouldering holds at the second stop halfway. Children can climb at low height and train their motor skills, while parents rest with a view of Niederau. You need no experience or rope.

Want more height? The Wildschönau also has a high ropes course, the Zauberwinkl Hochseilgarten, with a minimum height of 1.40 m; children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult. For current opening times and conditions, ask the tourist office in Niederau, and in warm weather choose the cooler hours of the day.

Tips

  • Combine the wall with the rest of the Wirbelweg.
  • Wear sturdy shoes; climbing shoes are not needed.
  • In warm weather choose the cooler hours.
  • The high ropes course has a minimum height of 1.40 m.

Practical

The Wirbelweg is close to Niederau, a short distance from the village. Ask the tourist office about the current route and the exact spot of the wall.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need experience to climb?

No, the wall has bouldering holds at low height and is meant for children to practise.
