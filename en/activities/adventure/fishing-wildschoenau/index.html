    Fishing in the Wildschönau

    Fishing for trout and char in the Wildschönauer Ache

    In the Wildschönau you can fish for trout and char in summer. There are two fishing waters: the Wildschönauer Ache, from Schönanger to the Kundler Klamm, and the Wörgler Bach, from Oberau to the Wörgler Berg. Fishing is possible from 1 May to 1 October.

    As a visitor you need a Tyrolean guest fishing card, valid for fourteen days, plus a day licence for the water you choose. The day licence is issued by the manager of the fishing water; ask at the municipal office or tourist office where to get one. This is not an activity for small children: it takes patience and quiet. For older children and teenagers it can be a calm, special experience in beautiful surroundings.

    Tips

    • Arrange the guest card and day licence before you go; you may not fish without the papers.
    • Early morning or evening are often the best times.
    • Wear sturdy shoes or boots; the banks can be wet and slippery.

    Practical

    The waters are in the valley itself and can be reached from Niederau by car in a few minutes up to about 15 minutes, depending on the stretch you choose. Licences can be arranged at the municipal or tourist office.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    When can you fish in the Wildschönau?

    From 1 May to 1 October, with a Tyrolean guest fishing card and a day licence.
