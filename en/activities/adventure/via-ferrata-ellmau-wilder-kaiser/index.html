    Via ferratas near Ellmau (Wilder Kaiser)

    From practice route to sporty: via ferratas in the Kaiser mountains

    Around Ellmau in the Wilder Kaiser there are several via ferratas, each with its own level of difficulty. Examples include the practice via ferrata at the Gaudeamushütte (around B/C), the Jubiläumssteig (around A/B) and the short, sporty Klamml via ferrata (D).

    The practice route is a good way to get to know the equipment and technique before choosing a longer route. For children and beginners a guide or a course is strongly recommended; safety comes first. You climb along cables, iron rungs and ladders on the limestone walls, with views across the Kaiser mountains. Always check the weather, the route and your own fitness before setting off.

    Place: Ellmau

    • AgeTeenagers and adults; children only with a guide and experience
    • DurationAbout 3 to 5 hours, depending on the route
    • CostEquipment hire and guide: check beforehand
    • DistanceAbout 40 min by car
    • SeasonSummer, in dry weather
    • LocationEllmau, Wilder Kaiser

    Tips

    • Start with the practice route before choosing a harder one.
    • Helmet, harness and via ferrata set are essential; rent them from a sports shop or through a guide.
    • Only go in dry, stable weather and, if in doubt, go with a mountain guide.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about 40 minutes by car to Ellmau, via Wörgl and Söll. From the village, trails lead to the various huts and climbing routes.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which via ferrata near Ellmau suits beginners?

    The practice via ferrata at the Gaudeamushütte is meant for getting to know the technique; ideally go with a guide.
