    Adventure in Tyrol for families · Summer

    Rafting on the Ziller

    By rubber raft with a guide down the river

    On the Ziller near Mayrhofen, rafting companies run trips in summer, including Actionclub Zillertal, the Freiluftakademie and Mountain Sports. You travel in a rubber raft with a qualified guide and work together as a team; the well-known stretch runs from Kreuzach near Mayrhofen to Hippach. Water level and difficulty depend on the season and the weather.

    The river is regarded as suitable for beginners and families and reaches grade 2+. The stretch is about 4 to 6 km long; including preparation, allow about two to two and a half hours, of which roughly one hour is on the water. The equipment is usually included.

    There are gentler family trips, usually from about 6 years, and livelier ones with a higher minimum age, roughly 10 to 12 years. You will almost certainly get wet. Book ahead with the provider and read the age and health conditions. If you are unsure, a gentle family trip is a fine introduction to rafting. You get instructions beforehand on paddling, safety and behaviour in the boat, and the guide sets the line. That lets parents with children enjoy the water in a relaxed way too.

    Tips

    • Book ahead and ask about the minimum age of the chosen trip.
    • Bring a towel and dry clothes.
    • Water levels are often higher in spring and early summer.
    • Wear clothing that can get wet and shoes that stay on.
    • Combine the trip with a walk or lunch in Mayrhofen.

    Practical

    From Niederau it is about 50 minutes by car to Mayrhofen in the Zillertal, where the trips start. The starting point lies between Mayrhofen and Hippach; the provider will tell you exactly where to be when you book. Including the journey there and back, allow half a day, and bring dry clothes for the drive home. A life jacket and helmet are usually provided by the operator.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    From what age can you raft on the Ziller?

    Providers usually offer family trips from about 6 years; more sporty trips have a higher minimum age.

    How long does a rafting trip on the Ziller take?

    About two to two and a half hours in total, of which roughly one hour is on the water.

    Is rafting on the Ziller difficult?

    No, the Ziller is regarded as suitable for beginners and families and reaches grade 2+.
