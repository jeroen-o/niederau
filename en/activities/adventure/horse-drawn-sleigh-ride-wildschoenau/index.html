    Horse-drawn sleigh ride in the Wildschönau

    Wrapped in blankets through the snow, pulled by horses

    In winter you can take a horse-drawn sleigh ride in the Wildschönau. You sit under a blanket while the horses pull you calmly past snowy meadows and through woods. It is a relaxed way to see the winter landscape, even if you do not ski.

    A well-known operator is Ossi Hausberger at the Jausenstation Foisching, above Niederau. According to tourist information he runs rides only in winter, every day except Monday and Tuesday, for groups of two or more and by appointment only. For special occasions or groups other rides are possible on request. At Foisching you will also find a cosy hut with a sun terrace and a deer park.

    • AgeAsk when booking
    • DurationVaries by operator
    • CostPrices vary; check in advance
    • DistanceNear Niederau (about 5 min by car)
    • SeasonWinter; not Mon and Tue
    • LocationFoisching, Niederau

    Tips

    • Dress warmly: you sit still and it can get chilly.
    • Book ahead; by appointment only.
    • Ask about the duration and route when booking.
    • Combine the ride with a visit to the deer park at Foisching.

    Practical

    The ride starts at the Jausenstation Foisching, above the Grafenweg area of Niederau; the hut is about half an hour's walk above the village. Book ahead with the operator or via your accommodation or the tourist office.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which days do the sleigh rides run?

    At Jausenstation Foisching rides run daily in winter except Monday and Tuesday, by appointment only and for two or more people.
