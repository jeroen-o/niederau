Pony and horse riding in the region

Haflingers and ponies on the alpine pastures for young and old

Haflingers are a well-known Tyrolean horse breed: compact, strong and usually calm by nature, which makes them suitable for beginners and children. According to the source there are riding options around St. Johann and the Wildschönau, from short pony rides for toddlers to longer rides on horseback or by carriage for adults.

Which operators exist and on what terms has not been checked. Ask the tourist office about riding schools and studs nearby and book beforehand. Ponies are too small for adults; children often ride under supervision, led by hand. If you like, combine the outing with a visit to a farm so the children also learn more about the animals.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1 to 3 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 15 to 35 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationSt. Johann and Wildschönau area

Tips

  • Book beforehand through the tourist office or the operator.
  • Wear closed shoes and long trousers.
  • Combine with a farm visit.

Practical

The offer is around St. Johann and the Wildschönau, about 15 to 35 minutes by car from Niederau. Ask the tourist office for the nearest operator.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
