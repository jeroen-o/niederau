    Adventure in Tyrol for families · Summer

    Pony and horse riding in the region

    Haflingers and ponies on the alpine pastures for young and old

    Haflingers are a well-known Tyrolean horse breed: compact, strong and usually calm by nature, which makes them suitable for beginners and children. In the area around St. Johann and the Wildschönau you can go riding, from short pony rides for toddlers to longer rides on horseback or by carriage for adults.

    Ask the tourist office about riding stables and stud farms nearby and book in advance. Ponies are too small for adults; children are often led by hand under supervision. For a first time a short ride is the wisest choice: it lets children get used to the animal, the saddle and the rhythm of walking without it becoming too much.

    If you like, combine the outing with a visit to a farm, so the children also learn more about the animals. When planning, take the weather and the desired length of the ride into account. If you want to ride yourself, ask the provider about options for beginners. Keep in mind that the animals need calm: ask children to speak softly and to check first whether they may pat the horse. A good provider explains the basic rules before you mount. That makes it a pleasant experience for child and animal alike.

    Tips

    • Book beforehand through the tourist office or the operator.
    • Wear closed shoes and long trousers.
    • Combine with a farm visit.
    • Start with a short ride for children who have never sat on a pony.
    • Bring a rain jacket; outdoor rides may also go ahead in changeable weather, depending on the provider.

    Practical

    Riding options are found around St. Johann and the Wildschönau, about 15 to 35 minutes' drive from Niederau. The distance depends on the provider you choose. Ask the tourist office for the nearest provider, for age limits and for the length of the rides, and preferably book ahead, especially in the holiday period. Allow one to three hours per ride, including getting to know the animal.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a Haflinger?

    A compact, strong and calm horse breed from Tyrol, suitable for beginners.

    Can adults ride a pony?

    No, ponies are too small for that; adults choose a horse or a carriage ride.

    Do you need to book in advance?

    It is sensible to do so; ask the tourist office or the provider about available times.
