Sunset at the Schatzberg

Winter evening colours above Auffach

On clear winter days the mountains around the Schatzberg turn pink, orange and purple in the late afternoon. If you want to see it, you can take the Schatzbergbahn up from Auffach and watch the sun go down from the top. It is quiet, especially once the skiers have headed back down.

In winter the cable car keeps set opening hours and does not necessarily run until after sunset. Ask beforehand whether a late ride is possible and when the last descent leaves. Allow roughly an hour and a half to two hours, including waiting time at the top. It gets cold quickly after sunset.

Place: Auffach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1.5 to 2 hours
  • CostPrices vary; check in advance
  • DistanceApprox. 15 min by car
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationSchatzberg, Auffach

Tips

  • Pick a clear day; there is little to see under cloud.
  • Look up the sunset time beforehand.
  • Bring a flask of hot tea and extra warm clothing.
  • Check the last descent time before going up.

Practical

The Schatzbergbahn starts in Auffach, roughly 15 minutes by car from Niederau. Ask in advance about the lift's operating hours and the last ride down.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Schatzbergbahn run until after sunset?

That is not certain; winter operating hours are limited. Ask about the last ride beforehand.
