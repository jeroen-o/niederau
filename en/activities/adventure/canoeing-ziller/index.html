Canoeing in the Zillertal

Calm paddling on the river in the wide valley

The Ziller flows through the Zillertal valley and joins the Inn near Strass. A guided canoe trip on the calmer stretches suits beginners: small rapids, shallow spots and plenty of nature. It is a gentle way to get to know paddling without it becoming too exciting straight away.

The trip is especially enjoyable for children from about 10 years old and for adults. You will probably get wet, so bring swimwear and dry clothes. The broad valley offers views of the mountains and the greenery along the banks. For current providers, starting points and times, the Zillertal tourist office is a good point of contact.

Place: Zell am Ziller

  • AgeFrom about 10 years (ask)
  • DurationAbout 2 to 3 hours
  • CostPrice varies; ask in advance
  • DistanceAbout 60 minutes by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationZillertal

Tips

  • Bring dry clothes and a towel.
  • Ask about swimming ability and age.
  • Choose a day with stable weather.

Practical

The Zillertal is about an hour's drive from Niederau, via the A12 and the Zillertal road. Ask the tourist office about providers and the starting point.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is canoeing on the Ziller suitable for beginners?

On the calmer stretches with a guide it is; ask about the difficulty of the trip.
