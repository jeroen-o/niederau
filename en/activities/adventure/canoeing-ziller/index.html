Canoeing in the Zillertal

Calm paddling on the river in the wide valley

The Ziller flows through the Zillertal valley and joins the Inn near Strass. A guided canoe trip on the calmer stretches suits beginners: small rapids, shallow spots and plenty of nature. It is a gentle way to get to know paddling without it becoming too exciting straight away.

The trip is especially enjoyable for children from about 10 years old and for adults. You will probably get wet, so bring swimwear and dry clothes. The broad valley offers views of the mountains and the greenery along the banks. For current providers, starting points and times, the Zillertal tourist office is a good point of contact.

If you prefer a bit more excitement, the Zillertal also offers rafting trips on the Ziller. A beginners' trip near Mayrhofen is about 4 km long and takes roughly 2.5 hours in total, of which about an hour is on the water. You need to be able to swim, so you can choose calmly between a gentle canoe trip and a livelier raft trip. Plan the trip for a dry, calm day and take it easy at the start: a first time paddling mainly takes some getting used to the water. Bear in mind the wind and the water level, which differ from day to day, and always follow the guide's instructions. That makes it a relaxed and safe introduction to the river.

Tips

  • Bring dry clothes and a towel.
  • Ask about swimming ability and age.
  • Choose a day with stable weather.
  • Ask whether a helmet and life jacket are included.
  • Wear shoes that can get wet and stay firmly on.

Practical

The Zillertal is about an hour's drive from Niederau, via the A12 and the Zillertal road. The Ziller joins the Inn at Strass, close to the motorway; further up the valley lies Mayrhofen, where rafting trips start. Ask the Zillertal tourist office about providers, the starting point and times, and allow a good part of a day for the return drive. Before leaving also look at the weather and the water level, as these decide whether a trip goes ahead.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is canoeing on the Ziller suitable for beginners?

On the calmer stretches with a guide it is; ask about the difficulty of the trip.

Can I also go rafting in the Zillertal?

Yes, for example near Mayrhofen: a beginners' trip of about 4 km on the Ziller, suitable for families; you need to be able to swim.

Where does the Ziller flow into?

Into the Inn at Strass, at the entrance to the Zillertal.
