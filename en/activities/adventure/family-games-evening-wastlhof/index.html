    Games evening at Hotel Wastlhof

    Board games and puzzles for the whole family

    Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau, a partner of this site, is a welcoming place for a family evening of board games, puzzles and Tyrolean village games. Such an evening is a calm way to end the day, especially when it rains outside or the children are tired from hiking.

    Frequency, time and conditions can vary by season; the reception can tell you whether an evening is planned and whether non-guests can join in. There is usually plenty to do for all ages, from simple card games for young children to larger puzzles and strategy games for parents and teenagers.

    The nice thing about a games evening is that no screens are involved: parents and children sit at the same table and get to know each other's style of play. Start with a simple game so everyone can join in, then move on to something more challenging. A hot drink makes it cosy. For a rainy day in Niederau this is an easy choice: there is nowhere to drive to and you can adapt the programme to the mood of the group. Let the children pick a game themselves too, so they feel involved.

    Tips

    • Ask reception when the evening takes place.
    • Ideal as a rainy-day activity.
    • Order a warm drink to go with it.
    • Start with a simple game so the youngest children can join in.
    • Bring a favourite game of your own if you have a particular one in mind.

    Practical

    The evening takes place at Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau, in the village itself. So there is no driving involved, and afterwards you can simply walk back to your home or holiday address. Ask the hotel about date, time and participation, especially if you are not a hotel guest, and get the youngest children to bed on time. Also ask at reception which games are available.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the games evening open to non-guests?

    Ask the reception of Hotel Wastlhof whether non-guests can join in.

    Which games are played?

    Board games, puzzles and Tyrolean village games, from simple card games to larger strategy games.

    Is the games evening suitable for young children?

    Yes, there is usually something for all ages, including simple card games for young children.
