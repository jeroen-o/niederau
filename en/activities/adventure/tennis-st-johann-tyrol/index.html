Tennis in St. Johann in Tirol

Tennis on outdoor and indoor courts in and around St. Johann

St. Johann in Tirol, about half an hour from Niederau, offers several places to play tennis, on both outdoor and indoor courts. In summer you play outdoors with the Wilder Kaiser and the Kitzbüheler Horn as a backdrop, a pleasant setting for a game with the whole family.

In the winter months there are tennis halls in St. Johann and Erpfendorf. Elsewhere in the region too, for instance at the Golf & Countryclub Lärchenhof and the Panorama Badewelt, you will find tennis courts. See the St. Johann tourist office page for current locations, and ask beforehand whether booking is needed and whether equipment can be hired.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • Reserve a court in advance.

Practical

St. Johann is about 30 to 35 minutes by car from Niederau. Ask in advance about location, availability and rates.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you play tennis there in winter?

Yes, in the winter months there are tennis halls in St. Johann and Erpfendorf; book beforehand.
