Torchlight walk in the Wildschönau

Evening winter walk by torchlight in the Wildschönau

In winter the Wildschönau puts guided torchlight walks on its programme. One example follows the Franziskusweg: it starts at the z'Bach mountain farming museum and ends in the centre of Oberau. There is also a "mystical torchlight walk", which in one winter season left on Wednesday evenings from the entrance of the Drachental family park.

The Franziskusweg is a contemplative path of about 1.6 kilometres between Niederau and Oberau, with nine bronze figures, and it is lit at night. From Christmas until 2 February, illuminated nativity scenes by the local nativity society line the route. Dates, times and conditions differ per walk; ask Wildschönau Tourism.

  • DistanceAbout 5 to 10 minutes by car

  • LocationWildschönau (including Oberau)

Tips

  • Dress warmly and bring gloves and a hat.
  • Bring a headlamp if one is requested.
  • Ask Wildschönau Tourism about day, time and minimum age.
  • Sturdy shoes with grip matter on snow and ice.

Practical

Starting points and times differ per walk; from Niederau you can usually reach the start in about 5 to 10 minutes by car or bus. Current dates and any registration via Wildschönau Tourism.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the Franziskusweg torchlight walk start?

At the z'Bach mountain farming museum; the route ends in the centre of Oberau. Ask for current dates.
