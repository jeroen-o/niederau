Adventure in Tyrol for families · Summer

Schatzberg cable car in summer

Gondola from Auffach into the hiking area

The Schatzbergbahn starts in Auffach and takes you up in a little over a quarter of an hour; halfway lies the middle station at Berghaus Koglmoos. The top station is at just over 1,770 metres. Up there is a large hiking area with mountain restaurants and huts such as the Schatzbergalm, and views towards peaks like the Gerngipfel and the Joelspitze.

For families it is a practical way to gain height without a long climb. At Koglmoos there is a water playground with a big slide and a trampoline, plus a goat enclosure and rabbits. From the middle station an easy route of about 4.5 km (about 1.5 hours) leads through the forest back down to Auffach, and there is an easy circuit to the small reservoir above the Gernalm. Those who want to go higher can choose a summit tour.

In 2026 the summer season runs from 4 June to 4 October; check the timetable and the weather beforehand. It is an outing where you set the pace yourself: a short stroll at the middle station for the little ones, or a longer route to the huts and summits for those used to altitude. Fine weather makes the view over the Wildschönau valley especially worthwhile.

Place: Auffach

Tips

  • Check the summer timetable and the weather beforehand.
  • Take a warm layer; it is cooler at the top than in the valley.
  • Children enjoy the water playground at Koglmoos.
  • You can rest at the Schatzbergalm or at Koglmoos.
  • Walk back to Auffach from the middle station if you want an easy descent.

Practical

Auffach is about 10 to 15 minutes by car from Niederau; the valley station is in the village. In summer the gondola runs daily throughout the season, with a last ascent in the late afternoon; plan your return around that. Prices and timetable are available from Ski Juwel or Wildschönau Tourism. The valley station is in the village, so you reach the lift without a long drive. In uncertain weather it is better to come earlier in the day, as the mountains sometimes cloud over towards midday.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
