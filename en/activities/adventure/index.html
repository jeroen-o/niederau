Adventure in Tyrol for families

Climbing parks, rafting, canyoning and other active outings in the region.

Those travelling with older children or teenagers often want something more exciting than a walk. In and around Tyrol there is plenty: climbing parks in the trees, via ferrata-style routes, rafting and canyoning, zip-lines and other outdoor activities. Many providers explicitly target families and have courses for different ages.

From Niederau some activities are close, while others need a drive of half an hour to an hour. Rivers in the area, such as the Inn and the Ziller, offer rafting possibilities, and there are climbing and adventure parks in the hills. The activities are usually run under supervision of trained guides who provide equipment and instruction.

Mind the minimum age, height and fitness that apply to each activity, and book in advance, especially in high season. Weather plays a big role: in rain or high water activities may be cancelled. This page offers a general guide with practical tips; the individual adventures are described below.

Tips

  • Book in advance, especially in high season.
  • Check minimum age and height for each activity.
  • Bring spare clothes and a towel.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and no loose jewellery.
  • Have a backup plan for bad weather.

When and for whom?

Adventure activities are generally available from May to September, depending on provider and weather. Many providers set a minimum age and sometimes a minimum height or weight; check this in advance. For children who like a challenge, climbing parks are a good start, while rafting and canyoning generally need more nerve and fitness.

Safety and preparation

Choose a recognised provider that works with certified guides and well-maintained equipment. Listen carefully to the instructions, wear the supplied helmet and harness and do not overestimate yourself. Wear suitable clothing and shoes and mind your health: if in doubt about fitness, pregnancy or medical conditions, consult the provider beforehand.

Frequently asked questions

From what age can children take part?

It varies by activity. Climbing parks often have courses for young children, while rafting and canyoning have a higher minimum age. Ask in advance.

Do I need to book in advance?

Recommended, especially in July and August. Some providers use fixed start times.

What happens in bad weather?

In a thunderstorm, high water or heavy rain activities may be cancelled. Ask about cancellation terms before booking.
