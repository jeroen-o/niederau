Overnight stay in a mountain hut

A night in a self-catering hut on the Markbachjoch

On the Markbachjoch above Niederau stands the Markbachjochhütte, a mountain hut where you can stay overnight. It is a self-catering hut with space for about 30 people, four dormitories with 2 to 14 beds, washrooms with toilet and shower, and a dining room with a wood pellet stove. The walk up takes about an hour and a half, or you can take part of the way by gondola.

Because it is self-catering, you organise food and drink yourself, so bring groceries. For families with somewhat older children it is an adventurous night high above the valley. Book well ahead and ask about conditions, price and what to bring yourself, such as bed linen. On the Markbachjoch there are also places to eat such as the Norderberg Alm, which are mainly open during the day.

Tips

  • Bring food, drink and a headlamp.
  • Pack warm clothing; nights are cold.
  • Book well ahead and ask what to bring yourself.
  • Check the gondola times before you set off.

Practical

The Markbachjoch can be reached from Niederau by gondola or on foot; the walk to the hut takes about an hour and a half. Ask about booking and conditions.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have to bring your own food?

Yes, the hut is self-catering; bring food and drink.
