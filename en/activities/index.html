Activities in and around Niederau

Everything to do with the family, summer and winter, around the Wildschönau.

Niederau lies in the heart of the Wildschönau, at a comfortable distance from the Inn Valley, the Alpbachtal and the Kitzbühel Alps. That makes it a good base for a family holiday with variety: a day of walking or cycling, an afternoon at a mountain lake, a trip to an animal park or, in winter, skiing and sledging.

On this page you will find the activities arranged by theme, from hiking and swimming to adventure and animals. Each theme starts with a general guide with practical tips, followed by the individual outings with an indication of the distance from Niederau. Always check the season, the weather and current opening times before setting off.
