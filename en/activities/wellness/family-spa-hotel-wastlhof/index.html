Spa and wellness at Hotel Wastlhof

Warm up and relax after a day in the cold

Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau is a partner of this site and stands right beside the Markbachjochbahn and the ski slope. The hotel has a wellness area with an indoor pool, several saunas, a steam bath, an infrared cabin, an ice cave and a whirlpool. Massages and beauty treatments are available for an extra charge. After a day of skiing or walking it is a pleasant way to finish, with no more driving needed.

Who may use the wellness area, what rules apply to children and at what times can vary, so this page names no fixed prices or times. Ask reception about the current options, the rules for children and any family arrangements. For guests staying several days it also makes an easy rest day between more active ones.

Tips

  • Ask about the rules for children in the wellness area.
  • Drink enough water before and after the sauna.
  • Bring swimwear and flip-flops.
  • Go in a quiet hour for more space.

Practical

The spa is inside the hotel in Niederau, so no transport is needed. Contact the hotel for access and conditions.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
