Spa and wellness at Hotel Wastlhof

Warm up and relax after a day in the cold

Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau is a partner of this site and has a wellness area for guests. After a day of skiing or walking in the cold, an hour of relaxation is a pleasant way to finish, for parents and often for children too.

Which facilities exist exactly, who may use them and when children are welcome varies, so this page names no fixed prices or times. Ask reception about current options and any family arrangements. Quieter moments are usually outside the busiest hours; ask the hotel.

Tips

  • Ask about the rules for children in the wellness area.
  • Drink enough water before and after the sauna.
  • Bring swimwear and flip-flops.
  • Go in a quiet hour for more space.

Practical

The spa is inside the hotel in Niederau, so no transport is needed. Contact the hotel for access and conditions.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
