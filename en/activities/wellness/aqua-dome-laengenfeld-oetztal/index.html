Aqua Dome Längenfeld

Thermal spa in the Ötztal with outdoor pools

The Aqua Dome in Längenfeld is a thermal spa resort in the Ötztal, known for its bowl-shaped outdoor pools with mountain views. It has indoor and outdoor pools and a sauna area, which makes it a good rest day after an active week. According to visitor information it is open daily, into the evening.

Children have their own family and kids' area, the Alpen Arche Noah, in a separate part of the spa, with among other things a toddler pool, slides and a children's water park, plus supervised kids' entertainment at set times. A visit easily lasts half a day or a full day.

Place: Längenfeld

  • AgeAll ages; separate kids' area
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostAdmission is charged; please enquire
  • DistanceAbout 1.5 hours by car
  • SeasonYear-round
  • LocationLängenfeld, Ötztal

Tips

  • Check how busy it is beforehand, especially at weekends and in holidays.
  • Bring swimwear, flip-flops and a bathrobe, or ask about rental.
  • An evening visit with a view from the outdoor pool is special.
  • Check the sauna and children's age rules on the website.

Practical

Längenfeld is about 1.5 hours by car from Niederau, via the A12 and the Ötztal. Check current opening times, prices and parking beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Aqua Dome suitable for children?

Yes, there is a separate family and kids' area (Alpen Arche Noah) with a toddler pool, slides and kids' entertainment. Check the rules per area, such as saunas, on the website.
