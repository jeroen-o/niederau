Patscherkofel Alpine Garden

University botanical alpine garden above Innsbruck-Igls

The Alpengarten near the top station of the Patscherkofelbahn belongs to the University of Innsbruck and is regarded as Austria's highest-altitude botanical garden. On roughly 1.5 hectares grow alpine plants from various mountain regions, including gentian, edelweiss and plants from the Himalayan area.

Entry to the garden is free; only the gondola ride up costs money. Early in summer the alpine roses bloom along the paths. You can keep the visit short or combine it with a walk on the Patscherkofel. A calm, worthwhile outing for families interested in nature.

Place: Innsbruck

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1.5 hours for the garden; gondola extra
  • CostGarden free; gondola costs extra
  • DistanceAbout 1 hour 10 by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationIgls, Innsbruck

Tips

  • Early summer is the best time for the alpine roses.
  • Let children look for plants and make it a game.
  • Take a jacket; it is cooler up top than in the valley.
  • Check the gondola's operating times beforehand.

Practical

Igls is near Innsbruck, about 1 hour 10 minutes by car from Niederau. The Patscherkofelbahn runs up from Igls; the garden is at the top station at over 1,900 m.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
