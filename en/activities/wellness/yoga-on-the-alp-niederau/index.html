Wellness and relaxation in the Wildschönau · Summer

Yoga on the alp

Stretch and breathe with a view over the valley

In summer, yoga is taught in the Wildschönau at the Vordere Feldalm, an alpine meadow at about 1,700 m with wide views. A yoga teacher runs classes regularly, usually on Thursdays, indoors or outdoors depending on the weather. There are versions with an alpine breakfast (yoga brunch) or a snack at sunset.

A class lasts about 75 minutes plus relaxation and a shared closing snack, in a small group (at most eight participants). You do breathing and balance exercises and classic poses such as sun salutations. You walk or cycle to the alp, or park at Horler Stigl and hike for a good hour. Ask about the age limit, dates and cost, and book in advance.

  • DurationAbout 75 minutes plus relaxation, plus the walk
  • CostPlease enquire in advance
  • DistanceAbout 1 hour on foot from Horler Stigl
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationVordere Feldalm, near Niederau

Tips

  • Dress in layers; it can be cool up there.
  • Bring a mat or ask about rental.
  • Book in advance; groups are small.
  • Keep your distance from livestock on the pasture.

Practical

The alp lies above Niederau in the Markbachjoch area. You reach it on foot or by e-bike, or park at Horler Stigl and walk for about an hour. Book beforehand and ask about current dates.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?

No, beginners can join; ask the provider about the age limit.
