Forest meditation

A quiet hour among the trees

In the woods around Niederau there is the chance to join a guided meditation in nature. You sit or stand in a quiet spot, focus on your breathing and listen to what the forest offers. It is a calm counterweight to a holiday full of action.

It mostly suits adults and teenagers who want something without performance pressure. The spot is close to the village, often after a short walk. Provider, day and conditions can change, so ask the tourist office or your hotel what is currently on offer. Wear comfortable clothes and put your phone on silent.

  • AgeAdults and teenagers
  • DurationAbout 1 hour
  • CostPlease enquire in advance
  • DistanceClose to Niederau
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationIn or close to Niederau

Tips

  • Wear comfortable clothes and sturdy shoes.
  • Put your phone on silent.
  • Bring a warm drink for afterwards.
  • Ask in advance about current options.

Practical

The starting point is close to Niederau, often after a short walk. Book through the tourist office or your hotel; ask about time and place.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for beginners?

Generally yes, no experience is usually needed; confirm with the provider.
