Summer kids' programme at Wastlhof

Play and craft activities for children at Hotel Wastlhof

Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau is a partner of this site and caters for families. In summer the hotel runs a children's programme with games, exercise and crafts, in and around the hotel.

Exact times, age groups and the weekly offer can change, so this page names no fixed days or prices. Ask reception on arrival for the current programme and whether Dutch-speaking staff are available. The programme is intended for guests staying at the hotel.

  • AgeChildren; ask about age groups
  • DurationVaries by activity
  • CostFor hotel guests; ask the hotel
  • DistanceOn site in Niederau
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationHotel Wastlhof, Niederau

Tips

  • Ask for the weekly programme on arrival.
  • Bring clothes and shoes that can get dirty.
  • Ask the hotel what the wet-weather alternative is.
  • Register your children in good time at reception.

Practical

The programme takes place at Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau itself, so no transport is needed. Contact the hotel for details.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the programme open to non-guests?

It is intended for hotel guests. Ask the hotel whether exceptions are possible.
