Animals and nature for children around Niederau

Farm animals, wildlife parks and zoos in and around the Wildschönau.

Children and animals make a good combination on holiday. In the Wildschönau and surroundings you meet animals in different ways: cows, sheep and goats on the mountain pastures, horses and ponies at riding centres and farms, and further afield zoos and wildlife parks. An ordinary walk can already become a small animal discovery.

A large zoo nearby is the Alpine Zoo in Innsbruck, which focuses on animals from the Alpine region. There are also smaller wildlife parks and farms where children can see or sometimes pet animals. The offer differs by season, so check opening times and conditions beforehand.

Respect for animals is important. Do not feed wild animals, keep your distance from cows with calves and follow the rules of the park or farm. This page offers a general guide with practical tips; the individual destinations for animal lovers are described below.

Tips

  • Bring old clothes or boots for a farm visit.
  • Check feeding times beforehand.
  • Keep your distance from animals on the pasture.
  • Make it a short, fun day with a picnic.
  • Ask your accommodation about farms nearby.

When and for whom?

Animal visits are possible all year, but the offer is greatest in the summer season. Smaller children especially enjoy animals they can pet and short visits; older children like explanations and feeding rounds. Do not plan too much for one day, and note feeding times, which vary by park.

Rules and safety

Only feed animals where expressly permitted and with the food provided. Wash your hands after petting. Children should be supervised around animals. On pastures cows can react protectively towards dogs and calves; keep your distance, walk calmly and keep dogs on a lead. Close gates behind you.

Frequently asked questions

Are there animal parks near Niederau?

Yes, the region has zoos and wildlife parks, such as the Alpine Zoo in Innsbruck. Driving time varies; check in advance.

Can children pet the animals?

Sometimes, especially on farms or in petting areas of parks. Ask on site and follow instructions.

Is it safe to walk past cows?

Generally yes, but be careful. Keep your distance, especially near calves, walk calmly and keep dogs on a lead.
