Haflinger show at the Fohlenhof Ebbs

An evening show with Tyrolean Haflinger horses near Kufstein

The Haflinger is a golden, sturdy horse breed from Tyrol. At the Haflinger Fohlenhof in Ebbs, near Kufstein, a summer show presents the versatility of the breed, with dressage, liberty work, vaulting and driving displays.

The show lasts about an hour and is held in July and August, mainly on Friday evenings. Dates and times can change from year to year, so check beforehand. The Fohlenhof also offers other activities such as guided tours; see the regional events calendar. A fun outing for children who love horses.

Place: Kufstein

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1 hour
  • CostAdmission charged; check current prices
  • DistanceApprox. 35 min drive
  • SeasonSummer (mainly July and August)
  • LocationEbbs near Kufstein (Tyrol)

Tips

  • Check the current show dates before you go; they change from year to year.
  • Bring an extra layer: evenings can be cool.
  • Ask about ticket sales beforehand and book ahead when it is busy.

Practical

Ebbs is about 35 minutes by car from Niederau, close to Kufstein in the Inn Valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Haflinger show?

Usually on Friday evenings in July and August; exact dates vary, so check beforehand.
