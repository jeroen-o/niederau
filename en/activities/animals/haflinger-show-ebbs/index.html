    Haflinger show at the Fohlenhof Ebbs

    An evening show with Tyrolean Haflinger horses near Kufstein

    The Haflinger is a golden, sturdy horse breed from Tyrol. At the Haflinger Fohlenhof in Ebbs, near Kufstein, a summer show presents the versatility of the breed, with dressage, liberty work, vaulting and driving displays.

    The Fohlenhof is regarded as the worldwide centre of Haflinger breeding. The stud has existed since 1947 and today houses around a hundred Haflingers; shows are held there each year at which the finest mares are presented. That makes it a nice place to see how such a breed is bred.

    The show lasts about an hour and is held in July and August, mainly on Friday evenings. Dates and times can change from year to year, so check beforehand. The Fohlenhof also offers other activities such as guided tours of the stud; see the regional events calendar. A fun outing for children who love horses. It is extra fun for children if they know something about the breed beforehand: the Haflinger is known for its blond mane and its friendly, willing temperament. They then watch more closely what the horses show during the performance.

    Place: Kufstein

    • CostAdmission charged; check current prices

    Tips

    • Check the current show dates before you go; they change from year to year.
    • Bring an extra layer: evenings can be cool.
    • Ask about ticket sales beforehand and book ahead when it is busy.
    • Combine the show with a guided tour of the stud if one is offered.
    • Arrive in good time so you can look at the horses beforehand.

    Practical

    Ebbs is about 35 minutes by car from Niederau, close to Kufstein in the Inn Valley. Drive via Wörgl down the valley towards Kufstein. Because the show is in the evening, you may drive back as darkness falls, so do not plan the return trip with children too tightly. Ask beforehand about parking and tickets. Plan for an evening, since the show lasts about an hour and you may want to look around afterwards.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is the Haflinger show?

    Usually on Friday evenings in July and August; exact dates vary, so check beforehand.

    Since when has the Fohlenhof in Ebbs existed?

    The Haflinger stud has existed since 1947.

    Is there also a tour of the Fohlenhof?

    Guided stud tours are offered about the history and breeding of the Haflinger; ask about dates beforehand.
