Trout fishing in the Wildschönauer Ache

Fishing for trout and char in the valley river

The Wildschönauer Ache runs through the valley and is a fishing water for trout and char. In summer it is a quiet pastime for a morning or evening by the water. There are two fishing waters in the region: the Wildschönauer Ache (from Schönanger to the Kundler Klamm) and the Wörgler Bach (from Oberau to the Wörgler Berg).

For guests the procedure is fixed: you need a Tyrolean guest fishing card, valid for 14 days and for the whole of Tyrol, plus a day permit for the specific water, issued by its manager. Fishing is only possible from 1 May to 1 October. For children it is mostly a lesson in patience; adult supervision is sensible.

Tips

  • Try early morning or evening.
  • Ask the tourist office or the manager about current rules and permits.
  • Good waders or sturdy shoes make the riverbank more comfortable.

Practical

The river runs through the valley, so the water is only minutes by car from Niederau. The guest card and day permits are available from the managers or issuing points in the Wildschönau; ask which water applies.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to fish?

A Tyrolean guest fishing card (valid 14 days) and a day permit for the relevant water from its manager.
