Schönangeralm show dairy

Cheese-making on the alp, with some 260 animals

The Schönangeralm near Auffach is the pride of 25 farms in the Wildschönau. Around 260 animals graze there from June to September. In the show dairy you can watch the cheesemaker produce alpine cheese, Emmental, Camembert, Tilsiter and herb cheese. Only milk from Schönauer cows is used, roughly 2,000 litres in all.

The cheese has won awards: the cheesemaker took gold and silver several times at the Cheese Olympics. Next to the dairy stands the Alpengasthof Schönangeralm, with a sunny terrace. It makes for an easy mix of animal-watching, cheese tasting and lunch in a lovely mountain setting. The trip fits well into a day in and around Auffach.

Place: Auffach

  • AgeFor all ages
  • DurationAbout 1 to 2 hours
  • CostViewing free; cheese and food extra
  • DistanceAbout 15 to 20 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (June to September)
  • LocationSchönangeralm, Auffach

Tips

  • Visit in summer (June to September), when the animals are on the alp.
  • Ask on site when the cheesemaker is at work.
  • Bring sturdy shoes; part of the way may be a walk.
  • Buy a piece of cheese as a souvenir and have lunch on the terrace.

Practical

The alp is near Auffach, the valley's highest village, about 15 to 20 min by car from Niederau. Check access and the dairy's current working times beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Which cheeses are made?

Among others alpine cheese, Emmental, Camembert, Tilsiter and herb cheese.

When are the animals on the alp?

Roughly from June to September.
