Aurach Wildlife Park near Kitzbühel

Native wildlife and farm animals in an open park with views

Aurach, close to Kitzbühel, is home to a wildlife park where you stroll through large open enclosures. You can see red deer, fallow deer, mouflon and lynx, along with a few more exotic animals such as llamas and yaks. The park looks out over the Kitzbühel Alps.

Children usually enjoy the petting barn most, where you can stroke alpacas, goats, rabbits and donkeys. In peak season the game is fed daily, a good moment to see the animals up close. Plan on half a day, including a break.

Place: Kitzbühel

Tips

  • Arrive in time for the feeding, around 2.30 pm in peak season.
  • Wear sturdy shoes, as the terrain rises and falls.
  • Check the opening days beforehand; outside peak season the park is not open every day.

Practical

From Niederau it is about 50 minutes by car, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel. Parking is available at the park.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Are the animals fed?

In peak season there is a daily feeding; times can change, so check beforehand.
