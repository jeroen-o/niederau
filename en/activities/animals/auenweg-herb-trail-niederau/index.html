Auenweg and herb trail Niederau

Short, pushchair-friendly walk with herb boards and a playground

The Auenweg in Niederau is a short loop of about 1 kilometre, almost flat and easily done in half an hour. The path follows a small stream, there are benches for a rest and it is fine with a pushchair. Boards along the route explain which herbs grow here and what they are used for, so it doubles as a herb trail.

There is a playground in the middle of the route. The walk starts at the walking-map board ("Wanderbuch") below the Markbachjochbahn, in the middle of the village, and can be done all year; in winter the path is gritted when needed. If you want more, continue to the Jausenstation Foisching; just above the hut a herd of red deer lives in a large enclosure.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 30 min (longer with stops)
  • CostFree
  • DistanceIn the village, on foot
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Bring a small herb guide or an app to identify plants.
  • Plan the playground as a break.
  • Do not pick anything unless you know it is edible or unprotected.
  • Optionally continue to Foisching for a snack and the deer park.

Practical

The walk starts in the middle of Niederau, below the Markbachjochbahn, and is reachable on foot from most places to stay in the village. No car needed; allow about 30 minutes, longer with stops.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Auenweg suitable for pushchairs?

Yes, the path is short, almost flat and described as family-friendly; there is a playground halfway.
