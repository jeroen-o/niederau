    Markbachjochalm: playground and goats

    Playground with mountain views and animals to feed

    Close to the top station of the Markbachjochbahn lies the Markbachjochalm, a favourite outing for families. Next to the mountain hut you will find a playground with swings, slides and climbing frames, with views of the surrounding peaks.

    There is also a small animal enclosure, where goats among others can be fed. The playground is open in summer only. You reach the alm by gondola from Niederau or on foot. A gentle walk from the top station to the hut suits small children well, and after playing you can have something to eat or drink on the terrace. This also makes the alm a pleasant, short-route goal for walkers.

    • AgeFor young children and families
    • DurationA few hours
    • CostPlayground free; gondola and food extra
    • DistanceAbout 5 min from Niederau
    • SeasonSummer
    • LocationNiederau, Markbachjoch

    Tips

    • Only bring animal feed if the alm allows it; ask on site what is permitted.
    • Combine the outing with a walk to the Holzalm, with cheese tasting and its own playground.
    • Bring sun cream and a jacket; it is cooler up the mountain than in the valley.
    • Pick a dry day; the playground is less fun in the rain.

    Practical

    The alm lies by the top station of the Markbachjoch gondola, a short ride from the centre of Niederau.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are there animals to see?

    Yes, there is an animal enclosure with goats among others, which you can feed.

    Is the playground open in winter?

    No, the playground is open in summer only.
