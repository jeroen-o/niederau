Cycling in the Wildschönau and the Inn Valley · Summer

Cycling through the Wildschönau valley

An easy valley ride from Niederau to Auffach

The Wildschönau has an easy valley route of about 30 kilometres, which takes roughly four hours by bike. From Niederau you follow the main road to Oberau, past the church. In Oberau you turn left towards Roggenboden and descend via the Schrattentalweg to Auffach; the end point at the head of the valley is the Schönangeralm.

The region rates the route as easy. For children who can ride well on their own it is a good introduction to the valley, and an e-bike helps less fit parents keep up. Bikes can be hired at, among others, the iBIKE BOX next to the open-air pool in Oberau (from early May) and at Sport Blachfelder. Ask for current maps.

  • AgeChildren who can ride on their own
  • DurationAbout 4 hours
  • CostFree; bike hire extra
  • DistanceIn Niederau
  • SeasonSummer (about May to October)
  • LocationNiederau to Auffach

Tips

  • Bring a picnic; there are lovely spots along the way.
  • Rent an e-bike for parents or a child seat for the youngest.
  • Allow enough time; the route is about 30 km.

Practical

The route starts in Niederau itself and follows the valley road. Bike hire is available in the valley; ask the tourist office or the rental shop for maps and current conditions.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the route suitable for children?

The route is rated easy but is about 30 km long; suitable for children who ride well on their own. Otherwise choose a shorter route.
