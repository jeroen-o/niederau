Cycling in the Wildschönau and the Inn Valley · Summer

Zillertal cycle path Mayrhofen to Zell am Ziller

A flat ride along the river through the Zillertal

The Zillertal cycle path is about 30 km long and follows the valley floor. It branches off the Inn cycle path at the Ziller bridge and runs via Uderns, Kaltenbach and Aschau alongside the Zillertalbahn to Zell am Ziller, then along the river to Mayrhofen. Other descriptions measure the route at over 36 km when starting from Wiesing.

The route is almost flat and popular with families: between Jenbach and Mayrhofen the height difference is only about 100 metres. You can ride sections, for example Mayrhofen to Zell am Ziller, and return by train or bus. The Zillertalbahn runs between Jenbach and Mayrhofen and carries a limited number of bikes; a historic steam train sometimes runs as well.

Zell am Ziller is a good lunch stop. Check beforehand whether the train carries bikes and whether there is space. Because the route runs alongside the railway, it is easy to change plans along the way: hop on the train at a station when tired, or keep riding if all goes well. That also makes the tour suitable for families with children of different ages.

Place: Zell am Ziller

  • AgeAll ages; flat and family-friendly
  • DurationHalf to full day, depending on the section
  • CostFree; bike rental and train extra
  • DistanceAbout 50 min by car to Zell am Ziller
  • SeasonSummer (approx. May to October)
  • LocationZillertal, Zell am Ziller

Tips

  • Ride a section and return by train or bus.
  • Plan a lunch stop in Zell am Ziller.
  • Ask about bike transport on the train in advance.
  • Choose a weekday if you want to be sure of space for the bikes on the train.
  • A steam train back turns the outing into a small adventure.

Practical

Zell am Ziller is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, Mayrhofen about 1 hour. The route splits easily into short stages, so you can match the length to the youngest rider. Park at one of the stations or in the village of your choice and use the Zillertalbahn for the return. Ask locally about bike rental. A rain jacket and sun cream belong in the bag.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the route flat?

Almost entirely; it runs along the valley floor and is popular with families.

Can you take your bike on the Zillertalbahn?

Yes, but in limited numbers; check the conditions and available spaces beforehand.

How long is the Zillertal cycle path?

About 30 km between Strass and Mayrhofen; from Wiesing it is over 36 km.
