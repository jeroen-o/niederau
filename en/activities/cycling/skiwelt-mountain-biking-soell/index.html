Mountain biking in the SkiWelt near Söll

Cable car rides and trails in the SkiWelt summer area

In summer, cable cars around Söll also carry bikes. You can ride up by lift and come back down on trails and forest tracks without a hard climb first. The Salvenbahn lifts you roughly 1,200 m in about 20 minutes. Options range from easy routes to more technical terrain.

Söll lies at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser and is a natural base for a day on the bike. A well-known intermediate circuit around the Hohe Salve is about 37 km long with roughly 1,100 m of climbing. SkiWelt publishes route and lift information, and rental shops can be found in the village. The season runs roughly from May to October. Beginners and children should stick to the easier routes; always bring a helmet.

Place: Söll

Tips

  • Always wear a helmet, and protection on technical trails.
  • Check beforehand whether the lift takes bikes and when it runs.
  • Rent an e-mountain bike if you want to avoid long climbs.

Practical

Söll is about 30 minutes from Niederau by car, via Wörgl. Ask the tourist office for current routes and lift times.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you take bikes on the lift?

SkiWelt runs summer lifts that carry bikes; check current times beforehand.
