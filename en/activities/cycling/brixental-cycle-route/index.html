Cycling through the Brixental

A long valley route past Hopfgarten, Westendorf and Kirchberg

The Brixental offers a long cycle route through the valley, past villages such as Hopfgarten, Brixen im Thale, Westendorf and Kirchberg towards Kitzbühel. It mostly runs along the valley floor with the occasional climb. You can ride the whole stretch or choose a section.

Along the way are meadows, riverbanks and places to eat for a lunch stop. An e-bike makes the hillier parts easier, and you can return by bus or train. Pick up a map at the tourist office and check the current route beforehand.

Place: Hopfgarten im Brixental

Tips

  • Pick a section if the full route is too long.
  • Rent an e-bike for the hills.
  • Return by bus or train.

Practical

Hopfgarten is about 25 minutes by car from Niederau and a good starting point. Rental is available in the Brixental villages.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to ride the whole route?

No, you can choose a section and return by bus or train.
