Cycling in the Wildschönau and the Inn Valley · Summer

Cycling through the Brixental

A long valley route from Wörgl via Hopfgarten and Westendorf to Kitzbühel

The Brixental cycle path runs from Wörgl through the Brixental to Kitzbühel, roughly 40 to 47 km, past Hopfgarten, Westendorf, Brixen im Thale and Kirchberg. It is rated easy and mostly follows the valley floor; some stretches use quiet side roads that also carry cars and tractors. You can ride the whole stretch or choose a section.

Along the way are meadows, riverbanks and places to eat for a lunch stop; just before Kitzbühel lies the Schwarzsee. An e-bike makes the hillier parts easier, and you can return by bus or train. Pick up a map at the tourist office and check the current route beforehand.

The route is exactly 46.7 km long and starts at the Innsteg in Wörgl; the whole ride takes about 3.5 hours. A railway line runs parallel to the cycle path, and in most villages you can board a regional train back with your bike. In Brixen im Thale there is a station about ten minutes' walk from the village centre. It is a route for those who want to enjoy themselves at an easy pace: you ride through an open valley with mountains on both sides, and village after village offers a place to stop. With children, a section between two villages is usually the most pleasant, after which you return by public transport.

Place: Hopfgarten im Brixental

  • AgeAll ages; sections suit families
  • DurationHalf to full day, depending on the section
  • CostFree; rental and return trip extra
  • DistanceAbout 25 min by car to Hopfgarten
  • SeasonSummer (approx. April to October)
  • LocationBrixental

Tips

  • Pick a section if the full route is too long.
  • Rent an e-bike for the hills.
  • Return by bus or train.
  • With the regional guest card the buses between Wörgl and Kitzbühel are free; ask your accommodation about it.
  • Start at Wörgl if you want to ride the whole route in one direction.

Practical

Hopfgarten is about 25 minutes by car from Niederau and a good starting point for part of the route; Wörgl, the official start, is also close by. Rental is available in the Brixental villages. You can leave your car at your starting point and return by bus or regional train; check the timetable beforehand and whether bikes may be taken along. Bring water, sun cream and a rain jacket, as the weather in the valley can change.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to ride the whole route?

No, you can choose a section and return by bus or train.

How long is the Brixentalradweg?

About 46.7 km from Wörgl to Kitzbühel; allow about 3.5 hours of cycling.

Is there a free bus with the guest card?

With the regional guest card the bus connections between Wörgl and Kitzbühel are free; ask your accommodation whether the card applies to you.
