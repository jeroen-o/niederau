Cycling and mountain biking at Brixen im Thale

Cycling from the valley station, from alm routes to trails

Brixen im Thale lies in the Brixental and is part of the SkiWelt. From the village, forest tracks lead up to mountain inns such as the Kandleralm at just over 1,100 m, a nice goal for an afternoon ride. The direct climb from the valley station to the Kandleralm is demanding; if you prefer not to climb, you can use the cable car.

The area suits families with cycling children, provided you pick routes of the right difficulty. Bikes can usually be rented near the valley station and in the village. An e-bike makes the climb much easier for adults.

There are longer routes through the area too. The Kandleralm round on foot is about 8.7 km, takes roughly 2.5 hours and involves 330 metres of climbing, with the highest point at 1,120 m. By bike it is about 4 km of steady climbing from the village to the Kandleralm. For experienced bikers, a harder route continues on to the Choralpe: about 9 km and just over 1,000 metres of climbing. If you prefer a shorter climb and more distance, you can explore the whole area along the quiet roads in the valley and make a shorter round from there. That way there is something for every level, from a relaxed ride to a serious mountain tour.

Tips

  • Pick an alm as the goal; it motivates children.
  • Bring a helmet and check the brakes before setting off.
  • Use an e-bike for the climb if fitness levels differ in the family.
  • Walk the Kandleralm round first to see whether the slope suits you.
  • For experienced mountain bikers the route to the Choralpe is a tough challenge.

Practical

Brixen im Thale is about 35 minutes from Niederau by car via Wörgl and the Brixental. The village also has its own railway station, about ten minutes' walk from the centre. Ask on the spot about current routes, the cable car's running times and bike and e-bike rental, and choose your route after checking the weather forecast. Bring a helmet, water and a rain jacket, even if the morning starts sunny.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an alm reachable by bike?

Yes, for example the Kandleralm at just over 1,100 m via a forest track from the village.

How high is the Kandleralm?

At just over 1,100 m; the highest point of the walking round is at 1,120 m.

How hard is the route to the Choralpe?

Hard: about 9 km with just over 1,000 metres of climbing, meant for experienced mountain bikers.
