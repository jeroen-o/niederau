    Cycling in the Wildschönau and the Inn Valley · Summer

    Mountain biking in the Pillerseetal

    A large bike region with trails for all levels

    The Pillerseetal around Fieberbrunn and St. Ulrich am Pillersee bills itself as part of Austria's largest bike region: more than 80 km of lines and trails, reached by nine cable cars on seven mountains. Since 2020 the region has teamed up with Saalbach Hinterglemm and Saalfelden Leogang, giving plenty of choice for multi-day trips.

    A good starting point for families is the Bike Area Streuboden near Fieberbrunn, with a flow trail, three lines and a pump track. The Schweinestberg trails run in an easy and a harder version for nearly 4 km from the middle station down to the valley. There are also hundreds of kilometres of touring routes for those who prefer a calmer ride. Ask locally for maps and current trail conditions.

    Place: Fieberbrunn

    • AgeFamilies on the easy routes; trails for experienced riders
    • DurationFull day
    • CostLift and rental prices vary; check beforehand
    • DistanceAbout 1 hour by car
    • SeasonSummer (approx. May to October)
    • LocationPillerseetal, Fieberbrunn

    Tips

    • Choose the quiet valley routes for children.
    • Wear a helmet, and protection on steep trails.
    • Check the lift times before you set off.

    Practical

    The Pillerseetal is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel or St. Johann in Tirol. It suits a full day out.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
