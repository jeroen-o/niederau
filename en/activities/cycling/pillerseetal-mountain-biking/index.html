Mountain biking in the Pillerseetal

A large bike region with trails for all levels

The Pillerseetal around Fieberbrunn and St. Ulrich am Pillersee bills itself as Austria's largest bike region: over 80 km of lines and trails reached by several cable cars. Its link with Saalbach Hinterglemm and Leogang gives plenty of choice for multi-day riders.

Families will find the quiet valley routes and easier trails most suitable, while experienced riders will find more technical terrain higher up. The area has nine cable cars on seven mountains. Ask locally for maps and current trail conditions.

  • AgeFamilies on the easy routes; trails for experienced riders
  • DurationFull day
  • CostLift and rental prices vary; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 1 hour by car
  • SeasonSummer (approx. May to October)
  • LocationPillerseetal, Fieberbrunn

Tips

  • Choose the quiet valley routes for children.
  • Wear a helmet, and protection on steep trails.
  • Check the lift times before you set off.

Practical

The Pillerseetal is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel or St. Johann in Tirol. It suits a full day out.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
