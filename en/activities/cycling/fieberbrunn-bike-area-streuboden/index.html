Bike Area Streuboden in Fieberbrunn

Flow trail, lines and pump track at Fieberbrunn

The Bike Area Streuboden near Fieberbrunn in the Pillerseetal opened in 2021 and has a flow trail, three lines and a pump track between the middle and valley stations of the Streuböden lift. The Schweinestberg trail of about 4 km adds undulating sections and wide berms.

It is a good spot for children and teenagers who already ride confidently, and for bike-park beginners. The lift takes you and your bike up and you ride back down. Wear a helmet and protection, and check opening times beforehand.

Tips

  • Start on the flow trail before trying other lines.
  • Wear a helmet and protection.
  • Check lift times and trail status beforehand.

Practical

Fieberbrunn is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel or St. Johann in Tirol. Plan a full day.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is in the bike area?
