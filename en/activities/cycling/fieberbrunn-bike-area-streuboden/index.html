Bike Area Streuboden in Fieberbrunn

Flow trail, lines and pump track at Fieberbrunn

The Bike Area Streuboden near Fieberbrunn in the Pillerseetal opened in 2021 and has a flow trail, three lines and a pump track at the middle station of the Streubödenbahn. The Schweinestberg trails also run for nearly 4 km from the middle station down to the valley, in an easy version with undulating sections and wide berms and a harder one for experienced riders.

It is a good spot for children and teenagers who already ride confidently, and for bike-park beginners. The lift takes you and your bike up and you ride back down. The area belongs to the Pillerseetal, which together with Saalbach Hinterglemm and Saalfelden Leogang forms Austria's largest bike region. Wear a helmet and protection, and check opening times beforehand.

Place: Fieberbrunn

Tips

  • Start on the flow trail before trying other lines.
  • Wear a helmet and protection.
  • Check lift times and trail status beforehand.

Practical

Fieberbrunn is about 1 hour by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel or St. Johann in Tirol. Plan a full day.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
