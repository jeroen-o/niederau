Cycling along the Inn from Wörgl to Kufstein

A flat ride to Kufstein fortress

The Wörgl to Kufstein section of the Inn cycle path is about 17 km long, runs downstream and is mostly flat. The route is rated easy and suits families and leisure cyclists. There are small rest areas along the way.

The finish is Kufstein with its fortress and old town. Allow a little over an hour of riding (about 1 hour 15 minutes) plus time for a visit. You can return by train with your bike; check the conditions beforehand. That makes a half-day outing without cycling the same way back.

Place: Wörgl

  • AgeAll ages (easy route)
  • DurationAbout 1 h 15 cycling, half a day with a visit
  • CostFree; bike rental and train extra
  • DistanceAbout 25 min by car to Wörgl
  • SeasonSummer; also possible in spring and autumn
  • LocationWörgl to Kufstein

Tips

  • Take the train back and check bike transport.
  • Allow time for Kufstein fortress.
  • Start early on hot summer days.

Practical

Wörgl is about 25 minutes by car from Niederau; park near the station or centre and start from there. Check the conditions for taking bikes on the train beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the route?

About 17 km, roughly 1 hour 15 minutes, mostly flat.
