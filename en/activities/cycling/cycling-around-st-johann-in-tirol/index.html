Cycling around St. Johann in Tirol

A flat ride through the open valley by the Wilder Kaiser

Around St. Johann in Tirol lies an open, green valley with cycle routes that are mostly flat, with views of the Wilder Kaiser. Over 200 kilometres of cycle and mountain bike paths run through the area around St. Johann, Oberndorf, Kirchdorf and Erpfendorf, with signposting and places to eat along the way.

A well-known family route is the valley loop Wörgl, Söll, Scheffau, Ellmau, Going and St. Johann, about 38 kilometres, which you can shorten or extend as you like. With an e-bike the light climbs are no problem either. Pick up a map from the tourist office and choose a length that suits the youngest rider.

Place: St. Johann in Tirol

  • AgeFrom about 6, riding independently
  • DurationHalf to full day, depending on route
  • CostFree (own bike); hire costs extra
  • DistanceAbout 40 to 45 min by car
  • SeasonSpring to autumn
  • LocationSt. Johann in Tirol

Tips

  • Get a map from the tourist office.
  • Choose an e-bike for the light climbs.
  • Plan a lunch stop along the way.
  • Ask about bike hire if you are not bringing your own.

Practical

St. Johann in Tirol is about 40 to 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is the route flat?

Mostly, with a few light climbs.
