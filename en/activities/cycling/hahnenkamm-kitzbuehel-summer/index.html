Hahnenkamm near Kitzbühel in summer

Walking and cycling on the Streif mountain

The Hahnenkamm is best known for the Streif, the famous winter downhill. In summer it is a walking and cycling area above Kitzbühel with paths, alpine meadows and views of the surrounding mountains.

The Hahnenkammbahn takes you up in comfort; for the descent you can walk or, on designated routes, cycle. Mountain inns along the way offer places to rest. For families it suits walkers best; mountain biking is more for experienced riders. Check current lift times beforehand.

Place: Kitzbühel

Tips

  • Plan a break at a mountain inn along the way.
  • Walk down if the children are not experienced riders.
  • Check the weather and lift times before leaving.

Practical

Kitzbühel is about 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl. The Hahnenkammbahn starts near the town centre.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
