Bike Park Hochzillertal, Kaltenbach

Mountain biking in the Zillertal with a trail, trial park and pump track

In Kaltenbach, in the Zillertal, the Bike Park Hochzillertal sits by the valley station of the lift. It has a singletrail of about 0.9 km (medium difficulty), a trial park and a pump track of more than 100 metres. It is a compact spot to practise technique or ride for a few hours.

The pump track is next to the sports shop at the valley station; the trail starts at the high-rope park in the village and runs through forest down to the valley station. The park is reported to be free to use and runs roughly from June to October. Check the current situation before you go.

Place: Kaltenbach

  • AgeDepending on skill; helmet recommended
  • DurationA few hours
  • CostReported to be free; check in advance
  • DistanceAbout 45 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationKaltenbach, Zillertal

Tips

  • A helmet and protective gear are sensible.
  • Start on the pump track to get a feel for the bike before heading onto the trail.
  • Check beforehand that the park is open.

Practical

Kaltenbach is about 45 minutes by car from Niederau, in the Zillertal via Wörgl and the B169. Parking is available near the village and the valley station.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
