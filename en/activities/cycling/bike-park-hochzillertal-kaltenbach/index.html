Bike Park Hochzillertal, Kaltenbach

Mountain biking in the Zillertal with a trail, trial park and pump track

In Kaltenbach, in the Zillertal, the Bike Park Hochzillertal sits by the valley station of the lift. It has a singletrail of about 0.9 km (medium difficulty), a trial park and a pump track of more than 100 metres, described as the longest in Austria. It is a compact spot to practise technique or ride for a few hours.

The trail starts at the high-rope park in the village and runs through forest in wide curves down to the valley station. There are roots and stones along the way and jumps for the bold, but children and adults can both manage it. You ride the pump track without pedalling, using body pressure alone, and it lies next to the sports shop at the valley station.

The park is reported to be free to use and runs roughly from June to October. Check the current situation before you go. For families with sporty children it is an accessible spot: the trail is short enough to ride several times in a row and the pump track invites play with speed and balance. Because everything is near the valley station, parents can watch calmly or try a lap themselves.

Tips

  • A helmet and protective gear are sensible.
  • Start on the pump track to get a feel for the bike before heading onto the trail.
  • Check beforehand that the park is open.
  • Take the trail slowly the first time; roots and stones are slippery when wet.
  • A mountain bike with good brakes works better than a city bike.

Practical

Kaltenbach is about 45 minutes by car from Niederau, in the Zillertal via Wörgl and the B169. Parking is available near the village and the valley station. Bring your own bike and protection; the park is not a rental location. You can combine the visit with a drive through the Zillertal, for example to Fügen or Zell am Ziller. The park is not a large bike resort but a small extra for a day in the Zillertal; plan a few hours for it and keep time free for the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

How long is the singletrail?

About 0.9 km, medium difficulty; children and adults can ride it.

How long is the pump track?

More than 100 metres; you ride it without pedalling, using body pressure.
