Cross-country skiing in the Wildschönau

Groomed trails along the valley for beginners and experienced skiers

The Wildschönau valley has about 50 km of cross-country trails, spread across the villages: roughly 16 km at Oberau, 14 km at Niederau and 10 km at Auffach. The landscape is open and rolling, with views of the surrounding mountains. Near Niederau there is, among others, the trail between Oberau and Niederau (about 3.8 km, intermediate).

Cross-country skiing is a calm sport for families who want to move together at their own pace. The trails are free to use and usually open well into March, depending on snow. Ask the tourist office for the current map and about lessons or equipment rental. Combine a route with lunch at a Gasthof.

  • AgeFrom about 8 years; beginners welcome
  • DurationHalf to full day
  • CostTrails free; rental and lessons separate
  • DistanceOften straight from Niederau
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationWildschönau valley

Tips

  • Ask the tourist office for the current trail map.
  • As a beginner, start with a short flat loop.
  • It is best early in the day on fresh snow.
  • Plan a lunch stop at a Gasthof.

Practical

The trails run along the valley, including near Niederau and between Oberau and Niederau. Check starting point and map with the tourist office.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
