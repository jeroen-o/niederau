Winter fun in Niederau and around

Snow fun without skis: sledging, winter walking, snowshoeing and more.

A winter holiday in Niederau is not only about skiing. If you prefer a calmer time outdoors or are travelling with young children, the Wildschönau and surroundings offer many ways to enjoy the snow. Think sledging, winter walks on prepared paths, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing and an afternoon of ice skating.

Many of these activities are easy for beginners too. A family-friendly toboggan run, a short winter walk with a break in a mountain hut or a horse-drawn sleigh ride makes a day special without needing much experience or equipment. For many children simply playing in the snow is enough: building a snowman, sliding on a sledge or having a snowball fight.

Availability depends on snow and weather, so check beforehand whether trails and runs are open. This page offers a general guide and practical tips; the individual winter activities in and around Niederau are listed below. Warm clothing and waterproof boots are half the job.

Tips

  • Bring a dry spare hat and gloves for children.
  • Hire a sledge on site or ask at your accommodation.
  • Combine a winter walk with a stop at a mountain hut.
  • Check whether runs and paths are open.
  • Plan breaks: children cool down faster than you think.

When and for whom?

Winter fun is mostly available in the winter months, from December into March, depending on snow. The activities are largely suitable for the whole family, including non-skiers. Small children can often join on quiet runs or play in the snow, while older children can sledge or go snowshoeing.

Safety and clothing

Dress in layers, wear waterproof gloves and boots and bring a hat. Sledge on the designated runs and preferably with a helmet for children. Keep your distance from other sledgers and brake in good time. When winter walking, stay on marked paths and avoid steep slopes with avalanche risk. Check current conditions before leaving.

Frequently asked questions

What winter activities are there without skis?

You can go sledging, winter walking, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing or ice skating. The offer depends on snow.

Is this suitable for young children?

Many activities are, especially quiet toboggan runs and short winter walks. Mind warm clothing and breaks.

Can I hire sledges or snowshoes?

Often yes, in the village or from sports shops and accommodations. Ask on site about the options.
