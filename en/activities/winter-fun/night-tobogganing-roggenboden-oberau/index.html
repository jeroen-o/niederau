Night tobogganing at Roggenboden

Tobogganing after dark, with a special lift system

The Roggenboden ski area in Oberau, close to Niederau, has a night toboggan run. According to the region the evening run is open twice a week, on Wednesdays and Thursdays, and the sledge can be hooked into a lift system so you do not have to walk back up.

An evening ride feels different from daytime: you glide through a snowy landscape in the dark. Always check the current days, times and whether the run is open, as these depend on snow and weather. This does not apply to the Lanerköpfl toboggan run near Niederau (about 5 km), which is a separate run.

  • AgeAll ages; small children with an adult
  • DurationAbout 2 hours
  • CostPlease enquire in advance
  • DistanceAbout 5 min by car
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationRoggenboden, Oberau

Tips

  • Check the day, time and whether the run is open beforehand.
  • Wear warm, waterproof clothes and gloves.
  • A headtorch or torch is handy for the way to and from the run.
  • Ask the tourist office about sledge rental.

Practical

Oberau is about 5 minutes by car from Niederau. The Roggenboden ski area is easy to reach from the valley; check current connections and times.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is night tobogganing?

According to the region on Wednesdays and Thursdays; check current days and conditions.
