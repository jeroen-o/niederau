Night tobogganing at Roggenboden

Tobogganing after dark, with a drag lift uphill

At the Roggenboden ski area in Oberau, close to Niederau, you can toboggan after dark in winter on a floodlit, wide and well-prepared slope. The sledge has an attached ski and can be hooked into the drag lift, so you do not have to walk back up.

According to the region, night tobogganing takes place on Wednesdays and Thursdays, from about 7:30 pm to 9 pm, on request. Registering in advance is necessary. Groups of around 20 or more can also book other days. You glide through a snowy landscape under lights, quite different from daytime. Prices and current dates vary by season.

Place: Oberau

Tips

  • Register in advance and check the day and time.
  • Wear warm, waterproof clothes and gloves.
  • Bring a hat or helmet for warmth and protection.
  • Ask about current prices when you register.

Practical

Oberau is about 5 minutes by car from Niederau. The Roggenboden ski area is at Oberau; register in advance and check the current times.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is night tobogganing?

According to the region on Wednesdays and Thursdays from about 7:30 pm to 9 pm, on request and with advance registration.
