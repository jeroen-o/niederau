Ice skating in the Wildschönau

Skating on the rink at the Familienpark Drachental

In winter the Familienpark Drachental in Oberau has an ice rink. Skates can be hired on site, and with advance registration ice stock shooting (a Tyrolean cousin of curling) is also possible. It is a good idea for an afternoon off the slopes: children practise on the ice while parents watch or join in.

In winter the park offers more than just the rink. Among other things there is a 5D cinema, a space for ball games in the belly of the dragon, a family ski slope with a drag lift and the Drachenherz bistro. In some winters a snow-tubing track is added, where you slide down a prepared snow slope on an inflated tyre, and on certain evenings there is music and food. For those who would rather not step onto the ice, the park is also a place to watch and have something to drink.

The park is generally closed on Mondays and open Tuesday to Sunday from 10 am to 6 pm, but times can vary. Check opening times and the rink's entry price beforehand. Bring warm gloves and a hat; it gets cold quickly on the ice.

Tips

  • Check opening times and prices beforehand.
  • Wear warm gloves and a hat.
  • A helmet is a sensible choice for beginners.
  • Register in advance if you want to try ice stock shooting.
  • Combine the rink with a visit to the bistro or one of the park's other winter attractions.

Practical

The Familienpark Drachental is in the Kirchen area near Oberau, about 5 to 10 minutes by car from Niederau. That makes it an easy alternative when the slopes are busy or the weather is poor. Check current times with the park or the tourist office, as opening hours in the winter season can differ from those in summer. The park is geared to families, so it also works well with young children.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you hire skates?

Yes, skates can be hired on site; do check current times and prices.

Can you also try ice stock shooting?

Yes, ice stock shooting is possible with advance registration.

What else is there to do in the park in winter?

Among other things a 5D cinema, a family ski slope with a drag lift and the Drachenherz bistro.
