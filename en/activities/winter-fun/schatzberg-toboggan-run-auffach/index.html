    Schatzberg toboggan run, Auffach

    About 5 km of tobogganing from the mid station to the valley station

    The toboggan run on the Schatzberg near Auffach is about 5 km long and of medium difficulty. You start at the middle station of the Schatzbergbahn (about 1,300 m) and glide down a height difference of about 430 m to the valley station. Part of the route runs through wooded slopes.

    The run suits families with children who are somewhat adventurous; young children are safest riding with an adult. The gondola takes you up, so there is no walking. Whether the run is open depends on snow and weather: check beforehand and ask about fares and sledge rental.

    The run lies in the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area and received the Tyrolean quality seal for toboggan runs during the tobogganing World Cup in Innsbruck/Igls. That makes it one of the nine natural toboggan runs in Tyrol that meet strict quality and safety criteria. It is one of the better ways for the whole family to spend a winter day outdoors together without skiing. You do not depend on skiing ability, and afterwards a hot chocolate near the valley station is a nice way to finish. Do look at the snow conditions and dress warmly.

    Tips

    • Check beforehand whether the run is open.
    • Wear sturdy shoes and waterproof clothing.
    • Have young children ride with an adult.
    • Ask at the valley station about sledge rental.
    • Wear gloves and a hat; the descent is faster than you expect.

    Practical

    Auffach is about 15 minutes by car from Niederau. The Schatzbergbahn starts in Auffach and takes you to the middle station, where the toboggan run begins; you then toboggan back down to the valley station. Check beforehand whether the run is open and whether a ski bus is running, and ask about parking and sledge rental. That way you can be sure your day runs smoothly. Arrive at a quiet time and bring warm clothes for afterwards.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long is the Schatzberg toboggan run?

    About 5 km, from the middle station to the valley station of the Schatzbergbahn.

    Can I rent a sledge?

    Yes, sledge rental is available; ask in advance about current conditions and prices.

    How difficult is the run?

    The run is of medium difficulty and over 5 km is manageable for families with somewhat adventurous children.
