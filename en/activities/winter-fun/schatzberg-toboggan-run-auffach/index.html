Schatzberg toboggan run, Auffach

About 5 km of tobogganing from the mid station to the valley

The toboggan run on the Schatzberg near Auffach is, according to the region, about 5 km long. You start at the gondola's mid station and glide through wooded slopes back to the valley. The difficulty is described as medium, with a height difference of about 430 m.

The run suits families with children who are somewhat adventurous; young children are safest riding with an adult. The gondola takes you up, so there is no walking. Whether the run is open depends on snow and weather: check beforehand and ask about fares and sledge rental.

  • AgeAll ages; small children with an adult
  • DurationAbout 2 to 3 hours
  • CostGondola ticket and sledge hire: please enquire
  • DistanceAbout 15 min by car
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationAuffach, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Check beforehand whether the run is open.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and waterproof clothing.
  • Have young children ride with an adult.
  • Ask at the valley station about sledge rental.

Practical

Auffach is about 15 minutes by car from Niederau. The Schatzbergbahn starts in Auffach; check parking and any ski bus.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
