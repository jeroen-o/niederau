Village walk in Ellmau

A village at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser

Ellmau lies right at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser, the limestone range that gives the village its characteristic skyline. A well-known route is the Ellmauer Dorfrunde, which passes the parish church of St Michael, a plain Baroque hall church built in 1740-1746.

A walk through the village and the surrounding meadows takes about an hour. With children, a visit to Ellmi’s Zauberwelt at the top station of the Hartkaiserbahn is a good addition, with play areas, an enchanted forest and water play. That makes Ellmau a simple destination for half a day, with plenty of room to wander and look at the rock faces.

Place: Ellmau

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 hour
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 40 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationEllmau, Wilder Kaiser

Tips

  • Bring a camera for the village and rock face together.
  • Plan a stop at Ellmi’s Zauberwelt if you are with children.
  • Check the opening times of the park and the cable car beforehand.
  • Dress in layers; mountain weather can change quickly.

Practical

Ellmau is roughly 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl towards the Wilder Kaiser.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
