Farm shop in Oberau

Regional products direct from Wildschönau farmers

In Oberau the Bauernladl sells products from farmers in the Wildschönau and surrounding area: cheese, bacon, jam, bread and handmade items among them. It is run by the ARGE Wildschönauer Bauernmarkt, a cooperative of local farmers.

According to the tourist office, the shop is open on Saturdays from 9 am to 12 noon. It is not a big event but a good place to pick up fresh food or gifts. Children can often taste something and choose a small treat. Check the current opening hours beforehand, also outside the main season.

Place: Oberau

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 30 to 60 min
  • CostFree to browse; purchases at your own cost
  • DistanceApprox. 5 to 10 min by car
  • SeasonAll year (check opening hours)
  • LocationOberau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Go on Saturday morning when the choice is widest.
  • Bring a cool bag for cheese and bacon.
  • Let children choose a product to try at home.
  • Check the current opening hours beforehand.

Practical

Oberau is about 5 to 10 minutes from Niederau by car or bus. The Bauernladl is in the village; ask the tourist office for the exact location.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Bauernladl in Oberau open?

According to tourist information on Saturdays from 9 am to 12 noon; check the current times.
