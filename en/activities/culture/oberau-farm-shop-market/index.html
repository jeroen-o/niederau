Farm shop in Oberau

Regional products direct from Wildschönau farmers

In Oberau the Bauernladl sells regional products from farmers in the Wildschönau and surrounding area: cheese, bacon, jam and bread, among other things, plus handmade items. The shop is run by the ARGE Wildschönauer Bauernmarkt, a cooperative of local producers, and puts weight on quality and sustainability.

The opening hours currently listed are Wednesday to Saturday from 9 am to 12 noon, and on Fridays also from 3 pm to 6 pm. It is not a big event but a pleasant stop for fresh food or a gift. Hours can change with the season, so check beforehand.

Place: Oberau

Tips

  • Go in the morning when the choice is widest.
  • Bring a cool bag for cheese and bacon.
  • Let children choose a product to try at home.
  • Check the current opening hours beforehand.

Practical

Oberau is about 5 to 10 minutes from Niederau. By bus you reach the Oberau Kellerwirt stop, one minute on foot from the shop; there is also parking close to the shop.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
