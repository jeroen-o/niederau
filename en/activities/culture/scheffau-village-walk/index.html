Village stroll in Scheffau

A quiet village at the Wilder Kaiser, with paths up to meadows and alpine pastures

With around 1,200 residents, Scheffau am Wilden Kaiser is the smallest of the four Kaiser municipalities, a quiet village at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser. You walk past farms with flower gardens and along calm streets; do not expect shopping streets or crowds.

After a loop through the village you can take one of the paths up to the meadows and alpine pastures above it. There are short, family-friendly rounds over the alpine meadows with views of the rock faces. Cows on or beside the path are part of the scene: keep your distance and close gates behind you.

The Scheffauer Dorfrunde is about 4.3 km long, takes roughly 1.3 hours and involves only about 90 metres of climbing and descending. The route starts at the parish church, where both Saints John, the Baptist and the Evangelist, stand at the high altar, and passes the town hall towards Söll to the Brandstadl, with views of the Wilder Kaiser. The village is a good example of the quiet, rural side of the Wilder Kaiser. If you want to stay longer, you can combine the walk with a visit to the church and a stop at a farm shop or café, if they are open. That way the afternoon passes without any rush.

Tips

  • Keep your distance from cows and grazing animals.
  • Bring water and a snack; there are few facilities.
  • Wear sturdy shoes if you head up to the meadows.
  • Start at the church; the route leads from there across easy, gentle ground through the village.
  • Take a look at the church with the two Saints John at the high altar.

Practical

Scheffau is roughly 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl towards the Wilder Kaiser. The village loop starts in the centre by the church, so park in or near the village and walk from there. The Brandstadl above the village can also be reached by gondola; ask beforehand about the lift's operating times and the weather, especially if you want to go higher. Wear comfortable shoes for the village loop and allow for changeable mountain weather.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scheffau busy?

No, it is the smallest of the four Kaiser municipalities and a quiet village; it suits a short, relaxed walk.

How long is the Scheffauer Dorfrunde?

About 4.3 km, walkable in roughly 1.3 hours with only about 90 metres of ascent and descent.

What is there for children on the Brandstadl?

At about 1,650 m lies the KaiserWelt Scheffau, an adventure park with trails through the KaiserWald; the Brandstadl can also be reached by gondola.
