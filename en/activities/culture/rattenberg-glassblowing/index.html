Glassblowing in Rattenberg

Austria's smallest town and its glass tradition

With about 400 inhabitants, Rattenberg on the Inn is Austria's smallest town. Its medieval townscape has survived almost intact, and the town is known for its glassmaking businesses and crystal glass shops.

At Kisslinger Kristall-Glas you can, according to sources, watch glassblowers at work from Monday to Friday, and with a glassblower's help you can blow a glass sphere of your own to take home. That is a fun experience for children. Combine it with a stroll through the narrow streets of the old town and a visit to the shops.

Place: Rattenberg

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 2 hours
  • CostWatching is often free; blowing your own costs extra
  • DistanceApprox. 25 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationRattenberg, Alpbachtal

Tips

  • Check beforehand that demonstrations are on (Monday to Friday).
  • You can blow glass yourself, but ask about price and age limit.
  • Glass is fragile; pack souvenirs well.
  • Allow time for a walk through the old town.

Practical

Rattenberg is roughly 25 minutes by car from Niederau, in the Alpbachtal; take the A12 to Rattenberg/Kramsach. Parking is on the edge of the town.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can I watch glassblowers at work?

At Kisslinger Kristall-Glas, according to sources, Monday to Friday; check times beforehand.
