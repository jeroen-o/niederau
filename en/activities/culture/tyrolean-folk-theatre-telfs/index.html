Tyrolean Folk Theatre Festival, Telfs

Summer folk theatre festival in Telfs, performed in German

The Tyrolean Folk Theatre Festival takes place every summer in Telfs, in the Inn Valley west of Innsbruck. It was founded in 1981 and focuses on folk theatre, mixing professional actors with local amateurs and using special venues.

Since 2023 the Birkenberg has been a venue, with a foyer by the Mariahilf Chapel. There are around 60 performances in total each year. For English or Dutch-speaking families language is a hurdle: plays are in German, sometimes in dialect. It suits teenagers and adults with some knowledge of German best.

  • AgeMainly from approx. 12 years
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 3 hours per performance
  • CostTicket prices vary by show; see the official website
  • DistanceApprox. 1 hour 15 min by car
  • SeasonSummer
  • LocationTelfs, Inn Valley

Tips

  • Check the programme; titles and venues change each year.
  • Bring warm clothes; outdoor evenings can get cool.
  • Book tickets in advance, especially for premieres.
  • Plan the drive back; Telfs is over an hour from Niederau.

Practical

Telfs lies in the Inn Valley west of Innsbruck, about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Niederau via the A12. Check the venue and parking for each performance.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

In what language are the performances?

In German, sometimes with dialect; translations are generally not provided.
