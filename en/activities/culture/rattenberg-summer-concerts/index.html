Concerts in Rattenberg

Music in the medieval lanes of Austria's smallest town

With around 440 inhabitants Rattenberg is Austria's smallest town and has kept almost its entire medieval townscape. In summer concerts are held in and around the town, including in the cloister of the Augustinian Museum, and there are summer open-air performances.

The programme changes each year, so check the events calendar or the tourist office for what is on during your day. An evening here combines well with a walk through the car-free old town and dinner on a terrace. With children an early concert or a short performance works best.

Place: Rattenberg

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 hours
  • CostAdmission or contribution; ask in advance about current prices
  • DistanceApprox. 30 min by car
  • SeasonSummer; programme varies
  • LocationRattenberg, Alpbachtal

Tips

  • Check the events calendar before you go; the programme changes each year.
  • Park outside the car-free old town and walk in.
  • Bring a jacket for concerts in the cloister or outdoors.
  • Combine with a visit to the glass workshops before they close.

Practical

Rattenberg is in the Inn Valley near Kramsach, about 30 minutes by car from Niederau via Wörgl and the A12.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find current concerts in Rattenberg?

Via the town's events calendar or the Alpbachtal tourist office.
