Tyrolean folklore evening in winter

Alpine music, folk dance and Tyrolean hospitality after a day in the snow

In winter the Wildschönau regularly hosts an evening of Tyrolean music and traditions, often in an inn or hotel. Such an evening usually consists of live music, singing, an explanation of customs and sometimes folk dance, with a regional dish or a drink.

It is a relaxed way to experience some local culture after skiing, also with children. The offer differs by week and venue, and is sometimes only for guests of a particular house. Ask your accommodation or the tourist office when and where there is an evening and whether you need to book.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 3 hours
  • CostPrice varies; ask in advance
  • DistanceIn or near Niederau
  • SeasonWinter
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Ask your accommodation or the tourist office for date and venue.
  • Book beforehand if needed.
  • Dress warmly for the way there and back in the evening.
  • Traditional dress is often appreciated but not required.

Practical

The offer is in or near Niederau, often within walking distance or with a short ride or shuttle. Ask in advance about location and transport.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book a folklore evening?

Often yes or advisable; ask your accommodation or the tourist office.
