Village walk in Söll

A SkiWelt village centre with a church, shops and summer events

Söll lies at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser and belongs to the SkiWelt ski area. The village centre is lively, with shops, restaurants and cafés. The Baroque parish church of Saints Peter and Paul, built between 1764 and 1768 on the site of a Romanesque predecessor from around 1050, is a nice starting point for a stroll through the village.

In summer the centre becomes a meeting place: on Wednesday evenings there is music, regional food, craft stalls and a children’s programme, and at the Söller Freitag bands play at several spots around the village. A walk through the centre takes about an hour and combines well with a coffee stop or lunch.

Place: Söll

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 hour
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 35 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationSöll, Wilder Kaiser

Tips

  • Check with the tourist office for summer events.
  • Combine with a visit to nearby Kufstein or Ellmau.
  • Stroll through the centre on a Wednesday evening or during the Söller Freitag for atmosphere and music.
  • In the rain a stroll past the shops is a good alternative.

Practical

Söll is roughly 35 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl towards the Wilder Kaiser.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there much to do in Söll outside the ski season?

Yes, there are shops, restaurants and recurring summer events in the centre, such as the Wednesday evening gathering and the Söller Freitag.
