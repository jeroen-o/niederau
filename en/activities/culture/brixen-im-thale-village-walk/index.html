    Village stroll in Brixen im Thale

    A quiet Brixental village with a large parish church and the Brixner Stadl

    Brixen im Thale lies in the Brixental, about 11 km west of Kitzbühel. On the village square stands the large parish church of Mariä Himmelfahrt, a late Baroque-Classicist building constructed between 1790 and 1795 to plans by court architect Wolfgang Hagenauer and consecrated in 1797. In 1812 it became the deanery church of the Brixental. It is about 38 m long with a double-tower facade; inside you will notice the high altar of polished reddish-brown stucco marble, the side altars and an ornate organ.

    The church and the compact village can be seen in about an hour. The parish also includes several chapels, two of them pilgrimage churches. Another sight is the Brixner Stadl, a 19th-century wooden barn now used as a cultural venue for concerts and exhibitions. If you are travelling with children, you can pair it with a playground or a short walk nearby, so that the cultural stop does not drag on.

    It is a pleasant stop when you are driving through the Brixental anyway, for instance on the way to Kitzbühel or Westendorf. Meadows and walking paths around the village invite those who want to stay longer.

    Tips

    • Take a look inside the church, but keep quiet.
    • Check the Brixner Stadl programme for concerts and exhibitions.
    • Park in the centre and take the loop at an easy pace.
    • Combine with another outing in the Brixental.
    • Take a close look at the high altar and the organ inside the church.

    Practical

    Brixen im Thale is roughly 30 minutes by car from Niederau, via the B170 through the Brixental. The village lies on the main road between Wörgl and Kitzbühel, so it is easy to combine with another destination in the valley. The church stands on the village square, where you can stop and explore the village on foot. There is usually room to park in the centre, though on busy days you may have to look around.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long does a visit to Brixen im Thale take?

    A walk through the centre including the church takes about an hour; combine with another outing for half a day.

    What is special about the parish church?

    It is one of the largest churches in the Tyrolean lowlands, designed by Wolfgang Hagenauer, with a stucco-marble high altar and an ornate organ.

    What is the Brixner Stadl?

    A 19th-century wooden barn now used as a cultural venue for concerts and exhibitions.
