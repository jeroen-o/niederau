Culture in the Wildschönau and surroundings

Farming heritage, old towns and Tyrolean traditions within driving distance of Niederau.

Tyrol is a region where tradition is still part of everyday life. In the Wildschönau you notice it in the timber farmhouses, the small wayside chapels and the village festivals where music and traditional dress are simply part of the day. With children you do not have to head straight for a museum: a walk through the valley already says a lot about how people lived from the mountains for centuries.

Still, it is worth setting aside a day for it. Further afield you will find open-air museums about farm life, old mining towns, the walled little town of Rattenberg on the Inn, and fortresses or castles that children can genuinely explore. Many of these fit nicely with an afternoon outdoors or a stop at a terrace.

This page brings together the cultural outings that suit a stay in Niederau. Organisers often keep their own calendars, so always check opening times, guided tours and any family programmes with the organisation beforehand, as they differ by season and can change.

Tips

  • Check opening times beforehand, especially out of season.
  • Plan no more than one cultural stop a day with children.
  • Combine a visit with a walk or a terrace stop in the same place.
  • Ask the museum about a children's trail or treasure hunt.
  • Keep a museum or old town as a back-up plan for rainy days.

Combining children and culture

Children last longer when there is something to do. Choose places with a trail, an animal, a treasure hunt or something to touch, and plan no more than one cultural stop a day. A short tour or your own route with a map often works better than a long guided visit. Bring a snack or plan a break, because distances in the Inn valley are short but the days fill up quickly.

Rain, winter and seasons

Culture is a good plan for a rainy day or a rest day between skiing. Note that some open-air museums and smaller museums are open only in the summer months or have limited hours outside peak season. Towns such as Rattenberg and Hall in Tirol are pleasant all year round, and many places hold Christmas markets in Advent. Always check current information before setting off.

Frequently asked questions

Are the outings suitable for small children?

Often yes, especially open-air places and small towns where children can walk around. Check each venue for age recommendations and whether a pushchair can come along.

Do I need to book in advance?

For guided tours and special programmes it can be necessary, particularly in holiday periods. Check the website or ask at your accommodation.

How far is it from Niederau?

Most cultural destinations in the Inn valley and Alpbachtal are roughly half an hour to an hour away by car, depending on the place and traffic.
