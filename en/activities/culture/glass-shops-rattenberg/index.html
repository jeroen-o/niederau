    Glass in Rattenberg

    Glassblowers, studios and shops in Tyrol's glass town

    Rattenberg in the Alpbachtal is known as a glass town and, with about 440 inhabitants, is the smallest town in Austria. It was first mentioned in 1254 and has kept its medieval townscape almost intact. Some four hundred years ago a glassworks was set up across the Inn in Kramsach; Rattenberg specialised in working and refining glass. The craft is still practised today.

    The lanes hold shops and studios selling glassware, from drinking glasses and jewellery to art pieces. At Kisslinger Kristall-Glas, whose original house has stood in the town since 1946, there is a glass furnace and glass is blown on the spot, so visitors can watch glassblowers and engravers at work.

    In the summer months you can blow your own glass sphere under guidance on certain afternoons and take it home. Check current days and opening hours beforehand. It makes a relaxed half-day outing; glass is fragile, so keep small children close. If you have time, wander through the town and look in several shops: each has its own pieces, from simple everyday items to finely worked crystal. Even without buying anything it is worth watching the craft for a while, as it makes for good photos and a pleasant chat with the makers.

    Place: Rattenberg

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationApprox. 1 to 2 hours
    • CostBrowsing is usually free; blowing your own costs extra
    • DistanceApprox. 30 min by car

    Tips

    • Ask beforehand which days guest glassblowing takes place.
    • Keep young children close in the shops.
    • Combine with a stroll through the car-free old town.
    • Pack fragile purchases well for the return trip.
    • Look up at the facades and gateways too; the whole town is a sight in itself.

    Practical

    Rattenberg is about 30 minutes by car from Niederau via Wörgl and the A12. Park outside the car-free old town and walk in; the town is so small that everything is within walking distance. You can combine the visit with nearby Kramsach or a walk along the Inn. Check the opening times of shops and studios beforehand. Thanks to its compact layout, a visit also fits well into a trip through the Alpbachtal, for example on a day of changeable weather when hiking is less appealing.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I blow glass myself?

    At Kisslinger Kristall-Glas this is possible on certain afternoons in summer; check current days beforehand.

    Why is Rattenberg called the glass town?

    After a glassworks was founded in Kramsach, Rattenberg specialised in refining glass; the craft is still alive.

    How big is Rattenberg?

    With about 440 inhabitants it is Austria's smallest town, so everything is within walking distance.
