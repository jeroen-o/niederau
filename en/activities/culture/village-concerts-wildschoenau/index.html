Village band concerts in the Wildschönau

Free open-air concerts by the local brass bands

In summer the Oberau Bundesmusikkapelle gives an open-air concert every Tuesday evening in the village centre, on the Dreizehnlindenplatz. In 2026 the series runs from 7 July to 1 September, with the concert starting at 8 pm. The programme is varied, from traditional brass music to more modern sounds, and refreshments are available.

The Mühltal band also plays concerts in summer; its dates change, so look at the current events calendar. It is a relaxed evening where children can move about freely. Dates can differ from year to year, so check the current programme.

Place: Oberau

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1.5 to 2 hours
  • CostPlease enquire in advance
  • DistanceApprox. 5 to 10 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (in 2026 from July to early September)
  • LocationDreizehnlindenplatz, Oberau

Tips

  • Arrive a little early to get a good spot.
  • Bring a jacket in case it cools in the evening.
  • Check the current dates in the Wildschönau events calendar.

Practical

Oberau is about 5 to 10 minutes from Niederau, by car or bus. The Dreizehnlindenplatz is in the village centre.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Oberau band play?

In 2026 every Tuesday at 8 pm on the Dreizehnlindenplatz, from 7 July to 1 September.
