Summer concerts in Schwaz

Serenades in the cloister and organ concerts in the town parish church

In July and August Schwaz regularly hosts summer concerts. The serenades are held in the arcade passage or, in good weather, in the courtyard of the Franciscan monastery, a tradition going back to 1948. There are also weekly organ concerts in the town parish church, which has the largest symphonic organ in Tyrol.

Schwaz was a wealthy mining town for centuries, and it still shows. A concert evening fits well into a day in town, for example with a walk through the compact centre beforehand. Dates and times change from year to year, so check the current programme.

Place: Schwaz

  • AgeMainly teenagers and adults
  • DurationApprox. 1.5 to 2 hours per concert
  • CostVaries by concert; ask in advance
  • DistanceApprox. 45 to 50 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (July and August)
  • LocationSchwaz, Inn Valley

Tips

  • Check the current programme; dates and venues change each year.
  • Arrive a little early and stroll through the centre of Schwaz.
  • Bring a jacket; the church and cloister can be cool.
  • Ask in advance whether tickets are required.

Practical

Schwaz is in the Inn Valley, about 45 to 50 minutes by car from Niederau via the A12. The centre is compact and easy to walk; parking is available in town.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the concerts in Schwaz take place?

The serenades in the Franciscan monastery, the organ concerts in the town parish church.
