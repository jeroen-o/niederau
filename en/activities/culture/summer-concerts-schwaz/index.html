    Culture in the Wildschönau and surroundings · Summer

    Summer concerts in Schwaz

    Serenades in the cloister and organ concerts in the town parish church

    In July and August Schwaz regularly hosts summer concerts. The serenades are held in the arcade passage or, in good weather, in the courtyard of the Franciscan monastery, a tradition going back to 1948. The monastery church, around 500 years old, is regarded as one of the most beautiful mendicant-order churches in the Alps and makes an atmospheric backdrop. There are also weekly organ concerts in the town parish church, which has the largest symphonic organ in Tyrol.

    In the Middle Ages Schwaz was known far beyond the region as a silver town and was a wealthy mining town for centuries. It still shows. A concert evening fits well into a day in town, for example with a walk through the compact centre beforehand. The building itself is also worth a visit: the monastery is a quiet place in the middle of town. Afterwards take your time to walk through the streets and have a drink.

    The serenades have counted for decades among the cultural highlights of the Schwaz summer. Dates and times change from year to year, so check the current programme. The concerts suit teenagers and adults best.

    Place: Schwaz

    • AgeMainly teenagers and adults
    • DurationApprox. 1.5 to 2 hours per concert
    • CostVaries by concert; ask in advance
    • DistanceApprox. 45 to 50 min by car
    • SeasonSummer (July and August)
    • LocationSchwaz, Inn Valley

    Tips

    • Check the current programme; dates and venues change each year.
    • Arrive a little early and stroll through the centre of Schwaz.
    • Bring a jacket; the church and cloister can be cool.
    • Ask in advance whether tickets are required.
    • Take a look at the Franciscan church itself; it is roughly 500 years old.

    Practical

    Schwaz is in the Inn Valley, about 45 to 50 minutes by car from Niederau via the A12. The centre is compact and easy to walk; parking is available in town. The monastery and the town parish church are within walking distance of each other. Check the current programme for dates, times and venues before you set off. Allow plenty of time for the drive back in the evening, as concerts end late.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do the concerts in Schwaz take place?

    The serenades in the Franciscan monastery, the organ concerts in the town parish church.

    Since when have there been serenades in the monastery?

    The first serenade in the cloister took place in 1948.

    When are the summer concerts?

    In July and August; the exact dates change each year.
