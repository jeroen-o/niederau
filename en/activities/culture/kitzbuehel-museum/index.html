Museum Kitzbühel

Town history, mining and the Alfons Walde collection

Museum Kitzbühel is on Hinterstadt in the centre of Kitzbühel, in an old town building that once served as a grain store and abuts the town wall. The exhibition tells the story of the region: crafts, mining going back to the Bronze Age, farming, faith and the rise of winter sports.

The attic and third floor house the Alfons Walde collection, with around sixty paintings plus graphics, photographs and drawings by the expressionist and architect (1891-1958). The museum is compact and combines well with a walk through the old town. Allow one to two hours.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 2 hours
  • CostAdmission charged; check current prices
  • DistanceApprox. 40 to 45 min by car
  • SeasonAll year (check opening hours)
  • LocationKitzbühel (Hinterstadt)

Tips

  • In the Walde collection look for the winter landscapes; they suit the region well.
  • Combine the museum with a walk through Kitzbühel's old town.
  • The museum is a good option for a rainy day.
  • Ask in advance about opening hours and current exhibitions.

Practical

Kitzbühel is about 40 to 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Hopfgarten and the Brixental. Parking is available in car parks near the centre; Hinterstadt is within walking distance of the old town.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to see at Museum Kitzbühel?

Town history, mining, crafts and winter sports, plus the Alfons Walde collection with around sixty paintings.
