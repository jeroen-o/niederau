Village walk in Thierbach

The quiet inner end of the Wildschönau valley

Thierbach is one of the villages of the Wildschönau and lies in the quieter, more remote part of the valley. You will find Tyrolean farmhouses, a small church and meadows with views of the surrounding mountains. There are no big attractions, which is exactly why it makes a pleasant place for an unhurried walk.

A loop through the village and its surroundings easily takes an hour to an hour and a half. You set the pace, and the paved sections are manageable with children and a pushchair. If you want more, longer hikes also start from Thierbach, and the Wildschönau mentions Thierbach as a place for nature discovery with children.

Place: Thierbach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 1.5 hours
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 10 min by car
  • SeasonAll year (mainly summer)
  • LocationThierbach, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Bring a snack and water; a small village has few facilities.
  • Leave the car in the village and walk a loop.
  • Combine the walk with an easy hike up the slopes.
  • Respect pasture land and leave cows alone.

Practical

Thierbach is roughly 10 minutes by car from Niederau, at the far end of the valley. You can also walk or take the hiking bus; ask about the current timetable.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Thierbach suitable for children?

Yes, the walk is quiet and not difficult. Thierbach is also mentioned in the Wildschönau as a place for nature discovery with children.
