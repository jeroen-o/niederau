Tyrolean Farmhouse Museum, Kramsach

Open-air museum with original farmhouses from across Tyrol

The Museum Tiroler Bauernhöfe in Kramsach is Tyrol's largest open-air museum and was founded in 1974. On site stand 14 original farmhouses and 23 outbuildings from all Tyrolean valleys, moved and rebuilt here. Between the buildings are a mill, a smithy and a threshing barn, among others.

Children can see ducks, chickens and geese and find out how people used to live and work. Over the years the museum has become more interactive with video stations and audio points. The grounds are near the Reintaler See, where you can walk after your visit. Allow about two to three hours.

Place: Kramsach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 3 hours
  • CostAdmission charged; check current prices
  • DistanceApprox. 30 to 35 min by car
  • SeasonSeasonal (spring to autumn); check dates
  • LocationKramsach, Alpbachtal

Tips

  • Allow at least two hours; the grounds are large and mostly outdoors.
  • Wear sturdy shoes for the paths between the farmhouses.
  • Combine the visit with a walk or picnic at the Reintaler See.
  • Check seasonal dates and current activities beforehand.

Practical

Kramsach is in the Inn Valley, about 30 to 35 minutes by car from Niederau via Wörgl and the A12 (Kramsach exit). There is a car park at the museum.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
