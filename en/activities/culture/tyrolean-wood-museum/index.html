Tyrolean Wood Museum in Auffach

Wood museum with a maze of 39 rooms at the entrance to Auffach

The Tyrolean Wood Museum stands at the entrance to Auffach and was created by its owner Hubert Salcher. Across more than 2,000 m² you walk through a maze of 39 rooms with over 3,000 wooden objects, from the history of the Wildschönau to the art of woodcarving.

Along the way you also meet funny and unusual pieces, such as Europe's smallest wedding chapel, a Pumuckl-style tree house for children and an exhibition on woodworm. A wood theme trail of about 1 km is linked to the museum. Dogs are not allowed. Children up to 1 metre in height enter free; with the Wildschönau Card there is a discount.

Place: Auffach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 1.5 hours
  • CostChildren up to 1 m free; ask for current rates
  • DistanceApprox. 15 min by car
  • SeasonSummer; in winter groups only on request
  • LocationAuffach

Tips

  • Check the opening times; in summer the museum is not open every day.
  • Take your time in the maze; children enjoy discovering everything.
  • Also walk the wood theme trail of about 1 km.
  • Dogs are not allowed inside.

Practical

Auffach is about 15 minutes by car from Niederau; the museum stands at the entrance to the village. Outside summer it opens only for groups on request.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Are dogs allowed?

No, dogs are not allowed in the museum.

Is the museum open in winter?

In winter only for groups of about 15 or more on request.
