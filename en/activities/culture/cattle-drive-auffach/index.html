Almabtrieb cattle drive in Auffach

Decorated cows return from the alp: a village festival

Each autumn the animals are brought down from the high pastures to the valley, and in Auffach this is a big village festival. More than 500 animals come down. Around midday the decorated cows arrive at the foot of the Schatzberg, greeted with applause and traditional folk music.

The festival starts around 10 am and runs until about 6 pm, at the valley station of the Schatzberg cable car. There is a craft market, a market with regional products, live music and local food. The date usually falls in September and varies by year; in 2026 it was 19 September. Check the current date with the tourist office.

Place: Auffach

  • AgeFor all ages
  • DurationA day or part of a day
  • CostFree entry; food and shopping extra
  • DistanceAbout 15 to 20 min by car
  • SeasonOnce a year, in September
  • LocationAuffach

Tips

  • Arrive in good time; the cows come in around midday and it gets busy.
  • Park early or take the bus; Auffach is small.
  • Bring a jacket; September can be fresh.
  • Try regional products at the market.

Practical

Auffach is about 15 to 20 min by car from Niederau. The festival takes place at the Schatzberg valley station in the village centre.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Almabtrieb in Auffach?

Usually in September; on 19 September in 2026. The date can differ each year.

How many animals come down?

More than 500.
