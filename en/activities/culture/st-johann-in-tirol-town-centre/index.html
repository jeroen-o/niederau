    St. Johann in Tirol town centre

    A baroque market town with painted facades and a Friday market

    St. Johann in Tirol is a baroque market town at the foot of the Kitzbüheler Horn and the Wilder Kaiser. The centre has houses with Lüftlmalerei, painted facades, such as the Schellhornhaus and the Schwarzinger-Haus. The Antoniuskapelle, with a fresco by Josef Schöpf in its dome, is also worth a look. You will also find shops, cafés and restaurants, and a museum on the town's history.

    From roughly mid-March to November a weekly market is held on the main square every Friday, with regional products and handicrafts. A visit takes about an hour and a half; it combines well with Kitzbühel or the Kitzbüheler Horn. There is also a signed village circuit.

    Tips

    • Plan your visit for a Friday if you want to see the weekly market.
    • Look closely at the painted facades (Lüftlmalerei).
    • Park in one of the car parks near the centre.
    • Combine with Kitzbühel or the Kitzbüheler Horn.

    Practical

    St. Johann in Tirol is roughly 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the Brixental road (B170) or the A12/B178.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is the weekly market in St. Johann?

    Every Friday during the day on the main square; exact times can vary, so check beforehand.
