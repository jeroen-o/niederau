Sennerei Westendorf dairy

Cheese and dairy from the Brixental, straight from the maker

The Sennerei Westendorf is a small, family-run dairy in the village, in the Brixental. Among other things it makes Tilsiter, Bergkäse, beer cheese and Brodakas, as well as butter, yoghurt, cream and curd. Brodakas is a sour-milk cheese and a Brixental speciality that, according to tourist information, is only available here. Quality, regional roots and freshness come first.

The shop sells the dairy's own products along with regional items such as bacon and sausages, eggs, honey, jam, spices and tea. Milk and yoghurt are also sold loose; you can bring your own container or bottle for them.

It is a nice stop if you want to take local products home as souvenirs. Whether tours or tastings are on offer depends on the time; ask on site or via the tourist office. A visit is short and easy: you step in, look around, perhaps taste something if that is possible and take cheese home for your room or apartment. It suits a family outing in which children mostly enjoy seeing where milk and cheese come from.

Place: Westendorf

Tips

  • Check opening hours beforehand; the shop is not open all day.
  • Try the Brodakas, a Brixental speciality.
  • Buy cheese as a souvenir and keep it cool on the way.
  • Ask on site whether a tour or tasting is possible.
  • Bring your own bottle or container if you want to buy loose milk or yoghurt.

Practical

The Sennerei is in Westendorf, roughly 30 minutes by car from Niederau, in the Brixental. The shop is not open all day and closes for breaks on some days; check the current opening hours. You can combine the stop with a walk or a visit to the village, and bring a cool bag if you want to buy cheese. Westendorf itself has a pleasant village centre; the dairy can be combined in a short stop with a coffee or a walk through the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
