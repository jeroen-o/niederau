Niederau Village Evening

A relaxed village evening with regional food and the village brass band

In summer Niederau holds a regular village evening on the Dorfabendplatz next to the Markbachjochbahn. Local clubs provide food and drink and offer regional specialities, with music in the background. Around 8.15 pm the Bundesmusikkapelle Niederau marches onto the square and gives a concert.

It is an easy evening for families: a few minutes on foot or by car, a relaxed atmosphere and room for children to wander. In 2026 the series ran from 27 May to 2 September, from 7 pm to 10 pm. Dates and times may change from year to year, so ask at the tourist office or your accommodation.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 2 to 3 hours
  • CostFree entry; food and drink at your own cost
  • DistanceIn Niederau itself
  • SeasonSummer (in 2026 from late May to early September)
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Arrive around 7 pm to eat in peace before the concert starts.
  • Bring a jacket; it cools down quickly in the mountains at night.
  • Try the regional dishes from the clubs.
  • Check the current dates with the tourist office beforehand.

Practical

The Dorfabendplatz is in Niederau next to the Markbachjochbahn, within walking distance of many places to stay in the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What time does the concert start at the Niederau Village Evening?

The evening starts at 7 pm; around 8.15 pm the Bundesmusikkapelle Niederau marches in and the concert follows.
