Open-air band concert in Oberau

Brass band music from the village band on Tuesday evenings

In summer the Bundesmusikkapelle Oberau gives an open-air concert every Tuesday evening at 8 pm in the village centre, at the bandstand on the Dreizehnlindenplatz. In 2026 the series runs from 7 July to 1 September, nine concerts in all. The programme ranges from traditional brass music to more modern sounds.

It is a simple, relaxed evening: you sit or stand on the square, listen and watch the village come together. Children usually enjoy the instruments. Refreshments are available. Check the current dates beforehand, as they can change in bad weather.

Place: Oberau

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 1.5 hours
  • CostPlease enquire in advance
  • DistanceApprox. 5 min by car
  • SeasonSummer (2026: 7 July to 1 September)
  • LocationOberau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Arrive in good time for a good spot.
  • Bring a warm jumper; evenings cool down quickly.
  • Check the current dates in the events calendar.
  • In bad weather the concert may change or be cancelled.

Practical

Oberau is roughly 5 minutes by car from Niederau. The concert is at the bandstand on the Dreizehnlindenplatz in the village centre.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is the concert in Oberau?

Every Tuesday at 8 pm, in 2026 from 7 July to 1 September. Check current dates beforehand.
