Forest and Family Trail in Thierbach

A short, quiet circular walk in the highest village of the Wildschönau

Thierbach lies at about 1,150 metres, is the smallest and highest village of the Wildschönau and is known for its peace and quiet. The Wald- und Familienweg runs here, an easy circular walk of about 3.2 km that takes roughly an hour.

Along the way are energy and rest spots, an energy tree and a forest playground for children at the Koglweg. It makes a pleasant walk for discovering nature with children at their own pace. There are no big attractions, and that is precisely the charm. For those who want more, longer hikes also start from Thierbach. A loop through the village combines well with a stop at one of the inns.

Place: Thierbach

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 hour
  • CostFree
  • DistanceApprox. 10 to 15 min by car
  • SeasonMainly summer
  • LocationThierbach, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Bring a snack and water; a small village has few facilities.
  • Plan a play stop at the forest playground on the Koglweg.
  • Combine the walk with an easy hike up the slopes.
  • Respect pasture land and leave cows alone.

Practical

Thierbach is roughly 10 to 15 minutes by car from Niederau, in the highest part of the valley. Ask the tourist office about the best starting point and any public transport.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Thierbach suitable for children?

Yes, the Wald- und Familienweg is short and easy and has a forest playground for children.
