Innsbruck old town and Hofburg

A city day out with the Golden Roof, the Hofburg and the Nordkette

Innsbruck is the capital of Tyrol and a pleasant city for a day out: the old town is compact and largely car-free. The Golden Roof, the balcony with 2,657 gilded copper shingles built for Emperor Maximilian I, is the best-known landmark. Close by are the Hofburg and the medieval arcaded streets.

If you like, combine the city with the Nordkette cable car, which climbs from the centre to the Hafelekar in three stages. A pass such as the Innsbruck Card can pay off if you visit several sights and lifts; work out beforehand whether it fits your plan. With children, a partial programme usually works better than trying to do everything in one day.

Tips

  • Take the train from Wörgl; parking in the centre is expensive and busy.
  • Work out in advance whether the Innsbruck Card pays off for your plans.
  • Ride the Nordkette cable car in the morning when it is quieter.
  • Choose two or three sights per day instead of trying to see everything.

Practical

Innsbruck is about 1 hour by car from Niederau via the A12. The train works too: take a regional train or Railjet from Wörgl to Innsbruck Hbf (approx. 30 to 40 minutes); the old town is a short walk from the station.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to Innsbruck without a car?

By train from Wörgl to Innsbruck Hbf; the old town is within walking distance of the station.
