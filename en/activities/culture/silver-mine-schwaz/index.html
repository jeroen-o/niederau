    Culture in the Wildschönau and surroundings · Summer

    Silver mine Schwaz

    By mine train into the mountain

    In the late Middle Ages Schwaz was one of Europe's largest mining towns. Silver was extracted around the town, and a large share of the silver traded at the time came from here. Today the Silberbergwerk is a visitor mine.

    A mine train takes you about 800 metres into the mountain, after which you continue on foot with a guide through galleries where miners worked in the dark for centuries. A tour takes about an hour and a half. It is around 12 degrees in the mine all year, so bring warm clothing and sturdy shoes. That makes it a good outing for a hot summer day or a rainy one.

    The Silberbergwerk has been a visitor mine since 1990, in the Sigmund-Erbstollen. In the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries Schwaz grew into Europe's largest mining city thanks to silver and copper, and the Fuggers played a major role. Jakob Fugger the Rich entered Schwaz mining in 1522. It is a good choice for a day of changeable weather: in the mine you notice neither rain nor heat. A visit gives a clear picture of how hard and dangerous the mining work was and why Schwaz grew so rich.

    Tips

    • Bring warm clothing and sturdy shoes; it is around 12 °C inside.
    • The mine train is a highlight for children.
    • Reserve or check tour times beforehand.
    • Children may come along, but they must be able to walk or be carried.
    • A pushchair cannot go into the mine; leave it behind.

    Practical

    Schwaz is roughly 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via the A12 towards Innsbruck (exit Schwaz). The ride on the mine train takes about seven minutes and goes some 800 metres into the mountain. After the ride you walk through the galleries with a guide, so bring warm clothing and sturdy shoes. Check the current tour times and booking options beforehand. When planning, allow for the tour times and about an hour and a half for the tour itself.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How cold is it in the mine?

    Around 12 degrees all year, so bring warm clothing even in summer.

    How long is the ride on the mine train?

    About seven minutes, to around 800 metres deep into the mountain.

    Can I take a pushchair into the mine?

    No, children must be able to walk or be carried, as you walk through the galleries after the ride in.
