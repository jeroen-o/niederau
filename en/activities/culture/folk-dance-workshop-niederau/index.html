Culture in the Wildschönau and surroundings · Summer

Learning folk dancing

Spinning, stamping and clapping to a Tyrolean beat

Tyrolean folk dances are a living tradition, including the Ländler, a waltz-like couple dance, and the Schuhplattler, in which dancers slap their shoes and thighs. In the Wildschönau the costume and music tradition is kept up, for example through the village bands and festivals.

On request, some accommodations or inns may arrange a short introduction to folk dancing, often with a local dance leader. The offer and times vary, so ask the tourist office or your accommodation whether it is available. Children usually find the Schuhplattler funny, and laughing is part of it.

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 hour
  • CostPrice varies; ask in advance
  • DistanceIn or near Niederau
  • SeasonSummer (varying offer)
  • LocationNiederau, Wildschönau

Tips

  • Ask your accommodation or the tourist office whether an introduction is on.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and clothes you can move in.
  • Take time to laugh; nobody expects perfection.
  • Combine with a village evening or concert in Niederau.

Practical

The offer is in or near Niederau, so usually reachable on foot or with a short ride. Ask in advance about location and times.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
