Visiting Zell am Ziller

Lively Zillertal village with the Gauderfest and the Zillertalbahn

Zell am Ziller lies in the Zillertal and is a traditional village with Tyrolean-style houses and the baroque parish church of St Veit from the 18th century, whose tower and baptismal font survive from a Gothic predecessor. It is best known for the Gauderfest, a large spring and costume festival held every year on the first weekend of May, with a Sunday parade of more than 2,000 people in traditional dress.

In summer it is a pleasant stop for coffee, a stroll through the village and a visit to the church. It also lies on the Zillertalbahn route between Jenbach and Mayrhofen; check the timetable if you want to ride the steam train. Zell itself is not a big programme, so combine it with another activity in the valley.

Place: Zell am Ziller

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationApprox. 1 to 2 hours
  • CostWalking around is free
  • DistanceApprox. 55 to 60 min by car
  • SeasonAll year; Gauderfest in early May
  • LocationZell am Ziller, Zillertal

Tips

  • The Gauderfest is on the first weekend of May; in summer the village is quieter.
  • Check the Zillertalbahn timetable if you want to ride the steam train.
  • Take a look inside the baroque parish church of St Veit.
  • Combine the village with a cable car, a walk or another outing in the Zillertal.

Practical

Zell am Ziller is about 55 to 60 minutes by car from Niederau: via Wörgl on the A12 to Jenbach and then up the Zillertal road. There is parking in the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Gauderfest in Zell am Ziller?

Every year on the first weekend of May.
