Ambras Castle, Innsbruck

Renaissance castle with a cabinet of curiosities above Innsbruck

Archduke Ferdinand II (1529-1595) had medieval Ambras extended into a Renaissance palace as a gift for his wife Philippine Welser, a merchant's daughter from Augsburg. The castle is regarded as the oldest museum in the world and keeps the archduke's collections.

The Kunst- und Wunderkammer is the only Renaissance cabinet of art and curiosities preserved in its original location; it includes ivory objects, a glass harmonica and a samurai armour. The Spanish Hall is a ceremonial hall about 23 metres long with a rich wooden coffered ceiling and counts among the finest Renaissance halls. The Habsburg portrait gallery holds over 200 portraits from the 14th to the 18th centuries, and Philippine's bath has survived too. Ferdinand also built up an armoury.

A large park with ponds surrounds the castle, where children can run around after the visit. Allow about two hours. The castle stands on a hill above the town, so even without visiting the halls the walk through the park with its views is a good reason to come. Combine it with a visit to the centre of Innsbruck if you want a full day of culture.

Tips

  • Visit the Wunderkammer; children usually enjoy the unusual objects.
  • Take your time in the Spanish Hall.
  • Use the park for a break or picnic after the visit.
  • Look at the Habsburg faces in the portrait gallery and let the children pick a favourite.
  • Look out for the samurai armour and other unusual pieces.

Practical

Ambras Castle is on the eastern side of Innsbruck, about 1 hour by car from Niederau via the A12. It makes a good day trip for a day of changeable weather. Check opening times, prices and public transport beforehand; the museum may be closed at certain times of the year. You can combine the visit with a stroll through the centre of Innsbruck. An audio guide or tour makes the collections more accessible for children.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
