Ambras Castle, Innsbruck

Renaissance castle with a cabinet of curiosities above Innsbruck

Archduke Ferdinand II (1529-1595) had medieval Ambras extended into a Renaissance palace as a gift for his wife Philippine Welser, a merchant's daughter from Augsburg. The castle is regarded as the oldest museum in the world and keeps the archduke's collections.

The Kunst- und Wunderkammer is the only Renaissance cabinet of art and curiosities preserved in its original location. The Spanish Hall counts among the finest Renaissance halls, and Philippine's bath has survived too. A park surrounds the castle, where children can run around after the visit. Allow about two hours.

Place: Innsbruck

Tips

  • Visit the Wunderkammer; children usually enjoy the unusual objects.
  • Take your time in the Spanish Hall.
  • Use the park for a break or picnic after the visit.

Practical

Ambras Castle is on the eastern side of Innsbruck, about 1 hour by car from Niederau via the A12. Check opening times, prices and public transport beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
