European Forum Alpbach

Annual international conference in the Tyrolean mountain village of Alpbach

Since 1945 scientists, politicians, business people, artists and students have gathered in Alpbach at the end of each summer for the European Forum. For over two weeks the programme is filled with lectures, debates and workshops, in a village otherwise known for its timber houses and flower-decked balconies.

For a family on holiday this is not a standard day out: the programme is aimed mainly at participants, and access and tickets vary by event. The Euregio Days were free to attend in 2026, so it is worth checking what is open to the public. Even without the forum, Alpbach is a lovely place for a walk or a terrace stop.

Place: Alpbach

Tips

  • Check early on the official website which parts are open to the public and whether you need a ticket.
  • Expect crowds and limited parking during the forum; arrive early or take the bus.
  • Combine the visit with a walk through the village or a short trail nearby.

Practical

Alpbach lies in the Alpbachtal valley, about 35 to 40 minutes by car from Niederau via Wörgl and the valley road. The forum takes place in the village and nearby congress buildings; ask in advance about access and parking.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When does the European Forum Alpbach take place?

Every year at the end of summer; the 81st edition ran from 24 August to 4 September 2026. Check the dates of future editions on the official website.
