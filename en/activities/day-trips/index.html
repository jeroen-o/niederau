Day trips from Niederau

Innsbruck, Kitzbühel, the Zillertal and more: many destinations within an hour's drive.

Niederau is well placed for anyone who wants to look further afield from the Wildschönau. A short road links the valley to the Inn valley, and from there a good number of destinations lie roughly an hour away: Kufstein and Kitzbühel, the Alpbachtal, the Zillertal and the city of Innsbruck. Even a half-day gets you quite far if you take it easy.

The variety is large. You can visit a town with old streets and a fortress, a valley full of mountain lifts and alpine pastures, or a lake where children can swim. For families a day trip works best when you keep the drive short and choose one or two things rather than trying to see everything.

Distances and drive times in this overview are approximate and depend on the season, traffic and roadworks. Use a current route planner and check opening times, toll roads and any vignette requirement before you leave.

Tips

  • Pick one main goal a day and leave the rest open.
  • Set off early to avoid traffic and crowds.
  • Check weather, road conditions and winter equipment before leaving.
  • Ask about the vignette for Austrian motorways.
  • Take a map or offline route in case reception drops.

Short drives, plenty to see

Children quickly tire of long car journeys. Choose a destination within about an hour's drive and plan a break on the way. In the mountains a drive can take longer than the kilometres suggest. Bring water, snacks and something to do in the car. A train trip through the Inn valley or the Zillertal can be a pleasant change, but check timetables and connections.

Season, weather and rules on the road

In winter, snow chains or winter tyres may be required or advisable, and some mountain roads close. In summer thunderstorms, small mountain roads and lakes are less attractive. A vignette is needed for Austrian motorways, and some alpine roads charge separate tolls. Find out in advance, for instance from your host or a traffic organisation. Parking in towns is often paid.

Frequently asked questions

Which towns are easy to reach?

Wörgl, Kufstein and Rattenberg are close; Kitzbühel and Innsbruck are about an hour's drive or slightly more, depending on traffic.

Can I use public transport?

Yes, buses run from the valley to Wörgl station, where trains continue. It takes more planning; check the current timetable.

What is a good day trip in the rain?

A town with museums and covered spaces, such as Innsbruck or Hall in Tirol, works well. Check opening times beforehand.
