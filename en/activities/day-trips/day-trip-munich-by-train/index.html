Day trip to Munich by train

A city day in Bavaria: Marienplatz, Englischer Garten and museums

Munich is little more than an hour by train from Wörgl, which makes it a feasible day out from Niederau. The Bavarian capital has a largely pedestrianised centre around Marienplatz, where the carillon of the New Town Hall plays at set times, and big green spaces such as the Englischer Garten, where surfers ride the standing wave on the Eisbach.

Families have plenty to choose from: the Deutsches Museum with its many hands-on exhibits, Hellabrunn Zoo, or an afternoon in a traditional beer garden. Going by train means no hunting for parking, and a full day is wise, as there is enough to fill several visits.

Place: Wörgl

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationFull day
  • CostTrain fares and admissions vary; check day tickets beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 1.5 to 2 hours by car, or about 20 min to Wörgl plus about 1 h 15 by train
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationMunich, Germany

Tips

  • The carillon on Marienplatz plays around 11 am and 12 noon according to common information; check current times.
  • Look into day tickets and group fares for the train beforehand; conditions change.
  • Plan one or two main visits (for example a museum and the park) rather than everything.
  • The Eisbach wave in the Englischer Garten can be watched for free from the bridge.

Practical

From Wörgl Hbf the train to Munich Hbf takes a little over an hour (about 1 hour 10 to 15 minutes; direct trains run too). Wörgl is roughly 20 minutes by car from Niederau. By car Munich is about 1.5 to 2 hours away, depending on traffic and border queues.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the train from Wörgl to Munich?

About 1 hour 10 to 15 minutes to Munich Hbf; direct trains also run. Add roughly 20 minutes' drive from Niederau to Wörgl.
