Kufstein Fortress

Fortress above the town with towers, museums and a huge open-air organ

The Kufstein Fortress rises high above the Inn and the town. It has centuries of history, with towers, courtyards, casemates and exhibitions about the region. The view across the Inn valley and towards the Kaisergebirge is a highlight.

A special feature is the Heldenorgel (Heroes' Organ), one of the world's largest open-air organs, which plays daily at noon; in July and August there is reportedly a second concert in the early evening. You reach the top on foot along the old ramps or by panoramic lift. Allow two to three hours for a visit, and dress warmly: it is often windy up there.

Place: Kufstein

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2 to 3 hours
  • CostAdmission varies; children pay less, check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 30 to 35 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationKufstein, Tyrol

Tips

  • The organ plays daily at 12 noon and can also be heard from the town; listen from the fortress for the full effect.
  • Let children go up along the ramps or by lift; check beforehand that the lift is running.
  • Dress warmly, especially in winter.
  • Combine with a stroll through Kufstein's centre.

Practical

Kufstein is about 30 to 35 minutes' drive from Niederau, via the A12. Parking is available in town; the lift to the fortress starts in the centre.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Heldenorgel play in Kufstein?

Daily at noon; in July and August also in the early evening according to sources. Check current times.
