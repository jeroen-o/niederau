Kitzbühel old town walk

Traditional façades, churches and shopping lanes in the Gams town

Kitzbühel is best known for winter sports and the Hahnenkamm races, but its old town is worth a visit even without skis. The two main streets, Vorderstadt and Hinterstadt, are pedestrian zones lined with traditional houses with oriel windows, Gothic gables and heavy overhanging eaves, plus cafés and shops.

At one end of the old town stand the parish church and the Liebfrauenkirche, with the Ölberg chapel between them. A stroll takes about two to three hours with stops. It is a calm programme for a day with poorer weather, and walking the streets costs nothing.

Place: Kitzbühel

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 2 to 3 hours
  • CostWalking is free; parking and refreshments extra
  • DistanceAbout 40 to 45 min by car
  • SeasonAll year
  • LocationKitzbühel, Tyrol

Tips

  • Park in a car park on the edge and walk the last stretch.
  • Combine the town with Museum Kitzbühel or a coffee break.
  • Look up current events in the town before visiting.
  • In summer, consider adding a mountain lift or the Schwarzsee.

Practical

Kitzbühel is about 40 to 45 minutes' drive from Niederau. Park in a car park; the old town itself is pedestrianised.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kitzbühel's old town car-free?

Yes, the two main streets, Vorderstadt and Hinterstadt, are pedestrian zones, so park in a nearby car park.
