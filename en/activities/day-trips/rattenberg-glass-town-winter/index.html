Rattenberg glass town in winter

Glass workshops and a medieval old town, even on a cold day

With around 440 inhabitants Rattenberg is Austria's smallest town, built against a rock face above the Inn, with a medieval core of small houses and lanes. It is known as the glass town of Tyrol.

At Kisslinger Kristall-Glas, active since 1820, you can watch glassblowers at work and browse the shop with its glassware. According to the operator, blowing your own bauble is only possible from May to October, so in winter you mainly come to look and shop. It is a good option in cold or wet weather; combine it with a walk through the old town and, if you like, the Augustinian Museum.

Place: Rattenberg

  • AgeAll ages
  • DurationAbout 1 to 2 hours
  • CostCheck access and prices beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 30 min by car
  • SeasonAll year; blowing your own only May to October
  • LocationRattenberg, Alpbachtal

Tips

  • Blowing your own is only possible from May to October; in winter you mainly watch.
  • Check beforehand whether demonstrations are on and what the opening hours are.
  • Afterwards take a lap around the car-free old town.
  • Pack fragile purchases well for the journey home.

Practical

Rattenberg is about 30 minutes' drive from Niederau, on the Inn in the Alpbachtal. Parking is possible at the edge of town; by train, Rattenberg-Kramsach station is within walking distance.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can I blow glass myself in Rattenberg in winter?

According to the operator this is only possible from May to October; in winter you can mainly watch and shop. Check current options beforehand.
