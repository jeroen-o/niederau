Olympic bob run Igls

Ride as a passenger in a guest bob on the Winter Games track

At Igls, high above Innsbruck, lies the Olympic bobsleigh and luge track, the Olympia Eiskanal, where the 1964 and 1976 Winter Olympics were held. In the winter season guests can ride in a guest bob with an experienced pilot and a brakeman in a genuine racing bob.

The ride is short but fast: the bob reaches speeds of up to about 115 km/h, with bends and strong lateral forces. It is an intense experience, not for young children or anyone prone to motion sickness. The minimum age is 10 years. Booking is recommended; check times, prices and rules on the official site.

The track is about 1,270 metres long and has 14 bends, including a spectacular roundabout. For the experience you wear clothing suited to the season, sturdy shoes and gloves; a helmet is provided. Besides the guest bob there is a newer variant, bob rafting, with speeds up to about 95 km/h and room for 3 to 4 people without a pilot. Anyone who prefers to watch can also soak up the atmosphere on the track in Igls; the track is an important part of Innsbruck's sporting past. For those who dare, riding along is an experience you will not soon forget.

Place: Innsbruck

Tips

  • Book ahead; places may be limited.
  • The minimum age is 10 years; check the current rules.
  • Wear gloves and sturdy shoes; a helmet is provided on site.
  • Check the current age and height limits, as they differ per variant.
  • Consider bob rafting if you are going in a group of 3 or 4.

Practical

Igls is about 55 to 65 minutes' drive from Niederau via the A12 and Innsbruck. The village lies high above Innsbruck, on the hills south of the city. Book ahead and ask about times, age limits and parking; in winter, allow for road conditions. You can combine the ride with a visit to Innsbruck's old town. Allow some extra time for the drive and for checking in at the track.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How fast is a guest bob?

Up to about 115 km/h; you ride with a pilot and a brakeman in a genuine racing bob.

How long is the bob track in Igls?

About 1,270 metres, with 14 bends.

What is the difference between guest bob and bob rafting?

The guest bob reaches up to about 115 km/h with a pilot; bob rafting up to about 95 km/h, for 3 to 4 people without a pilot. Age limits differ.
