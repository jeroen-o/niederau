Olympic bob run Igls

Ride as a passenger in a guest bob on the Winter Games track

At Igls, high above Innsbruck, lies the Olympic bobsleigh and luge track, the Olympia Eiskanal, where the 1964 and 1976 Winter Olympics were held. In the winter season guests can ride in a guest bob with an experienced pilot and a brakeman in a genuine racing bob.

The ride is short but fast: the bob reaches speeds of up to about 115 km/h, with bends and strong lateral forces. It is an intense experience, not for young children or anyone prone to motion sickness. The minimum age is 10 years. Booking is recommended; check times, prices and rules on the official site.

Place: Innsbruck

Official website

  • AgeFrom 10 years
  • DurationShort ride; about 1 hour in total
  • CostPrice varies; check beforehand
  • DistanceAbout 55 to 65 min by car

  • LocationIgls, Innsbruck

Tips

  • Book ahead; places may be limited.
  • The minimum age is 10 years; check the current rules.
  • Dress warmly and wear sturdy shoes.
  • Combine with a visit to Innsbruck's old town.

Practical

Igls is about 55 to 65 minutes' drive from Niederau via the A12 and Innsbruck. Book and ask about times and parking beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How fast is a guest bob?

Up to about 115 km/h; you ride with a pilot and a brakeman in a genuine racing bob.
