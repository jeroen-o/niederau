    Swarovski Crystal Worlds

    Art and crystal in and beneath the Giant at Wattens, with a play tower

    The Swarovski Crystal Worlds are in Wattens, next to the crystal company's headquarters. The entrance is the head of a giant about 17 m tall with a stream of water from its mouth; beneath it lie the Chambers of Wonder, a series of artistic rooms combining crystal, light and art.

    For families there is a play tower on four levels and a maze, plus a park with artworks. It is an indoor-friendly programme that also works in poor weather. Allow two to three hours, and check current opening hours and admission prices on the official site beforehand; there is also a shop and a restaurant.

    Place: Wattens

    • AgeAll ages
    • DurationAbout 2 to 3 hours
    • CostAdmission varies; check beforehand
    • DistanceAbout 40 to 45 min by car
    • SeasonAll year
    • LocationWattens, Tyrol

    Tips

    • Buy tickets online where possible and check current opening hours.
    • Come on a weekday morning for fewer crowds.
    • Plan the play tower for children who tire of the art.
    • Parking is available on site; check the cost.

    Practical

    Wattens is about 40 to 45 minutes' drive from Niederau, via the A12 towards Innsbruck, exit Wattens. By public transport you travel via Innsbruck or Wattens with a bus.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a play area for children?

    Yes, there is a four-level play tower and a maze, plus a playground in the park.
