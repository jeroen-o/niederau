    The hotel by the slopes in the Wildschönau

    Hotel Wastlhof in Niederau: right by the slope, with the drag lift in front of the door and the ski storage.

    If you are looking for a hotel by the slopes in the Wildschönau, look at Niederau. Hotel Wastlhof stands in the middle of the village: a four-star hotel with wellness, run by the Brunner family for three generations. The hotel stands right by the slope: the drag lift is in front of the door of the house and the ski storage, so you simply step in. The cross-country trails and the walking paths also start almost at the door. Hotel Wastlhof is a partner of this site; we have been coming there ourselves for more than fifty years.

    Winter: the drag lift at the door

    The hotel stands right by the slope. The drag lift is in front of the door of the house and the ski storage: you put on your skis and simply step into the lift. According to the hotel’s information, the ski bus stop is about 50 m from the house, the toboggan run about 100 m, the Lahnerköpfl lift about 400 m and the Markbachjochbahn gondola to the Markbachjoch about 500 m. The nearest cross-country trail starts right at the house. With enough snow you can ski back to the hotel. Distances are approximate.

    Sledges can be hired. If you want to get to know the slopes, hostess Margit takes you on a ski tour of her favourite runs.

    Summer: garden, tennis and walking paths

    In summer the hotel has a relaxation garden with a generous outdoor pool, a sunbathing lawn and parasols. Guests play tennis free of charge at the Wildschönau tennis complex, and the walking paths start almost at the door. The hotel also has its own riding stables with about fifteen horses, including Haflingers; they have been there for more than fifty years.

    After the ski day: pool and wellness

    After a day outdoors you can head for the indoor pool (12 × 6 m, about 30 °C) or the outdoor pool, which is heated all year round. The hotel also has a wellness area. That is exactly the combination families like: the children in the water, the parents in the wellness.

    Rooms, apartments and suites

    You can choose from rooms, apartments and suites in the hotel and in the new Alpin Lodge. The hotel’s website and information are also available in Dutch. For prices, room types and availability, always check the hotel’s own website. You may also find the hotel under its older name, Sport- und Vitalhotel Wastlhof.

    • Hotel Wastlhof, Wildschönauerstraße Niederau 206, 6314 Wildschönau, Austria

    A personal recommendation

    We have been coming to the Wastlhof for more than fifty years, in summer and winter. This is a personal recommendation and not an advertisement: Hotel Wastlhof is a partner of Niederau.nl and provided photos; no fee was paid or received.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Hotel Wastlhof really by the slopes?

    Yes. The hotel stands right by the slope: the drag lift is in front of the door of the house and the ski storage, so you simply step into the lift. The hotel itself is in the middle of Niederau.

    Is the hotel also worth it in summer?

    Yes. There is a garden with an outdoor pool, tennis, walking paths close to the hotel and its own riding stables. See also our pages on hiking and the Wildschönau.
