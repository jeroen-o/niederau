    Privacy policy

    What data this website processes: very little.

    Niederau.nl is built without tracking. No cookies are set, there is no analytics software and no advertising networks. Below is exactly what does happen.

    Who is responsible?

    Jeroen Oversteegen, Darthuizerberg 1, 3825 BK Amersfoort, The Netherlands. Email: jeroen@oversteegen.nl. See also the legal notice.

    Visiting the website

    The website uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. No external fonts, scripts, maps or videos are loaded. Searching the site happens entirely in your own browser; search terms are not sent or stored.

    The pages are delivered through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). In doing so, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and prevent abuse. I have no access to this data and do not use it. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

    Contact by email

    If you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering questions (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I keep the email no longer than necessary and do not pass it on to third parties.

    Links to other websites

    If you click a link to another website, that website’s privacy policy applies. I have no influence over it.

    Your rights

    You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your personal data. Email jeroen@oversteegen.nl for this. If you disagree with how I handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

    Changes

    If the website changes, for example by adding a new service, I will update this statement. The date of the last change is at the bottom of the page.
