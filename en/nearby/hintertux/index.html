Zillertal

Hintertux

Glacier ski area at the head of the Tuxertal (municipality of Tux)

Hintertux is the highest village in the Tuxertal, a side valley of the Zillertal near Mayrhofen. It belongs to the municipality of Tux and is best known for the Hintertux Glacier, a glacier ski area where skiing is said to be possible almost all year round.

The area is also interesting outside the ski season. The high mountains and the glacier give a landscape quite different from the Wildschönau, and the valley offers various walking options. Bear in mind that it can be cold on the glacier even in summer.

For families staying in Niederau this is a longer day trip: a drive of well over an hour and a half. Check the glacier lifts' operating times and snow conditions beforehand, and dress warmly.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 95 min.
  • RegionZillertal
  • Best forSuits skiers and walkers who want to be high in the mountains.

What to see and do

  • The Hintertux Glacier: skiing and snowboarding, including outside the usual winter season.
  • Glacier lifts to high altitude for mountain views, in summer too; check operating times beforehand.
  • Walking in the Tuxertal and the high mountain surroundings.
  • A drive through the Tuxertal, branching off the Zillertal at Mayrhofen.
  • Combine the day with a visit to Mayrhofen on the way there or back.

From Niederau

About 95 minutes by car from Niederau: via Wörgl, along the A12 to the Zillertal exit, through the Zillertal to Mayrhofen and then up the Tuxertal to the head of the valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hintertux in the Zillertal?

Hintertux is in the Tuxertal, a side valley of the Zillertal near Mayrhofen, and belongs to the municipality of Tux.

Can you ski there in summer too?

The glacier ski area is promoted as usable almost all year, but conditions vary; check the current situation beforehand.
