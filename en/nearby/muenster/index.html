Münster

Quiet village in the Tyrolean lowlands, near Lake Zireiner and the Rofan range

Münster (in Tirol) sits on a low rise at the foot of the Rofan mountains in the Alpbachtal, at about 534 m. The village has just over 3,000 residents and lies near the River Inn between Wörgl and Rattenberg. It is a calm place without heavy tourist crowds.

The surroundings are what it is best known for: Lake Zireiner, a small mountain lake beneath the Rofanspitze, is regarded as one of the most photographed mountain lakes in Tyrol. In the village you will find the parish church and several chapels, and the coat of arms shows a dancer with two gloves, a nod to the glove dance held on Whit Monday.

For families, Münster works well as the base for a relaxed day out: a swim at the open-air pool with its large sunbathing lawn, a mountain walk, a horse-drawn carriage ride or, in winter, a toboggan run. From Niederau it is roughly half an hour away. Bear in mind that the village itself is small and has no big nightlife. If you want a full day, Münster combines well with Rattenberg or Alpbach. In the mountains always bring sturdy shoes and clothing for changeable weather, and check the forecast and opening times beforehand.

What to see and do

  • Lake Zireiner and the Rofanspitze (2,259 m) are a popular summer hiking goal.
  • The Vorderes Sonnwendjoch (2,224 m) attracts experienced hikers.
  • The open-air pool with a sunbathing lawn of almost 5,000 m² is a good summer stop for children.
  • The Höllenstein toboggan run (about 4.3 km) is lit in its lower section, handy in winter.
  • A large horse stud in the village offers rides and carriage trips; ask ahead about availability.
  • The parish church and chapels such as the Grünangerl chapel make a short cultural stop.

From Niederau

About 25 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then along the Inn valley towards Rattenberg/Münster.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How high is Münster?

The village lies at about 534 m; the municipal area reaches into the Rofan range and includes the Vorderes Sonnwendjoch (2,224 m).

Is there something to do in Münster in hot weather?

Yes, the village has an open-air pool with a large sunbathing lawn; check the opening times beforehand.
