Wildschönau

Oberau

The main village of the Wildschönau, right next to Niederau

Oberau is the largest and most central village in the Wildschönau and lies a short distance from Niederau. It is home to the municipal office, the tourist office, shops, supermarkets and several places to eat. For families staying in Niederau it is therefore the place for groceries and a quick restaurant visit.

It is also interesting for activities. The Bergbauernmuseum z'Bach shows how mountain farmers used to live and work, and is a pleasant way to introduce children to the old life of the valley. The village also has its own swimming facilities and a summer programme for children. Do check current opening times beforehand.

Because distances in the valley are small, Oberau combines easily with a walk or cycle ride along the valley. It is not a place with one big attraction, but one with a pleasant, practical village feel and plenty to do in the surroundings.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 5 min.
  • RegionWildschönau
  • Best forSuits families looking for something practical and close by.

What to see and do

  • Bergbauernmuseum z'Bach: a look at how mountain farmers once lived
  • Swimming facilities in the village, handy on warm summer days
  • Shops and supermarkets for groceries and souvenirs
  • Walking and cycling routes through the valley, starting in or near the village
  • In winter: cross-country trails and toboggan runs in the valley, nearby

From Niederau

About 5 minutes by car from Niederau, along the main valley road through the Wildschönau.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Oberau far from Niederau?

No, Oberau is about 5 minutes by car from Niederau, in the same valley.

What is there for children in Oberau?

Among other things the Bergbauernmuseum z'Bach and the swimming facilities; check opening times beforehand.
