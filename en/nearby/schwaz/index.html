    Schwaz

    Old mining town in the Inn valley with silver heritage and a show mine.

    Schwaz lies at just over 530 m in the Inn valley and is the centre of the Silberregion Karwendel. In the 15th and 16th centuries it was one of the largest mining towns in Europe, thanks to silver and copper. Around 1500 most of Europe's silver reportedly came from the mines here.

    The Silberbergwerk is the best-known sight: a mine train carries you about 800 m into the mountain, followed by a tour that classically lasts around 90 minutes. High above the town stands Freundsberg Castle, built around 1150, with a museum on the town's history. In the centre the large parish church with two bell towers stands out.

    For families staying in Niederau, Schwaz makes a good day trip combining history and hands-on experience, also in less settled weather. Bring warm clothing for the mine and check opening times and age advice beforehand. The mine stays about equally cool all year, a welcome change even on hot summer days. For very small children it is wise to ask beforehand whether the tour is suitable.

    What to see and do

    • Visit the Silberbergwerk, with the mine train ride and a guided tour.
    • Freundsberg Castle above the town, with a museum on the town's history.
    • The large parish church with two bell towers in the centre.
    • Stroll through the historic town centre.
    • Combine with Jenbach or a trip into the Zillertal.

    From Niederau

    About 40 minutes by car from Niederau, roughly via Wörgl and the Inn valley motorway (A12).

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Schwaz suitable for children?

    Yes, especially the Silberbergwerk with its mine train appeals to children. Bring warm clothes and check the age advice.

    How far is Schwaz from Niederau?

    About 40 minutes by car, depending on traffic.
