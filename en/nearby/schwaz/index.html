Schwaz

Old mining town in the Inn valley with silver heritage and a show mine.

Schwaz lies in the Inn valley and, in the late Middle Ages and early modern period, was one of Tyrol's most important mining towns, mainly for silver and copper. That heritage still shapes the town.

The main visitor attraction is the Silberbergwerk show mine, where a small train takes you into the mountain; check opening times and age advice. In the centre stands the large Gothic parish church of Schwaz, an impressive building.

For families staying in Niederau, Schwaz makes a good day trip that mixes history with hands-on experience. Warm clothing is useful underground.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 40 min.
  • RegionInn valley
  • Best forSuits families wanting history and experience.

What to see and do

  • Visit the Silberbergwerk, including the mine train ride.
  • See the Gothic parish church in the centre.
  • Freundsberg Castle above the town (check accessibility).
  • Stroll through the historic town centre.
  • Combine with Jenbach or a trip into the Zillertal.

From Niederau

About 40 minutes by car from Niederau, roughly via Wörgl and the Inn valley motorway (A12).

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Schwaz suitable for children?

Yes, especially the show mine with its mine train appeals to children. Bring warm clothes and check age advice.

How far is Schwaz from Niederau?

About 40 minutes by car.
