Alpbachtal

Alpbach

Tyrol's flower village in a side valley of the Alpbachtal

Alpbach is a mountain village in a side valley of the Alpbachtal and is known as one of the prettiest villages in Austria. The wooden farmhouses with flower boxes, the small church and the sloping meadows make it photogenic. The building style is strikingly uniform, because the village has long safeguarded its character.

Beyond tourism, Alpbach is known for the European Forum Alpbach, an annual gathering of academics, politicians and thinkers held in August. In summer the village is a good base for walks in the surrounding mountains, and in winter it is part of the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area.

For families from Niederau, Alpbach makes a lovely half-day or full-day outing: a walk, a coffee on a terrace and a stroll through the village centre. It is also a cosy destination in winter.

What to see and do

  • Village centre with wooden farmhouses and flowers

  • Walks in the surrounding mountains (summer)
  • Skiing in Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau (winter)
  • Cosy terraces and mountain huts around the village

From Niederau

About 35 minutes by car from Niederau, via Oberau and the Inn Valley road towards Brixlegg, then up into Alpbach.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is Alpbach known for?

For its uniform wooden building style, its flowers and the annual European Forum Alpbach in August.

Can you ski in Alpbach from Niederau?

Alpbach belongs to Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau, as does Niederau, so one ski pass covers the area.
