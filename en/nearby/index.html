Villages and towns around Niederau

From Oberau to Innsbruck: places to explore from the Wildschönau.

Niederau lies at the entrance to the Wildschönau, at about 828 metres, and from this valley the surroundings are easy to explore. In the valley itself lie Oberau, Auffach and Thierbach, each with its own character: Oberau lies in the middle of the valley at about 936 metres, Thierbach higher up at around 1,180 metres. Just outside the valley the Inn valley begins, with larger places such as Wörgl, Kufstein, Schwaz, Hall in Tirol and Innsbruck, with shops, museums and old town centres.

This page gives an overview of the villages and towns in the wider area, grouped by region: the Wildschönau, the Alpbachtal with Alpbach and Rattenberg, the Brixental with Kitzbühel, the Kufsteinerland, the Inn valley and the Zillertal. For each place you can read what makes it worth a visit and roughly how far it is from Niederau.

Distances are approximate and depend on traffic and season. Many destinations lie within about an hour's drive, some within half an hour. Always check opening times and events beforehand with the organisation or municipality.

Wildschönau

The quiet side valley where Niederau lies: farming villages, meadows and Ski Juwel.

Oberau

Central village in the Wildschönau, right next to Niederau

Auffach

Village at the foot of the Schatzberg, with a cable car and hiking area

Thierbach

The smallest and highest church village of the Wildschönau, at about 1,150 m

Mühltal

Hamlet near Oberau, starting point for the Kundler Klamm

Alpbachtal

Flower-filled mountain village Alpbach and the valleys around Reith, Brixlegg and Kramsach.

Alpbach

Tyrol's flower village at about 1,000 m, known for its timber buildings

Reith im Alpbachtal

Village at the entrance of the Alpbachtal, with a lake and the Juppi Zauberwald

Brixlegg

Market town in the Inn Valley with mining history and a museum

Rattenberg

Austria's smallest town, known for glass and Inn-Salzach houses

Kramsach

Village with an open-air museum and small lakes by the Inn

Kundl

Inn Valley village, start of the Kundler Klamm gorge to the Wildschönau

Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

SkiWelt ski area, Kitzbühel and Tyrolean villages with meadows and mountains.

Hopfgarten im Brixental

Brixental village with a gondola up the Hohe Salve

Itter

Small village with Schloss Itter in the Brixental

Brixen im Thale

Village at the highest point of the Brixental, with the Filzalmsee

Westendorf

Flower-filled village below the Choralpe, with the Alpinolino children's area

Kirchberg in Tirol

Lively village in the Brixental, at the mouth of the Spertental

Kitzbühel

Historic town in the Kitzbühel Alps, known for the Hahnenkamm race

Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Kufstein fortress, the Wilder Kaiser mountains and lakes for swimming.

Söll

Village by the Wilder Kaiser with Hexenwasser and the Hohe Salve

Ellmau

Village below the Wilder Kaiser, known from the TV series Der Bergdoktor

Kössen

Quiet village in the Kaiserwinkl, close to the German border

Walchsee

Village on one of Tyrol's warmest swimming lakes, in the Kaiserwinkl.

Thiersee

Quiet valley near Kufstein, known for its Passion Play.

Kufstein

Town on the Inn with an imposing fortress and a pleasant centre.

Inn valley

The broad main valley of Tyrol with Innsbruck, Hall, Schwaz and Wörgl.

Wörgl

Lively town in the Inn valley, handy as a stop and for a rainy day.

Jenbach

Railway town in the Inn valley, gateway to the Achensee and Zillertal.

Schwaz

Old mining town in the Inn valley with silver heritage and a show mine.

Wattens

Inn Valley town of Swarovski, with Kristallwelten and the Wattental

Hall in Tirol

Medieval town of salt and coins, with Hasegg Castle and a mining museum

Innsbruck

The Tyrolean capital with the Golden Roof, old town and a cable car up the Nordkette

Zillertal

A large, well-known valley with Mayrhofen, Hintertux and plenty of mountain experiences.

Fügen

Main village of the lower Zillertal with Spieljoch and the Erlebnistherme

Kaltenbach

Zillertal village with Hochzillertal, the Murmelland and the Höhenstraße

Zell am Ziller

Zillertal brewery and gold village with the Gauderfest and Zillertal Arena

Gerlos

Mountain village at about 1,250 m on the road over the Gerlos Pass

Mayrhofen

Well-known village at the end of the Zillertal, with Penken and Ahorn

Hintertux

Glacier village at the head of the Tuxertal, with skiing all year
