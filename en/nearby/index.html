Villages and towns around Niederau

From Oberau to Innsbruck: places to explore from the Wildschönau.

Niederau lies at the entrance to the Wildschönau, and from this valley the surroundings are easy to explore. In the valley itself lie villages such as Oberau, Auffach and Thierbach, each with its own character. Just outside the valley the Inn valley begins, where larger places such as Wörgl, Kufstein and Innsbruck lie, with shops, museums and old town centres.

This page gives an overview of the villages and towns in the wider area, grouped by region. For each place you can read what makes it worth a visit and roughly how far it is from Niederau. Distances are approximate and depend on traffic. Always check opening times and events beforehand.

Wildschönau

The quiet side valley where Niederau lies: farming villages, meadows and Ski Juwel.

Oberau

The main village of the Wildschönau, right next to Niederau

Auffach

The valley's highest village, home to the Schatzberg

Thierbach

Peaceful hamlet on the sunny slope of the Wildschönau

Mühltal

Hamlet by the stream in the Wildschönau valley

Alpbachtal

Flower-filled mountain village Alpbach and the valleys around Reith, Brixlegg and Kramsach.

Alpbach

Tyrol's flower village in a side valley of the Alpbachtal

Reith im Alpbachtal

Sunny village at the entrance of the Alpbachtal, with a swimming lake

Brixlegg

Small town in the Inn Valley with mining history and a castle

Rattenberg

Tiny town on the Inn, known for its glass art

Kramsach

Village with an open-air museum and small lakes by the Inn

Kundl

Inn Valley village, mainly a convenient stopover

Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

SkiWelt ski area, Kitzbühel and Tyrolean villages with meadows and mountains.

Hopfgarten im Brixental

Brixental village with a cable car to the Hohe Salve

Itter

Small village with a famous castle in the Brixental

Brixen im Thale

Brixental village with views of the Hohe Salve

Westendorf

Brixental village for skiing, hiking and a relaxed atmosphere

Kirchberg in Tirol

Lively village in the Brixental, close to Kitzbühel

Kitzbühel

Historic town in the Kitzbühel Alps, known for the Hahnenkamm race

Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Kufstein fortress, the Wilder Kaiser mountains and lakes for swimming.

Söll

Village by the Wilder Kaiser with Hexenwasser and the Hohe Salve

Ellmau

Village at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser, known from the TV series Der Bergdoktor

Kössen

Quiet village in the Kaiserwinkl, close to the German border

Walchsee

Village on a warm lake in the Kaiserwinkl, popular with families.

Thiersee

Quiet valley near Kufstein, known for its Passion Play.

Kufstein

Town on the Inn with an imposing fortress and a pleasant centre.

Inn valley

The broad main valley of Tyrol with Innsbruck, Hall, Schwaz and Wörgl.

Wörgl

Lively town in the Inn valley, handy as a stop and for a rainy day.

Jenbach

Railway town in the Inn valley, gateway to the Achensee and Zillertal.

Schwaz

Old mining town in the Inn valley with silver heritage and a show mine.

Wattens

Small Inn Valley town known for Swarovski Kristallwelten

Hall in Tirol

Medieval town with a historic old town, Hasegg Castle and the mint

Innsbruck

The Tyrolean capital with the Golden Roof, old town and mountains close by

Zillertal

A large, well-known valley with Mayrhofen, Hintertux and plenty of mountain experiences.

Fügen

Gateway to the Zillertal with Spieljoch, Hochfügen and family fun

Kaltenbach

Zillertal village with the Hochzillertal ski area and summer walks

Zell am Ziller

Zillertal's main village with the Gauderfest, a brewery and Zillertal Arena skiing

Gerlos

Mountain village on the Gerlos Pass at the top end of the Zillertal

Mayrhofen

Well-known mountain village at the end of the Zillertal with plenty to do all year

Hintertux

Glacier ski area at the head of the Tuxertal (municipality of Tux)
