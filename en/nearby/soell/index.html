Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Söll

Village by the Wilder Kaiser with Hexenwasser and the Hohe Salve

Söll lies at about 703 metres at the foot of the Hohe Salve, between Wörgl and St. Johann, and has around 3,700 inhabitants. It belongs to the Wilder Kaiser holiday region and to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, one of the largest connected ski areas in Austria.

The best-known spot is the Hohe Salve (about 1,828 m), with a panorama stretching from the Wilder Kaiser and the Hohe Tauern with the Großglockner to the Kitzbühel Alps. Halfway up lies Hochsöll at about 1,355 metres, the starting point of the Hexenwasser. This is a themed walking trail with more than 50 stations where children can play with water using all their senses, walk barefoot through spring water or bake bread over a fire pit. The trail is reached by the Hexenwasser gondola, and in winter there is a winter walking version.

For families from Niederau, Söll is a suitable day out with children, without long distances. In winter there are slopes for all levels, mostly blue and red. Check beforehand whether the lifts and the Hexenwasser are open and what the current prices are.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 40 min.
  • Elevation703 m
  • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
  • Best forSuits families with children and anyone seeking mountain views.

What to see and do

  • Hexenwasser at Hochsöll (about 1,355 m): a themed walking trail with 50+ stations and water play for children.
  • The Hohe Salve (about 1,828 m) with a 360-degree panorama over the Wilder Kaiser, the Hohe Tauern and the Kitzbühel Alps.
  • Walks with views of the Wilder Kaiser, to alpine huts.
  • Skiing in winter as part of the SkiWelt, with many blue and red runs.
  • A winter walking route at the Hexenwasser in winter.

From Niederau

About 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and towards Kufstein/Söll.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Hexenwasser?

A themed trail at Hochsöll on the Hohe Salve above Söll with more than 50 water and play stations for children, reached by the Hexenwasser gondola.

Is Söll part of the same ski area as Niederau?

No, Söll belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, Niederau to Ski Juwel. Check the current ski pass arrangements.
