Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Söll

Village by the Wilder Kaiser with Hexenwasser and the Hohe Salve

Söll lies at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser in the Kufsteinerland and is a well-known tourist village. It is part of the Skiwelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental and looks out on the steep Kaiser mountains.

The Hohe Salve is the best-known spot in both summer and winter. On the mountain lies Hochsöll, home to the Hexenwasser theme trail, a walking route with water play areas and attractions for children. There is a chapel at the summit, and the view stretches far across the Tyrolean mountains.

For families from Niederau, Söll is a good day out with children: there is plenty to do without long distances. In winter there are slopes for all levels. Check beforehand whether the lifts and the Hexenwasser are open and what the current prices are.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 40 min.
  • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
  • Best forSuits families with children and anyone seeking mountain views.

What to see and do

  • Hexenwasser at Hochsöll: a walking trail with water play areas for children (summer).
  • The Hohe Salve with cable car, summit chapel and wide views.
  • Walks with views of the Wilder Kaiser.
  • Skiing in winter as part of the Skiwelt.
  • A stroll around the village with its cafés and shops.

From Niederau

About 40 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and towards Kufstein/Söll.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Hexenwasser?

A themed trail on the Hohe Salve above Söll with water play areas and attractions for children, mainly in summer.

Is Söll part of the same ski area as Niederau?

No, Söll belongs to the Skiwelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, Niederau to Ski Juwel. Check the current ski pass arrangements.
