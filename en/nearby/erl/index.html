Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Erl

Passion play village on the Inn with the Tiroler Festspiele Erl

Erl lies in the far north-east of Tyrol, on the Inn, close to the German border and north of Kufstein. The municipality has about 1,400 residents and lies at about 476 m. It is a small village in open countryside, known for two cultural traditions.

The first is the Passion Play. The village looks back on a tradition of more than 400 years; the plays are staged every six years in the Passionsspielhaus, built in 1957-1959 to a design by Robert Schuller and seating up to 1,500. The latest edition ran from May to October 2025, mostly at weekends. The second is the Tiroler Festspiele Erl, a classical music festival founded in 1998 by Gustav Kuhn. In 2012 the Festspielhaus was added, a modern building with 732 seats.

For families with older children a performance can make a special outing; check the programme, running time and suitability for children beforehand. On other days Erl is mainly a quiet place for a walk or bike ride along the Inn. The village has few attractions, so it suits those who want to combine culture with a day in the Kufsteinerland.

What to see and do

  • Passionsspielhaus (1957-1959, up to 1,500 seats), home of the Passion Play staged every six years.
  • Tiroler Festspiele Erl: classical music festival, since 1998; check the programme beforehand.
  • Festspielhaus from 2012 (732 seats) by Delugan Meissl Architects, also worth a look as a building.
  • Walking and cycling along the Inn in open countryside.
  • Kufstein and its fortress are about 15 km away.

From Niederau

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau: via Wörgl to Kufstein, then north to Erl on the A93 or local roads.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How often is the Passion Play held in Erl?

Every six years. The latest edition was in 2025, from May to October; ask the organisers about the next one.

When did the Festspielhaus open?

On 26 December 2012, with 732 seats, alongside the older Passionsspielhaus.
