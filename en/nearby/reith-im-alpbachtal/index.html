Alpbachtal

Reith im Alpbachtal

Sunny village at the entrance of the Alpbachtal, with a swimming lake

Reith im Alpbachtal lies on a sunny terrace above the Inn, at the entrance to the Alpbachtal. It is a quiet village mixing farming and tourism, with views of the mountains on both sides of the valley.

Of note is the Reither See, a small lake nearby where you can swim in summer and let children play. Check access and facilities beforehand. It is one of the better-known swimming spots in the area.

For families from Niederau, Reith is a pleasant short trip, easy to combine with Brixlegg, Rattenberg or Alpbach. In winter the village is quiet, but it lies near the ski area. It is mainly a place to walk, swim and unwind.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 30 min.
  • RegionAlpbachtal
  • Best forSuits families wanting to combine swimming with quiet walks.

What to see and do

  • Reither See: swimming and play in summer
  • Walks with views over the Inn and the Alpbachtal
  • Quiet village centre with church
  • Easy to combine with Brixlegg, Rattenberg or Alpbach
  • Close to the ski area in winter

From Niederau

About 30 minutes by car from Niederau, via Oberau and the Inn Valley road towards Brixlegg.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim at Reith?

Yes, in summer at the Reither See; check access and facilities beforehand.

Is Reith far from Alpbach?

No, both are in the Alpbachtal and can be combined by car in a short time.
