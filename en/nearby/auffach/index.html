Wildschönau

Auffach

The valley's highest village, home to the Schatzberg

Auffach lies at the head of the Wildschönau and is the most remote village in the valley. It is best known for the Schatzberg, the ski and hiking mountain reached from the village by the Schatzbergbahn. This makes Auffach a starting point for skiing in winter and for higher-altitude walking in summer.

The Schatzberg is also part of the large Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area, where one ski pass covers the whole area. The slopes at Auffach suit a broad range of skiers and can be reached from Niederau in roughly a quarter of an hour, also by ski bus.

In summer you will find quiet walking paths and alpine pastures around Auffach, with mountain walks that children can manage well. The village itself is small and peaceful; if you are coming for a day in the mountains, Auffach combines nicely with a visit to a mountain hut.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 15 min.
  • RegionWildschönau
  • Best forSuits walkers and skiers looking for a quieter side of the valley.

What to see and do

  • Schatzbergbahn: cable car from the village for skiers and walkers
  • Schatzberg ski area, part of Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau (winter)
  • Walks across alpine pastures and to mountain huts (summer)
  • Quiet, small-village feel at the head of the valley
  • Fine views over the Wildschönau from higher up

From Niederau

About 15 minutes by car from Niederau, along the valley road via Oberau and further up the valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get from Niederau to Auffach?

By car about 15 minutes via Oberau; in winter a ski bus also runs.

Is the Schatzberg part of Ski Juwel?

Yes, the Schatzberg belongs to the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area.
