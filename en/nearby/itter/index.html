Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

Itter

Small village with a famous castle in the Brixental

Itter is a small village on the edge of the Brixental, between Wörgl and Hopfgarten. It is mainly mentioned for Schloss Itter, a castle that became known for a battle in May 1945, in the last days of the Second World War.

The castle is not a standard attraction: whether and when it can be visited varies and may be limited. Check beforehand before making a special trip. The village itself is quiet and rural.

Itter works well as a short stop on a drive through the Brixental, for example combined with Hopfgarten. It is too small for a full day.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 25 min.
  • RegionBrixental and Kitzbühel Alps
  • Best forSuits history fans and as a short stopover.

What to see and do

  • See Schloss Itter from outside (check access beforehand)
  • Quiet walk in the surroundings
  • Short stop on the route through the Brixental
  • Combine with Hopfgarten

From Niederau

About 25 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then into the Brixental. Estimate.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Itter known?

Mainly for Schloss Itter and a battle in May 1945.

Can you visit Schloss Itter?

This is not always possible; enquire beforehand.
