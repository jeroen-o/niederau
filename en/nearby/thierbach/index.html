Thierbach

Peaceful hamlet on the sunny slope of the Wildschönau

Thierbach is a small, peaceful settlement in the Wildschönau valley, on a sunny slope above the main valley road. It has scattered farms, a small church and plenty of greenery. If you want a spot without crowds, you will find a pleasant, scenic setting here.

For families from Niederau, Thierbach is mainly interesting as the starting point for short walks, routes past farms and meadows, and as a quiet stop on the way through the valley. It is not a destination with big attractions, but it is a good place to see the agricultural character of the Wildschönau.

In winter the village lies close to the valley's ski area and can be combined with a day's skiing or a walk through the snow. Ask locally about current walking routes and opportunities for a visit.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 10 min.
  • RegionWildschönau
  • Best forSuits those seeking quiet and families who like walking.

What to see and do

  • Short walks past farms and meadows
  • Quiet vantage point above the valley
  • A chance to see agricultural valley life up close
  • Close to the valley's ski area in winter
  • Easy to combine with a visit to Oberau or Auffach

From Niederau

About 10 minutes by car from Niederau, along the valley road through the Wildschönau.

Frequently asked questions

Is Thierbach far from Niederau?

No, about 10 minutes by car, in the same valley.

What can you do in Thierbach?

Mainly walking and enjoying the peace and views; there are no major attractions.
