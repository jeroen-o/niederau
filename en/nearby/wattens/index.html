Wattens

Small Inn Valley town known for Swarovski Kristallwelten

Wattens lies in the Inn Valley between Schwaz and Hall in Tirol, east of Innsbruck. The town is best known as the home of the crystal company Swarovski and of Swarovski Kristallwelten, a visitor attraction with art, installations and a large garden.

For a stay in Niederau, Wattens is a natural stop on a day trip towards Innsbruck. The town itself is quiet and somewhat industrial in character; the usual reason to stop here is the Kristallwelten, possibly combined with a walk in the surrounding hills or a drive on to Hall or Innsbruck.

It suits families with children who like a varied, indoor-friendly programme, for example on a rainy day. Please check the Kristallwelten website for current opening times and prices, which can vary by season. It combines easily with Hall in Tirol, only a few kilometres further on.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 45 min.
  • RegionInn valley
  • Best forSuits families after a day trip with a set attraction, also in poor weather.

What to see and do

  • Swarovski Kristallwelten: art and installations around crystal, with a garden and play area (check current details beforehand).
  • Walking in the hills around the Inn Valley, for instance on the slopes above Wattens in summer.
  • A short visit to the centre for a coffee or ice cream on the way back.
  • Combine with Hall in Tirol or Innsbruck on the same day.

From Niederau

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau, usually via Wörgl and the A12 Inn Valley motorway towards Innsbruck.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
