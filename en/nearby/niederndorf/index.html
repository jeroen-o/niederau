Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Niederndorf

Quiet village on the Inn near Kufstein, with the Kaisergebirge and many walking routes

Niederndorf lies at about 500 m on the eastern bank of the Inn in the lower Inn valley, roughly 10 km from Kufstein. It is a small holiday village with around 600 guest beds, on the edge of the Kufsteinerland and with views of the Zahmer Kaiser.

The village is mainly attractive to walkers. There are several circular routes such as the Dorf-Runde, the Hechenberg-Runde and the Jennbach-Runde. On the Hechenberg stands the pilgrimage chapel Maria Heimsuchung from 1778, with a bell from 1557. The Hatzenstädt dairy has been making organic mountain cheese for over 70 years and has a 5 km eco-cultural trail. In the village itself there is a small museum on local crafts.

For children there is a 1.2 km toboggan run from the Hechenberg down to the village in winter. There are also two cross-country trails, and larger ski areas are nearby. The Kaisertal hike is considered one of the most beautiful in the Kaisergebirge. From Niederau, Niederndorf is a day trip of about half an hour to three quarters of an hour each way. It suits families who want a relaxed walk and are not looking for crowds.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 45 min.
  • Elevation500 m
  • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
  • Best forSuits families who want a relaxed walk.

What to see and do

  • Circular walks such as the Dorf-Runde, Hechenberg-Runde and Jennbach-Runde.
  • Pilgrimage chapel Maria Heimsuchung on the Hechenberg, built in 1778.
  • Hatzenstädt dairy with mountain cheese and a 5 km eco-cultural trail.
  • Hike through the Kaisertal in the Kaisergebirge.
  • 1.2 km toboggan run from the Hechenberg to the village in winter.

From Niederau

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau: via Wörgl on the A12 towards Kufstein. Regional buses (lines 4030 and 4036) also serve the village.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a toboggan run in Niederndorf?

Yes, in winter there is a toboggan run of about 1.2 km from the Hechenberg to the village. Check current conditions beforehand.

Is Niederndorf suitable for walking with children?

Yes, there are several circular walks from the village, such as the Dorf-Runde, that are manageable.
