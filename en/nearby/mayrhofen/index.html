Mayrhofen

Well-known mountain village at the end of the Zillertal with plenty to do all year

Mayrhofen lies at the end of the main Zillertal, where the valley splits into four side valleys, including the Tuxertal and the Zillergrund. It is one of the best-known tourist resorts in Tyrol, with a lively centre of shops, cafés and restaurants.

The village draws visitors in every season. In winter the pistes of Penken and Ahorn are the main attraction, in summer mostly walkers and mountain bikers, and the resort is known for activities aimed at adventurous guests.

For families staying in Niederau, Mayrhofen is a well-chosen day trip of about an hour and a quarter by car. You can walk, take a mountain lift up, or simply stroll through the village. Check the current operating times of the mountain lifts in advance.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 65 min.
  • Best forSuits families and active guests looking for one place with plenty of choice.

What to see and do

  • Mountain lifts up the Penken and the Ahorn for summer walking and winter skiing.
  • Walking in the side valleys around Mayrhofen, for example towards the Zillergrund.
  • Strolling through the village with its shops, cafés and restaurants.
  • Mountain biking and other active summer pursuits; ask locally about providers.
  • Skiing and snowboarding on Penken and Ahorn in winter.

From Niederau

About 65 minutes by car from Niederau: via Wörgl, along the A12 to the Zillertal exit, then along the Zillertaler Straße (B169) to the end of the valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mayrhofen feasible as a day trip from Niederau?

Yes, the drive takes roughly 65 minutes each way via Wörgl and the Zillertal.

Which ski mountains belong to Mayrhofen?

Penken and Ahorn are the two well-known mountains at Mayrhofen.
