Achenkirch

Village at the northern end of Lake Achensee, with lake, mountains and cross-country skiing

Achenkirch lies at about 930 m at the northern end of Lake Achensee. It is one of the villages around the lake and already close to the German border. The village has a baroque parish church, the Anna chapel on a hill and the Sixenhof, a local history museum first mentioned in 1361.

For a day trip from Niederau, Achenkirch is a distant but varied destination. In summer everything revolves around the water and the mountains: swimming, paddleboarding, sailing, windsurfing, hiking and cycling. Between Achenkirch and Maurach there is a high ropes course for those who like to climb. In summer there are also concerts by the local brass band.

In winter the area is particularly attractive for cross-country skiers, with many kilometres of trails in the region around Lake Achensee. The nearby Christlum ski area is geared towards families. Expect a long drive and check the weather, as the day is more pleasant with a firm plan, especially with children. The village suits families who like nature and quiet best.

What to see and do

  • Swimming, paddleboarding, sailing and windsurfing on Lake Achensee in summer.
  • Waldhochseilgarten Achensee between Achenkirch and Maurach (check opening times beforehand).
  • The Sixenhof, a local history museum in a farmhouse first mentioned in 1361.
  • Baroque parish church and Anna chapel as a short cultural stop.
  • Cross-country skiing in winter and family-friendly skiing at nearby Christlum.

From Niederau

About 80 minutes by car from Niederau: via Wörgl on the A12 to the Wiesing/Achensee exit, then the B181 past Maurach to Achenkirch.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Achenkirch?

Achenkirch lies at about 930 m at the northern end of Lake Achensee in Tyrol, close to the German border.

Can you go cross-country skiing in Achenkirch in winter?

Yes, the region around Lake Achensee has many kilometres of trails. Check current snow conditions beforehand.
