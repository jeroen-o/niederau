    Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

    Going am Wilden Kaiser

    A quiet Kaiser village with a Baroque church, bathing lake and the Astberg

    Going am Wilden Kaiser lies at about 773 m at the foot of the Kaiser mountains, in the Sölllandl in the Kitzbühel district. The municipality has around 2,000 residents and is calmer than its neighbours Söll and Ellmau.

    The village is known for its Baroque parish church, which holds a Madonna from 1480, among other treasures. In summer there is the Goinger bathing lake, the Moor & more bog area, the Pony Alm on the Astberg and the Astbergsee, where the Kaiser range is mirrored in the water. The tourist season runs roughly from May to November.

    In winter Going belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental and is regarded as its eastern, snow-rich part. There are also cross-country trails, toboggan runs, winter walking paths and an ice rink. A good target for families from Niederau who want a calm day in the Kaiser area. The village is compact and easy with children; combine it with a walk to the Astbergsee or a visit to Ellmau. Check current opening times beforehand.

    What to see and do

    • The Baroque parish church, which holds a Madonna from 1480 among other things.
    • The Goinger bathing lake, a family swimming spot in summer.
    • Moor & more: a bog landscape with information on plants and animals.
    • Pony Alm and Astbergsee on the Astberg, with the Kaiser range reflected in the lake.
    • In winter, the toboggan run from the Astberg down to Going or Ellmau and the SkiWelt pistes.

    From Niederau

    About 40 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then via Söll and Scheffau towards Ellmau and Going.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you swim in Going?

    Yes, in summer there is the Goinger bathing lake; check opening times and conditions beforehand.

    Is Going a ski village?

    Going lies in the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental and has its own access to the Going-Ellmau ski area.
