    Zillertal

    Tux

    Municipality in the Tux valley with Lanersbach and the Hintertux Glacier

    The municipality of Tux lies in the Tux valley, a side valley of the Zillertal that branches off at Mayrhofen. It consists of five villages: Vorderlanersbach, Lanersbach, Juns, Madseit and Hintertux. Lanersbach and Vorderlanersbach sit on a sunny plateau at about 1,300 metres. From Mayrhofen to Hintertux the road climbs roughly 850 metres.

    The valley is best known for the Hintertux Glacier, described as Austria's only year-round snow-sure ski area. Hintertux also has a thermal spa fed by mineral springs. In summer the valley is a base for walks, with a nature trail along the Tuxbach, canyoning and climbing courses for children and adults, among other things.

    From Niederau it is a considerable drive, so plan a full day. It suits families who want reliable snow or who want to go a little higher into the mountains in summer. The tourist office is Tux-Finkenberg; check current opening times and lift and spa prices beforehand. Allow plenty of time for the drive through the valley, as traffic in the Zillertal can be busy in high season and at weekends; an early start is best.

    What to see and do

    • Hintertux Glacier: skiing and high-altitude walking, nearly year-round.
    • Thermal spa in Hintertux with mineral water.
    • Nature trail along the Tuxbach, suitable for families in summer.
    • Canyoning and climbing courses for children and adults.
    • Sunny plateau around Lanersbach with walking routes and views.

    From Niederau

    About 70 minutes by car from Niederau (Lanersbach; Hintertux about 10 minutes further): via Wörgl and the A12 to Jenbach, along the Zillertal to Mayrhofen and into the Tux valley.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which villages make up Tux?

    Vorderlanersbach, Lanersbach, Juns, Madseit and Hintertux.

    Why do people drive to Hintertux?

    For the Hintertux Glacier, a ski area usable nearly all year, and the thermal spa with mineral water.
