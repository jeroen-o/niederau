Brixen im Thale

Brixental village with views of the Hohe Salve

Brixen im Thale lies in the Brixental, some way south of Hopfgarten and Itter, at the foot of the Hohe Salve. The village belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental ski area and in winter has direct access to the slopes.

In summer the surroundings suit hiking and cycling, with the Hohe Salve as a well-known viewpoint. The village itself is rural in character, with a church and a few places to eat.

From Niederau it is a short drive for a day of skiing on the other side of the SkiWelt or for a summer hike. Check current lift times and children's facilities beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 35 min.
  • Best forSuits families who ski or hike.

What to see and do

  • Ski in the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental (winter)
  • Hike towards the Hohe Salve (summer)
  • Cycle through the Brixental
  • Village walk and a look at the church
  • Combine with Hopfgarten

From Niederau

About 35 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then through the Brixental. Estimate.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brixen im Thale part of a ski area?

Yes, it belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental.

Which mountain is above the village?

The Hohe Salve, a well-known viewpoint mountain.
