Kramsach

Village with an open-air museum and small lakes by the Inn

Kramsach lies in the Inn Valley near the Alpbachtal, right next to Rattenberg. It is best known for the Museum of Tyrolean Farmhouses, an open-air museum with relocated farmhouses from across Tyrol, and for the Reintalersee and other small lakes nearby.

The lakes sit between forest and rock faces and are popular in summer for a walk or a dip; which lakes allow swimming and what entry costs, ask locally. The region's glass tradition also appears in Kramsach.

For families, Kramsach is a relaxed outing that fits in half a day, especially combined with Rattenberg. In winter the museum may have limited opening; check beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 30 min.
  • RegionAlpbachtal
  • Best forSuits families with children who enjoy the outdoors and old farmhouses.

What to see and do

  • Visit the Museum of Tyrolean Farmhouses (open-air museum of old farms)
  • Walk around the Reintalersee and the other small lakes
  • Swim or picnic at the lakes in summer (check local rules)
  • Combine with a visit to nearby Rattenberg
  • Cycle along the Inn (summer)

From Niederau

About 30 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then west through the Inn Valley. Estimate; check the route beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kramsach a good outing in wet weather?

Partly: the museum is mostly outdoors, though some buildings can be entered. Combine with Rattenberg, where studios are indoors.

Can you swim in Kramsach's lakes?

This varies by lake and may cost money; ask locally or at the tourist office.
