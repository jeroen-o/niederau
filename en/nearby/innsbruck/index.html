Innsbruck

The Tyrolean capital with the Golden Roof, old town and mountains close by

Innsbruck is the capital of Tyrol, set in the Inn Valley and surrounded by mountains. The city mixes a historic old town with modern architecture and easily reached mountain scenery, including the Nordkette above the city.

The best-known sight is the Goldene Dachl in the old town, an oriel with gilded roof tiles. The Hofburg, the Hofkirche, the Bergisel ski jump and the Alpenzoo are other well-known places. From the centre the Nordkettenbahn takes visitors up into the mountains in a short time.

For a family staying in Niederau, Innsbruck makes a full day trip with culture, shopping and mountain views. Allow enough time for the drive and parking; check current opening times and prices of attractions beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 60 min.
  • Elevation574 m
  • Best forSuits culture, a city day, shopping and mountains in one.

What to see and do

  • Old town with the Goldene Dachl and the narrow streets around it.
  • Nordkettenbahn: mountain railway from the city up for views.
  • Alpenzoo: a zoo of Alpine animals, fun for children.
  • Bergisel ski jump with views over the city.
  • Winter: Christmas markets in the centre (check dates beforehand).

From Niederau

About 60 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the A12 towards Innsbruck; parking in a car park is easiest.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Goldene Dachl?

An oriel in Innsbruck's old town with a gilded copper roof; it is the city's best-known landmark.

Is Innsbruck doable as a day trip from Niederau?

Yes, the drive takes about 60 minutes each way.
