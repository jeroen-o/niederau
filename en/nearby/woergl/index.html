Wörgl

Lively town in the Inn valley, handy as a stop and for a rainy day.

Wörgl lies in the Inn valley at a junction of roads and railway lines and is the nearest larger town to Niederau. It is not a classic holiday village but a practical, lively town with shops, restaurants and services.

Historically, Wörgl is known for the so-called Wörgl free-money experiment of the 1930s, a local complementary currency that drew international attention. For families it is mainly of interest for its leisure facilities, such as the Wörgler Wasserwelt swimming complex; check current opening times.

Wörgl is a suitable place for shopping, a rainy day or a stop on the way to Kufstein, Innsbruck or the Zillertal.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 20 min.
  • Best forSuits a rainy day, shopping and short stops.

What to see and do

  • Swimming at the Wörgler Wasserwelt (check opening times).
  • Shopping and eating in the town centre.
  • The history of the Wörgl free-money experiment.
  • Handy stop on the way to Kufstein, Innsbruck or the Zillertal.
  • Walking or cycling along the Inn.

From Niederau

About 20 minutes by car from Niederau, via Oberau and the Inn valley.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What was the Wörgl free money?

A local complementary currency from the 1930s, introduced during the crisis, which became internationally known.

How far is Wörgl from Niederau?

About 20 minutes by car.
