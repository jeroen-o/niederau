    Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

    Kufstein

    Town on the Inn with an imposing fortress and a pleasant centre.

    Kufstein lies at about 500 m on the River Inn, close to the German border, at the foot of the Kaiser mountains. With roughly 18,000 inhabitants it is the second-largest town in Tyrol and the main town of the Kufsteinerland. Its skyline is dominated by Kufstein Fortress, standing on a rock about 90 m above the Inn.

    The fortress is more than 800 years old and houses a museum and the Heroes' Organ in the Bürgerturm, an organ from 1931 known as the largest open-air organ in the world. It is played every day at 12 noon. According to the fortress website the grounds are open daily; the organ season runs from April to October. Check current times and prices beforehand.

    For families staying in Niederau, Kufstein makes a good day trip: fortress, old town and the Inn embankment are easy to explore with children. The Kaiser mountains behind the town offer walking options. Parking in the centre can be busy, so allow some extra time. Allow half a day to a full day, depending on how much you want to see. When it rains, the fortress museum is a good alternative.

    What to see and do

    • Visit Kufstein Fortress, with its museum and views over town and Inn.
    • Hear the Heroes' Organ, played daily at 12 noon (organ season April to October).
    • Stroll through the old town and along the Inn.
    • Summer: walking in the Kaiser mountains behind the town.
    • Winter: Advent atmosphere in the old town (check dates).

    From Niederau

    About 35 minutes by car from Niederau, roughly via Wörgl and the Inn valley motorway (A12).

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is the Heroes' Organ played in Kufstein?

    Reportedly daily at 12 noon, during the organ season from April to October. Check current times and access beforehand.

    How far is Kufstein from Niederau?

    About 35 minutes by car, depending on traffic.
