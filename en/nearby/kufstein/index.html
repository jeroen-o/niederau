Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Kufstein

Town on the Inn with an imposing fortress and a pleasant centre.

Kufstein lies on the River Inn close to the German border and is the main town of the Kufsteinerland. Its skyline is dominated by Kufstein Fortress, towering above the old town.

The fortress is the main draw. On its grounds stands the Heroes' Organ, a large open-air organ that is reportedly played daily around midday; check times and access beforehand. Below are the old-town streets and the Inn embankment, easy to explore with children.

For families staying in Niederau, Kufstein is a suitable day trip that also works in less settled weather. The Kaiser mountains rise directly behind the town, with walking options in summer and winter.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 35 min.
  • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
  • Best forSuits families wanting culture and a town outing.

What to see and do

  • Visit Kufstein Fortress with views over the town and the Inn.
  • Hear the Heroes' Organ on the fortress grounds (check times).
  • Stroll through the old town and along the Inn.
  • Summer: walking in the Kaiser mountains above the town.
  • Winter: Christmas-market atmosphere in the old town around the festive season (check dates).

From Niederau

About 35 minutes by car from Niederau, roughly via Wörgl and the Inn valley motorway (A12).

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
