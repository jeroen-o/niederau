Brixlegg

Small town in the Inn Valley with mining history and a castle

Brixlegg is a small town in the Inn Valley, on the edge of the Alpbachtal. It is known for its long history of copper ore and metalworking; the metal industry is still important to the town. For many visitors Brixlegg is mainly a place to pass through on the way to Alpbach or Rattenberg.

Above the town stands Schloss Matzen, a castle best admired from the outside; check beforehand whether a visit is possible. The surroundings offer walks along the Inn and on the slopes above the town.

For families from Niederau, Brixlegg is a practical stop: there are shops and places to eat, and you pass through on the way to the Alpbachtal. A short walk or cycle ride along the Inn makes a pleasant addition to a day out.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 30 min.
  • RegionAlpbachtal
  • Best forSuits families wanting a stopover or culture and history.

What to see and do

  • Schloss Matzen above the town (ask about visiting)
  • History of copper mining and metalworking
  • Walk or cycle ride along the Inn
  • Shops and places to eat for a stopover
  • Easy to combine with Rattenberg, Kramsach or Alpbach

From Niederau

About 30 minutes by car from Niederau, via Oberau and the Inn Valley road towards Kramsach.

Frequently asked questions
