Brixlegg

Market town in the Inn Valley with mining history and a museum

Brixlegg is a market town in the Inn Valley, on the edge of the Alpbachtal. It is one of the oldest settlements in Tyrol and was first mentioned in 788. For centuries life here revolved around mining and processing copper and silver; the state-owned smelter was the largest in Tyrol. That past still shapes the town.

The Tiroler Bergbau- und Hüttenmuseum, housed in the old school of 1876, shows on about 400 m2 how ore was once mined and smelted. Schloss Matzen itself cannot be visited, but the castle park is open to walkers. The Innradweg runs along the Inn, and the Burgenweg links Brixlegg to Rattenberg past castles and viewpoints.

Brixlegg has a railway station and is a shopping town, so a practical stop on the way. For families from Niederau it fits well with Rattenberg, the glass town, or a day in Alpbach. Check opening times of the museum and park beforehand. This gives families a compact, varied stop with history, walking and cycling close together.

What to see and do

  • Tiroler Bergbau- und Hüttenmuseum in the old school of 1876
  • Grounds of Schloss Matzen (the castle itself cannot be visited)
  • Burgenweg between Brixlegg and Rattenberg
  • Innradweg along the Inn for a cycle ride
  • Shops, places to eat and a station for a stopover

From Niederau

About 30 minutes by car from Niederau, via the valley road to Kundl and then along the Inn Valley towards Brixlegg.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to see in Brixlegg?

Among other things the Tiroler Bergbau- und Hüttenmuseum and the grounds of Schloss Matzen; many visitors combine it with Rattenberg.

Is Brixlegg far from Niederau?

About 30 minutes by car, via Kundl and the Inn Valley.
