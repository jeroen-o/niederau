Hall in Tirol

Medieval town with a historic old town, Hasegg Castle and the mint

Hall in Tirol lies on the Inn, about ten kilometres east of Innsbruck. In the Middle Ages it was important for salt production and coin minting, which is still easy to see in the compact old town: narrow streets, colourful façades and church towers.

Its best-known building is Hasegg Castle, home to the Münze Hall, a museum about the history of coin minting. The centre is quiet and easy to explore on foot, with cafés, ice-cream shops and small stores.

Hall suits a relaxed day of culture and strolling, and combines well with Wattens or Innsbruck. Children enjoy the museum most if they like discovering things; check current opening times with the tourist office beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 50 min.
  • RegionInn valley
  • Best forSuits culture, history and a relaxed town day.

What to see and do

  • Stroll through the old town with its narrow streets and churches.
  • Hasegg Castle with the Münze Hall, a museum about coin minting.
  • A café terrace or ice cream in the centre.
  • Winter: an atmospheric town in the valley, a quiet day alongside skiing.

From Niederau

About 50 minutes by car from Niederau, usually via Wörgl and the A12 towards Innsbruck (Hall exit).

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
