Kirchbichl

Inn valley village with a warm mud-bottom lake and the prehistoric Grattenbergl hill

Kirchbichl lies at about 520 m in the lower Inn valley, directly on the River Inn between Wörgl and Kufstein. It is not a classic tourist village, which is exactly why it is calm, with mountain views all around and a short distance from Niederau. For anyone heading into the valley, it is an easy, short drive.

The best-known spot is the Badesee Kirchbichl, a small natural bathing lake of just over a hectare with a muddy bottom. Its water is said to be the warmest open-air bathing spot in Tirol, and it is one of the oldest baths in the area. There are a restaurant, a kiosk, a beach volleyball court, a tennis court and a playground. The Grattenbergl is also a remarkable hill: a prehistoric settlement, the largest in North Tirol in the Bronze Age, and a site of the Tyrolean fight for freedom. It has been a natural monument since 1956.

In summer you can cycle or walk along the Inn. Kirchbichl suits families who want a short, relaxed swimming trip combined with a touch of history.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 25 min.
  • Elevation520 m
  • RegionInn valley
  • Best forSuits families looking for a short swimming trip with a little history.

What to see and do

  • Badesee Kirchbichl: natural bathing lake with a muddy bottom, known for its warm water, with restaurant, kiosk and playground.
  • Beach volleyball court and tennis court at the lake.
  • Grattenbergl: prehistoric hilltop settlement and natural monument since 1956.
  • Cycling or walking along the Inn on the Inn valley cycle path.
  • Walks in the hills around the village with views over the valley.

From Niederau

From Niederau about 25 minutes by car, via Wörgl (A12/B171 towards Kufstein). Check the route beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kirchbichl a good place to swim?

Yes, the Badesee Kirchbichl is a small natural lake with a muddy bottom known for its warm water, with a kiosk, restaurant and playground. Check opening times and admission beforehand.

What is the Grattenbergl?

A hill near Kirchbichl with a prehistoric settlement, a site of the Tyrolean fight for freedom and a natural monument since 1956.
