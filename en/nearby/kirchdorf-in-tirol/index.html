Kirchdorf in Tirol

Village at the foot of the Kaiser mountains with gorge, lake and spa

Kirchdorf in Tirol lies at about 640 m at the foot of the Kaiser mountains, between the Kaiserwinkl, the Pillerseetal and St. Johann in Tirol. Around 2,300 people live here. The village is a good base for anyone who wants to explore the nature of the Wilder Kaiser without the crowds of the big resorts.

Among the best-known outings are the Kaiserbachtal, the Teufelsgasse and the river adventure trail along the Großache. The Hintersteiner See is also very popular with families, a bathing lake with clear water and a beach area in the Wilder Kaiser nature reserve. In the village itself, the Kaiserquell wellness centre offers an outdoor pool and saunas, among other things.

In summer there are plenty of walking, mountain-bike and climbing routes, plus high ropes courses and swimming spots for children. Kirchdorf suits families who want to combine nature, water and a calm atmosphere, and who may add a day trip to St. Johann or Kufstein.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 55 min.
  • Elevation640 m
  • RegionBrixental and Kitzbühel Alps
  • Best forSuits families looking for water, nature and walking beneath the Wilder Kaiser.

What to see and do

  • Hintersteiner See: a bathing lake with clear water and a beach area in the Wilder Kaiser nature reserve.
  • Kaiserbachtal: walking and relaxing in a valley beneath the Kaiser cliffs.
  • Teufelsgasse: a striking natural trail, suitable as a short walk.
  • River adventure trail along the Großache, a calm route beside the water.
  • Kaiserquell wellness centre with outdoor pool, tennis courts and saunas in the village.
  • Climbing, mountain biking and high ropes courses in the surrounding area in summer.

From Niederau

From Niederau about 55 minutes by car, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel towards St. Johann in Tirol. Check the route beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Kirchdorf in Tirol?

At about 640 m at the foot of the Kaiser mountains, between the Kaiserwinkl, the Pillerseetal and St. Johann in Tirol.

Can you swim near Kirchdorf?

Yes, the Hintersteiner See in the Wilder Kaiser nature reserve is a bathing lake with a beach area, and the Kaiserquell wellness centre in the village has an outdoor pool. Check opening times beforehand.
