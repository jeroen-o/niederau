    Ehrwald

    At the foot of the Zugspitze, home of the Tiroler Zugspitzbahn

    Ehrwald lies in the Reutte district, within the Tiroler Zugspitz Arena, at the foot of the Wetterstein range and the 2,962 m Zugspitze. The village sits at about 994 m and, together with Lermoos, Biberwier, Berwang and Heiterwang, forms the Zugspitz Arena region.

    The big draw is the Tiroler Zugspitzbahn: a modern cable car with two cabins for 100 people each takes you to the Zugspitzplatt at almost 2,950 m. On clear days the view stretches far, and the 'Faszination Zugspitze' experience museum is an option even in poor weather. The Ehrwalder Almbahn leads up to the Ehrwalder Alm at about 1,500 m, where popular walks begin, including the one to the Seebensee.

    In winter there are pistes, a ski kindergarten and floodlit toboggan runs. A special tradition is the Bergfeuer Ehrwald around the summer solstice, on the UNESCO list of intangible cultural heritage since 2010. From Niederau, Ehrwald is a long but manageable day trip by car. For safety's sake, always check current weather and opening information when planning your day.

    What to see and do

    • The Tiroler Zugspitzbahn to the Zugspitzplatt (top station at about 2,950 m), with the 'Faszination Zugspitze' museum.
    • The Ehrwalder Almbahn to the Ehrwalder Alm (about 1,500 m), starting point for walks.
    • The walk to the Seebensee with the Zugspitze as a backdrop.
    • In winter: skiing on the Ehrwalder Alm, a ski kindergarten and floodlit toboggan runs (check current times).
    • The Bergfeuer Ehrwald around the solstice, UNESCO heritage since 2010.

    From Niederau

    About 1.5 to 2 hours by car from Niederau, via the Inn valley motorway towards Innsbruck and on via Telfs and the Fernpass or via Mieming; plan a full day.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    How high does the Tiroler Zugspitzbahn go?

    The top station is at about 2,950 m on the Zugspitzplatt; the summit of the Zugspitze is 2,962 m. Check the weather and current timetable beforehand.

    Is Ehrwald doable as a day trip from Niederau?

    Yes, but allow about 1.5 to 2 hours each way; leave early and choose one main goal, such as the Zugspitzbahn or the Ehrwalder Alm.
