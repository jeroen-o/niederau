Wildschönau

Mühltal

Hamlet near Oberau, starting point for the Kundler Klamm

Mühltal is a small hamlet belonging to Oberau, on the road through the Wildschönau. The name comes from an old mill that once stood here. There are a few traditional houses and inns, and not much more: it is not a tourist centre.

Still, Mühltal is a well-known starting point for families. It is regarded as the gateway to the Kundler Klamm, a gorge along the Wildschönauer Ache between Mühltal and Kundl that is counted among the most beautiful in Austria. The route is about 7 km long, crosses wooden bridges and passes through small tunnels, and can also be done with a pushchair. The gorge is open from 1 April to mid-November and closed in winter.

If you would rather not walk, in summer the Wildschönauer Bummelzug, a slow narrow-gauge train, runs from Mühltal to the gorge entrance. A hiking route also leads up from Mühltal to Thierbach. Bring a picnic or eat in Oberau; check the Bummelzug timetable beforehand. This makes Mühltal a simple, child-friendly outing that needs little preparation and fits well into half a day.

What to see and do

  • Kundler Klamm: about 7 km along the Wildschönauer Ache, also manageable with a pushchair (1 April to mid-November)
  • Wildschönauer Bummelzug from Mühltal to the gorge entrance (summer)
  • Hiking route up to Thierbach
  • Rural atmosphere with traditional houses and inns
  • Easy to combine with Oberau or Niederau

From Niederau

About 10 minutes by car from Niederau, along the valley road through the Wildschönau.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Kundler Klamm open?

From 1 April to mid-November; the gorge is closed in winter.

How do I reach the Kundler Klamm without walking?

In summer the Wildschönauer Bummelzug runs from Mühltal to the entrance; check the timetable beforehand.
