Mühltal

Hamlet by the stream in the Wildschönau valley

Mühltal is a small hamlet in the Wildschönau, set along the stream that runs through the valley. The name refers to the mills that once worked here. It is not a tourist centre but a quiet, rural spot.

For families, Mühltal is mainly interesting as part of a walk or cycle ride through the valley, for example along the stream and on quiet paths. You will see farms, meadows and small wooden buildings typical of the valley.

Sights and facilities are limited; bring a picnic or combine the hamlet with a visit to Oberau or Niederau, where there are shops and places to eat. For current routes and details, the Wildschönau tourist office is the best point of contact.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 10 min.
  • RegionWildschönau
  • Best forSuits those seeking quiet, walkers and cyclists.

What to see and do

  • Walking or cycling along the stream through the valley
  • Quiet paths past farms and meadows
  • Typical wooden buildings and rural atmosphere
  • A good spot for a picnic along the way
  • Easy to combine with a visit to Oberau or Niederau

From Niederau

About 10 minutes by car from Niederau, along the valley road through the Wildschönau.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mühltal a village?

No, it is a small hamlet in the Wildschönau with few facilities.

Where can I get information?

At the Wildschönau tourist office, which gives current routes and tips.
