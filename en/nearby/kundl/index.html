Kundl

Inn Valley village, mainly a convenient stopover

Kundl lies in the Inn Valley between Wörgl and Rattenberg, at the gateway to the Alpbachtal. It is not a classic tourist resort but a working village in the valley, with well-known pharmaceutical industry.

For families, Kundl is mainly of interest as a stop on the way to Alpbach, Rattenberg or Kramsach. The surroundings suit a gentle cycle or walk along the Inn across the flat valley floor.

Do not expect a large range of attractions. If you want to plan a day out, choose one of the surrounding places as your goal and treat Kundl as part of the route. I could not verify specific sights; ask the tourist office.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 25 min.
  • Best forSuits those who want a quiet bike ride or a stopover.

What to see and do

  • Cycle or walk along the Inn across the flat valley floor (summer)
  • Stopover on the way to Alpbach or Rattenberg
  • Quiet stroll through the village and surroundings
  • Local cafes and restaurants as a break during a day trip through the Inn Valley

From Niederau

About 25 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then a short way west. Estimate.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kundl a tourist destination?

Not really; it is mainly a village in the Inn Valley and handy as a stopover.

What nearby is good for families?

Rattenberg, Kramsach and Alpbach are a short drive away.
