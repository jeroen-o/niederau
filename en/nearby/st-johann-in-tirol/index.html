Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

St. Johann in Tirol

A lively market town with Baroque houses, a climbing forest and many outings

St. Johann in Tirol lies in the Kitzbühel Alps, north of Kitzbühel. The area spans roughly 670 to 2,231 m in altitude. It is a proper small town with shops, restaurants and cafés, which makes it a good day out in less settled weather.

In the centre are Baroque houses such as the Schellhornhaus and the Schwarzinger-Haus, the Baroque parish church from the 18th century and Schloss Hohenhaus, built in the 16th century as a hunting lodge. Families can visit the Hornpark climbing forest and the Moor & More Erlebniswelt, where you learn about the plants and animals of a bog. The Knödelfest (dumpling festival) is also well known.

In summer there are about 200 km of walking trails; in winter St. Johann has its own ski area. From Niederau it is a manageable day trip through the Brixental and across the Kitzbühel Alps. The town offers plenty of places to eat, which is handy if your group has different wishes. Check the opening times of attractions beforehand.

What to see and do

  • The Baroque town centre with the Schellhornhaus and the Schwarzinger-Haus, among others.
  • The Baroque parish church from the 18th century.
  • Schloss Hohenhaus, built in the 16th century as a hunting lodge.
  • The Hornpark climbing forest for families with children.
  • The Moor & More Erlebniswelt about bog landscapes.
  • The Knödelfest with its long dumpling table.

From Niederau

About 55 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then through the Brixental towards Kitzbühel and St. Johann.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do in St. Johann in poor weather?

You can stroll through the Baroque centre, visit cafés and shops and see the church and Schloss Hohenhaus; check opening times of attractions beforehand.

Is there a climbing forest in St. Johann?

Yes, the Hornpark climbing forest. Check times and minimum age beforehand.
