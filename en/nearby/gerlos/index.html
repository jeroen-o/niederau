Zillertal

Gerlos

Mountain village on the Gerlos Pass at the top end of the Zillertal

Gerlos lies in a side valley at the upper end of the Zillertal, on the road over the Gerlos Pass towards Salzburgerland. It is a quiet mountain village best known as a winter sports resort and is part of the Zillertal Arena ski area.

In winter the village is a natural base for skiers, in summer mainly for walkers and cyclists. It sits on the border between the Zillertal and the Pinzgau, so a drive over the Gerlos Pass combines easily with a visit to the surroundings, for example the Krimml waterfalls on the other side of the pass.

For families staying in Niederau, Gerlos makes a good day trip if you prefer a small mountain village to a large tourist centre. Allow a drive of a little over an hour, and check current lift opening times and any toll-road charges with the tourist office beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 75 min.
  • RegionZillertal
  • Best forSuits families and skiers looking for a quiet mountain village.

What to see and do

  • Skiing in the Zillertal Arena, to which Gerlos is linked along with other villages.
  • Summer walking in the mountains around Gerlos, from short village strolls to longer mountain hikes.
  • A drive over the Gerlos Pass, a well-known mountain route between Tyrol and Salzburgerland.
  • The Krimml waterfalls on the Salzburg side of the pass, a good combination with a visit to Gerlos.
  • Cycling and mountain biking nearby; ask locally about routes and rental.

From Niederau

About 75 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then along the A12 to the Zillertal exit and up the valley through Zell am Ziller to Gerlos. Check beforehand for any toll or pass regulations.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Gerlos from Niederau?

Roughly 75 minutes by car, via Wörgl and the Zillertal. The exact time depends on traffic.

Which ski area does Gerlos belong to?

Gerlos is part of the Zillertal Arena.
