Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

Westendorf

Flower-filled village below the Choralpe, with the Alpinolino children's area

Westendorf lies at the entrance to the Brixental, on a sunny plateau below the Choralpe, at roughly 800 metres. The village has more than 3,700 inhabitants and is known for its carefully tended flower displays: it was once named one of Europe's prettiest flower villages in the Entente Florale competition.

In summer a wide network of signposted walking paths and cycle routes starts here, running through the Windautal and across the alpine pastures. In the summer months the Alpenrosenbahn takes you up to the Talkaser at around 1,770 metres. There you will find Alpinolino, a discovery area for children with nature and wildlife trails, plus a viewpoint over the Brixental and the Hohe Tauern. In high summer there are special play days for children.

In winter Westendorf belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, with lifts starting close to the village. For a family from Niederau it makes a relaxed day out in a different valley, with more space and fewer crowds than Kitzbühel. Check the current opening times of the lifts and Alpinolino before you go.

What to see and do

  • Take the Alpenrosenbahn (summer) up to the Talkaser at about 1,770 m, with a viewpoint over the Brixental and the Hohe Tauern.
  • Alpinolino: a discovery area for children with nature and wildlife trails.
  • Walking and cycling on more than 250 km of signposted paths through the Windautal and across the alpine pastures.
  • Skiing in the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental in winter.
  • A stroll through the village with its flower displays.

From Niederau

About 35 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and then along the Brixental towards Kitzbühel.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Westendorf part of the same ski area as Niederau?

No. Westendorf belongs to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, Niederau to the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau. They are separate ski areas with their own passes; check the current arrangements beforehand.

What is there for children to do in Westendorf in summer?

The Alpenrosenbahn takes you to Alpinolino on the Talkaser, a discovery area for children. In summer the lift runs roughly from May to November; check the current times.
