Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

Jochberg

A mountain village between Kitzbühel and Pass Thurn with an old copper mine

Jochberg lies at about 924 m in the heart of the Kitzbühel Alps, halfway between Kitzbühel and Pass Thurn (1,274 m). It is a village shaped by farming and tourism and is quieter than its famous neighbour.

In summer there is a forest pool on the edge of the village and more than 60 km of marked hiking trails. A cable car takes you to the Kupferplatte show mine, where copper was mined 3,000 years ago; above ground you can follow the Miners' Path. St. Michael's Church is Jochberg's oldest church and has paintings by Simon Faistenberger. An hour's walk leads to the Sintersbach waterfall.

In winter Jochberg belongs to KitzSki and is linked via the Resterhöhe with the Kitzbühel-Kirchberg ski area. From Niederau it makes a pleasant day trip through the Brixental, with the mine and the mountain as highlights. For the mine tour it is wise to book ahead and bring a warm jumper, as the tunnels are cool. Check the current cable car times.

What to see and do

  • Kupferplatte show mine: cable car and guided tours of the old copper mining.
  • The Miners' Path above the mine with information on mining history.
  • The Sintersbach waterfall, about an hour's walk.
  • The forest pool on the edge of the village in summer.
  • Berggasthof Resterhöhe at about 1,730 m; reached by lift in winter, on foot, by bike or shuttle in summer.
  • The pilgrimage church of St. Michael with paintings by Simon Faistenberger.

From Niederau

About 60 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, through the Brixental via Kitzbühel to Jochberg.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Kupferplatte?

A show mine reached by cable car; copper was mined here about 3,000 years ago and guided tours are possible.

How do you get to the Resterhöhe?

By lift in winter; on foot, by bike or shuttle in summer. Check current schedules beforehand.
