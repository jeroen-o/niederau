Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

St. Jakob in Haus

Smallest municipality in the Pillerseetal, sunny and quiet, with Familienland

St. Jakob in Haus lies at roughly 855 m between Fieberbrunn and St. Ulrich am Pillersee. With around eight hundred residents it is the smallest municipality in the Kitzbühel district, and tourism is its main source of income.

The village is known as the sunniest in the Pillerseetal, in both summer and winter. That makes it a pleasant starting point for hikes, for example to the Buchensteinwand (1,558 m) with the Jakobskreuz. A pilgrimage route also passes through the village. Because of its modest size the village is easy to get around and quiet. It is not a place for nightlife, but it works well for a relaxed day in nature.

For families, Familienland Pillerseetal is a well-known attraction, with a family roller coaster, a rafting course, Viking land and a climbing hall. In winter you can cross-country ski on a sunny trail that continues via St. Ulrich to Hochfilzen. At the end of June the Stoabergmarsch takes place, a 24-hour hiking event heading towards Leogang. If you want to see more, combine your visit with nearby Fieberbrunn or the Pillersee at St. Ulrich, both only a few kilometres away.

What to see and do

  • Familienland Pillerseetal with a family roller coaster, rafting course, Viking land and climbing hall.
  • Hiking to the Buchensteinwand (1,558 m) and the Jakobskreuz.
  • Pilgrimage route and Besinnungsweg through the village.
  • Cross-country skiing on a sunny trail continuing to Hochfilzen.
  • Stoabergmarsch, a 24-hour hiking event at the end of June.

Frequently asked questions

What is Familienland Pillerseetal?

An adventure park for families with a family roller coaster, a rafting course, Viking land and a climbing hall. Check opening times and prices beforehand.

Can you cross-country ski from St. Jakob in Haus?

Yes, the trail is described as sunny and continues via St. Ulrich am Pillersee to Hochfilzen.
