Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

Ellmau

Village at the foot of the Wilder Kaiser, known from the TV series Der Bergdoktor

Ellmau lies in the Kufsteinerland, directly beneath the steep rock walls of the Wilder Kaiser. It is a tourist village with plenty of accommodation and an open valley view. German-speaking visitors know Ellmau because the TV series Der Bergdoktor is partly filmed here.

In winter Ellmau belongs to the Skiwelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental; the Hartkaiser cable car starts from the village. In summer there are walks to alpine huts, cycling routes and fine views of the Wilder Kaiser.

For families from Niederau, Ellmau is a lovely day trip if you want to see the Kaiser mountains without driving too far. The village is quieter than Kitzbühel and suits those who prefer walking to shopping. Check the current opening times of cable cars and huts beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 45 min.
  • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
  • Best forSuits walkers, families and fans of Der Bergdoktor.

What to see and do

  • The Hartkaiser cable car for summer walking and winter skiing.
  • Walks to alpine huts with views of the Wilder Kaiser.
  • Cycling through the valley around Ellmau.
  • Skiing in the Skiwelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental.
  • Bergdoktor filming locations in the village for fans of the series.

From Niederau

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Söll.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Ellmau well known?

For its location beneath the Wilder Kaiser and because the TV series Der Bergdoktor is partly filmed there.

Which ski area does Ellmau belong to?

Ellmau belongs to the Skiwelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental.
