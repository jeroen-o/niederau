    Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

    Ellmau

    Village below the Wilder Kaiser, known from the TV series Der Bergdoktor

    Ellmau lies at about 820 metres in the Sölllandl, on the sunny south side of the Wilder Kaiser. It is a tourist village with plenty of accommodation and a wide view of the steep rock faces. German-speaking visitors know Ellmau above all as the setting of the TV series Der Bergdoktor; in summer, tours of filming locations are organised on set days, roughly every other Tuesday.

    In winter the Ellmau-Going lift gives access to the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, with around 284 km of slopes in total. At the top station of the Hartkaiserbahn, a ten-seater gondola since 2015, you will find Ellmis Zauberwelt in summer: a play world for children with trampolines, swings, a castle with a slide, a maze and water features. Walkers can go from the Wochenbrunner Alm to the Gruttenhütte.

    The Kaiserbad swimming pool and a 27-hole golf course are also in the village. For a family from Niederau, Ellmau is a lovely day trip to see the Kaiser mountains without driving far; it is quieter than Kitzbühel. Check the current opening times of cable cars and huts beforehand.

    • Drive from Niederauapprox. 45 min.
    • Elevation820 m
    • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
    • Best forSuits walkers, families with children and fans of Der Bergdoktor.

    What to see and do

    • Hartkaiserbahn and Ellmis Zauberwelt: a children's play world at the top station (summer).
    • Walk from the Wochenbrunner Alm to the Gruttenhütte, with views of the Wilder Kaiser.
    • Tours of Bergdoktor filming locations in summer (roughly every other Tuesday).
    • Skiing in the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental via the Ellmau-Going lift.
    • The Kaiserbad pool and a 27-hole golf course in the village.

    From Niederau

    About 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and Söll.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is Ellmau well known?

    For its location beneath the Wilder Kaiser and because the TV series Der Bergdoktor is set and filmed there.

    Which ski area does Ellmau belong to?

    The SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental (around 284 km of slopes in total).
