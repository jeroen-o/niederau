    Zillertal

    Schlitters

    Quiet village on the Ziller with a bathing lake and the Zillertalbahn

    Schlitters sits in the lower part of the Zillertal, beside the Ziller river at roughly 548 m. It is a small village of a little over 1,400 residents, first mentioned in records as early as the year 936. The valley is still wide and sunny here, with gently sloping, wooded hillsides on either side.

    The village is not a big tourist hub, and that is part of its appeal. At the bathing lake, a leisure area of just over two hectares with a water surface of about one hectare, children can swim and play; there is also a playground and room for tennis, football and mini-golf. The parish church of St Martin dates from around 1500 and makes a quiet stop for anyone who wants to see the village itself.

    From Niederau, Schlitters is an easy half-day outing. It suits families with younger children looking for a relaxed afternoon by the water, and cyclists who want to ride a stretch of the Zillertal cycle path. You can combine it with a visit to one of the larger towns further up the valley.

    • Drive from Niederauapprox. 45 min.
    • Elevation548 m
    • RegionZillertal
    • Best forSuits families with young children and relaxed cyclists.

    What to see and do

    • The Schlitters bathing lake, with a playground, tennis, football and mini-golf, is a good summer stop with children.
    • The parish church of St Martin, from around 1500, is a quiet sight in the village.
    • The Zillertal cycle path runs past the lake and the Ziller and suits an easy, flat ride.
    • The Zillertalbahn narrow-gauge railway has a station in the village.
    • Hiking routes lead out of the village, for example towards the Larchkopf; a village festival is traditionally held in summer.

    From Niederau

    About 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and the A12 towards the Zillertal (Zillertal exit), then the road along the Ziller.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you swim in Schlitters?

    Yes, Schlitters has a bathing lake with a playground and sports facilities. Check current opening times and access conditions beforehand.

    How far is Schlitters from Niederau?

    Roughly 45 minutes by car via Wörgl and the A12, which makes a suitable half-day trip.
