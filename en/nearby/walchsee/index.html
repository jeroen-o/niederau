    Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

    Walchsee

    Village on one of Tyrol's warmest swimming lakes, in the Kaiserwinkl.

    Walchsee lies at about 670 m in the Kaiserwinkl, a corner of the Kufsteinerland between the Zahmer and Wilder Kaiser mountains, close to the German border. The village sits on a lake of roughly 100 hectares that often reaches 24 degrees or more in summer, making it one of the warmest swimming lakes in Tyrol.

    Around the water there are sunbathing lawns, a playground, a beach volleyball court, cafes with terraces and an aqua park with, among other things, a water trampoline. You can also row, surf and fish. Right next to the lake lies the Schwemm, at more than 65 hectares the largest continuous moor in North Tyrol, a quiet spot for a walk.

    For families staying in Niederau, Walchsee is above all a summer swimming day. The atmosphere is that of a small, easy-to-navigate village. Winter is quiet, with cross-country skiing, snow walking, sledging and some skiing nearby; check current conditions beforehand. In peak season it is wise to arrive early, as the best spots by the water fill up quickly. A picnic, towels and sun cream complete the day.

    What to see and do

    • Swimming in Lake Walchsee, with sunbathing lawns, playground and aqua park.
    • Rowing, surfing or fishing on the lake.
    • Walking in the Schwemm, the large moor beside the lake.
    • Summer: walking and cycling with views of the Kaiser mountains.
    • Winter: cross-country skiing, snow walking and sledging.

    From Niederau

    About 50 minutes by car from Niederau, roughly via Wörgl and Kufstein; plan the route beforehand.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Lake Walchsee suitable for swimming?

    Yes, it is a well-known swimming lake with good water quality and often reaches around 24 degrees in summer. There are sunbathing lawns and a playground.

    How far is Walchsee from Niederau?

    About 50 minutes by car, depending on traffic.
