    Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

    Reith bei Kitzbühel

    Quiet village close to Kitzbühel, with the Schwarzsee for swimming

    Reith bei Kitzbühel lies at roughly 762 m in the Kitzbühel Alps, about five kilometres from Kitzbühel. The village is quieter than its famous neighbour and has kept much of its traditional character.

    The best-known outing is the Schwarzsee, regarded as one of the warmest moorland lakes in Tyrol, reaching around 25 degrees in summer. The Gieringer Weiher and Vogelsberger Weiher are also swimming spots. Together they lie in the Bichlach walking and cycling area. Because the village is so close to Kitzbühel, you can combine a visit with a stroll through the town or an afternoon by the lake.

    The surroundings invite hiking and cycling, and a short walk leads to the Schleierwasserfall waterfall. In winter there are guided winter walks and a sledging run on the Gaisberg, popular with families. Kitzbühel itself is about ten minutes away by car. It is a good choice if you want a calm day without a long drive. Bring a towel and sun cream, and keep an eye on the weather forecast.

    What to see and do

    • Schwarzsee: a moorland lake that can reach about 25 degrees, with an outdoor pool and boat hire.
    • Gieringer Weiher and Vogelsberger Weiher as further swimming spots.
    • Schleierwasserfall, a short walk from the village.
    • Walking and cycling routes in the Bichlach area.
    • Sledging run on the Gaisberg and winter walks in winter.

    From Niederau

    About 35 minutes by car from Niederau, roughly via Hopfgarten and Kirchberg towards Kitzbühel. Check the current route before setting off.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you swim near Reith?

    Yes, the Schwarzsee, the Gieringer Weiher and the Vogelsberger Weiher are swimming spots. The Schwarzsee is known as one of the warmest lakes in the area.

    How far is Reith from Kitzbühel?

    About five kilometres, so around ten minutes by car.
