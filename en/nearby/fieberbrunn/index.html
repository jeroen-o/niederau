    Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

    Fieberbrunn

    Largest village in the Pillerseetal, with a ski area and Timoks Wilder Welt

    Fieberbrunn lies at about 790 m in the Pillerseetal, a side valley of the Kitzbühel Alps to the north-east of Kitzbühel. It is the largest municipality in the valley and a practical base for a day out from the Wildschönau.

    In winter, Fieberbrunn gives access to the Skicircus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn ski area. There are also cross-country trails and winter walks, which suit anyone who prefers to avoid the busy slopes. The valley feels calmer than the large tourist hubs nearby, which many families appreciate. Plan a full day out, especially if you want to combine several activities.

    In summer the surroundings mainly appeal to hikers and mountain bikers. Families can visit Timoks Wilder Welt, which offers an alpine coaster, water play, a climbing park and a rope course. If you want a quieter outing, you can combine the short trip to the nearby Pillersee with a visit to the Jakobskreuz. If you travel with children, you can spread the programme well: a morning at the adventure park, a picnic on the way and a short walk in the late afternoon.

    What to see and do

    • Timoks Wilder Welt: alpine coaster, water play, climbing park and rope course for children.
    • The Jakobskreuz and Antonius Chapel on the Buchensteinwand, billed as the world's largest accessible summit cross, open all year.
    • Hiking and mountain biking routes around the village in summer.
    • Skicircus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn for winter sports.
    • Cross-country skiing and winter walking in the Pillerseetal.

    From Niederau

    About 50 minutes by car from Niederau, roughly via Wörgl and Kitzbühel (B178/B161). Check the current route before setting off.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is there to do in Fieberbrunn with children?

    In summer, Timoks Wilder Welt with its alpine coaster, water play, climbing park and rope course is a popular choice. In winter there are the ski area and winter walking routes.

    At what altitude is Fieberbrunn?

    The village lies at about 790 m, slightly lower than Niederau (828 m).
