Ruhpolding

Day trip to Bavaria with leisure park, Rauschberg and biathlon arena

Ruhpolding lies in the Chiemgau in Bavaria, Germany, at around 800 m, just across the border from Tyrol. It is a holiday village for both summer and winter, surrounded by mountains and forest, and a good choice for a day in Germany from Niederau.

For families, the best-known address is the Freizeitpark Ruhpolding, a leisure park with around sixty attractions that is usually open from Easter to All Saints' Day. If you prefer the mountains, head for the Rauschberg, with its views and walking routes for different abilities. In the village itself you will find the local history museum.

In winter, Ruhpolding is known for the Chiemgau Arena, which hosts biathlon World Cups and World Championships. It is a relaxed outing with plenty of choice, even when the weather changes. Check the park's opening times beforehand, as they can vary from year to year. Ruhpolding is a good alternative when it rains in Tyrol, as the park and museum draw many visitors and there is also something to do indoors. Bring ID and check the current opening times of the mountain lift.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 90 min.
  • Elevation799 m
  • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
  • Best forSuits families with children who want to combine a leisure park and a mountain outing.

What to see and do

  • Freizeitpark Ruhpolding: leisure park with around 60 attractions, usually open from Easter to All Saints' Day.
  • Rauschberg: a viewpoint mountain with walking routes for different abilities.
  • Chiemgau Arena: venue for biathlon World Cups and World Championships in winter.
  • Local history museum in the village on the region's past and traditions.
  • Walking and cycling in the forests and valleys around Ruhpolding in summer.

From Niederau

About 1 hour 30 minutes by car from Niederau: via Kufstein and Kössen towards the Chiemgau (Germany). Bring your passport or ID card for the border.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
