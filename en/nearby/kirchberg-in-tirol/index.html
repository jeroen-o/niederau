Kirchberg in Tirol

Lively village in the Brixental, at the mouth of the Spertental

Kirchberg in Tirol lies at about 860 metres in the Brixental, where the Spertental joins the main valley, a few kilometres west of Kitzbühel. With more than 5,000 inhabitants it is one of the larger villages in the region, with shops, restaurants and a centre that is lively all year round. The parish church, dating from the thirteenth century, is dedicated to Saint Ulrich.

In winter the Gaisbergbahn starts from the village towards the Kitzbühel ski area. In summer you can combine the same mountain with a walk: the Gaisberg is about 1,770 metres high and can be reached on foot or by gondola. In winter there is also a toboggan run that is lit up once a week. The Spertental nature reserve offers quiet walks, including a Kneipp facility, and a wide network of running and Nordic walking routes runs through the valley.

For a family from Niederau, Kirchberg combines easily with Kitzbühel: the village is livelier than Westendorf but less busy than Kitzbühel itself. There is also a bathing lake, which is used for summer events as well. Check opening times and current programmes beforehand.

What to see and do

  • Take the Gaisbergbahn up the Gaisberg (about 1,770 m): walking in summer, skiing and tobogganing in winter.
  • Quiet family walks in the Spertental nature reserve, including a Kneipp facility.
  • Running and Nordic walking routes through the valley (a network of about 88 km).
  • The old parish church dedicated to Saint Ulrich in the village centre.
  • The Kirchberg bathing lake in summer.

From Niederau

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and through the Brixental.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Kirchberg from Kitzbühel?

The two places are a few kilometres apart; by car it is a short drive of roughly ten minutes.

Is Kirchberg worth a visit in summer too?

Yes, the village is a starting point for walking and cycling trips through the Brixental, the Spertental and up the Gaisberg; there is also a bathing lake.
