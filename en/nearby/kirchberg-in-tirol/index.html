Kirchberg in Tirol

Lively village in the Brixental, close to Kitzbühel

Kirchberg in Tirol lies in the Brixental, a short distance from Kitzbühel. It is one of the larger and livelier villages in the valley, with shops, restaurants and a centre that is active all year round. The village lies in the Kitzbühel Alps.

In winter Kirchberg is a ski village: the Gaisberg lift starts from the village and gives access to the ski area around Kitzbühel. In summer you can walk and cycle from the village into the valley; the side valley Spertental is a quiet option for a family walk.

For families from Niederau, Kirchberg combines well with Kitzbühel: spend part of the day in the village and then drive on. It is livelier than Westendorf but less busy than Kitzbühel itself. Check opening times of lifts and shops beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 45 min.
  • RegionBrixental and Kitzbühel Alps
  • Best forSuits families and skiers wanting to combine Kitzbühel with a village day.

What to see and do

  • Winter skiing via the Gaisbergbahn towards the Kitzbühel ski area.
  • Summer walking and cycling in the Brixental.
  • A family walk in the quiet side valley Spertental.
  • Shopping and a bite to eat in the village centre.
  • Easy to combine with a visit to Kitzbühel.

From Niederau

About 45 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl and through the Brixental.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Kirchberg from Kitzbühel?

The two places are close together; by car it is a short drive of a few minutes.

Is Kirchberg worth a visit in summer too?

Yes, the village is a starting point for walking and cycling trips through the Brixental and the Spertental.
