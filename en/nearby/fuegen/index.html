Fügen

Gateway to the Zillertal with Spieljoch, Hochfügen and family fun

Fügen lies at the entrance to the Zillertal, a wide, sunny valley with plenty of facilities for families. The village sits in the valley and has its own mountain, the Spieljoch, reachable by cable car in summer and winter.

From Fügen you also reach the Hochfügen ski area and are close to Hochzillertal; together these are often referred to as one region. In summer there are walking trails, mountain-bike routes and a well-known indoor pool nearby.

Fügen suits families who want a varied day in the mountains without too long a journey: the drive from Niederau is relatively short via Wörgl and the Zillertal road. Please check current lift operations and opening times beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 50 min.
  • Best forSuits families, summer walking and winter skiing.

What to see and do

  • Spieljoch: cable car with walks and views in summer.
  • Skiing and snowboarding at Hochfügen and nearby in winter.
  • Walking and mountain biking in the side valleys of the Zillertal.
  • Zillertal cycle path along the Ziller for a relaxed ride.
  • Swimming pools and spa facilities in the region for a rainy day.

From Niederau

About 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, the A12 and the Zillertal exit (Strass) towards Fügen.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Things to do in and around here

Hiking in Hochfügen

Summer Quiet hiking above the Zillertal at around 2,000 metres

Frequently asked questions

What can you do in Fügen?

Take the cable car up the Spieljoch, walk, cycle and in winter ski at Hochfügen.

How long is the drive from Niederau to Fügen?

About 50 minutes.
