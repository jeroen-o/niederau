    Zillertal

    Fügen

    Main village of the lower Zillertal with Spieljoch and the Erlebnistherme

    Fügen lies at the entrance to the Zillertal, at about 545 m, and with more than 4,000 inhabitants is the main village of the lower part of the valley. It has a reputation as a family-friendly holiday destination and belongs to the Erste Ferienregion im Zillertal.

    From the village a modern ten-person gondola takes you up the Spieljoch (1,858 m), an adventure mountain aimed at families. Near the top station you will find an adventure playground, a water play park, a children's climbing park and a show mine, among other things. In winter the ski area runs on via Hochfügen, at about 1,500 m, to Hochzillertal. In the valley Fügen has a local-history museum and the Erlebnistherme Zillertal with slides, a wave pool and a children's area, a good alternative when it rains.

    For families from Niederau, Fügen is an easy and varied day trip, in summer and winter. Check opening days of the lift and the pool beforehand.

    What to see and do

    • Erlebnisberg Spieljoch (1,858 m): gondola, adventure playground, water play park, climbing park and show mine.
    • Monsterroller and MountainCart: guided summer descent of the Spieljoch, from about 10 years.
    • Erlebnistherme Zillertal: slides, wave pool and a children's area, all year round.
    • Skiing in winter in the Hochfügen-Hochzillertal area and on the Spieljoch.
    • Local-history museum in the village and the pilgrimage church of St. Pankraz near Uderns.
    • Cycling and walking in the flatter part of the Zillertal and its side valleys.

    From Niederau

    About 50 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, the A12 and the Zillertal exit (Strass) towards Fügen.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What can you do in Fügen with children?

    Take the gondola up the Spieljoch (adventure playground, water play park, climbing park), visit the Erlebnistherme Zillertal, and ski in winter.

    How long is the drive from Niederau to Fügen?

    About 50 minutes.
