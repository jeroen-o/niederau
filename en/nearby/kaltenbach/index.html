Zillertal

Kaltenbach

Zillertal village with Hochzillertal, the Murmelland and the Höhenstraße

Kaltenbach is a village in the Zillertal at about 558 m, a few kilometres from the A12. It belongs to the Erste Ferienregion im Zillertal, together with Fügen, Ried and Hochfügen among others. The village itself is rural and quiet; the real draw is the mountain above it.

From Kaltenbach the Hochzillertal cable car climbs to the Hochzillertal area. Since 2004 this ski area has been linked with Hochfügen, together offering around 86 km of pistes up to almost 2,400 m. In summer the higher ground is a walking area along the Zillertaler Höhenstraße. At the Kaltenbacher Skihütte, at roughly 1,800 m, you will find the Murmelland with marmots, a playground, a small petting zoo and a water play area. The village also has a stop on the Zillertalbahn, the steam railway between Jenbach and Mayrhofen.

For a family from Niederau, Kaltenbach suits a ski day or a walking day with children. Check lift operating times and snow conditions beforehand.

What to see and do

  • Skiing in Hochzillertal-Hochfügen: about 86 km of pistes, linked since 2004.
  • Murmelland Zillertal at the Kaltenbacher Skihütte (about 1,800 m): marmots, playground and water play area.
  • Walking in the Hochzillertal area along the Zillertaler Höhenstraße in summer.
  • The Zillertalbahn steam railway between Jenbach and Mayrhofen; Kaltenbach has a stop.
  • Cycling and e-biking in the valley.

From Niederau

About 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, the A12 and the Zillertal road towards Kaltenbach.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Which ski area lies above Kaltenbach?

Hochzillertal, which has been linked with Hochfügen since 2004 and together has about 86 km of pistes.

What is the Murmelland?

A marmot park at the Kaltenbacher Skihütte at about 1,800 m, with a playground, small petting zoo and water play area.
