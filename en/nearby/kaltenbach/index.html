Kaltenbach

Zillertal village with the Hochzillertal ski area and summer walks

Kaltenbach is a village in the Zillertal, on the left side of the valley between Fügen and Ried. Above the village lies the Hochzillertal ski area, which together with Hochfügen and Spieljoch forms a large area.

In winter guests come mainly to ski; in summer it is a place for walking and quietly enjoying nature. The village itself is small and rural, without a busy main street.

For a family from Niederau, Kaltenbach is mainly interesting as a ski-day destination or a walking day in the Zillertal. Check current lift information and snow conditions beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 55 min.
  • Best forSuits skiing and walking in the quiet Zillertal.

What to see and do

  • Skiing in Hochzillertal, part of the linked area with Hochfügen and Spieljoch.
  • Walking on the slopes above the village in summer.
  • Cycling along the Ziller on the Zillertal cycle path.
  • A short visit to the village for lunch or coffee.

From Niederau

About 55 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, the A12 and the Zillertal road towards Kaltenbach.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Which ski area lies above Kaltenbach?

Hochzillertal, which together with Hochfügen and Spieljoch forms a larger area.

How far is Kaltenbach from Niederau?

About 55 minutes by car.
