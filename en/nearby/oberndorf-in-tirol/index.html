Oberndorf in Tirol

Quiet village below the Kitzbüheler Horn with an outdoor pool and walking trails

Oberndorf in Tirol lies at roughly 687 m in the Leukental valley, between the Kitzbüheler Horn and the Kaiser mountains. It is a quiet village of about 2,000 residents, close to Kitzbühel and St. Johann in Tirol. The municipal area rises up to the slopes of the Kitzbüheler Horn, one of the best-known mountains in the Kitzbühel Alps.

The village has a few distinctive sights. At the Müllnerhof stands an exceptionally large wooden fountain, carved from a single big tree. The local mining past on the Rerobichl is a reminder that silver and copper were extracted here for centuries. The parish church and several chapels are listed monuments that are worth a look.

In summer the Bichlachbad, an outdoor pool with a large sunbathing lawn and a long water slide, is a firm favourite with children. Walkers will find trails towards the Kitzbüheler Horn, with views of the Wilder Kaiser, the Hohe Tauern and the Karwendel. Oberndorf suits families who want to combine a relaxed day with swimming, a walk and a visit to nearby Kitzbühel.

What to see and do

  • Bichlachbad: an outdoor pool with a large lawn and a long water slide, a pleasant family stop in summer.
  • Wooden fountain at the Müllnerhof: an unusually large fountain carved from a single tree.
  • The mining history on the Rerobichl, where silver and copper were once extracted, with a small museum-style reminder.
  • Parish church (Philip and James), the Rerobichl chapel and Leopold Seeber's war memorial are listed monuments in the village.
  • Walking towards the Kitzbüheler Horn with views of the Wilder Kaiser, Hohe Tauern and Karwendel.
  • Kitzbühel is close by for shopping, a stroll through the old town or a day of skiing in winter.

From Niederau

From Niederau about 45 minutes by car, via Wörgl and Kitzbühel (or via Hopfgarten and Kirchberg, depending on traffic and road conditions). Check the route beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
