Breitenbach am Inn

Sunny Inn valley municipality with the Berglsteinersee and the Blessenberg

Breitenbach am Inn lies at about 510 m on the sunny north side of the Inn valley, between Kufstein and Schwaz, surrounded by rolling hills. The municipality has nearly 3,500 residents and, at around 38 km², is fairly extensive. It is a quiet village without big tourist crowds, a short drive from Niederau.

On the western edge of the municipality lies the idyllic Berglsteinersee. Walkers can climb the Blessenberg (1,743 m) for a fine view over the Inn valley. There are also the parish church of St Peter and several listed chapels, such as the Mariahilf chapel, the Kämpfer chapel, the Antonius chapel and the Firstwald chapel.

In winter there are cross-country trails near Schönau-Angerberg and a family-friendly ski area in the Alpbachtal Wildschönau region. In December a Perchtenlauf takes place, an old folk tradition with costumes and masks. Breitenbach suits families who want a quiet outing with a lake, a walk and local tradition.

What to see and do

  • Berglsteinersee: an idyllic small lake on the western edge of the municipality.
  • Blessenberg (1,743 m): a hike with views over the Inn valley.
  • Parish church of St Peter and chapels such as Mariahilf, Kämpfer, Antonius and Firstwald.
  • Perchtenlauf in December: a traditional procession with costumes and masks.
  • Cross-country trails near Schönau-Angerberg in winter.

From Niederau

From Niederau about 30 minutes by car, via Wörgl and the Inn valley towards Kufstein. Check the route beforehand.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do in Breitenbach am Inn?

Hiking on the Blessenberg, visiting the Berglsteinersee and the chapels in the village; in winter there are cross-country trails nearby.

When is the Perchtenlauf?

In December. The exact date varies each year; check with the municipality or tourist office beforehand.
