Zell am See

Town in Salzburgerland on Lake Zell, with the Schmittenhöhe as its mountain.

Zell am See is in Salzburgerland, on the shore of Lake Zell and at the foot of the Schmittenhöhe. The Kitzsteinhorn glacier is nearby. The mix of lake, mountain and glacier makes the Zell am See-Kaprun region popular in both summer and winter.

The Schmittenhöhe ranges from about 757 to 1,978 m and offers views over the lake. In winter it is a ski area with almost 80 km of pistes; in summer there are walks and viewpoints. On the lake you can swim, walk and boat.

For families staying in Niederau, Zell am See is a day trip of about 1 hour 15 minutes by car. It is in Salzburgerland, so outside Tyrol, but the drive is manageable. Plan a day for lake and mountain, or choose the cable car on a clear day. The official Zell am See-Kaprun tourism site has current operating times and prices. Allow enough time, bring warm clothing and a packed lunch, and check the weather forecast beforehand so the trip is worthwhile for everyone.

What to see and do

  • Lake Zell: swimming, walking and boating in summer.
  • Schmittenhöhe (757 to 1,978 m) with views over the lake.
  • Schmittenhöhe ski area with almost 80 km of pistes in winter.
  • Kitzsteinhorn glacier nearby, a day trip in its own right.
  • Walks along the lake and through the town centre.

From Niederau

From Niederau about 1 hour 15 minutes by car; the route runs via Wörgl and Kitzbühel towards Salzburgerland.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zell am See in Tyrol?

No, it is in Salzburgerland, about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Niederau.

How big is the Schmittenhöhe ski area?

It has almost 80 km of pistes and ranges from about 757 to 1,978 m.
