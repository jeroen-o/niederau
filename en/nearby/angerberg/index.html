    Kufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser

    Angerberg

    Sunny mountain terrace above the Inn valley with chapels and an ice cave

    Angerberg is a municipality on a sunny terrace above the Inn valley, at about 650 m. It lies in the Kufsteinerland, around 2.7 km north of Wörgl and about 14 km southwest of Kufstein. It is a scattered, rural village.

    What makes it special is the mix of views, quiet and walking options. Many chapels line the paths, and on the high plateau above the village lies the Hundalm ice and stalactite cave. According to the sources available, it is a publicly accessible ice cave in western Austria. The Adlerweg trail also runs through the area.

    For families, Angerberg is best for walks with a goal, such as a visit to the cave, or a quiet circuit to the pilgrimage church of Mariastein. From Niederau it takes about 25 minutes via Wörgl. There are few facilities in the village itself, so plan the day in advance and bring water and snacks. The cave lies high up, so warm clothing is sensible even in summer. Ask the tourist office about the season and opening times, as these can change from year to year.

    • Drive from Niederauapprox. 25 min.
    • Elevation650 m
    • RegionKufsteinerland and Wilder Kaiser
    • Best forSuits families and hikers looking for quiet, views and an unusual cave.

    What to see and do

    • The Hundalm ice and stalactite cave (entrance at about 1,520 m) is a special goal for hikers; ask ahead about opening times.
    • Numerous chapels along the walking paths give the area its own character.
    • The pilgrimage church of Mariastein can be reached on a short family circuit across the terrace.
    • The Adlerweg trail runs through the area for those who want a longer trek.
    • In summer the plateau offers walks with views over the Inn valley.
    • In winter it is good for quiet walks; check snow conditions beforehand.

    From Niederau

    About 25 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then north up to the Angerberg terrace.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the Hundalm ice cave?

    An ice and stalactite cave on the plateau above Angerberg, reportedly a publicly accessible ice cave in western Austria. Check opening times beforehand.

    How far is Angerberg from Wörgl and Kufstein?

    About 2.7 km north of Wörgl and roughly 14 km southwest of Kufstein.
