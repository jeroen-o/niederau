Zillertal

Aschau im Zillertal

Quiet Zillertal village with a castle, camping and the Höhenstraße

Aschau im Zillertal lies in the middle section of the Zillertal at about 560 metres. The village belongs to the Erste Ferienregion im Zillertal, known for its family-friendly offering. The Zillertalbahn, a narrow-gauge railway from 1902 between Jenbach and Mayrhofen, stops in the village.

Its best-known buildings are the late-classical parish church Maria vom Siege and Aschau Castle on a hill, with views over the valley. In the centre there is an access point to the Zillertaler Höhenstraße, a 48-kilometre panoramic road that is also a starting point for walks and cycle rides. Aschau is also known for its campsites, counted among the better ones in Europe.

A traditional event in the area is the Zillertaler Gauderfest. From Niederau, Aschau makes a good half or full day: combine a ride on the Zillertalbahn with a walk or a visit to nearby villages. In winter the ski areas Hochzillertal-Hochfügen and Zillertal Arena are close. Allow plenty of time for the drive through the valley, as traffic in the Zillertal can be busy in high season and at weekends; an early start is best.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 50 min.

  • Best forSuits families, campers and fans of quiet villages.

What to see and do

  • Aschau Castle on its hill with views over the valley.
  • Parish church Maria vom Siege, late classical.
  • A ride on the historic Zillertalbahn (Jenbach - Mayrhofen).
  • Zillertaler Höhenstraße: 48 km panoramic road for walking and cycling.
  • Winter: Hochzillertal-Hochfügen and Zillertal Arena nearby.

From Niederau

About 50 minutes by car from Niederau: via Wörgl and the A12 to Jenbach, then into the Zillertal towards Mayrhofen.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Zillertalbahn stop in Aschau?

Yes, the narrow-gauge railway between Jenbach and Mayrhofen stops in Aschau.

What is the Zillertaler Höhenstraße?

A panoramic road of about 48 km above the valley, accessible from Aschau among other places; popular with walkers and cyclists.
