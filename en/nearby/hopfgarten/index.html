Brixental and Kitzbühel Alps

Hopfgarten im Brixental

Brixental village with a cable car to the Hohe Salve

Hopfgarten im Brixental lies at the entrance to the Brixental, between Wörgl and Kitzbühel. The village is one of the places in the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental ski area and sits at the foot of the Hohe Salve, a well-known viewpoint mountain.

In winter the lifts into the ski area start here. In summer the surroundings are good for hiking and cycling, and the Hohe Salve has a chapel with a well-known panorama. There is also plenty nearby for children.

From Niederau, Hopfgarten is close enough for a day out, for example to ski another side of the SkiWelt or to head up the Hohe Salve on a summer day. Check current lift operating times beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 30 min.
  • RegionBrixental and Kitzbühel Alps
  • Best forSuits families who want to ski or hike with views.

What to see and do

  • Ski in the SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental (winter)
  • Hike or ride up the Hohe Salve for panoramic views (summer)
  • Hiking and cycling in the Brixental
  • Village stroll and a drink on a terrace
  • Combine with Itter or Brixen im Thale

From Niederau

About 30 minutes by car from Niederau: via Oberau to Wörgl, then through the Brixental towards Kitzbühel. Estimate.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.

Yes, it is one of the villages of SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental.

What is the Hohe Salve?
