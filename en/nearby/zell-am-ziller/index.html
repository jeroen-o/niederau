    Zillertal

    Zell am Ziller

    Zillertal brewery and gold village with the Gauderfest and Zillertal Arena

    Zell am Ziller lies in the heart of the Zillertal and is one of the valley's best-known villages. Above it begins the Zillertal Arena, a ski area with more than 140 km of pistes that turns into walking and mountain-biking terrain in summer.

    The best-known event is the Gauderfest, held for centuries on the first weekend of May and part of Austria's UNESCO intangible cultural heritage. The parade takes place on the first Sunday of May and draws around 20,000 visitors. For the festival the Zillertal Bier brewery makes the Gauderbock, a 7.8 per cent bock beer available only briefly. The brewery itself has existed for more than 500 years.

    Families will find, nearby, the Rosenalm with its Spruce Castle, and the Goldschaubergwerk, which shows the region's gold-mining past. For a family from Niederau, Zell is a day trip in summer and winter; check current dates and opening times beforehand.

    What to see and do

    • Gauderfest on the first weekend of May: a traditional festival with parade, Zillertal customs and Gauderbock beer.
    • Zillertal Arena: ski area with more than 140 km of pistes for all levels.
    • Walking and mountain biking from the village in summer.
    • Rosenalm with the Spruce Castle, a fantasy world for children.
    • Goldschaubergwerk: Zell's gold-mining history, which can be combined with a hike.

    From Niederau

    About 60 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, the A12 and the Zillertal road; the route can be busy in high season.

    Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
