Zell am Ziller

Zillertal's main village with the Gauderfest, a brewery and Zillertal Arena skiing

Zell am Ziller lies in the middle of the Zillertal and is regarded as one of the valley's main villages. It sits at the foot of the ski area belonging to the Zillertal Arena, which also includes Gerlos and Königsleiten.

The Gauderfest, a traditional beer festival held in spring, is its best-known event. The village also has a brewery; the Zillertal has a strong tradition of regional products.

For a family from Niederau, Zell suits a day trip in summer or winter, for instance by cable car or with a visit to the village. Check current Gauderfest dates and opening times beforehand.

  • Drive from Niederauapprox. 60 min.
  • Best forSuits tradition, skiing and family day trips.

What to see and do

  • Gauderfest in spring: a traditional beer festival with Zillertal customs.
  • Zillertal Arena: ski area with slopes for different levels in winter.
  • Walking and cycling from the village in summer.
  • Brewery and regional products in the village.
  • Cable car from the village for views (check current details beforehand).

From Niederau

About 60 minutes by car from Niederau, via Wörgl, the A12 and the Zillertal road; the route can be busy in high season.

Always check times, prices and opening dates with the provider beforehand; they can change.
