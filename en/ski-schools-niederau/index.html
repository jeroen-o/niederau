    Ski schools in Niederau and Oberau

    Ski lessons in the Wildschönau: the ski schools in Niederau and Oberau and what to look for when choosing.

    Anyone learning to ski in Niederau, or wanting to improve their technique, can choose from several ski schools in the valley. This page lists the schools that the Wildschönau tourist board names in its regional overview for Niederau and Oberau. It is not a complete list and not an advertisement: there are also ski schools in Auffach and in the neighbouring Alpbachtal.

    Prices, group sizes and lesson times differ per school and per season. They are deliberately not given here; ask the ski school directly.

    The ski schools

    • 1st Ski School Wildschönau (Niederau): a ski school with tradition that teaches skiing safely and professionally
    • Active Ski School Niederau: lessons for all age groups
    • Alpin ski school (Oberau): professional ski lessons

    Source: regional overview of the Wildschönau tourist board. The names may be spelled slightly differently in practice.

    What to look for when choosing

    Book well in advance in high season, around Christmas and in the spring holidays, because groups fill up quickly. Ask which language the instructor speaks and how large the groups are, especially for children. A group lesson is often enough for beginners; a private lesson pays off if you want to progress quickly or if your child is nervous.

    Also ask where the lesson starts and ends, how the lunch break is arranged and whether a helmet comes with the lesson or has to be rented separately. For children a helmet on the slope is strongly recommended.

    Close to the slopes

    In Niederau the ski area starts at the Markbachjoch gondola and at the lift on the Lanerköpfl. Guests at Hotel Wastlhof are right by the slope with the drag lift in front of the door and can practise their first metres there. You make arrangements with a ski school yourself.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which ski schools are there in Niederau?

    The tourist board lists the 1st Ski School Wildschönau and the Active Ski School in Niederau, among others. In Oberau there is the Alpin ski school.

    What does a ski lesson cost?

    Prices differ per school and season and are therefore not on this page. Ask the ski school directly.
