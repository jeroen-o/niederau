    What does a ski pass cost in Niederau? Indicative Ski Juwel prices 2026/27

    An overview of roughly what a lift pass for Niederau and Ski Juwel costs, and how to save.

    Niederau lies in the Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau ski area. One ski pass lets you ski the whole area. This page gives indicative prices for the 2026/27 high season, as they stood with third parties at the end of July 2026. Ski Juwel itself sets the exact prices; always check them on the official site before you book or buy.

    Indicative prices, high season 2026/27

    According to the Skiinfo price list, which is based on the ski area’s data (as of 23 July 2026), a day pass in the high season costs roughly:

    • Adult (and senior): € 71.50 per day
    • Youth (born 2008 to 2010): € 57.50 per day
    • Child (born 2011 to 2020): € 36.00 per day
    • Children born in 2021 or later: free, subject to the applicable terms of carriage
    • Two-day pass, adult: € 138.50
    • Week pass, adult: € 403.00 (the source does not state the number of days)

    These are indicative third-party prices, not an offer. Prices may change during the season and differ by period.

    What determines the price?

    The price depends on the period (high season or outside it), your age (for children the year of birth counts), the number of days and whether you buy in advance. The sources give different dates for the high season; Ski Juwel states the exact periods on its own site. Without buying in advance you usually pay the standard price at the ticket office.

    How to save on your lift pass

    Start with Ski Juwel’s own offer: there are advance sales, multi-day passes and a season pass for frequent visitors. On a family holiday compare the price per child, because the youngest children ski free. Note that the Wildschönau Card does not include the ski pass; it covers other benefits. And ask your hotel or tour operator whether a ski pass is part of the package or can be ordered at a discount.

    Official prices and buying in advance

    The current, binding prices and the advance sale are on Ski Juwel’s own site.

    • Ski Juwel: tickets and prices (advance sale)
    • Skiinfo: Ski Juwel ski pass prices (source of the indicative prices above)

    Frequently asked questions

    What does a day pass for Niederau cost?

    For the 2026/27 high season the Skiinfo price list (as of July 2026) gives € 71.50 for an adult and € 36.00 for a child (born 2011 to 2020). Check the current price with Ski Juwel.

    Do children ski free?

    According to the source, children born in 2021 or later travel free under the applicable terms of carriage. If in doubt, ask the lift office.

    Is the ski pass included in the Wildschönau Card?

    No. The Wildschönau Card gives other benefits, such as the ski bus and guided walks; you buy the ski pass separately.
